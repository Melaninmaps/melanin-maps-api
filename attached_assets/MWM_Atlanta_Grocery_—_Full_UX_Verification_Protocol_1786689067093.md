## Known Limitations to Note (Not Failures)

- **Nourish + Bloom** and **Goodr** have no phone numbers — these stores do not publish a phone line. This is correct and expected.
- **Goodr's website** (goodr.co) is the parent organization's main site (food waste solutions company). The Edgewood community market is one of their programs. The website loads correctly and is the authoritative URL for this business.
- **Sevananda** is technically a community co-op (member-owned), not a single-owner Black business. It has been on EatOkra (Black-owned business directory) since its founding and serves the Atlanta Black community. This designation has been confirmed by the MWM editorial team.
- **Nourish + Bloom** also has locations in Fayetteville, GA and other Atlanta neighborhoods (Freedom Pkwy, Pittsburgh Yards). Only the **Cascade Rd** Atlanta location is listed in this batch. Future batches may add the others as they become fully operational.
- Map coordinates for **Goodr** and **Wadada** are derived from address geocoding and may be approximate by a city block. Exact parcel-level coordinates will be refined in a future enrichment pass.
