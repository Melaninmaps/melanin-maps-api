08.13 Dimension Eight — Sustainability
Sustainability measures whether positive outcomes continue without constant intervention.
Indicators include:
recurring funding;
repeat participation;
institutional support;
volunteer continuity;
leadership succession;
documented processes.
08.14 Dimension Nine — Replicability
Replicability measures whether success can responsibly occur elsewhere.
The Model evaluates:
documented process;
adaptability;
cost;
accessibility;
cultural flexibility;
required expertise.
Not every catalyst should be replicated.
Some remain intentionally local.
08.15 Community Catalyst Formula
Catalyst Score =
(Opportunity Creation × .18)
+(Community Activation × .16)
+(Participation Growth × .15)
+(Relationship Multiplication × .12)
+(Resource Circulation × .11)
+(Community Momentum × .10)
+(Collaboration Quality × .08)
+(Sustainability × .06)
+(Replicability × .04)
Normalized to 0–100.
08.16 Catalyst Confidence
Every Catalyst Score includes a Confidence Score.
Confidence evaluates:
evidence quality;
outcome verification;
source independence;
community diversity;
duration of observation;
attribution confidence.
Labels:
Score	Confidence
90–100	Verified
80–89	High
65–79	Moderate
45–64	Emerging
20–44	Low
0–19	Unknown

08.18 Positive Multipliers
Positive multipliers may apply for:
serving historically underserved communities;
creating opportunities repeatedly;
measurable downstream impact;
intergenerational benefit;
cross-community collaboration;
high reciprocity;
long-term consistency;
institutional partnerships;
verified mentorship outcomes.
08.19 Catalyst Penalties
Penalties may apply for:
performative participation;
opportunity hoarding;
exclusionary practices;
extractive partnerships;
abandoned initiatives;
repeated unfulfilled commitments;
fabricated impact claims;
manipulated participation numbers;
repeated cancellation without explanation.
08.20 Momentum Decay
Momentum naturally decreases over time.
Rapid-decay examples:
one-time viral events;
temporary campaigns.
Slow-decay examples:
mentorship;
institutions;
recurring annual programs;
long-term community organizations.
Historical impact remains visible within the Legacy & Learning Engine™.
08.21 Business Catalyst Recognition
Businesses may qualify as Community Catalysts when they consistently:
hire locally;
mentor entrepreneurs;
host events;
collaborate with nonprofits;
support schools;
provide scholarships;
sponsor meaningful community activity;
create safe gathering spaces;
elevate other businesses.
Revenue alone does not qualify a business as a Community Catalyst.
08.22 Individual Catalyst Recognition
Individuals may qualify through:
mentorship;
volunteerism;
organizing;
connecting people;
preserving culture;
solving problems;
sharing resources;
supporting newcomers.
Follower count has minimal influence.
08.23 Organizational Catalyst Recognition
Organizations are evaluated by:
measurable outcomes;
community trust;
collaboration;
transparency;
continuity;
opportunity generation.
08.24 Anti-Gaming Rules
Manipulation includes:
fake attendance;
fake volunteer hours;
fabricated referrals;
duplicate participation;
purchased engagement;
coordinated nominations;
falsified outcomes;
misleading grant claims.
Manipulation reduces confidence before reducing scores.
