07.41 Cultural Ambassador Safety Rules
A Cultural Ambassador may be held from partnership or promotion when:
active serious safety concern exists;
harassment or exploitation is alleged credibly;
minors are placed at risk;
meetup practices are unsafe;
sponsorship conceals a safety issue;
false travel or location guidance could create harm;
emergency information is misrepresented;
retaliation occurs.
Safety Intelligence Engine™ outputs control safety treatment.
07.42 Community Protection Rules
The Engine must protect communities from:
cultural extraction;
false representation;
nonconsensual storytelling;
decontextualized trauma;
monetization without return;
erasure of local contributors;
displacement-producing promotion;
exploitative tourism;
appropriation;
misleading “authenticity” claims;
harmful exposure of sensitive places;
commodification of sacred or private practices.
07.43 Sensitive Cultural Knowledge
Certain cultural knowledge may be:
sacred;
private;
community-controlled;
location-sensitive;
vulnerable to exploitation;
inappropriate for mass promotion.
The Engine must support restrictions such as:
limited visibility;
no geotagging;
no commercial use;
community permission required;
educational context only;
no AI summarization;
no public reproduction;
archival protection.
07.44 Platform Promotion Treatment
A high Cultural Influence Score does not guarantee promotion.
Promotion requires:
BIF approval;
Trust threshold;
Safety threshold;
community evidence;
current disclosure;
no active hold;
context relevance;
promotion-policy compliance.
Paid promotion may occur only among already eligible entities.
07.45 Community Recognition Treatment
The Cultural Influence Engine™ may nominate an entity for:
Cultural Ambassador status;
Community Milestone™;
Community Recognition;
Hidden Gem review;
Living Legacy™ progression;
Legacy Ambassador review;
Cultural Stewardship recognition.
Final recognition is determined by the Community Recognition System™ and Governance.
