## My recommendation for how we work over the next few days

Rather than trying to build each deck from scratch, let's build the **Master Deck** first. Every slide should earn its place by answering a specific question and telling a compelling part of the story.

Once that foundation is complete, we can create the specialized decks by selecting and reordering slides, then adding audience-specific material where needed. That approach keeps every presentation consistent while making each one feel purpose-built.

I genuinely think what we're creating is much bigger than a pitch deck. We're building the communication system for Mapping with Melanin™—a single source of truth that will guide how investors, businesses, community members, partners, employees, and the media all come to understand and believe in your vision. I think that will become one of the company's most valuable assets because it ensures the story stays consistent as your team and the organization grow.
