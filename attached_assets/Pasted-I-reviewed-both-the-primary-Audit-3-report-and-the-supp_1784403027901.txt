# Three findings I consider the most serious

## 1. Community nominations immediately creating live businesses

This is my highest concern.

A community nomination should never bypass your carefully designed verification and stewardship process.

We've spent months designing:

* Business Verification
* Community Recognition
* Hidden Gems
* Trust Engine™
* Governance
* Stewardship

Allowing a nomination to immediately create an active listing undermines all of that. 

### My constitutional decision

A nomination should create a **Candidate Business**, not an active business.

Lifecycle:

```text
Community Nomination

↓

Candidate Business

↓

Review

↓

Owner Contact

↓

Owner Claim

↓

Verification

↓

Active Listing
```

That preserves community discovery without sacrificing accuracy.

---

## 2. Defaulting to `blackOwned = true`

I consider this a constitutional issue, not just a data bug.

No ownership designation should ever be inferred simply because a field was omitted. 

My rule would be:

> **Ownership is declared or verified. It is never assumed.**

If unknown:

```text
Ownership Status

Unknown
```

Not:

Black-owned.

---

## 3. Three disconnected submission paths

This is exactly the kind of thing audits are meant to find.

A business owner should never have to wonder which doorway is the "real" one. 

I would redesign this conceptually.

Everything becomes:

```text
Business Intake™

↓

How are you joining?

• I'm the owner.
• I'm nominating a business.
• I'm requesting ownership.
• I'm correcting an existing listing.
```

Different workflows.

One institution.

# One constitutional issue I'd elevate

The supplement identifies a conflict between your stated philosophy and the current promotion logic.

The code currently places paid promotion at the top of discovery while your constitutional principle says:

> **Trust cannot be purchased.** 

I don't think those ideas can coexist without refinement.

My recommendation is to establish this principle:

> **Promotion may increase visibility only among businesses that already satisfy the platform's trust requirements. Promotion cannot elevate a business above constitutional trust safeguards.**

In practice, that means a business that is under review, suspended, or failing trust standards should never be boosted because it paid.

# A new product decision

I want to introduce a concept I don't think we've explicitly named.

## Business Stewardship™

When a business enters the platform, Mapping With Melanin™ becomes a steward of that listing.

That stewardship includes responsibilities:

* accuracy;
* communication;
* ownership integrity;
* fair representation;
* correction pathways;
* respectful retirement when a business closes.

This is broader than moderation.

It's an institutional commitment.
