* what you tell investors
* what you tell businesses
* what you tell founding members
* what your Zoom presentation includes
* what testers should and should not expect

It also prevents a situation where a feature is described as "working" because the code exists, when in reality it hasn't been exercised on iPhone, Android, and web.

I would make one additional request to Replit:

> **For every feature marked "Complete," please state the evidence.** Was it confirmed by unit tests, Playwright, manual testing, physical iPhone testing, physical Android testing, or founder verification? Do not mark any feature as "Complete" based solely on code inspection.

Once we have that inventory, we'll know exactly where Mapping With Melanin™ stands today, and we can decide whether it's ready for more testers or whether a few critical gaps should be closed first. That gives us a factual basis for the decision rather than relying on assumptions or optimistic status reports.
