I actually think this is an important point for your mission.

It's saying:

> "We're making businesses that have historically been harder to discover easier to discover, while helping everyone navigate their communities safely and confidently."

Those are very different messages.

## Across the diaspora

This is something I think can become one of Mapping With Melanin™'s signatures.

Rather than trying to force one vocabulary everywhere, let the app naturally reflect the language of different communities.

For example:

| Region                             | Natural community language                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| United States                      | Vibe, Spot, Neighborhood Favorite, Hidden Gem                                                                                |
| Caribbean                          | Lime (gather socially), Reasoning (deep conversation, especially Jamaica), Fete (celebration), Yard (home/community)         |
| West Africa                        | Chop Spot (casual eatery), Compound (shared family/community space), Market Day                                              |
| South Africa                       | Braai (barbecue gathering), Ubuntu (community/humanity), Kasi (township/neighborhood)                                        |
| Brazil                             | Quilombo (historically significant Afro-Brazilian communities; use with care and context), Roda (circle/community gathering) |
| United Kingdom                     | Local, High Street Favorite, Community Hub                                                                                   |
| France (Afro-diaspora communities) | Quartier (neighborhood/community)                                                                                            |

I would be careful not to overuse these or apply them where they don't belong. Instead, KinfolkAI could recognize regional language or allow users to choose a preferred cultural voice over time.
