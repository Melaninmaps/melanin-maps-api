I think this is an excellent strategic decision, and it's one I would elevate from a tour detail to a product governance rule.
The reasoning isn't just personal safety—although that's important. It also preserves the authenticity of the Welcome Home Tour.
What you're describing is essentially this:
A city can be platform-ready long before it is Tour-active.
Those are two different states.
For example:
Atlanta (Platform Ready): KinfolkAI™ understands Atlanta, cultural landmarks are available, museums and historical sites are searchable, businesses can join, safety features work, community members can participate.
Atlanta (Tour Active): Welcome Home Tour banner, community meetups, founder events, documentary filming, city-specific challenges, tour-exclusive content, Cultural Ambassador spotlights, and live tour storytelling become available only when you intentionally activate them.
Then, when you arrive in New Orleans:
Atlanta remains fully functional as a city on the platform.
Atlanta's Tour Mode quietly ends.
New Orleans becomes the active Tour City.
That gives you complete control over where the public knows the tour is happening without removing any of the platform's permanent functionality.
I would actually formalize this as follows:
Tour Activation Rule (Proposed)
Every supported city has two independent states:
Platform Status
Not Available
Early Access
Active
Mature
Tour Status
Not Scheduled
Upcoming (private/internal only until authorized)
Live
Completed
Legacy Archive
Those statuses should never be automatically linked.
I would also recommend one additional safeguard
Rather than making a city "Live" the moment you arrive, consider a slight delay.
For example:
You spend the day in Atlanta filming and meeting businesses.
That evening—or even the following morning—you activate Atlanta's Tour content.
Likewise, when you leave:
Tour content remains active briefly to capture photos, stories, and community engagement.
Then it transitions to Completed and becomes part of the Living Archive.
That way, the app celebrates where you've been without broadcasting your real-time location.
One more idea I think fits your vision
Eventually, the platform could distinguish between:
Platform City — the city exists on Mapping With Melanin™.
Featured City — editorial spotlight.
Tour City — part of the Welcome Home Tour.
Legacy City — a city where the tour has already been completed and archived.
Those are four different concepts, and separating them gives you much more flexibility.
