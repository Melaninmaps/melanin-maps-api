# AUDIT 2 — ONBOARDING, PROFILES, IDENTITY & MEMBER JOURNEY

I actually want to raise the bar before we send this to Replit.

This audit isn't about screens.

It's about the **first 15 minutes** someone spends with Mapping With Melanin™.

If we get those 15 minutes right, everything else becomes easier.

---

# What I want this audit to answer

Not:

> Does onboarding work?

But:

> **Does onboarding create confidence, belonging, and excitement while collecting only the information we genuinely need?**

---

# Areas to audit

## PART A — First Impression

I want Replit to document:

* Every screen in exact order.
* What the user sees.
* What actions are available.
* What can be skipped.
* What cannot.
* How long it takes.

Then I want them to answer:

> **If someone had never heard of Mapping With Melanin™, would they understand what makes this platform different before creating an account?**

---

## PART B — Welcome Experience

This is one place where I already have a recommendation.

I don't want users to land immediately on a form.

I would rather see:

```text
Welcome Home.

You are joining a community built to help people discover
opportunity, connection, culture, and trusted places.

Let's personalize your experience.
```

Then continue.

People remember feelings before forms.

---

## PART C — Every Field Review

This is where I want us to become extremely disciplined.

For **every single field** collected during onboarding, Replit should answer:

### 1. Why do we collect it?

### 2. Is it legally required?

### 3. Is it required for functionality?

### 4. Could it be optional?

### 5. Could we ask later instead?

### 6. Does AI use it?

### 7. Does it affect recommendations?

### 8. Does it affect safety?

### 9. Is it displayed publicly?

### 10. Can the user change it later?

---

Example:

Birthday

Instead of asking:

Do we collect birthday?

Ask:

Why?

Maybe all we actually need is:

18+

21+

Under 18

Birth month (optional)

Birthday celebration (optional)

We should never collect more than we truly need.

# Constitutional Commentary

This will be my contribution to Audit 2.

> Onboarding is not an administrative process.
>
> It is the institution's first conversation with a new member.
>
> Every screen should answer an unspoken question:
>
> **"Do I belong here?"**
>
> Information should be requested only when there is a clear purpose, communicated honestly, protected responsibly, and used to create a better experience for the member.
>
> We do not collect information because we can.
>
> We collect only what helps us strengthen community, improve safety, personalize discovery, or fulfill a legal obligation.
>
> Trust begins before the first recommendation is ever made.

# One addition I'd like to make

I want every audit to end with a new section called:

## "The Human Test"

For Audit 2, that question is:

> **Imagine a 22-year-old college student, a 67-year-old retiree relocating to a new city, and a first-time business owner all complete onboarding. Would each of them understand what Mapping With Melanin™ is, why we asked for their information, and what to do next?**

If the answer is "no," then onboarding isn't finished—even if every screen works perfectly.
