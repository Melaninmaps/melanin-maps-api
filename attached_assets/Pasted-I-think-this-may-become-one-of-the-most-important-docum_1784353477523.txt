# Additional Recommendations for Counsel

In addition to identifying trade secrets, I would ask your attorney to advise on:

* **Trade secret governance** (NDAs, access controls, confidentiality policies, employee and contractor agreements).
* **Trademark strategy** for names like *KinfolkAI™*, *Community Ripple™*, *Community Echo™*, *Community Confidence™*, *Hidden Gem™*, and other branded concepts. Not every term will be registrable, but it's worth evaluating systematically.
* **Copyright strategy** for the Constitution, Legacy Collection, Storytelling System, and editorial materials.
* **Documentation practices** that demonstrate you've consistently treated these systems as confidential—important if you ever need to assert trade secret rights.
* **Defensive publication vs. secrecy** decisions for each innovation: some ideas may be stronger as protected know-how than as public disclosures.
