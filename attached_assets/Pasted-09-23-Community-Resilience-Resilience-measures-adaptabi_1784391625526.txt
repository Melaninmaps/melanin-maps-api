09.23 Community Resilience
Resilience measures:
adaptability;
redundancy;
mutual support;
emergency response;
institutional flexibility;
social cohesion.
Communities able to recover quickly score higher.
09.24 Intervention Priority
Communities may receive internal intervention priority based upon:
rapid decline;
severe inequity;
opportunity collapse;
resource failure;
leadership instability;
institutional distrust.
Intervention priority does not become a public ranking.
09.25 Community Improvement Recognition
Communities demonstrating meaningful improvement may qualify for:
Recovery Recognition;
Community Momentum acknowledgment;
Community Catalyst prioritization;
additional Opportunity recommendations.
Recognition is based on improvement rather than perfection.
09.26 Anti-Gaming Rules
Manipulation includes:
fabricated participation;
fake business growth;
manipulated surveys;
coordinated voting;
artificial engagement;
false institutional reporting.
Manipulation primarily reduces confidence.
09.27 Public Explanation Rules
Approved:
"Community participation has increased."
"Opportunity access has improved."
"Recovery efforts are showing measurable progress."
Prohibited:
"This city is unsafe."
"This neighborhood is failing."
"People should avoid this community."
"The algorithm has declared this community unhealthy."
09.28 Internal Output Contract
The Index returns:
Community Health Score;
Health Confidence;
Health State;
Momentum Classification;
Dimension Scores;
Intervention Priority;
Improvement Status;
Human Review Flag.

09.30 Human Review Requirements
Human review is required when:
rapid deterioration occurs;
evidence conflicts;
intervention recommendations are significant;
manipulation is suspected;
Governance thresholds are exceeded;
institutional disputes arise.
09.31 Constitutional Rules
The Community Health Index™ must:
reward improvement;
preserve dignity;
avoid stigmatization;
recognize recovery;
separate historical hardship from current progress;
remain evidence-based;
remain auditable;
support intervention—not judgment.
