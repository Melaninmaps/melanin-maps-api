### My recommendation on testing

**Do this final targeted coverage pass + compact browser regression, then approve the testers.**

I would **not** run another giant audit after that. At some point testing itself becomes the audit. You've already done an extraordinary amount of internal inspection; the next useful information comes from people who don't know how the platform is “supposed” to work.

Also, I would change the tester instructions Replit drafted before you send them. They currently tell testers exactly what to search and how things should work. That's useful for a structured second round, but **your first round should include some open-ended testing** so you can see what people naturally try. The current briefing instructs them to test specific searches such as braiding and Ethiopian food. 

I'd give testers two phases:

**First 20–30 minutes:** *“Use MWM however you naturally would. Don't worry about testing a checklist. Tell us what you tried, what confused you, what delighted you, and where you got stuck.”*

**Then structured testing:** give them the specific Map, Kinfolk, Library, Reviews, Vibes, media, Add-a-Place and safety exercises.

That way you don't accidentally train your testers to behave like Replit instead of behaving like users.

One security cleanup before distribution: the tester briefing in this document includes a shared first-time password in plain text.  I would have Replit issue individual temporary credentials or at minimum rotate that shared credential after this round rather than letting it become your standing tester password.
