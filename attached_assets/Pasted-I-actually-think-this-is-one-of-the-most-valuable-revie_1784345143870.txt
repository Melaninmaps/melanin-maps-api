## My recommendation for tomorrow's workflow

I think we've reached a point where the process itself is becoming as valuable as the edits. Here's the sequence I would use:

1. Merge **all three sets of notes** (Founder, First-Time Audience, Creative Director) into one master review document.
2. Review **one page at a time**, applying that four-part structure so no insight gets lost.
3. Let Replit produce **visual comparison mockups** where there are design choices, instead of jumping straight to implementation.
4. Approve each page only when all three perspectives align.
5. Once the Experience Chapter is complete, use that same review process for every future Presentation Chapter in the Legacy Collection.

One final thought about what Kevin wrote: the fact that he asked "Who is she?" and questioned wording instead of colors tells me he was engaging with the story, not just the design. That's exactly the kind of feedback you want from someone seeing the presentation for the first time. It means your review team is naturally covering different dimensions of the experience—and together, those perspectives are stronger than any one reviewer alone.
