I especially want the **Apple reviewer + tester login checks to remain permanent**, because the worst outcome is Replit successfully implementing something unrelated and silently breaking the exact authenticated pathway Apple is evaluating.

The other important change is that Replit now has a clear rule: **regression repair interrupts expansion**. If Kinfolk worked yesterday and stops after a Library migration, they do not move on to Community Intelligence and leave Kinfolk broken. They diagnose what the new change affected first.

That should eliminate a lot of the pattern we've been dealing with: *something gets built → Replit celebrates the build → you discover three screens later that something older disappeared.*
