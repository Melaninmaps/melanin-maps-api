MAPPING WITH MELANIN™ — CROSS-ROLE PROFILE PRIVACY,
COMMUNITY ACTIVITY VISIBILITY, AND SENSITIVE DISCLOSURE STANDARD

BRAINSTORMING AND DOCUMENTATION ONLY — NO IMPLEMENTATION

FOUNDER INTENT

Business Owners, Cultural Ambassadors, Community Organization
representatives, mentors, and professionals are also Community Members.

Their profiles should overlap enough to create meaningful community
understanding, trust, and impact—but not in ways that expose private
searches, saves, attendance, identity, location, family information,
anonymous reports, or other sensitive activity.

The platform must help people find safe and affirming places while
recognizing that the world does not yet consistently protect people who
are publicly identified with every community, event, belief, health
condition, or life circumstance.

Visibility must therefore be:

- Deliberate
- Granular
- Contextual
- Reversible
- Private by default for sensitive activity
- Technically enforced
- Never required for platform access

Do not implement.
Do not change schemas.
Do not alter profiles.
Do not begin collecting new activity.
Do not make prior private activity public.

1. ONE ACCOUNT, SEPARATE ROLE CONTEXTS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Document how one account may hold:

- Community Member
- Verified Community Member
- Business Owner
- Verified Business Owner
- Cultural Ambassador
- Community Organization representative
- Mentor
- Contributor
- Trusted Contributor
- Areas of Expertise

Define separate data and visibility contexts for:

A. COMMUNITY MEMBER PROFILE
B. BUSINESS PROFILE
C. CULTURAL AMBASSADOR PROFILE
D. COMMUNITY ORGANIZATION PROFILE
E. PROFESSIONAL EXPERTISE DISPLAY
F. PRIVATE KINFOLKAI CONTEXT
G. KINFOLK CIRCLE CONTEXT
H. ANONYMOUS CONTRIBUTION CONTEXT

A role may use shared identity fields such as name or profile image only
when the member has approved that relationship.

Do not automatically expose private Community Member behavior on a
Business Owner, Ambassador, professional, or organization profile.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2. PROFILE LINKING CONTROL
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A member must choose whether public profiles are linked.

Potential controls:

- Show that I own this business
- Show this business on my Community Member profile
- Show my Community Member profile from my business page
- Show my Cultural Ambassador role
- Show my professional expertise
- Keep these profiles publicly separate

Document:

- Default settings
- Effects of each choice
- Search visibility
- Whether historical activity becomes linked
- How unlinking works
- What remains visible after unlinking
- How verified statuses are displayed separately

Do not force profile linking as a condition of business ownership,
verification, promotion, or membership.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3. COMMUNITY ACTIVITY VISIBILITY MODEL
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Create three visibility levels:

A. SHARE BY DEFAULT

Only for activity the member knowingly creates as public, such as:

- Public reviews
- Public recommendations
- Public event submissions
- Public posts
- Public business updates
- Public resources submitted

B. ASK ME EACH TIME

Examples:

- Event attendance
- Charitable-event participation
- Volunteer milestones
- Mentorship milestones
- Businesses supported
- Community impact totals
- Relocation reflections
- Local-guide activity
- Cultural-event participation
- Professional-community activity

C. ALWAYS PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

- Search history
- Saved places
- KinfolkAI conversations
- Private messages
- Kinfolk Circle activity
- Anonymous employer reports
- Anonymous safety reports
- Precise location
- Verification documents
- Child-related information
- Health-related searches
- Legal-service searches
- Financial-hardship activity
- Religious activity
- Sexual-orientation-related activity
- Political activity
- Immigration-related activity
- Domestic-violence resources
- Crisis-resource use

Members may choose to share a specific item from a sensitive category,
but no broad setting should automatically publish future sensitive
activity.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4. SENSITIVE EVENT AND IDENTITY STANDARD
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Example:

A member searches for or attends LGBTQIA+ events.

KinfolkAI may use that activity privately to improve that member's
experience when the member has consented to personalization.

KinfolkAI must not infer:

- Sexual orientation
- Gender identity
- Relationship status
- Political beliefs
- The reason for attendance

KinfolkAI must not automatically publish:

“Marcus attended 3 LGBTQIA+ events.”

Instead, it may ask privately:

“You attended several community events this month. Would you like to
share any of that activity?”

Offer granular choices:

- Share only the total number of community events
- Share one selected event
- Share the event category
- Share the organization supported
- Save this as a private milestone
- Do not share
- Never ask me to share this type of activity

Document similar standards for:

- Faith events
- Political events
- Health-support events
- Legal clinics
- Recovery or crisis groups
- Immigration events
- Disability-support events
- Domestic-violence resources
- Child and family events

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5. PUBLIC IMPACT WITHOUT SENSITIVE EXPOSURE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Design options that allow meaningful public participation without
revealing unnecessary detail.

Examples:

- “Marcus supported 4 community events this month.”
- “Marcus volunteered with local organizations.”
- “Marcus helped welcome new Community Members.”
- “Marcus contributed information that helped others.”
- “Johnson Family Bakery supported two neighborhood food drives.”

Members may choose whether to name:

- The event
- The organization
- The category
- The location
- The date
- The business involved
- Their role

Do not publish a precise timeline that could reconstruct a member's
movements.

6. BUSINESS OWNER AS COMMUNITY MEMBER
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Document how a Business Owner may share:

- Charitable partnerships
- Community events hosted
- Volunteer work
- Mentorship
- Local hiring
- Scholarships
- Donations
- Community milestones
- Personal founder story

Separate:

A. BUSINESS ACTIVITY
Activity performed by or officially on behalf of the business.

B. OWNER PERSONAL ACTIVITY
Activity performed by the owner as a private Community Member.

Do not automatically convert personal attendance or searches into
business-profile information.

Do not let business staff or co-owners access the owner’s private
Community Member activity through the business dashboard.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7. ANONYMOUS CONTRIBUTION SAFEGUARDS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Anonymous employer, safety, discrimination, and other protected
contributions must remain technically separated from public-profile
activity.

KinfolkAI may privately provide impact feedback:

“Your anonymous workplace insight helped Community Members make more
informed decisions.”

It must not:

- Add the submission to the public profile
- Reveal authorship to the employer
- Reveal authorship to another member
- Include identifying timing or details
- Link the anonymous report to public mentorship activity
- Suggest publicly that the member authored the report

KinfolkAI may separately ask:

“Your insight has helped others. Would you like to explore mentoring in
your field? This would not reveal or connect you to your anonymous
submission.”

Document database, analytics, logging, and recommendation boundaries
required to preserve anonymity.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8. KINFOLKAI PERSONALIZATION VERSUS DISCLOSURE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Create a strict separation between:

- Information KinfolkAI may use privately for the member
- Information the member has chosen to display publicly
- Information shared inside a specific Kinfolk Circle
- Information available to a business
- Information available to administrators
- Information used only as an aggregated community signal

Required principle:

“A member’s private activity may improve their own experience, but it
must not become someone else’s information without explicit permission.”

Document how consent is stored and enforced.

Silence, account creation, event attendance, search behavior, or a prior
unrelated sharing choice must not count as consent.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
9. CHILD AND TEEN CONTROLS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

For minors:

- Search history private
- Saves private
- Event attendance private by default
- Precise location never public
- No public movement timeline
- No adult discovery based on child activity
- No direct adult contact outside approved structures
- Stronger default profile limits
- Parent-managed sharing where legally and developmentally appropriate
- Teen agency preserved within approved safety boundaries
- Sensitive-event categories never publicly suggested for sharing
- Anonymous reports remain anonymous from parents where law, safety, and
  product policy require appropriate confidentiality, subject to legal
  review

Define separate standards for:

- Under 13
- Ages 13–15
- Ages 16–17
- Adults

Identify legal and policy review required before implementing child
accounts.

11. DATA CLASSIFICATION AND ACCESS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Classify profile-related information as:

- Public by member action
- Public by explicit sharing consent
- Role-shared
- Circle-shared
- Private personal context
- Anonymous protected contribution
- Sensitive protected information
- Administrative restricted information
- Aggregated de-identified signal

For every data field identify:

- Source
- Purpose
- Default visibility
- Eligible viewers
- Retention
- Edit control
- Deletion control
- Consent requirement
- Whether it may influence recommendations
- Whether it may appear publicly
- Whether it may be aggregated
- Whether it is excluded from training or model improvement

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
12. SAFETY AND ENFORCEMENT
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

This standard must be technically enforced, not dependent only on copy.

Recommend:

- Server-side authorization
- Field-level access controls
- Separate API response models by role
- Separate anonymous-report identifiers
- Consent records with timestamps and scope
- Privacy-safe analytics
- Audit logs for visibility changes
- Automated tests for cross-role leakage
- Tests ensuring anonymous contributions never appear publicly
- Tests ensuring business dashboards cannot retrieve owner private data
- Tests ensuring Circle information remains Circle-specific
- Tests ensuring past private activity is not published after a setting
  change
- Emergency privacy rollback or content-removal process

Any privacy breach involving sensitive activity should be classified as
P0.
