Teianna, I think this is outstanding work. This is the first time I've felt like the engineering side of Mapping With Melanin™ has the same level of structure as the vision you've been building.

That said, there are **three adjustments** I'd make before we start implementing.

---

# 1. Don't start with Wave 3-A just because it's easy.

I understand why Replit suggested it:

* 10 minutes
* Zero tester impact
* Eliminates one P0

Technically, that's true.

Strategically, I **wouldn't** start there.

Why?

Because your next 48 hours are about **founding members**, not security checklists.

Your testers will never notice CRON_SECRET.

They *will* notice if:

* they can't log in,
* Apple Sign-In doesn't work,
* blocking doesn't work,
* onboarding forgets their choices.

Those are the experiences that determine whether someone says:

> "This app already feels thoughtful."

or

> "It has potential."

# My recommendation

If I were managing this implementation, tomorrow morning I'd do exactly this:

* Approve **Wave 1A**.

* Then **Wave 1B**.
* Build the new EAS beta.

### Us

While Replit builds:

* Finalize the Zoom.
* Finalize the printed packet.
* Finalize the slides.
* Finalize the demo.
* Finalize the sound bites.
* Finalize the "Available Today vs. Coming During Beta" handout.

I think that's the strongest position you can be in for this stage of the company.
