09. COMMUNITY HEALTH INDEX™
Future-State Production Specification v1.0
Classification: Community Well-Being, Resilience, and System Vitality Intelligence Engine
Status: Review Mode — designed as the launch-state model
Confidentiality: Highest restriction; Community Health scoring, resilience calculations, intervention thresholds, vitality weighting, predictive deterioration indicators, and recovery methodologies are confidential trade secrets and are not publicly disclosed.
09.1 Purpose
The Community Health Index™ measures the overall condition of a community as it exists today.
Unlike the Community Engine™, which evaluates whether individuals experience belonging, and unlike the Community Catalyst Model™, which evaluates what creates positive movement, the Community Health Index™ evaluates the condition of the ecosystem itself.
It determines:
how resilient a community is;
whether people are thriving or merely participating;
how resources circulate;
how opportunity is distributed;
how trust changes over time;
how communities respond to challenges;
where intervention is needed;
where recovery is occurring;
where long-term deterioration is emerging;
whether the community is becoming healthier or weaker.
The governing question is:
Given everything we know, is this community becoming stronger, more connected, more resilient, and more capable of supporting its members over time?
09.2 Foundational Principle
Healthy communities are living systems.
A healthy community is not simply one with:
low crime;
many businesses;
high participation;
large population;
economic wealth.
Instead, healthy communities demonstrate:
resilience;
adaptability;
trust;
reciprocity;
opportunity;
belonging;
stewardship;
continuity.
The Community Health Index™ measures these characteristics simultaneously.
09.3 Definition of Community Health
Community Health is the collective capacity of a community to:
support its members;
solve problems;
create opportunity;
preserve culture;
recover from disruption;
sustain relationships;
circulate resources;
strengthen belonging;
maintain trust;
improve over time.
Health is never treated as a permanent condition.
Communities continuously improve or decline.
