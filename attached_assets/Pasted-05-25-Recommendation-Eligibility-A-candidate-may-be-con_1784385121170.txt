05.25 Recommendation Eligibility
A candidate may be considered for recommendation when:
Discovery Score ≥ 70
and
Discovery Confidence ≥ 65
and
BIF recommendation threshold is satisfied
and
Safety minimum is satisfied
and
Trust minimum is satisfied
and
no active recommendation hold exists
A candidate with lower confidence may be shown as:
emerging;
worth exploring;
newly added;
community-submitted;
awaiting verification;
limited evidence available.
It must not be described as strongly recommended.
05.26 Promotion Eligibility
Promotion is a separate decision.
The Discovery Engine™ may only identify a candidate as promotion-eligible from a discovery perspective.
Final promotion requires BIF approval.
Baseline requirements:
Discovery Score ≥ 78
Discovery Confidence ≥ 75
Trust minimum satisfied
Safety minimum satisfied
Community evidence sufficient
No active governance hold
No undisclosed conflict of interest
Paid status does not alter eligibility
A paid candidate failing eligibility remains ineligible.
Payment may determine placement only among candidates already approved for promotion and only under clearly disclosed rules.
05.27 Organic, Sponsored, and Curated Results
The Engine distinguishes:
Result type	Meaning
Organic	Ranked without payment
Sponsored	Paid placement among eligible candidates
Curated	Selected by approved editorial or community process
Recognition-based	Surfaced due to earned status
Emergency-priority	Surfaced due to urgent need
User-saved	Surfaced because the user previously saved it
Partner-supplied	Provided through approved data partnership
Emerging	Limited evidence but potentially relevant
Sponsored results must be labeled.
Sponsored status may not increase Trust, Safety, Community, Cultural Influence, or Discovery quality scores.
05.28 Ranking Methodology
After eligibility and scoring, candidates are ranked in five layers:
Layer 1 — Hard Constraint Satisfaction
Layer 2 — BIF and Safety Eligibility
Layer 3 — Discovery Relevance Score
Layer 4 — Diversity and Exploration Re-ranking
Layer 5 — Presentation Rules
The initial rank is not necessarily the displayed rank.
05.29 Hard Constraint Satisfaction
Candidates satisfying all explicit hard requirements rank above candidates that do not.
The Engine must not rank a partially matching result above an exact match solely because the partial match is more popular, more profitable, or more heavily reviewed.
