# Here's what I think could become one of Mapping with Melanin™'s signature features...

Instead of asking users to browse a giant directory, let them ask KinfolkAI naturally:

* "I need rent assistance this month."
* "I'm looking for grants to start a Black-owned coffee shop."
* "Are there scholarships for nursing students?"
* "My apartment lease ends in 30 days."
* "Where can I get free legal help?"
* "Find childcare assistance near me."
* "I'm looking for second-chance employers."
* "Show me free coding bootcamps."
* "I need a food pantry that's open today."
* "I'm trying to buy my first home."

KinfolkAI would then query your vetted sources and return a **personalized roadmap** instead of a list of links.

## One recommendation I'd strongly make

Create an internal **Source Trust Score** that every resource receives before it reaches a user.

For example:

* 🟢 **Official Source** — Government agency or official public institution.
* 🟢 **Verified Organization** — Recognized nonprofit, accredited school, established foundation, or confirmed business.
* 🟡 **Community Confirmed** — Shared by community members and supported by multiple reports or confirmations.
* ⚪ **Community Shared** — Newly submitted by a member and awaiting additional confirmation.

That system reinforces the values you've described throughout the app: **protecting people while empowering communities through trustworthy information.**
