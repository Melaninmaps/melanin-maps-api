07.30.2 Match Formula
Ambassador–Business Match Score =
  (Cultural Alignment × 0.20)
+ (Audience Relevance × 0.12)
+ (Community Alignment × 0.18)
+ (Values and Trust Compatibility × 0.18)
+ (Purpose Fit × 0.12)
+ (Reciprocity Potential × 0.10)
+ (Geographic Fit × 0.05)
+ (Operational Readiness × 0.05)
A high match score does not authorize partnership without both parties’ consent.
07.31 “Show Love” Nomination Logic
Community members may nominate a Cultural Ambassador, business, institution, or creator through a “Show Love” pathway.
A nomination may:
enter recognition review;
increase evidence collection priority;
identify overlooked influence;
support Hidden Gem or Community Recognition review;
create a partnership candidate.
A nomination may not:
directly increase a score;
establish verification;
guarantee promotion;
bypass Trust or Safety review;
create public status without evidence.
Coordinated nominations are screened for manipulation.

07.33 Cultural Influence Decay
Different signals decay at different rates.
07.33.1 Rapid-decay signals
Examples:
trending engagement;
viral attention;
current campaign activity;
event-specific visibility.
0–7 days:       100%
8–30 days:       75%
31–90 days:      45%
91–180 days:     20%
Over 180 days:   10%
07.33.2 Moderate-decay signals
Examples:
business support outcomes;
repeat community participation;
partnership performance;
educational usefulness.
0–90 days:      100%
91–180 days:     90%
181–365 days:    75%
1–2 years:       55%
2–3 years:       35%
Over 3 years:    20%
07.33.3 Slow-decay signals
Examples:
cultural grounding;
institutional trust;
long-term stewardship;
preserved history;
sustained mentorship.
0–1 year:       100%
1–3 years:       95%
3–5 years:       85%
5–10 years:      75%
Over 10 years:   65%
07.33.4 Persistent evidence
Certain evidence remains historically relevant:
major cultural works;
institution building;
verified exploitation;
significant correction;
preserved archives;
Legacy milestones;
formal accountability findings.
Persistent evidence may lose current ranking influence but remains in the Legacy & Learning Engine™.
