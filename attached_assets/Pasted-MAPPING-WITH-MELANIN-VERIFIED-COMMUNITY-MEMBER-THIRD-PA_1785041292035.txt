Replit previously described a Community Member identity-verification
process involving a third-party provider and an estimated cost of
approximately $1.50 per verification.

Because there is a direct cost for each verification, we previously agreed
that:

- Community Member is the free base membership identity.
- Free Community Members do not need to submit a selfie or photo ID simply
  to join or belong.
- Eligible paid members may choose to become Verified Community Members.
- Verification is an added trust designation, not a requirement for basic
  community membership.
- Verification does not automatically make someone a Cultural Ambassador.
- Personal member verification and business-ownership verification are
  separate workflows.

I need you to determine whether the third-party verification system,
vendor integration, keys, environment variables, routes, database fields,
or UI previously built for this process still exist.

Do not modify code, schemas, environment variables, provider accounts,
membership tiers, prompts, production data, or verification settings.

B. GOVERNMENT-ID VERIFICATION

Checks an identity document and may validate name, age, or document
authenticity.

C. SELFIE-TO-ID MATCHING

Compares the live person with the photograph on an identity document.

D. COMMUNITY TRUST DESIGNATION

The member-facing result displayed by Mapping With Melanin™.

Do not describe a selfie-only process as full identity verification if it
does not confirm identity.

6. RECOMMEND THE APPROPRIATE VERIFICATION STANDARD
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

After tracing what exists, recommend the most appropriate future-state
process for Verified Community Members.

Evaluate at least these options:

OPTION A — Liveness/selfie only

Potential benefit:
Lower cost and friction.

Limitation:
May confirm a real person but not confirm legal identity.

OPTION B — Government ID only

Potential benefit:
Document-based identity check.

Limitation:
May not confirm the submitting person matches the ID holder.

OPTION C — Government ID plus selfie/liveness match

Potential benefit:
Stronger identity assurance.

Limitation:
Greater cost, friction, privacy sensitivity, and accessibility concerns.

OPTION D — Tiered verification

Example:
- Standard verified status through liveness
- Enhanced verification for sensitive activities through ID plus liveness

Explain whether tiered verification would create unnecessary confusion or
whether it would appropriately match verification strength to risk.

Recommend based on actual platform needs, including:

- Reviews
- Community posts
- Safety reports
- Kinfolk Circles
- Mentorship
- Meetups
- Cultural Ambassador applications
- Parent or child-related participation
- Marketplace activity
- Business ownership claims
- Community Organization management

Do not recommend collecting government ID merely because a vendor
supports it. The verification level must be proportional to the actual
risk and benefit.
