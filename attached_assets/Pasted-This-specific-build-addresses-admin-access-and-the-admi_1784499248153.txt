This specific build addresses **admin access and the admin login-return flow**, not every login issue.

It fixes:

* `/admin` preserving its destination through login.
* Returning you to `/admin` after successful authentication.
* Already-signed-in admins skipping the login form.
* Centralized, normalized admin authorization.
* Admin recognition by either:

  * normalized email in `ADMIN_EMAILS`, or
  * `role === "admin"` in the database.

It does **not by itself** prove or fix all of these broader authentication items:

* Phone login persistence.
* Repeated SMS behavior.
* Email login for accounts originally created by phone.
* Password reset on your canonical admin account.
* Apple Sign-In.
* Session persistence in the mobile app.
* Favorite/save actions preserving the session.
* The mobile client only clearing auth on a true invalid-session 401.

Those were handled in separate fixes and still need founder retesting together.
