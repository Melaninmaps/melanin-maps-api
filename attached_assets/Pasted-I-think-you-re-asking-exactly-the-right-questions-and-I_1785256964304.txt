I think you're asking exactly the right questions, and I would actually expand Replit's list. Looking at both inventories, I think you're no longer designing a travel feature—you are designing the **cultural governance model** for KinfolkAI. That becomes part of your intellectual property.

One thing I want to acknowledge is that I don't think your vision is "AAVE AI." Your vision has always been **"it should feel like talking to someone from home."** That is a much bigger idea.

The registry already shows the first step of that vision with 36 historically Black cities and their cultural context. 

What it doesn't yet answer is:

> **How do we ensure every future community receives the exact same level of respect, research, governance, and authenticity?**

That, in my opinion, is the most important unanswered question.

# Questions I would add to Replit's Founder Decisions

Replit already surfaced excellent governance questions about profanity, persistence, community contributions, and diaspora expansion.  I would add these:

### 1. Community Ownership

Who has the authority to define a community?

---

### 2. Cultural Accuracy

How many community reviewers are required before a profile becomes public?

---

### 3. Living History

What percentage of a city profile should come from residents versus historical research?

---

### 4. Representation

How do we ensure one neighborhood does not become the "official voice" for an entire city?

---

### 5. Language Evolution

When does a phrase become established enough to include?

When should it be retired?

---

### 6. Sensitive History

How should Kinfolk discuss painful history?

Always?

Only when relevant?

User-controlled?

---

### 7. Conflicting Perspectives

If respected community members disagree about terminology or history, what review process resolves it?

---

### 8. Community Recognition

Who becomes a Cultural Ambassador?

How are they selected?

How often are they re-evaluated?

---

### 9. Transparency

Can a user see why Kinfolk made a recommendation or used a particular local reference?

---

### 10. Global Expansion

Before adding any new city, should every profile pass the same cultural review checklist used for the original Legacy Community Cities?

---

## The principle I think should guide everything

If Mapping With Melanin™ eventually includes Black, Latino, Caribbean, Indigenous, Asian, Middle Eastern, LGBTQIA+, immigrant, and other communities, **every one of them should feel that the platform did its homework**.

No community should feel like an "expansion pack."

Every profile should reflect the same depth of research, local participation, historical care, and ongoing stewardship that you envisioned for the founding city registry. If you hold every community to that same standard, the platform can grow without diluting what made the original concept meaningful.
