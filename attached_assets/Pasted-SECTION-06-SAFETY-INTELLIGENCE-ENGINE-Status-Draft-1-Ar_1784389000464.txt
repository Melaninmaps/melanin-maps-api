SECTION 06
SAFETY INTELLIGENCE ENGINE™
Status: Draft 1 (Architectural Foundation)
06.1 Governing Question
Every safety decision made by the platform begins with a single question:
"Given everything we know right now, what is the safest recommendation we can responsibly make without creating unnecessary fear or unnecessary confidence?"
The Safety Intelligence Engine™ is not a crime predictor.
It is not a neighborhood ranking system.
It is not a policing tool.
It is a decision-support system that continuously evaluates available information to help community members make more informed decisions while clearly communicating uncertainty.
06.2 Guiding Principles
Safety is never treated as binary.
The engine recognizes:
physical safety
psychological safety
cultural safety
discrimination risk
accessibility
environmental hazards
emergency readiness
situational context
Each dimension contributes independently.
A place may be:
physically safe
culturally unsafe
or
physically risky
culturally welcoming
Both realities may exist simultaneously.
