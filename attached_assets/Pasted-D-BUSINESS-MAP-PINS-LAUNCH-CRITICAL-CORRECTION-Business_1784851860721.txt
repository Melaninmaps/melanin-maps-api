D. BUSINESS MAP PINS — LAUNCH-CRITICAL CORRECTION
Business pins are a core Mapping With Melanin™ feature and must be included in the Android map stability work.
Audit and implement the smallest safe correction so that approved businesses reliably appear on the map without causing Android crashes or performance degradation.
Required behavior:
Approved businesses with valid coordinates must render as map pins.
Invalid or missing coordinates must be skipped safely and logged without crashing the screen.
Pins must remain visible after:
initial map load;
location timeout or location denial;
app background/foreground;
map pan and zoom;
business-data refresh.
Tapping a business pin must open the intended business preview/profile path.
Business pins must be visually distinguishable from:
HBCUs;
cultural landmarks;
safety alerts;
user location.
Prevent large batches of custom marker views from crashing Android.
Determine whether Android should use:
simpler native marker images;
memoized custom markers;
marker batching;
viewport-based rendering;
clustering at lower zoom levels.
Do not remove businesses from the map as a workaround.
Do not replace the business-map experience with the old static pin stub.
Preserve the future design direction where business pins preview the business experience users will receive.
Return:
current source of business map data;
whether it is production data, seeded data, or static constants;
number of business markers rendered on initial load;
exact filtering rules;
exact pin-tap behavior;
performance risk on Android;
smallest implementation needed for VC68;
regression tests for map load, pin visibility, pin tapping, zooming, panning, and large marker counts.
One important distinction:
Business pins must be fixed now.
The full HBCU flagship redesign can still wait.
HBCUs can remain a separate marker/layer for VC68, but they should not be allowed to destabilize business pins.
The build gate for VC68 should now be:
Backend readiness fix deployed and verified.
Android login/signup correction retained.
Map tiles load.
Location spinner times out safely.
Business pins render reliably.
Pin tap opens business preview/profile.
Cultural/HBCU markers do not crash the map.
App stays open on the map for several minutes without interaction.
That keeps the next build focused on the real launch promise: users open the map and can actually discover businesses.
