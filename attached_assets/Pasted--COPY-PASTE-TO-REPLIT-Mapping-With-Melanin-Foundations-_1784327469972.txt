# Mapping With Melanin™ Foundations Book — Proposed Living Structure

## Implementation Status: Review Mode

This is a planning and documentation proposal only.

Do not build, modify, generate, commit, deploy, or replace any existing materials until I explicitly authorize implementation.

---

## Purpose

The **Mapping With Melanin™ Foundations Book** will become the permanent, living record of:

* why the company exists;
* how the brand communicates;
* how products and features are developed;
* how designers, developers, contractors, volunteers, and AI partners work with us;
* how the community contributes;
* how our launch, tour, documentary, and cultural history evolve;
* and how the company protects its identity as it grows.

This should become the internal “Bible” for Mapping With Melanin™.

It is not simply a presentation.

It is a continuously updated brand, product, culture, development, and historical archive.

---

# Recommended Format

The Foundations Book should exist in several connected forms:

1. **Master Foundations Book**

   * Full internal version
   * PDF and editable presentation format
   * Contains all standards, policies, operating principles, and historical documentation

2. **Presentation Version**

   * A polished visual version for team members, partners, contractors, and selected stakeholders
   * Shorter and easier to present

3. **Video Version**

   * Narrated visual version
   * Includes founder voice, community footage, city footage, music, interviews, and app demonstrations

4. **Documentary Archive**

   * Long-term collection of approved photos, interviews, stories, music, video footage, and tour documentation
   * Used later for the official Mapping With Melanin™ documentary

5. **Public Heritage Version**

   * A carefully selected public-facing version that tells the story of the company without revealing confidential development, financial, legal, or trade-secret information

---

# Important Structural Recommendation

The complete Foundations Book should **not automatically appear as the final slides of every presentation**.

That would make individual decks too long and would expose internal standards that may not be relevant to every audience.

Instead:

* The **Master Deck** contains the complete Foundations Book.
* Individual decks include a short final page called:

  * “Built From Our Foundations”
  * “Our Storytelling Standards”
  * or “The Mapping With Melanin™ Foundation”
* That page may link internally to the full Foundations Book when appropriate.
* Selected standards can be included in an appendix for team, designer, developer, volunteer, and ambassador presentations.

The Foundations Book remains the source of truth. Other decks inherit from it.

# How We Build the Foundations Book

## Phase One — Written Source of Truth

Create the complete written content first.

Do not begin with visual slides.

Every section should be reviewed and approved as text before design begins.

## Phase Two — Visual System

After the written content is approved:

* define section templates;
* apply approved colors;
* apply typography;
* apply custom gold outlined icons;
* define photography layouts;
* define video placeholders;
* define archival captions;
* create version and date markers.

## Phase Three — Master Book

Build the complete internal version.

## Phase Four — Audience Editions

Create condensed versions for:

* designers and developers;
* Cultural Ambassadors;
* volunteers;
* businesses;
* investors;
* community members;
* documentary collaborators.

## Phase Five — Living Archive

Add approved:

* tour photography;
* member stories;
* city pages;

* artist submissions;

* press;

# Documentation Rule

Every major addition must include:

* title;
* date;
* version;
* owner;
* approval status;
* public or confidential classification;
* related source files;
* legal consent status where relevant.

The book should include a visible changelog.

Example:

| Version | Date      | Change             | Approved By |
| ------- | --------- | ------------------ | ----------- |
| v1.0    | July 2026 | Initial standards  | Founder     |
| v1.1    | TBD       | Tour archive added | Founder     |
| v1.2    | TBD       | Voice Guide added  | Founder     |

# Recommendation

The Foundations Book should end with two permanent sections:

## Living History

Reserved for approved photographs, stories, launch milestones, city chapters, community contributions, music, and documentary history.

## What Comes Next

Reserved for the evolving vision, future opportunities, and approved roadmap.

This keeps the document alive without forcing operational standards to be rewritten every time the company reaches a new milestone.
