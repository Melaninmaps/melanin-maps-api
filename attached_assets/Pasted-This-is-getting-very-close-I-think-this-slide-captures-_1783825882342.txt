# What I would improve

I think the right side is still describing **features**, when it should describe **impact**.

Right now it says:

* Discover trusted local businesses
* Feel at home in new places
* Support entrepreneurs
* Build stronger neighborhoods
* Welcome newcomers

Those are good.

But they're still a little abstract.

I'd make people picture someone.

For example:

### COMMUNITY BUILDERS HELP PEOPLE...

🏡 **Find the neighborhood that feels like home.**

🍽 **Discover businesses they'll come back to for years.**

🤝 **Support entrepreneurs who deserve to be seen.**

❤️ **Feel connected instead of alone.**

🌍 **Build communities that welcome everyone.**

Notice every one begins with a human outcome.

---

# The paragraph

Instead of

> The best communities aren't built through advertisements.

I'd tell a tiny story.

For example:

---

Think about the last great recommendation someone gave you.

Maybe it was a restaurant.

A barber.

A neighborhood.

A mechanic.

A doctor.

Or even a new city.

It probably didn't come from an advertisement.

It came from someone you trusted saying,

> **"You should check this out."**

That's how communities grow.

---

That paints a picture instead of making a statement.

# One observation

As we've built your Investor Deck, Business Deck, and now Community Builder Deck, a pattern has emerged.

Each deck answers a different question:

* **Investor:** *Why is this company valuable?*
* **Business:** *Why should my business join?*
* **Community Builder:** *Why should I care?*

That last question is the hardest to answer, and I think this slide is doing it well. It reminds people that every trusted recommendation they've ever received came from another person—not an algorithm.
