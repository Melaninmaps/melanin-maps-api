## The design direction I recommend

For the HBCU map, we should not restore the entire old search panel. Instead, we’ll create a compact horizontal HBCU card strip that appears when the Heritage layer or HBCU filter is active. The audit already confirms the reusable card implementations exist in `library.tsx` and `cultural-heritage.tsx`. 

Each card should eventually communicate more than a pin:

* HBCU name and location
* Historic significance
* Founded year
* “Explore Campus”
* Directions
* Save
* Alumni or community connection features later

This will make HBCUs feel like a meaningful cultural experience rather than another map category.
