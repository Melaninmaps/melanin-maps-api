# 13. OPPORTUNITY ENGINE™

## Future-State Production Specification v1.0

**Classification:** Opportunity Discovery, Matching, and Future Potential Intelligence Engine
**Status:** Review Mode — designed as the launch-state model
**Confidentiality:** Highest restriction; opportunity matching algorithms, prediction models, prioritization logic, eligibility calculations, opportunity weighting, recommendation methodologies, and optimization rules are confidential trade secrets and are not publicly disclosed.

---

# 13.1 Purpose

The Opportunity Engine™ identifies meaningful opportunities that align with a member's context, goals, needs, values, capabilities, location, and stage of life.

Unlike traditional recommendation systems that optimize engagement, the Opportunity Engine™ optimizes human advancement.

It determines:

1. which opportunities are most relevant;
2. which opportunities are realistically attainable;
3. which opportunities produce the greatest long-term benefit;
4. which opportunities increase belonging;
5. which opportunities strengthen communities;
6. which opportunities reduce barriers;
7. which opportunities create measurable upward mobility;
8. which opportunities should be surfaced immediately;
9. which opportunities should wait until conditions improve;
10. which opportunities are most likely to improve a person's life trajectory.

The governing question is:

> **Given everything we know, which opportunity is most likely to create meaningful positive change for this member while strengthening the broader community?**

---

# 13.2 Foundational Principle

People do not lack potential.

People often lack visibility into opportunities.

The Opportunity Engine™ exists to reduce that gap.

Its responsibility is not simply to recommend opportunities.

Its responsibility is to recognize opportunity before the individual even knows to search for it.

Opportunity is treated as something that can be discovered, unlocked, shared, expanded, and multiplied.

# 13.5 Core Evaluation Dimensions

The Opportunity Engine™ returns a normalized score from **0–100**.

| Dimension            |   Weight |
| -------------------- | -------: |
| Relevance            |      18% |
| Readiness            |      15% |
| Community Benefit    |      14% |
| Long-Term Potential  |      13% |
| Accessibility        |      12% |
| Belonging Alignment  |      10% |
| Growth Potential     |       8% |
| Trust & Verification |       6% |
| Timing               |       4% |
| **Total**            | **100%** |

---

# 13.6 Dimension One — Relevance

Measures how closely an opportunity aligns with:

* stated goals;
* current interests;
* life stage;
* professional aspirations;
* cultural preferences;
* community priorities;
* travel intent;
* relocation plans.

Formula:

```text id="opp13a"
Relevance =
Intent Alignment
× Context Alignment
× Need Alignment
× Goal Alignment
```

High relevance without readiness may delay recommendation rather than suppress it.

---

# 13.7 Dimension Two — Readiness

Measures whether the member is currently prepared.

Examples:

* qualifications;
* experience;
* required documentation;
* certifications;
* financial readiness;
* geographic availability;
* time commitment.

The Engine never assumes readiness based solely on demographics.

---

# 13.8 Dimension Three — Community Benefit

Measures whether accepting the opportunity benefits both the individual and the broader community.

Examples:

* local hiring;
* mentorship;
* entrepreneurship;
* cultural preservation;
* volunteer leadership;
* educational advancement.

The strongest opportunities improve both individual outcomes and collective outcomes.

---

# 13.9 Dimension Four — Long-Term Potential

Evaluates:

* career progression;
* leadership potential;
* educational advancement;
* financial stability;
* entrepreneurship;
* community influence.

Temporary gain receives less value than sustainable advancement.

---

# 13.10 Dimension Five — Accessibility

Measures barriers including:

* financial cost;
* transportation;
* language;
* disability access;
* digital access;
* childcare;
* documentation requirements;
* scheduling.

Lower barriers increase Opportunity Score.

---

# 13.11 Dimension Six — Belonging Alignment

Measures whether the opportunity supports:

* inclusion;
* community connection;
* cultural respect;
* mentorship;
* psychological safety;
* authentic participation.

Belonging Alignment incorporates outputs from:

* Context Engine™
* Community Engine™
* Safety Intelligence Engine™

---

# 13.12 Dimension Seven — Growth Potential

Growth includes:

* new skills;
* leadership;
* entrepreneurship;
* confidence;
* professional advancement;
* network expansion;
* community participation.

Growth is evaluated over years—not weeks.

---

# 13.13 Dimension Eight — Trust & Verification

Measures:

* source credibility;
* institutional legitimacy;
* verification status;
* transparency;
* historical reliability.

Verified opportunities receive higher confidence.

---

# 13.14 Dimension Nine — Timing

The same opportunity may be excellent—

—but at the wrong time.

Timing evaluates:

* current workload;
* seasonality;
* application deadlines;
* relocation stage;
* educational calendar;
* family commitments;
* community events.

The Engine may delay rather than discard an opportunity.

---

# 13.15 Opportunity Formula

```text id="opp13b"
Opportunity Score =
(Relevance × .18)
+(Readiness × .15)
+(Community Benefit × .14)
+(Long-Term Potential × .13)
+(Accessibility × .12)
+(Belonging Alignment × .10)
+(Growth Potential × .08)
+(Trust & Verification × .06)
+(Timing × .04)
```

Normalized to **0–100**.

# 13.22 Opportunity Equity

The Engine continuously evaluates whether opportunities are:

* discoverable;
* accessible;
* fairly distributed;
* visible to newcomers;
* inclusive of historically underserved communities.

Opportunity equity is monitored without introducing preferential scoring based solely on identity.

# 13.23 Anti-Gaming Rules

Manipulation includes:

* fake job postings;
* fraudulent scholarships;
* misleading grants;
* artificial scarcity;
* false deadlines;
* fabricated partnerships;
* duplicate listings.

Manipulation reduces confidence before affecting Opportunity Score.

---

# 13.24 Public Explanation Rules

Approved:

* "This opportunity aligns with your current goals."
* "This recommendation matches your interests and readiness."
* "This opportunity may strengthen both your growth and your community."

Prohibited:

* "You were guaranteed to succeed."
* "This is the perfect opportunity."
* "The algorithm knows your future."

# 13.35 Final Governing Rule

The Opportunity Engine™ exists because meaningful opportunity changes lives—but only when people can discover it, access it, trust it, and act upon it.

Its purpose is not simply to recommend jobs, scholarships, businesses, events, or programs.

Its purpose is to ensure that people are connected with opportunities that help them grow while simultaneously strengthening their families, neighborhoods, institutions, businesses, and communities.

Every recommendation should increase possibility.

Every recommendation should reduce unnecessary barriers.

Every recommendation should create pathways toward belonging, resilience, leadership, and lasting prosperity.

Its final governing rule is:

> **Connect every person with the opportunities most likely to strengthen their future while expanding possibility, equity, resilience, and belonging for the communities they help build.**
