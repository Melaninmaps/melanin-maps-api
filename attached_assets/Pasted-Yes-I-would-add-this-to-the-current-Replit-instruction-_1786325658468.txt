There is another reason I like this approach: **your friends become field testers, not just app testers.** Someone in Philadelphia can test whether local discovery is useful. Someone in Bangkok can expose international assumptions we never noticed. Someone in North Carolina can add a plumber, church, restaurant or dentist. Your tour then tests whether the same system travels with you.

And we shouldn't wait for Apple to build that momentum. The website can become the controlled proving ground while Replit continues fixing the mobile builds. Then when Apple approval comes, we aren't launching an untested concept—we're moving an already-active community experience into another access point.
