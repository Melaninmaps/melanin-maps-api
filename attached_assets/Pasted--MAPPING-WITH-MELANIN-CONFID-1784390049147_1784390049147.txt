06.51 Internal Output Contract
The Safety Intelligence Engine™ returns a structured assessment to the Belonging Intelligence Framework™.
The output must preserve the difference between:
concern;
protective capacity;
confidence;
immediacy;
recommendation treatment;
search treatment;
alert treatment;
promotion treatment.
A single combined safety value may not replace these outputs.

06.52 Required Database Structures
The production Safety Intelligence Engine™ requires, at minimum:
safety_assessments
safety_dimension_scores
safety_evidence_items
safety_report_intakes
safety_report_relationships
safety_report_permissions
safety_source_records
safety_corroboration_records
safety_pattern_records
safety_pattern_members
safety_protective_capacity_records
safety_corrective_action_records
safety_override_records
safety_alert_records
safety_alert_audiences
safety_alert_updates
safety_recommendation_treatments
safety_search_treatments
safety_promotion_holds
safety_manipulation_signals
safety_manipulation_responses
safety_reporter_protection_records
safety_entity_response_records
safety_appeal_records
safety_human_review_records
safety_public_explanations
safety_engine_versions
safety_audit_records
These structures may be incorporated into the BIF database architecture or implemented as linked production tables.
Sensitive report content must be separated from public-facing safety records.
Access permissions must distinguish:
public information;
internal moderation information;
restricted safety information;
reporter-identifying information;
emergency information;
legal or Governance information.

Every consequential Safety Intelligence Engine™ decision must record:
safety assessment ID;
request ID;
evaluated entity;
evaluated location;
timestamp;
engine version;
context profile;
urgency level;
evidence period;
evidence items considered;
evidence items excluded;
exclusion reasons;
source relationship types;
verification status;
anonymity status;
reporter-sharing permissions;
Evidence Quality Scores;
source multipliers;
decay applied;
corroboration factor;
source independence factor;
geographic precision;
dimension applicability;
dimension concern scores;
dimension confidence scores;
dimension protective-capacity scores;
raw Concern Score;
Protective Capacity Score;
Safety Confidence Score;
Immediacy Level;
pattern status;
pattern threshold applied;
corrective actions;
corrective-action verification;
concern multipliers;
override level;
override reason;
recommendation treatment;
search visibility treatment;
promotion treatment;
alert eligibility;
alert audience;
alert activation and expiration;
anti-manipulation signals;
anti-manipulation response;
reporter-protection actions;
accused-entity response opportunity;
human-review status;
public explanation code;
BIF decision reference;
appeal status;
final outcome.
The audit record must preserve enough information to reproduce the decision using the version active at the time.
