## I think this should become one of the foundational principles you give Replit.

I would ask them to stop thinking in terms of **content categories** and start thinking in terms of **life journeys**.

Today, a creator is labeled by what they make:

* Travel Creator
* Food Creator
* Parenting Creator
* Business Creator

But people aren't one thing.

A person's life evolves, and their contributions should evolve with them.

Imagine a creator's profile becoming a timeline:

* ✈️ Shared travel experiences.
* 🎓 Helped students choose an HBCU.
* 💍 Documented wedding traditions.
* 👶 Shared the realities of becoming a parent.
* 🧁 Opened a bakery.
* 🤝 Mentored new entrepreneurs.

They haven't changed audiences—they've invited the community to grow alongside them.

That's why I think the Cultural Ambassador program should eventually become a **Journey Ambassador** program. Cultural Ambassadors remain a specialized role focused on preserving and sharing community knowledge. But Journey Ambassadors are people who choose to walk alongside others through meaningful life chapters.

That distinction is powerful because it recognizes two equally valuable forms of contribution:

* **"Let me teach you about my community."**
* **"Let me walk with you through something I've already experienced."**

I honestly believe that second idea is one of the most valuable things we've designed. It transforms the platform from a place where people consume information into a place where they pass wisdom from one generation, one life chapter, and one community member to the next. I don't know of another platform that frames community contribution in quite that way.
