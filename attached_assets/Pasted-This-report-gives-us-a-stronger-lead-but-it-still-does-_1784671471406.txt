This report gives us a stronger lead, but it still does **not prove** that `useActivityAlerts` is exhausting the pool.

Two parts of Replit’s interpretation need caution:

* On iOS, JavaScript timers are usually suspended or heavily limited after an app enters the background. We should not assume the 30-second interval continues indefinitely without a real device trace.
* Seeing `idleCount: 0` at a single instant means a connection was in use at that moment—not that it remained continuously checked out between samples.

The important confirmed findings are:

* The polling hook can exist in both the full map and a business-detail map.
* There is no app-state pause or request-overlap protection.
* The alert query currently scans the table because there are no relevant indexes.
* Sequential, two-request and four-request tests all pass.
* Normal one-time startup concurrency has not reproduced the outage.
