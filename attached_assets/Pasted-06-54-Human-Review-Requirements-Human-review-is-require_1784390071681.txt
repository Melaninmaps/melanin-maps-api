06.54 Human Review Requirements
Human review is required when:
a Critical Override is activated;
a High-Risk Override affects recommendation or search visibility;
immediate danger is reported;
a report alleges severe violence, stalking, threats, or serious bodily harm;
discrimination reports establish or may establish a pattern;
retaliation is alleged;
children or parent-managed minors are involved;
evidence involves private or precise location information;
an alert may materially affect reputation or public behavior;
public wording could imply guilt or categorical danger;
promotion is held because of a serious report;
a single severe report triggers a precautionary action;
official and community evidence conflict;
corrective action materially changes the score;
an accused entity disputes material facts;
a reporter alleges suppression;
manipulation is suspected;
evidence may be forged;
a safety score changes by more than 20 points in 30 days;
a recommendation could expose a user to substantial harm;
the context involves accessibility misrepresentation;
an emergency notice may be outdated or geographically mismatched;
a new evidence type is used;
an appeal is submitted;
the Engine Orchestration Rules do not contain an approved pathway.
Review priority must correspond to immediacy.
Immediacy	Review target
4 — Emergency	Immediate
3 — Urgent	Within 15 minutes where operationally possible
2 — Priority	Within 4 hours
1 — Time-sensitive	Within 24 hours
0 — Informational	Standard review queue
These are internal operational targets, not public guarantees.
06.55 Emergency Failure Conditions
The Safety Intelligence Engine™ must fail safely.
If the Engine becomes unavailable:
emergency contact and routing information must remain accessible;
active official emergency alerts must remain visible through a protected fallback channel;
no personalized safety assurance may be issued;
no entity may be described as safe because the Engine is unavailable;
ordinary promotion decisions must be held;
safety-sensitive recommendations must be restricted;
existing active overrides remain in effect until reviewed;
new community reports must still be accepted where operationally possible;
report timestamps and evidence must be preserved;
human escalation must remain available;
the failure must be logged;
the BIF must receive SAFETY_ENGINE_UNAVAILABLE.
Fallback output:
{
  "safety_engine_status": "UNAVAILABLE",
  "concern_score": null,
  "protective_capacity_score": null,
  "confidence_score": 0,
  "confidence_label": "Unknown",
  "personalized_safety_recommendation_allowed": false,
  "promotion_allowed": false,
  "existing_overrides_preserved": true,
  "official_alert_fallback_active": true,
  "emergency_routing_available": true,
  "human_review_required": true
}
06.55.1 Partial failure
If only one safety dimension is unavailable:
mark the dimension UNAVAILABLE;
do not assign zero;
recalculate only when approved;
reduce overall confidence;
apply applicable score caps;
disclose missing information where relevant;
hold high-consequence recommendations when the missing dimension is material.
06.55.2 Alert-delivery failure
If an alert cannot be delivered:
retry through approved channels;
preserve the intended audience;
log failure reasons;
do not broaden the audience without authorization;
escalate when the alert is Emergency or Urgent;
issue an update when service is restored if the information remains actionable.
