I would also add **one permanent protection** after this incident: the app-wide error boundary should not allow Kinfolk to take down the rest of the site. Your screenshot shows a Kinfolk-related JavaScript failure reaching the Mapping With Melanin global error screen. Even before we know the root cause, that's an architectural weakness worth addressing **after** diagnosis. Kinfolk should have its own local error boundary/fallback.

And I agree with your business-media requirement. The distinction should be:

**Claim status controls who can manage the official business profile. It should not determine whether Mapping With Melanin can contain community-contributed information about that place.**

So an unclaimed listing can still become alive with community reviews, photos, videos, recommendations, and social links, while ownership/admin powers remain restricted.

For outside-platform media, I would keep the architecture we discussed: store the **original post URL + platform + contributor + moderation status + business ID**, rather than copying another creator's video into your own storage by default. Then Mapping With Melanin can embed/link the original where the platform permits it. That preserves attribution and keeps the business/creator's existing social presence relevant.

On the sundown-town layer, I would be particularly strict about **diagnose-before-reseed**. If that data previously existed, Replit should locate it and reconnect it rather than produce a new dataset and potentially erase the provenance of the original research.

And after what happened today, I would make one rule permanent:

> **“Deployment successful” means the actual production URL was opened after deployment, key user journeys were performed successfully, and visual proof was captured. A successful build command alone is never a successful production deployment.**
