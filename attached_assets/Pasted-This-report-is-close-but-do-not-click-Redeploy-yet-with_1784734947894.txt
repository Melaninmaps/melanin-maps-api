## One important Apple correction

Apple does require:

* a working public privacy-policy URL for every iOS app;
* a functional support URL with accurate contact information;
* all links in the app to work. ([Apple Developer][1])

But a `/delete-account` webpage by itself is **not enough**. Because the app supports account creation, users must be able to initiate deletion **inside the app**. A website may finish the deletion process only when the app links directly to that specific page. ([Apple Developer][2])

So Replit must also confirm during your device walkthrough:

> **Profile/Settings → Delete Account** is visible, functional, and deletes the entire account rather than merely logging out or deactivating it.
