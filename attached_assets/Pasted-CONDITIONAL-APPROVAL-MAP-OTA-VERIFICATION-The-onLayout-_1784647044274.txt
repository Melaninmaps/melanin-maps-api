Important corrections to Replit’s report
The statement that Apple Maps always mounts at 0×0 inside tab navigators and never reloads is still a theory until logs prove that Build 93 actually received a zero-size layout. The change may fix it, but the report should say “suspected root cause addressed”, not confirmed root cause.
Also, API results do not prove voice works. A 401 only proves the protected endpoint responds without authentication. It does not prove:
the app sends the valid token;
microphone permission works;
speech recognition works;
TTS audio downloads;
audio plays through the device speaker.
Likewise, a 200 from Kinfolk chat does not prove the installed app receives and displays the response.
