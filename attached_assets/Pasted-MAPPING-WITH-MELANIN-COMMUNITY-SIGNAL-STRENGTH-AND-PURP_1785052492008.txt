MAPPING WITH MELANIN™ — COMMUNITY SIGNAL STRENGTH AND
PURPOSEFUL DATA STANDARD

BRAINSTORMING AND DOCUMENTATION ONLY — NO IMPLEMENTATION

Add this requirement to the Community Intelligence Constitution,
KinfolkAI architecture, Community Health architecture, Business
Intelligence vision, Cultural Ambassador impact model, Living Community
Feed, and Future-State Register.

CORE FOUNDER PRINCIPLE

KinfolkAI must understand scale.

A raw count is not automatically a meaningful community signal.

Example:

- 100 relevant searches among 500 people = 20%
- 100 relevant searches among 50,000 people = 0.2%

Although both contain 100 searches, the first may represent a substantial
local pattern while the second may represent ordinary background
activity.

KinfolkAI must not treat these signals as equivalent.

Information must be collected and interpreted for a defined purpose.
Mapping With Melanin™ must not collect information merely because it may
be useful someday.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. COMMUNITY SIGNAL STRENGTH MODEL
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Document a model containing at least:

A. PREVALENCE

The proportion of the relevant population represented by the signal.

B. VOLUME

The number of independent members, reports, searches, saves, events,
businesses, or verified records supporting the signal.

C. MOMENTUM

Whether the signal is increasing, decreasing, stable, seasonal, or a
one-time anomaly.

D. RELEVANCE

Whether the signal meaningfully applies to the specific member, business,
professional, organization, Cultural Ambassador, neighborhood, or
community receiving the insight.

E. CONFIDENCE

Whether the signal has enough recent, independent, representative, and
privacy-safe evidence to support a conclusion.

Do not finalize mathematical weights during this documentation phase.
Create configurable architecture and founder-review requirements.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2. DEFINE THE CORRECT DENOMINATOR
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

For each insight, identify the appropriate relevant population.

Potential denominators include:

- Active members in a ZIP code
- Active members within a defined radius
- Members who searched the relevant category
- Members planning travel to the location
- Members who selected a relevant interest
- Families who voluntarily selected family-related preferences
- Businesses in a specific category
- Verified local contributors
- Members active during the measured time period

Do not compare a local signal against the complete national membership
unless the question genuinely concerns the national population.

Document how KinfolkAI chooses and explains the denominator.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3. MINIMUM SAMPLE AND PRIVACY RULES
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A high percentage from a very small group must not automatically trigger
an insight.

Examples:

- 4 of 10 = 40%, but limited evidence
- 100 of 500 = 20%, stronger evidence
- 5,000 of 50,000 = 10%, highly supported evidence

Develop minimum sample requirements appropriate to:

- General consumer interests
- Business opportunities
- Community needs
- Safety and discrimination reports
- Health-related searches
- Legal-service searches
- Family and child-related information
- Employment and workplace reports
- Financial concerns
- Protected or sensitive characteristics

Sensitive categories require stronger anonymity and aggregation
thresholds.

KinfolkAI must not reveal insights where individuals could reasonably be
identified.

Do not expose exact counts for small or sensitive groups.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4. UNIQUE PEOPLE VERSUS REPEATED ACTIONS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Distinguish:

- 100 searches by 100 people
- 100 searches by 10 people
- 100 repeated searches by one person

Document:

- Unique-member counts
- Total actions
- Repeat behavior
- Bot or abuse filtering
- Anonymous-session handling
- Cross-device duplication
- Time-window deduplication

Do not treat repeated actions by one person as equivalent to independent
community demand.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5. TIME, BASELINE, AND SEASONALITY
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Every signal must include an appropriate time period.

Compare signals against:

- Previous period
- Same period last year, when available
- Normal seasonal pattern
- Community-specific baseline
- Category-specific baseline
- Relevant comparison communities, only when responsible

Examples:

- Back-to-school interest in August
- Brunch searches around holidays
- Moving searches near school-year changes
- Tax assistance in tax season
- Family activities during summer
- Heating repair during winter

KinfolkAI should distinguish normal seasonality from meaningful new
momentum.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
6. DATA QUALITY AND REPRESENTATION
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Document how KinfolkAI accounts for:

- Uneven platform adoption
- Communities with few MWM members
- Visitor activity versus resident activity
- Free versus paid member participation
- Highly active members
- Missing information
- Reporting campaigns that increase awareness
- Duplicate reports
- Moderation removals
- Delayed data
- Stale data
- Geographic boundary differences
- Population changes

KinfolkAI must not imply that MWM member behavior represents an entire
city when the sample does not support that conclusion.

Required language may include:

- “Among active MWM members in this area...”
- “Based on recent searches within Mapping With Melanin™...”
- “This is an emerging signal with limited information.”
- “The available information may not represent the full community.”

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7. BUSINESS INSIGHT STANDARD
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Business insights must use proportional, contextual language.

Unacceptable:

“100 people searched for brunch. Start brunch and increase sales.”

Acceptable:

“Brunch represented approximately 20% of restaurant-related searches
among active MWM members in your area over the past four weeks, compared
with 11% during the prior period. This may be worth exploring based on
your menu, staffing, costs, licensing, and business goals.”

Business guidance must:

- Never guarantee revenue
- Never exaggerate outcomes
- Explain the relevant population
- Explain the time period
- Distinguish searches from purchases
- Consider the business’s objectives and capabilities
- Invite evaluation rather than command action
- Offer realistic planning support
- Recommend community businesses or professionals where relevant
- Clearly distinguish organic insights from sponsored opportunities

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
8. COMMUNITY NEED AND OPPORTUNITY STANDARD
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

KinfolkAI may identify potential community needs only when signals are
sufficiently supported.

Examples:

- Skilled-trade demand
- Medical-professional shortages
- Legal-service gaps
- Childcare needs
- Accessibility services
- Mentorship needs
- Cultural event demand
- Business-category gaps
- Volunteer demand
- Education and apprenticeship opportunities

Use language such as:

- “May indicate an unmet need”
- “Suggests sustained local interest”
- “Relatively few verified providers are currently listed”
- “More information is needed before treating this as a confirmed
  shortage”

Do not declare a shortage solely because few businesses are listed on MWM.
The platform’s own coverage gaps must be considered.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
9. SAFETY AND DISCRIMINATION SIGNALS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Safety-related interpretation requires additional controls.

Consider:

- Unique reporters
- Relevant visitor or resident volume
- Reporting period
- Corroboration
- Severity
- Recency
- Duplicate incidents
- Public findings
- Historical context
- Community support or intervention
- Increased awareness of the reporting feature
- Underreporting
- Limited platform adoption

An increase in reports may mean:

- Conditions worsened
- Reporting access improved
- Awareness increased
- One event generated multiple related reports
- More MWM members visited the area

KinfolkAI must communicate uncertainty and must not assign community-wide
meaning from raw report totals.

Historical sundown-town data must remain separate from current report
rates.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
10. PURPOSEFUL DATA COLLECTION STANDARD
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Before collecting any new information, document:

1. The specific community or member benefit
2. The decision or experience it may responsibly improve
3. Whether less personal information could accomplish the same result
4. Whether the field is required or optional
5. How consent is obtained
6. Who can access it
7. Whether it is used individually or only in aggregate
8. How long it remains useful
9. When it expires or stops affecting results
10. How the member can view, correct, disable, or delete it
11. What harm could result from misuse
12. Whether the Community Intelligence Constitution permits its use

If no meaningful benefit and responsible use can be identified, do not
collect the information.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
11. MEMBER-FACING EXPLANATION
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Every community insight should support progressive disclosure.

LEVEL 1 — PLAIN RESULT

Example:
“Brunch interest is growing nearby.”

LEVEL 2 — WHY THIS RESULT

Example:
“Brunch represented 20% of recent restaurant searches among active MWM
members in this area, up from 11% in the prior period.”

LEVEL 3 — LEARN MORE

May include:

- Relevant population
- Unique-member count or safe range
- Time period
- Comparison baseline
- Confidence level
- Important limitations
- Data sources
- Last update
- Correction process

Do not overwhelm members with statistical terminology at Level 1.

Do not hide limitations from members who choose Learn More.
