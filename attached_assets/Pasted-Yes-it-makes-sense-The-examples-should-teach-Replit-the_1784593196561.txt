## When someone is searching for or adds a business

I agree with your cadence:

1. One initial notice.
2. No alert for every individual search.
3. Weekly or biweekly aggregated updates.
4. More detailed information only after the business claims and verifies its profile.

### Initial business notice

**SMS—only when the business has already consented to receive texts:**

> Mapping With Melanin™ community activity has reached your business. Your profile was recently searched for or added by a community member. Claim it to confirm your information and see available insights: [secure link]. Reply STOP to opt out.

A warmer version:

> Your business is being discovered on Mapping With Melanin™. A community member recently searched for or added your business. Claim your profile to confirm the details and learn more: [secure link]. Reply STOP to opt out.

Do **not** say:

> “See who searched for you.”

That suggests the business will receive private user identities, which it should not.

### Initial email

**Subject:** Your business is being discovered on Mapping With Melanin™

> Hi [Business Name],
>
> Members of the Mapping With Melanin™ community are helping others discover businesses and places they value. Your business was recently searched for or added to the platform.
>
> You can claim your profile to confirm your business information, add your story and media, respond to community feedback, and choose which updates you receive.
>
> [Claim and verify your profile]
>
> We protect member privacy, so individual search identities and private activity are not shared.

### Weekly summary for an unclaimed business

> **Your weekly Mapping With Melanin™ activity**
>
> Your business appeared in community activity [X] times this week through searches, saves, profile visits, or recommendations.
>
> Claim your profile to confirm your information and access the details available to verified owners.
>
> [Claim profile]

Only include metrics the platform actually captures.

### Claimed-business summary

> **Your community activity this week**
>
> [X] profile views
> [X] saves
> [X] requests for directions
> [X] shares
> [X] new community contributions

Kinfolk can phrase the summary naturally, but every number and recommendation must come from actual data.

## When a member enters someone’s phone number to invite them

I would **not** let Mapping With Melanin™ silently text any number merely because a member typed it in.

The safest flow is:

1. Member enters the number.
2. App shows the exact invitation.
3. Member taps **Send Invitation**.
4. Prefer opening the phone’s native Messages app with the text prefilled.
5. The member sends it personally.

That keeps the invitation person-to-person and avoids the platform collecting or repeatedly messaging a nonmember.

Suggested prefilled text:

> [First name] invited you to Mapping With Melanin™—a community-powered way to discover places, businesses, resources, and people wherever life takes you. Take a look: [personal referral link]

More personal:

> I thought you might enjoy Mapping With Melanin™. It helps you discover community, businesses, resources, events, and more at home or wherever you go. Here’s my invite: [link]

For a platform-sent SMS, the recipient must have a proper consent path before recurring texts. Twilio’s compliance guidance notes that merely providing a phone number does not necessarily establish consent for recurring messages, and recurring programs need clear opt-in and opt-out handling. ([Twilio][1])

Therefore:

* one user-directed invitation;
* no follow-up texts unless the recipient opts in;
* include sender identity and platform identity;
* never upload the inviter’s full contacts;
* honor STOP immediately.

## When someone sends an `@mention`

This should feel intriguing but not expose the content on a lock screen.

### Push notification

> **Someone brought you into the conversation.**

or:

> **You were mentioned in a community conversation.**

or slightly warmer:

> **Your voice was invited into a conversation.**

### In-app notification

> **[Display Name] mentioned you in [Group or Post].**
> “A short, age-appropriate preview…”
> **View conversation**

For private or sensitive groups:

> **You have a new mention in a private conversation.**

Do not show the post preview on the lock screen unless the user allows detailed previews.

### When the mention is about a business

> **[Member] mentioned [Business Name] in a community conversation.**

The verified owner can open the business notification center, but private member details should remain protected according to the post’s visibility.

## The correct message hierarchy

Use different language by channel:

**Push**

> You were mentioned in a community conversation.

**In-app**

> Jordan mentioned you in Philadelphia Creatives.

**Email digest**

> You had 3 mentions and 2 replies this week.

**SMS**
Reserve this for explicitly enabled, important categories—not ordinary mentions by default.

## Recommended frequency controls

For members:

* Connection requests: Immediate
* Mentions and direct replies: Immediate or Digest
* Community activity: Daily/Weekly/Off
* Business and event discovery: Weekly/Off
* Safety alerts: Separate controls
* Kinfolk suggestions: Limited and optional

For businesses:

* Claim/security/verification: Immediate
* New review requiring attention: Immediate or Daily
* Searches/views/saves: Weekly or Biweekly
* Community mentions: Daily or Weekly
* Performance summary: Weekly or Monthly
* Promotional opportunities: Optional

The core rule should be:

> **Immediate messages are for actions requiring attention. Aggregated messages are for activity worth understanding.**

That preserves curiosity without becoming noisy or manipulative.
