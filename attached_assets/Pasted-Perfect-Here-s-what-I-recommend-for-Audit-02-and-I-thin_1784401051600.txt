# AUDIT 02 — Onboarding, Profiles, Identity & Member Journey

If Authentication answers:

> **"Can I get in?"**

Then Audit 02 answers:

> **"Do I immediately understand who this platform is for, what it can do, and how to become part of the community?"**

This is where first impressions are made.

---

# My expectation for this audit

I don't want Replit to tell us:

> "There are 17 onboarding screens."

I want them to tell us:

> "A new member goes from account creation to becoming an active community member through this exact journey..."

If they can't tell that story, then the onboarding experience isn't fully designed yet.

---

# What we'll review

## A. Account Creation Experience

Questions we'll answer:

* What is the first screen?
* Is it welcoming?
* Does it explain Mapping With Melanin™?
* Does it explain why information is collected?
* Can users skip steps?
* Should they?

---

## B. Profile Creation

I want to inspect every field.

For each one we'll ask:

### Why does this exist?

If we don't have a good answer...

it shouldn't be there.

---

Example:

Birthday

Why?

Age verification?

Recommendations?

Birthday celebrations?

Legal requirement?

Children's safety?

If we don't need the full birthday...

Should we collect only month/year?

Or age bracket?

Less data = less risk.

---

Example:

Race / Ethnicity

Very important.

How is it asked?

Is it optional?

Can multiple identities be selected?

Can users decline?

How does it affect recommendations?

Does it affect anything?

How is it stored?

How is it protected?

Should it ever be shown publicly?

## One improvement I'd like to make to our audit process

I want every audit to end with this question:

> **If someone downloaded Mapping With Melanin™ today and this was the ONLY feature they experienced, would they understand why this platform exists?**

If the answer is "no," then even if the code is flawless, the feature isn't finished.

That question will keep us anchored to your mission throughout this entire review.

When you're ready, I'll draft the same detailed read-only audit prompt for **Audit 02** that we created for Authentication, so Replit receives the same level of specificity. I think maintaining that consistency across all audits will give us the clearest possible picture of the platform.
