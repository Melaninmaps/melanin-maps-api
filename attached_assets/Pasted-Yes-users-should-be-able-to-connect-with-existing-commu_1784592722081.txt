Yes—users should be able to **connect with existing community members** and **invite people who have not joined yet**. I would not make contact syncing the only path, and I would not upload anyone’s address book automatically.

Because RC2 is currently frozen and ready for the next builds, this should be logged for RC3 rather than added to Build 92/66.  

## Recommended experience

### 1. Find people already on Mapping With Melanin™

Give users a **Find Community** screen with:

* Search by name or username
* People from shared groups or Kinfolk Circles
* Mutual connections
* Shared interests
* People in the same selected city or community
* Cultural Ambassadors, mentors, creators, or business owners they follow

Suggestions should explain why someone appears:

> **3 mutual connections**

> **Also follows Philadelphia events**

> **Member of the Anime Circle**

Avoid suggestions based on sensitive assumptions such as health conditions, religion, exact location, or private searches.

### 2. Invite someone who is not registered

The safest launch method is a personal referral link:

> **Invite to Mapping With Melanin™**

The member can share it through:

* Text
* Email
* WhatsApp
* Messenger
* QR code
* Copy link
* The phone’s normal share menu

This does not require Mapping With Melanin™ to collect or store the recipient’s contact information. The user chooses where and to whom the invitation is sent.

The link should include the inviter’s referral code so the new member can be connected after registration, subject to both people’s privacy choices.

### 3. Optional contacts feature

Contacts can be offered later as an explicit option:

> **Find people you already know**

Before requesting permission, explain:

> “We’ll use your contacts only to help identify people you may know or help you send invitations. We won’t message anyone without you choosing to send it.”

Preferred approach:

* User taps **Find from Contacts**
* App asks permission in context
* Matching happens on-device where practical
* Do not retain the full address book
* Do not send automatic invitations
* Do not repeatedly ask after the user declines
* Provide a way to disconnect and delete imported matching data

For the initial release, I would use the system share sheet and referral links first. It is simpler, safer, and less likely to make people uncomfortable.

## Connection model

I recommend one clear mutual relationship:

> **Connect**

Flow:

1. Member taps **Connect**
2. Recipient receives a request
3. Recipient accepts, declines, or blocks
4. Only accepted connections receive connection-level profile access

Businesses can be **Followed** or **Saved**, while people are **Connected**. That keeps the relationship types clear:

* Connect with people
* Follow groups, creators, and businesses
* Save places and resources

## Privacy and minor protections

For adults:

* Public profile discoverability: On/Off
* Allow connection requests: Everyone / Mutuals / Nobody
* Show in local suggestions: On/Off
* Social links: Public / Connections / Private

For users under 18:

* Hidden from adult discovery suggestions by default
* No exact-location matching
* Restrict unsolicited adult connection requests
* No public contact details
* Social links private by default
* Block invitations into adult-rated groups or events

A user should also be able to remove a connection without notifying the other person, block them, and report harassment.
