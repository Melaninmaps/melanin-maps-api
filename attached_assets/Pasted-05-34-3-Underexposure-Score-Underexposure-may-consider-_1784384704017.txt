05.34.3 Underexposure Score
Underexposure may consider:
low impression share;
low follower count relative to outcomes;
low advertising activity;
low search position;
limited mainstream coverage;
low review volume with strong repeat behavior;
strong local recognition not reflected online;
new platform entry with older community presence;
geographic underrepresentation.
Underexposure must not be confused with lack of quality.
05.35 Community Need Score
Community Need measures whether surfacing the candidate may help address an identified gap.
It may evaluate:
service scarcity;
resource shortage;
geographic gap;
cultural-access gap;
transportation gap;
affordability gap;
representation gap;
opportunity gap;
time-sensitive need;
underserved search demand.
05.35.1 Formula
Community Need Score =
  Demand Intensity
× Scarcity
× Relevance
× Accessibility Potential
× Evidence Confidence
Normalized to 0–100.
05.35.2 Demand Intensity
Demand may be established through:
repeated unsuccessful searches;
waitlist interest;
saved topic activity;
community requests;
resource inquiries;
route demand;
relocation questions;
verified community surveys;
unmet opportunity searches.
Demand must be aggregated and privacy-protected.
05.35.3 Scarcity factor
Availability within relevant area	Scarcity factor
No known eligible option	1.00
One eligible option	0.90
Two to three	0.75
Four to six	0.55
Seven to ten	0.35
More than ten	0.20
Scarcity may be category-, context-, price-, access-, or ownership-specific.
05.36 Opportunity Discovery
Opportunity Discovery applies to:
employment;
contracts;
grants;
scholarships;
mentorship;
business partnerships;
vendor opportunities;
housing leads;
educational programs;
fellowships;
community leadership roles;
internships;
volunteer opportunities.
Opportunity candidates require:
source identification;
deadline;
eligibility criteria;
location or remote status;
compensation or benefit disclosure where applicable;
application method;
current availability;
fraud screening;
conflict disclosure;
expiration handling.
Expired opportunities must not remain ranked as active.
05.37 Resource Discovery
Resource Discovery prioritizes practical accessibility over popularity.
Required considerations:
verified availability;
eligibility clarity;
hours;
location;
transportation;
language access;
cost;
documentation requirements;
urgency;
capacity;
application process;
privacy burden;
current contact information.
A resource with high reputation but unavailable capacity may rank below a less-known resource that can currently help.
05.38 Cultural Heritage Discovery
Cultural Heritage Discovery may surface:
HBCUs;
museums;
landmarks;
archives;
neighborhoods;
memorials;
historic businesses;
cultural institutions;
festivals;
oral histories;
historical routes;
community traditions;
educational resources.
Ranking may prioritize:
verified historical significance;
community stewardship;
cultural relevance;
preservation quality;
accessibility;
educational value;
local connection;
current availability.
Popularity has limited influence.
