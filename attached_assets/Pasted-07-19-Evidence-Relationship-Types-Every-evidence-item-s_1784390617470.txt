07.19 Evidence Relationship Types
Every evidence item should identify the source relationship.
Relationship	Example
Community member	Person affected by the work
Business partner	Business featured or supported
Institution partner	Museum, HBCU, archive
Audience member	Viewer, reader, participant
Event participant	Attendee
Collaborator	Co-creator or project partner
Employee or contractor	Worked directly with entity
Cultural subject	Person or group represented
Sponsor	Financial partner
Independent evaluator	Reviewer or researcher
Platform record	Verified system outcome
Self-reported	Submitted by evaluated entity
Anonymous	Source not publicly identified
Unknown	Relationship cannot be determined
Self-reported impact requires corroboration before it may strongly influence partnership or recognition eligibility.
07.20 Outcome Attribution
The Engine must distinguish correlation from attributable influence.
An outcome may be classified as:
Attribution level	Meaning	Factor
Verified direct	Tracked action clearly linked	1.00
Strongly attributable	Multiple supporting indicators	0.90
Probable	Reasonable link with limitations	0.75
Contributory	Influence likely helped among other causes	0.60
Possible	Weak relationship	0.35
Unknown	No reliable attribution	0.00
Examples of stronger attribution:
trackable referral;
campaign code;
direct user confirmation;
business analytics;
application source;
event-registration source;
verified link click followed by completion;
repeated business confirmation.
Attribution systems must respect privacy and data minimization.
07.21 Qualified Action Values
Not all actions have equal cultural or community value.
Action	Baseline value range
View or impression	1–3
Meaningful completion of video/article	3–6
Save or follow for future use	4–8
Contextual share	5–10
Visit to business profile or website	6–12
Verified business visit or purchase	10–20
Event attendance	10–20
Resource use	12–25
Opportunity application	12–25
Mentorship connection	20–35
Paid collaboration created	20–40
Community program formed	30–50
Sustained institutional partnership	40–60
Demonstrated multi-stage community outcome	50–100
These values are internal baselines and require Weight Register approval.
07.22 Commercial Influence Rules
Commercial activity is permitted.
Commercial activity is not automatically extraction.
The Engine distinguishes:
disclosed sponsorship;
affiliate relationship;
paid partnership;
commission;
platform promotion;
gifted product or service;
unpaid community support;
editorial selection;
personal recommendation.
