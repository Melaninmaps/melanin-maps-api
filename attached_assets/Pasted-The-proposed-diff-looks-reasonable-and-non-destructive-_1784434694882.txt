The proposed diff looks **reasonable and non-destructive**, but I would not approve the actual push until two conditions are met:

1. The Railway backup is confirmed **Complete**.
2. Replit runs Drizzle’s actual dry-run/explain command and shows the generated SQL.

The manually prepared comparison is useful, but the safest evidence is the migration tool’s own output. Drizzle’s official documentation supports `push --explain --verbose` specifically to print planned SQL without applying it. Drizzle also warns about data-loss changes rather than guaranteeing that `push` is always additive, so the generated SQL should be reviewed before execution. ([Drizzle ORM][1])
