## One thing I would add—and I think it's critical.

Kinfolk should always be honest about *why* it is making a suggestion.

For example:

> **"We've noticed more families in your community are searching for toddler-friendly travel. Since you've chosen to share your parenting journey, you may be uniquely positioned to help. Would you like to create something? No thanks is always okay."**

Or:

> **"People who follow your museum guides have recently searched for Afro-Mexican history in Cabo. If this interests you, your perspective could fill an important gap."**

That transparency matters. Creators should never feel manipulated by an invisible algorithm. They should feel like they're collaborating with Kinfolk to serve a community.

I honestly think that is the philosophical difference between Mapping With Melanin™ and traditional social media:

> **Algorithms chase engagement. Kinfolk cultivates contribution.**

If you preserve that principle, I think creators will come to trust the platform because they'll understand that every suggestion has one purpose: helping someone else on their journey—not simply generating another click.
