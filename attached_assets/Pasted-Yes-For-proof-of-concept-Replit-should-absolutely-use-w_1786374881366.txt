I would also give Replit one principle that matters a lot here: **do not make “minority-owned” the admission requirement for the whole database.** Your platform can contain a plumber, church, doctor, museum, spa, daycare, or restaurant because it is useful to discovery; promotion, verification, community relevance, ownership designation, and ranking are separate questions.

That will let the proof of concept feel like an actual **life map**, instead of a restaurant directory with a few cultural businesses added to it.

And for houses of faith specifically, I would rather see **five well-verified, meaningfully different institutions in a city** than fifty churches pulled from a generic directory. We need enough diversity for the tester to prove that “Black Catholic,” “AME,” “mosque,” “Ethiopian Orthodox,” etc. actually mean something to search.
