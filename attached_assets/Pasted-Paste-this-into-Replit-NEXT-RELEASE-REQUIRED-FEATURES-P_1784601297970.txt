>
> Members should be able to build a trusted community without creating popularity mechanics.
>
> Use:
>
> * **Connect** for mutual member relationships;
> * **Follow** for businesses, creators, organizations, and groups;
> * **Save** for places, events, and resources.
>

>

> * receive a connection request;
> * accept;
> * decline;
> * cancel a pending request;
> * remove a connection silently;
> * block;
> * report;
> * view connection status on a member profile.
>
> Do not use public friend counts, popularity rankings, or follower competition.
>
> ## Circles integration
>
> Connections and Circles must work together without becoming the same feature.
>
> * A connection is a mutual relationship between two members.
> * A Circle is a group organized around travel, interests, planning, family, or community.
> * A member may invite an accepted connection into a Circle.
> * Public Circles may still be discoverable according to existing privacy rules.
> * Private Circles remain invitation-based.
> * Blocking a member must remove or prevent inappropriate Circle interaction according to the approved safety rules.
>
> Do not break existing Kinfolk Circles, tier limits, itineraries, or circle privacy.
