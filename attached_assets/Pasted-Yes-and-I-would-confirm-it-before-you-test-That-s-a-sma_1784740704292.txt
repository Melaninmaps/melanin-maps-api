### Why I'm recommending this

There are two common, valid designs:

**Option A (what I'd expect for your app):**

* Account is deleted.
* Email becomes available for reuse.
* You can create a new account later with the same email.

**Option B (also valid):**

* Account is deleted/anonymized.
* Email remains reserved forever.
* You cannot register again with that email.

Neither is inherently wrong—but you should know which one your app implements **before** using an email you may want later.

Given where you are in the App Store process, I'd spend five minutes verifying this rather than discovering afterward that your test email is permanently reserved.
