Release Readiness Review: Mapping With Melanin™ Build 97
Executive Summary
This review evaluates Build 97 as a complete product, focusing on its ability to communicate the platform’s vision, provide a meaningful experience for 30 pilot testers, and obtain Apple approval before your tour begins in six days.

Overall Recommendation: CONDITIONAL GO
The core product is compelling and the primary technical blocker (database exhaustion) has been addressed. However, the release is currently a NO-GO until three critical “Product Integrity” fixes are applied to ensure Apple approval and a professional tester experience.


1. The Apple Reviewer’s Perspective
If I were the Apple reviewer, here is what I would flag for rejection in the current state of Build 97:

A. Performance & Completeness (Guideline 2.1) - ACTIVE BLOCKER
	•	Rejection Risk: The previous rejection was due to a database outage. While the fix is implemented, it has not been deployed to Railway production. If Apple reviews the app before the deployment and a manual Railway restart, they will hit the same 500 errors.
	•	Correction: Deploy the Build 97 backend to Railway and perform a manual restart to clear the leaked connection pool immediately.

B. Metadata & Config (Guideline 5.1.1) - ACTIVE BLOCKER
	•	Rejection Risk: Your `Info.plist` contains generic template strings for Face ID and Motion detection. Apple frequently rejects apps that request permissions without a specific, user-facing explanation.
	•	Correction: Remove `NSFaceIDUsageDescription` and `NSMotionUsageDescription` from `Info.plist` if these features are not used.

C. Build Number Conflict - ACTIVE BLOCKER
	•	Rejection Risk: App Store Connect will reject the binary upload if the `buildNumber` is not incremented from 96 to 97.
	•	Correction: Ensure `app.json` is updated to `"buildNumber": "97"`.

3. Product Vision & Feature Audit
A. Heritage Places - SHIP NOW
	•	Status: Fully implemented with a dedicated database schema and map integration.
	•	Value: This is the “soul” of the app. It distinguishes Mapping With Melanin™ from a standard business directory.
	•	Recommendation: Ensure the “View Details” deep-links work perfectly, as this is where the storytelling happens.

B. Historical Sundown Towns - SHIP AS “HISTORICAL REFERENCE”
	•	Status: Partially planned; data import not confirmed.
	•	Risk: If shipped as a “Safety Warning,” it could trigger legal or Apple “objectionable content” flags.
	•	Recommendation: Ship this as a read-only historical layer within the Heritage feature.
	◦	Minimum Viable Presentation: Use the `cultural_sites` model.
	◦	Mandatory: Include the Tougaloo College attribution and a clear disclaimer: “Historical record only. Does not reflect current safety or demographics.”
	◦	Do NOT include any “danger scores.”

C. KinfolkAI - SHIP NOW
	•	Status: GPT-4o backed with live weather and user preference integration.
	•	Value: This is your primary “wow” feature.
	•	Recommendation: Monitor OpenAI costs closely during the first 48 hours of testing.

D. Community Feed - SHIP NOW
	•	Status: Functional with reporting and moderation.
	•	Value: Provides the “social proof” that the app is alive.
	•	Recommendation: Seed the feed with 5-10 high-quality posts before the testers arrive so they don’t see an empty screen.
