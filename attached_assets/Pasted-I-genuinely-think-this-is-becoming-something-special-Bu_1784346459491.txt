# There are three things I would change.

## 1. I would not replace "Page" with "Moment."

This is actually the one place where I disagree with my own earlier thinking.

After sitting with it, I think we've conflated two different things.

A **Moment** is part of the storytelling.

A **Page** is part of the publication.

Those are different.

For example:

**Experience Chapter**

**Page 5**

contains

**The Trust Moment**

Likewise:

* Page 11 contains **The Kinfolk Moment**
* Page 21 contains **The Welcome Home Moment**

That gives you two useful layers:

**Publishing layer**

* Volume
* Chapter
* Page

**Storytelling layer**

* Moment
* Emotion
* Purpose

That distinction will become incredibly valuable when you're creating coffee table books, documentaries, websites, and printed editions. The same "Moment" might appear on different pages or in different media.

So I'd preserve both.
