That's actually good news. We can cross **voice input** off the critical list.

So the issue isn't **"Can I speak?"**

The issue is **"What does KinfolkAI™ do after I speak?"**

From what you've found today, I'd separate the audit into **Capabilities** and **Intelligence**.

### Capabilities (Can it do it?)

From your testing, I'd verify each of these:

* ⏳ Weather (using shared location)
* ⏳ Navigation and directions
* ⏳ Nearby minority-owned businesses
* ⏳ Business hours
* ⏳ Phone calls
* ⏳ Website opening
* ⏳ Save to favorites
* ⏳ Hidden Gem nominations
* ⏳ Community events
* ⏳ Safety information
* ⏳ Neighborhood recommendations
* ⏳ Travel planning
* ⏳ Relocation assistance
* ⏳ Business comparisons
* ⏳ Reading business reviews
* ⏳ Explaining *why* it recommended something

### Intelligence (Does it think naturally?)

This is where I think the biggest gap is.

For example:

**You:** "What's the weather?"

Expected behavior:

> "You're in Philadelphia. It's currently 84° and sunny with a chance of thunderstorms this evening."

Not:

> "What city?"

---

**You:** "Find me brunch."

Expected:

> "Here are the highest-rated minority-owned brunch spots within 10 miles."

---

**You:** "Take me there."

Expected:

> Launch navigation to the last business discussed.

---

**You:** "Is this neighborhood safe?"

Expected:

> Use the current map location or the business being viewed without asking again.

---

### The opening message

I'd also change the placeholder text. Instead of a generic prompt, I'd use something that reflects the platform's mission.

For example:

> **Ask me about your community, nearby businesses, safety, travel, or anything else.**

Or even more conversational:

> **Where can I help you today?**

---

## One feature I'd add

I also think KinfolkAI™ should have **suggested prompts** under the input box, especially for new users. They could rotate based on context.

For example:

**At home**

* 📍 Hidden Gems near me
* ☕ Find coffee nearby
* 🎉 What's happening this weekend?
* 🛡️ Is this neighborhood safe?

**While traveling**

* 🍽️ Minority-owned restaurants
* 🏨 Find accommodations
* 🚶 Plan my day
* ☀️ What's today's weather?

That helps users discover what KinfolkAI™ can do without guessing.
