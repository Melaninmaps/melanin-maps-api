05.16 Dimension Four — Temporal Fit
Temporal Fit measures whether the candidate is relevant and available when needed.
It evaluates:
open-now status;
event date and time;
application deadline;
service hours;
seasonal availability;
appointment timing;
active versus expired opportunity;
evidence freshness;
travel dates;
long-term relevance.
05.16.1 Temporal scoring rules
Condition	Score
Confirmed available at required time	100
Highly likely available	85
Availability partially confirmed	65
Time information outdated	40
Availability unknown	25
Confirmed unavailable	0
For relocation, heritage, or historical discovery, operational hours may receive lower weight than long-term relevance.
05.17 Dimension Five — Evidence Sufficiency
Evidence Sufficiency measures whether the Engine has enough reliable information to rank the candidate responsibly.
It uses the BIF Evidence Quality Score and considers:
source reliability;
verification;
corroboration;
recency;
specificity;
independence;
conflicts of interest;
volume;
consistency.
05.17.1 Evidence Sufficiency formula
Evidence Sufficiency Score =
  BIF Evidence Quality Score
× Evidence Coverage Factor
× Entity Identity Confidence
Normalized to 0–100.
A high relevance candidate with low evidence may remain visible but receive limited recommendation status.
05.18 Dimension Six — Community Value
Community Value measures how the candidate contributes to connection, reciprocity, support, access, voice, continuity, or shared benefit.
The Discovery Engine™ does not calculate the underlying Community Score.
It receives an approved output from the Community Engine™.
05.18.1 Community Value input
Community Value Score =
  Community Engine Adjusted Score
× Context Relevance Factor
Community Value may improve ranking but may not override:
safety blocks;
hard filters;
verification requirements;
explicit user exclusions;
governance holds.
05.19 Dimension Seven — Cultural Relevance
Cultural Relevance measures how meaningfully the candidate relates to the user’s expressed cultural, historical, community, or diaspora objective.
It may consider:
verified ownership;
historical significance;
culturally relevant service;
local cultural roots;
diaspora connection;
language familiarity;
community storytelling;
cultural programming;
creator attribution;
tradition preservation;
community recognition.
The Discovery Engine™ receives cultural evidence from:
Cultural Influence Engine™;
Legacy & Learning Engine™;
Trust Engine™;
Community Engine™;
verified entity records.
Cultural relevance must not be inferred solely from imagery, names, neighborhood demographics, or marketing language.
05.20 Dimension Eight — Opportunity or Utility Value
This dimension measures the practical usefulness of the candidate.
Examples:
solves the requested need;
provides access to a resource;
creates economic opportunity;
supports travel;
meets eligibility requirements;
provides a verified service;
reduces effort or uncertainty;
offers meaningful connection;
fits budget or transportation limits;
can be acted upon now.
05.20.1 Formula
Utility Value Score =
  Need Satisfaction
+ Actionability
+ Availability
+ Outcome Potential
+ Friction Reduction
- Practical Barrier Penalty
