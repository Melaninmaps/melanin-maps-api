It makes perfect sense, and I actually think this could become one of the defining features of Mapping with Melanin™.

Most platforms ask businesses to answer:

> "Tell us about your business."

That's not what creates connection.

People don't remember mission statements.
They remember **people, stories, values, and passion.**

I would position these as **conversation starters**, not interview questions. Let businesses choose 2–5 prompts so every introduction feels authentic rather than scripted.

---

# Meet the Owner

### Why did you start this business?

What happened in your life that made you decide this needed to exist?

---

### What does this business mean to you?

Not what you do—but what it represents.

---

### What do you hope people feel when they walk through your door?

Describe the experience you want someone to have.

---

### What's something people usually don't know about your business?

Share something that surprises people.

---

### What are you most proud of?

A moment, accomplishment, customer, or lesson.

---

# The Last Question (My Favorite)

Instead of ending with "Visit us," end with:

> **"If someone remembers only one thing about you after watching this video... what do you hope it is?"**

That answer is almost always genuine.

## I also have an idea that I think fits Mapping with Melanin™ perfectly.

Rather than just showing a blank recording screen, KinfolkAI could coach owners before they record. It might say:

> **Need some inspiration?**
>
> People connect with stories more than sales. Talk as if you're welcoming a new neighbor into your business for the first time.
>
> You don't have to be perfect. Just be yourself.

This shifts the experience from "making a marketing video" to "introducing yourself to your community," which aligns beautifully with the purpose of your platform. It reinforces that Mapping with Melanin™ isn't just helping people discover businesses—it's helping them discover the people behind them.
