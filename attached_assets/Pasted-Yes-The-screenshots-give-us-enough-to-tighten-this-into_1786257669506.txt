## The four safety tiers

I particularly want to lock this piece because I think your instinct is right: **people should not be deciding whether their lived experience was “Medium” or “High.”**

My recommendation is to keep a normalized internal severity/priority field for analytics and moderation, but let the user choose **spoken experiences**.

The strongest general-purpose progression is:

**Something felt off → I felt unsafe → I needed to leave or get help → Someone could be in immediate danger**

That language tells us much more than `Low / Medium / High / Critical`.

And then we customize the language based on where they entered the report.

For police/ICE:

**The interaction concerned me → I felt targeted or unsafe → Force, detention, or serious misconduct occurred → There is an immediate safety threat**

For travel/sundown-town context:

**Sharing historical or local context → Recent experiences made me concerned → I felt targeted or unsafe here → There may be an immediate danger**

For restaurants/businesses:

**Something felt off → I felt unsafe or unwelcome → I needed to leave/get help → Someone could be in immediate danger**

The backend can still translate those into something like:

`priority_1 / priority_2 / priority_3 / priority_4`

without ever making the member understand our internal severity model.

And **Excessive Force/Misconduct should itself elevate priority**. Someone should not have to tap “Excessive Force” and then wonder whether that counts as Medium or High.

One more distinction I added intentionally: **historical sundown-town information is not itself an incident report.** Someone should be able to contribute local/historical context without implying they personally experienced current danger. That will help protect both the integrity of your safety feature and the communities being described.

This gives Replit a finite correction pass with proof gates. After they return the read-only findings and implementation proof, we can review it once, authorize the push, and stop allowing “the endpoint exists” to substitute for **the member can actually see and use the feature.**
