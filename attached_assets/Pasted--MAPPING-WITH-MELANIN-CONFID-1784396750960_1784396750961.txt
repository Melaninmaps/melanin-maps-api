# 24.1 Purpose

The Constitutional Intelligence Layer™ is the supreme reasoning architecture governing every intelligent system inside Mapping With Melanin™.

Unlike individual engines, which answer specialized questions, the Constitutional Intelligence Layer™ determines **how all engines think together**.

It exists to ensure that every recommendation, conversation, recognition, safety assessment, opportunity, partnership, moderation decision, and future capability remains constitutionally consistent regardless of technological change.

It determines:

1. which engines participate;
2. how disagreements are resolved;
3. which evidence receives priority;
4. when constitutional principles override optimization;
5. when automation must stop;
6. how uncertainty is managed;
7. how institutional memory influences present decisions;
8. how future evolution preserves founding principles;
9. how constitutional consistency survives organizational growth;
10. how the Invisible Architecture™ remains one coherent intelligence rather than twenty-three separate systems.

The governing question is:

> **If every engine reached a different conclusion, what decision would remain most faithful to the Constitution of Mapping With Melanin™?**
