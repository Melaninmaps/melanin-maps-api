  06.1   Governing Question                                   ✅
         "Given everything we know right now, what is the
         safest recommendation we can responsibly make
         without creating unnecessary fear or unnecessary
         confidence?"
         Explicit non-purposes: not a crime predictor, not a
         neighborhood ranking system, not a policing tool.
         Classification: decision-support system.

  06.2   Guiding Principles                                   ✅
         Safety is never binary. Eight independent dimensions:
         physical / psychological / cultural / discrimination
         risk / accessibility / environmental hazards /
         emergency readiness / situational context.
         A place may be physically safe and culturally unsafe
         simultaneously — both realities are preserved.

  06.3   Safety Objects (8 object classes)                    ✅
         Person / Business / Neighborhood (day/night/event/
         weather/crowding) / Event / Travel Route (walking,
         driving, transit, rideshare, late-night) /
         Institution / City

  06.4   Safety Categories (8 independent categories)        ✅
         Physical Safety (violence, theft, harassment,
         traffic, lighting, environmental)
         Cultural Safety (bias, discrimination,
         microaggressions, representation, welcoming)
         Identity Safety (race, ethnicity, religion,
         nationality, gender, disability, LGBTQ+,
         visible cultural expression)
         Environmental Safety (flooding, heat, air quality,
         weather, construction, natural disasters)
         Health Safety (hospitals, urgent care, pharmacies,
         emergency response time, AED availability)
         Accessibility Safety (wheelchairs, visual,
         hearing, mobility, transportation)
         Family Safety (children, parents, caregivers,
         family amenities)
         Digital Safety (marketplace scams, fraud, fake
         listings, identity abuse, impersonation)

  06.5   Risk Philosophy                                      ✅
         Not "Is this place safe?" but:
         "Safe for whom? Under what conditions?
         At what time? Based on which evidence?"

  06.6   Evidence Hierarchy (4 tiers)                        ✅
         Tier 1 — Verified firsthand, verified businesses/
                  users, official emergency alerts
         Tier 2 — Multiple independent community reports,
                  historical consistency, partner orgs
         Tier 3 — Single verified observation, trusted
                  public datasets, moderated submissions
         Tier 4 — Unverified community submissions,
                  anonymous reports, emerging patterns
         Rule: Tier 4 may influence monitoring only —
         never independently determines a recommendation.

  06.7   Safety Confidence Score™                            ✅
         Two separate values per recommendation:
           Risk Score (0–100): level of concern
           Confidence Score (0–100): certainty of assessment
         Example A: Risk 82 / Confidence 19 =
           "Concerning signals, very limited evidence"
         Example B: Risk 64 / Confidence 97 =
           "Moderate concern, extensive evidence"
         Purpose: prevents overreaction to isolated incidents
         AND prevents false reassurance from sparse data.

  06.8   Core Principle                                       ✅
         "The Safety Intelligence Engine™ exists to reduce
         uncertainty — not to eliminate it."
         Responsibility: help members make more informed,
         context-aware decisions while preserving dignity,
         fairness, and trust. NOT to promise safety.
