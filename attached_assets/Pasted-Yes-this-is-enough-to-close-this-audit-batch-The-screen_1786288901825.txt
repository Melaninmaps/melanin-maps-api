A few of your product instincts from this pass are worth explicitly locking in.

For **@mentions**, I would absolutely allow a member to tag the **business profile directly**, not just some separate MWM tag identity. The user should never have to think about your database architecture. They type `@Thee Pink...`, they see the business with its city/category, tap it, and the mention links to the canonical MWM business profile. The same interaction searches people too, separated visually into **People** and **Businesses**. If there is no match, **Add this business** becomes the natural next action.

For the **map**, your latest description is the clearest version so far: it should be quiet until the user tells it what they want. `The Pink Tea Cup` may give one result; `Allure Hair` might give four; `barber` should trigger location + relevant refinements instead of throwing the database at them. The Business Directory can be where I intentionally say **“hair stores in Atlanta”** while sitting in Pennsylvania.

For **sundown towns**, I like your subtle-overlay concept with one protection: **history and present danger cannot share one undifferentiated darkness scale.** I would let historical context create a discreet baseline indication, then allow **recent credible safety evidence** to deepen the gold/amber emphasis. When the user taps the area, the explanation should say whether they're seeing historical context, recent community reports, an official advisory, or some combination. That gives the traveler useful situational awareness without turning the map into a fear map.

And on **Kinfolk's Soul Food response**: visually, I agree—it finally looks much closer to what Kinfolk should feel like. But now Replit has to prove those businesses came from your actual data. That is the next level of quality control: **a beautiful answer isn't enough; it needs provenance.**

We now have enough to give Replit one disciplined pass, demand proof **before** a production push, and stop bouncing from screenshot to screenshot fixing symptoms.
