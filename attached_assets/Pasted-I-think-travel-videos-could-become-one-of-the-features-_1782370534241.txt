I think travel videos could become one of the features that makes Mapping with Melanin™ truly different. Imagine someone searching for Brazil, Atlanta, or Accra and seeing authentic videos from people in the community instead of polished travel ads. That could become a major reason people keep coming back.
Here's what I would do
🆓 Community Member (Free)
Allow them to:
✅ Upload up to 5 videos
✅ Upload photos
✅ Create posts
✅ Review businesses
✅ Share travel experiences
✅ Appear in search results
This helps your content library grow quickly.
⭐ Premium Community
Unlimited content creation plus creator tools.
♾️ Unlimited video uploads
🎥 Longer videos
📌 Featured travel guides
📈 Creator analytics (views, likes, saves)
🤖 AI-generated captions and hashtags
🗂️ Create destination collections (e.g., "My Favorite Places in Brazil")
🏅 Creator badge
🚀 Priority placement in destination searches
💰 Eligibility for future creator partnerships or monetization
This gives people a reason to upgrade without preventing free users from contributing.

My recommendation
I would not hide travel videos behind a paywall.
Your community-generated content is what will help your platform rank in search, keep people engaged, and build trust.
Instead, monetize the tools, not the ability to contribute.

One feature I think could become iconic
I'd add a button called:
"Show Me the Vibe."
Instead of reading dozens of reviews, users tap Show Me the Vibe and instantly watch short community videos from that city, neighborhood, or business.
For example:
Searching Rio de Janeiro could show clips of neighborhoods, restaurants, beaches, nightlife, and cultural events.
Searching a restaurant could show diners' experiences, the atmosphere, and the food from real visitors.
That's the kind of feature that can make Mapping with Melanin™ memorable and encourage users to return—not because they need to find one business, but because they enjoy exploring destinations through authentic community experiences.
