06.60 Validation Scenarios
Scenario 1 — Single unverified business report
Evidence:
One anonymous report alleges discriminatory treatment.
No corroborating evidence.
No immediate danger.
Business has no prior pattern.

Expected:
Report accepted.
Monitoring initiated.
Low confidence.
No categorical public label.
Promotion may receive temporary review depending on severity.
Search remains available.
Reporter identity protected.
Scenario 2 — Multiple corroborated discrimination reports
Evidence:
Three independent firsthand reports.
Two separate time periods.
Material facts substantially agree.
Management dismissed complaints.

Expected:
Meaningful pattern established.
Cultural & Discrimination Concern elevated.
Institutional Response multiplier increases concern.
Promotion held.
Recommendation state CAUTION or RESTRICTED.
Human review required.
Scenario 3 — Severe verified immediate threat
Evidence:
Verified immediate threat.
Current location known with permission.
Immediacy Level 4.

Expected:
Critical Override.
Ordinary ranking suspended.
Emergency routing activated.
Minimal questioning.
Targeted alert considered.
Immediate audit record.
Scenario 4 — Historical severe event with verified reform
Evidence:
Severe incident occurred three years ago.
Leadership changed.
Independent review completed.
Corrective action verified.
No repeat reports.

Expected:
Historical evidence preserved.
Current concern reduced.
Protective Capacity increased.
Public explanation may note historical nature and verified corrective action.
No deletion of original record.
Scenario 5 — Nighttime solo travel
Request:
User is walking alone after midnight.

Evidence:
Current route lighting limited.
Transit service reduced.
No active incident alert.
Nearby alternative route available.

Expected:
Solo Traveler at Night profile.
Physical and Digital/Transaction weights elevated.
Route-specific caution.
Alternative route surfaced.
No claim that the area is categorically unsafe.
Scenario 6 — Official weather emergency
Evidence:
Official flash-flood warning.
Affected geography matches user route.
Warning currently active.

Expected:
Mandatory safety context.
Environmental concern elevated.
Official Emergency or Route Alert.
Normal ranking may be suspended.
Alert automatically expires or updates with official notice.
Scenario 7 — Accessibility misrepresentation
Request:
Confirmed wheelchair access required.

Evidence:
Business claims accessible entrance.
Multiple verified reports show stairs and no alternative entry.

Expected:
Accessibility requirement fails.
Accessibility concern elevated.
Misrepresentation penalty.
Recommendation restricted.
Promotion held pending correction.
Scenario 8 — Marketplace scam pattern
Evidence:
Four independent users report off-platform payment pressure.
Listing identity cannot be verified.
Transaction attempts share a repeated pattern.

Expected:
Digital concern elevated.
Meaningful fraud pattern.
Listing held or blocked.
Affected users may receive targeted fraud alert.
Human review required.
Scenario 9 — Coordinated false-report campaign
Evidence:
Twenty reports submitted rapidly.
Identical wording.
Shared network indicators.
No corroborating firsthand evidence.

Expected:
Reports not treated as twenty independent sources.
Source Independence reduced.
Public score frozen.
Manipulation review opened.
No immediate punitive public label.
Scenario 10 — Popular business with active safety hold
Evidence:
High ratings and large following.
Credible severe report under active review.
Paid promotion scheduled.

Expected:
Popularity has no protective effect.
Payment cannot remove hold.
Promotion suspended.
Search treatment determined separately.
Scenario 11 — Community silence
Evidence:
No safety reports.
Low platform participation.
Limited verified information.

Expected:
Concern Score may be low or unavailable.
Confidence remains Low or Unknown.
No “safe” claim.
Unknown is not converted into positive evidence.
Scenario 12 — High concern, low confidence
Evidence:
One severe but unverified report.
Limited details.
No corroboration yet.

Expected:
High potential concern.
Low confidence.
Private urgent review.
Narrow precautionary measures permitted.
No broad categorical public alert without additional basis.
Scenario 13 — Moderate concern, high confidence
Evidence:
Consistent verified reports of poor nighttime lighting.
No reports of violence.
Condition remains current.

Expected:
Moderate Physical Concern.
High confidence.
Actionable nighttime caution.
No exaggerated danger language.

Evidence:
Employee reports safety issue.

Expected:
Retaliation signal.

Evidence:

Expected:
