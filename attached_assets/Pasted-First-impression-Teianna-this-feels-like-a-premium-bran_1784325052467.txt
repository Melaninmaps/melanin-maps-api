# One thing I still want to change

This is the only major design recommendation I'd make.

Earlier you said:

> "These latest decks look more black than our warm moody browns."

Looking at the PDF, I think your instinct was right.

The deck reads closer to **very dark chocolate/black** than to the rich, welcoming browns that define Mapping With Melanin™. 

I would create a **brand color system** and use it consistently across every future deck:

* **Primary background:** Deep Espresso Brown (#2C170E)
* **Secondary surfaces/cards:** Walnut Brown (#3B1F0E)
* **Primary text:** Warm Ivory (#F6F1EB)
* **Accent:** Rich Gold (#D6AE55)
* **Secondary accent:** Antique Gold (#B98B37)
* **Dividers and outlines:** Soft metallic gold with reduced opacity (about 70%)

That shift would make the deck feel less like a generic luxury presentation and more like **Mapping With Melanin™**—warm, welcoming, grounded, and unmistakably your own.

## My biggest compliment

What I love most is that this deck finally stops talking *about features* and starts talking about *people*.

By the time someone reaches the end, they don't remember a list of app capabilities. They remember a feeling:

> **"I want to be part of that."**

That's much harder to achieve than explaining what the app does—and this version gets remarkably close. I think with one final typography pass and a warmer color treatment, you'll have a presentation that feels truly distinctive.
