07.25 Emerging Ambassador Rules
An emerging Cultural Ambassador may qualify with:
limited audience size;
high cultural grounding;
strong community relationships;
clear reciprocity;
strong authenticity;
promising outcomes;
consistent participation.
The Engine must not require mainstream visibility before recognizing emerging influence.
Emerging status must not be presented as inferior.
07.26 Community Ambassador Rules
A Community Ambassador demonstrates:
direct community connection;
repeat participation;
measurable benefit;
trustworthy representation;
responsible disclosure;
community accessibility;
relationship continuity;
support for others.
A Community Ambassador may be locally influential without broad public reach.
07.27 Established Ambassador Rules
An Established Ambassador ordinarily demonstrates:
multi-period evidence;
reliable outcomes;
strong authenticity;
meaningful reciprocity;
consistent community connection;
responsible commercial conduct;
cross-community or regional reach;
correction and accountability;
no active serious hold.
07.28 Institutional Ambassador Rules
An Institutional Ambassador may represent or work closely with:
HBCUs;
archives;
museums;
cultural institutions;
professional associations;
historic organizations;
educational systems;
long-standing community networks.
Institutional status requires:
verified institutional relationship;
cultural responsibility;
governance compliance;
appropriate authority;
accurate representation;
durable stewardship.
07.29 Legacy Ambassador Rules
Legacy Ambassador status is reserved for sustained, demonstrable, intergenerational cultural stewardship.
It may require:
long-term cultural contribution;
preserved knowledge;
created opportunities for others;
institution or program building;
strong attribution practices;
continuity across leadership or generations;
verified community benefit;
Living Legacy™ progression;
Institution Test compliance;
Generational Test compliance.
Legacy status must not be awarded because of fame alone.
