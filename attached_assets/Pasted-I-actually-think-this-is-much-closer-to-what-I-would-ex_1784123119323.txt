I actually think this is **much closer to what I would expect from a premium platform** than a lot of subscription pages I see. It feels professional and, most importantly, it answers **"what's in it for me?"** instead of just listing features.

That said, I would make a few adjustments before launch.

---

# 👍 Things I Really Like

### 1. "Business Success Promise"

This is excellent.

> Every upgrade answers one question: Why would I pay more?

That's exactly how business owners think.

I'd probably make the second paragraph a little stronger:

> **Every tier is designed to help you earn more, save time, and grow your business—not just unlock more features.**

It's a little shorter and more benefit-focused.

---

### 2. Showing Marketplace Fees

I LOVE that you're transparent.

Most platforms hide fees.

You don't.

That builds trust.

---

### 3. Website Click-through = 0%

Keep this.

It reinforces one of Mapping with Melanin™'s biggest differentiators.

> We don't charge you when someone visits your website.

I would actually highlight this.

Maybe:

**FREE Website Clicks**

or

**0% Website Referral Fee**

That's a selling point.

---

# Things I'd Change

## Product Sales

Right now it reads:

> Product sales • $0–25 → 5%

I don't think merchants naturally think in price brackets.

Instead I'd explain **why**.

For example:

> Small purchases
>
> Mid-sized purchases
>
> Large purchases

Or simply list:

* Physical products
* Digital products
* Event tickets
* Donations

Then explain the fee schedule in your Business Guide.

---

## Booking Fee

Right now:

> In-app bookings 10%

That depends.

If you're processing payments...

10% is reasonable.

If the booking simply redirects to their own booking system...

I would charge **0%**.

You've consistently told me your philosophy is:

> We direct customers to businesses.

I'd stay consistent with that.

---

## Event Tickets

I'd probably make this simpler.

Instead of

5–8%

I'd choose

6%

or

7%

People generally like certainty.

---

Every subscription helps us:

# My Biggest Suggestion

If you look at this page as a whole, it tells users **how much they'll pay**, but it doesn't yet tell them **how much they'll gain**.

For each tier, I'd add a small sentence like:

**Starter**

> Build your online presence.

**Growth**

> Reach more customers.

**Professional**

> Grow your community and automate engagement.

**Scale**

> Expand your business across cities.

**Legacy**

> Maximize visibility, analytics, and enterprise tools.

Those benefit-driven summaries help people immediately see which tier fits their goals.

---

## Overall Score

If I were reviewing this as an investor or as a business owner, I'd honestly give it about a **9/10**.

The design is clean, the messaging is on-brand, and the transparency around fees is a strength. The biggest opportunity now is to emphasize the **value** of membership just as much as the pricing. Once you add the "included benefits," the matching community membership, and a brief "your membership supports our mission" section, I think this page will feel much more compelling and distinctly "Mapping with Melanin™" rather than just another subscription screen.
