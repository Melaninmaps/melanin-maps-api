First, don't be hard on yourself about losing the original list. In some ways, this is an opportunity. Since Mapping With Melanin™ has evolved from a Black travel app into a **multicultural, diaspora-centered platform**, I actually think we can build something better than what we originally had.

And I love the direction of your question.

The answer is **yes**, but I would be intentional about *how* we use culturally expressive language.

Rather than trying to find phrases that every culture literally says (because those differ), we should capture **shared experiences and values** that resonate across many communities in the African diaspora and other cultures without pretending they're identical.

For example:

## Belonging

These communicate "I feel like I belong here."

* Feels Like Home
* Home Away From Home
* My Kind of Place
* Found My People
* Instant Family
* Warm Welcome
* Welcoming Energy
* Stay Awhile
* Safe Space
* Good People Here

Those feelings are nearly universal.

# Expressions that cross many cultures

Instead of slang that belongs to one community, think about expressions that describe experiences almost everyone recognizes:

* They understood the assignment.
* Worth the drive.
* Don't sleep on this place.
* Hidden gem.
* Put your people on.
* Tell everybody.
* Save this one.
* We need more places like this.
* They did it right.
* This is the spot.
* Come hungry.
* Come as you are.
* Bring your people.
* Feels like family.
* Made with love.
* You belong here.

Notice these aren't tied to one ethnicity, but they still feel warm and conversational.

# I also have another idea...

I actually think Mapping With Melanin™ could become known for its **own vocabulary**.

Rather than borrowing phrases from everywhere, the community could recognize a uniquely Mapping With Melanin language.

For example:

### Kinfolk Picks

Businesses the community consistently loves.

### Put Your People On

Recommend this place to others.

### Welcome Home

A business that makes people feel like they belong.

### Culture Keeper

Businesses preserving traditions.

### Community Anchor

Organizations strengthening neighborhoods.

### Worth the Journey

Places people intentionally travel to visit.

### Heart of the Neighborhood

Businesses central to their local community.

### Soul Spot

Places that leave a lasting impression.

### Family Table

Places perfect for gathering.

### Legacy Business

Multi-generational or historically significant businesses.

## My favorite direction

I think the platform should use **three different layers**:

**Layer 1 – Objective**

* Black-Owned
* Women-Owned
* Veteran-Owned
* Verified
* Family-Owned

**Layer 2 – Community**

* Community Trusted
* Hidden Gem
* Put Your People On
* Neighborhood Favorite
* Welcome Home
* Culture Keeper

**Layer 3 – Experience**

* Date Night
* Family Time
* Solo Friendly
* Celebration Spot
* Creative Scene
* Work & Study
* Quiet Escape

That separation keeps the meaning clear:

* **Who they are** (objective facts)
* **What they mean to the community** (community recognition)
* **How it feels to visit** (experience)

Of everything we've designed together over the past several months, I think this has the potential to become one of Mapping With Melanin™'s signature strengths. Instead of feeling like a generic review platform with generic tags, it would develop a recognizable language that reflects belonging, trust, heritage, and community—values that can resonate across cultures while still honoring each community's distinct identity. I think that's much more aligned with the vision you've been building than simply recreating Yelp-style categories with different labels.
