Parents can invite their children to join under their account at no additional cost.
Each child receives:
Their own secure login
Their own profile
Their own saved places
Their own interests
Their own trips and itineraries
But the parent remains the account owner.
Safety Features
Parents could choose what they want to share.
Optional Controls
✅ View planned trips
✅ Share live location during a trip (only when enabled)
✅ Emergency contacts
✅ SOS notifications
✅ Receive safety alerts for saved cities
✅ Approve friend requests (for younger users)
✅ Allow or disable messaging
✅ Content filtering by age
Everything should be optional so families can choose the level of independence that fits their needs.

Family Profiles
Because safety doesn't stop when your children grow up.
With Family Profiles, eligible children can create their own account under yours at no additional cost, allowing your family to discover trusted businesses, receive personalized recommendations, and explore new communities together.
Whether they're traveling for sports, college, work, vacation, or simply moving into their first apartment, you'll know they're equipped with the same community-driven insights and safety tools you trust.
