That aligns perfectly with your vision. People don't just join Mapping With Melanin™—**communities come online together.**

Finally, one additional permission I would add for administrators is **role-based administration**. Not every admin should have every capability. For example:

* **Super Admin** (you): full access, launch controls, role management, imports, exports, backups, and system settings.
* **Operations Admin**: waitlist management, approvals, launches, communications.
* **Community & Safety Admin**: moderation, reports, verification, but no access to backups or user-role changes.
* **Support Admin**: assist users and view accounts, but cannot change launch waves or system configuration.

That gives you room to grow your team without granting everyone unrestricted control over your platform. I think it fits well with the long-term vision you've been building.
