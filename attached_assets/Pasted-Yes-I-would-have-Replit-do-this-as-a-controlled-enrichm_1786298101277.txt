And I would make **one addition to the philosophy** behind this audit: a website is not just something we want because the directory looks complete. It becomes part of the business's **identity evidence and provenance**.

For example, if AMINA has an official site showing its address, cuisine, owners and current operations, that source can help corroborate several pieces of the listing. But it still does not give MWM permission to label it “Community Trusted,” “safe,” etc. Those remain community-derived concepts.

Also, for the historical businesses in your Tour Guides, I would not consider **“no independent website” a failure**. A 70-year-old institution may have a modest Facebook page but be documented by a city tourism authority, historical society, NPS, museum or reputable cultural institution. In that situation, I would rather MWM link to a **credible historical source** than attach the wrong commercial website just so every database row has a URL.

And this audit will give us something else valuable: a clean list of official social accounts. That eventually makes your **Show Me the Vibe / social-video connection** much easier because we'll already know which Instagram/TikTok/YouTube accounts actually belong to each business.
