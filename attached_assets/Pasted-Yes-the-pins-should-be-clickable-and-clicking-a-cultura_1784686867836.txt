Yes — the pins should be clickable, and clicking a cultural site such as **Howard University** should open useful context. Right now, the map proves the records are loading, but the user experience is incomplete because the markers have no visible label, callout, or detail view.

The ideal behavior is:

* Tap the Howard marker.
* A callout appears with:

  * Howard University
  * HBCU
  * Washington, DC
  * a short one-line description
  * **View Details**
* Tap **View Details** to open a dedicated cultural-site page with:

  * history and significance
  * full description
  * photos
  * address
  * directions
  * website
  * save/share
  * related nearby businesses or places

For launch, I would at minimum require a marker callout with the site name and category. A full detail page is better, but the callout is the immediate usability fix.
