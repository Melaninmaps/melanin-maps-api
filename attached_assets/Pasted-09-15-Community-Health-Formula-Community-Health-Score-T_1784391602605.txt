09.15 Community Health Formula
Community Health Score =
(Trust Stability × .18)
+(Opportunity Distribution × .16)
+(Community Participation × .15)
+(Resource Accessibility × .13)
+(Relationship Strength × .12)
+(Cultural Vitality × .10)
+(Resilience & Recovery × .08)
+(Leadership & Stewardship × .05)
+(Future Readiness × .03)
Normalized to 0–100.
09.16 Community Health Confidence
Confidence considers:
evidence quality;
evidence diversity;
historical consistency;
source independence;
geographic coverage;
recency.
Confidence labels:
Score	Label
90–100	Verified
80–89	High
65–79	Moderate
45–64	Emerging
20–44	Low
0–19	Unknown
09.17 Community Health States
Communities may be classified internally as:
Score	Internal State
90–100	Thriving
80–89	Healthy
70–79	Stable
60–69	Developing
45–59	Vulnerable
25–44	Fragile
0–24	Critical
These labels are internal only and are not shown publicly without Governance approval.
09.18 Positive Indicators
Positive indicators include:
increasing trust;
recurring volunteerism;
growing mentorship;
improving business survival;
educational success;
expanding opportunity;
collaborative leadership;
healthy civic participation;
improving cultural preservation;
successful recovery.
09.19 Community Stress Indicators
Stress indicators include:
declining participation;
institutional distrust;
persistent resource shortages;
neighborhood isolation;
increasing business turnover;
declining volunteerism;
leadership burnout;
repeated unresolved conflict;
opportunity concentration;
worsening inequity.
Stress indicators lower confidence before drastically lowering scores.
09.20 Health Momentum
The Index tracks direction.
Communities may be:
Rapidly Improving;
Improving;
Stable;
Uncertain;
Declining;
Rapidly Declining.
Momentum influences intervention recommendations but does not replace the Community Health Score.
09.21 Community Recovery Recognition
Recovery may increase scores when evidence demonstrates:
measurable improvement;
community-led initiatives;
institutional collaboration;
improved trust;
sustained participation;
successful intervention.
Historical hardship is preserved but does not permanently suppress current health.
09.22 Resource Equity
The Index evaluates whether resources reach:
newcomers;
historically underserved groups;
elders;
families;
youth;
persons with disabilities;
entrepreneurs;
students.
Availability without accessibility receives limited credit.
