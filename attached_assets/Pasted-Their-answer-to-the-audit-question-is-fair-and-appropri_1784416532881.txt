## Why this is the right order

There are three possibilities:

1. **Playwright passes consistently.**
   That supports the password/autofill hypothesis, though we should still improve the browser experience and error recovery.

2. **Playwright reproduces your failure.**
   Then we have a genuine web client or transport defect with exact logs and screenshots.

3. **Playwright is intermittent.**
   Then the issue may be response loss, timeout, race condition, or deployment/network instability—something curl would not expose.
