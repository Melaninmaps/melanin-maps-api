## Confirmed blocker: account deletion is not working

The app told you the account was deleted, but you could immediately sign back in using the same email and password. That means the deletion flow either:

* only logged you out,
* only showed a success message,
* soft-deleted a profile but left the authentication account active,
* or failed silently.

Apple requires apps that permit account creation to let users initiate deletion inside the app, and the process must offer deletion of the entire account record and associated personal data—not merely deactivation or logout. ([Apple Developer][1])

**Do not submit until this is corrected and proven with a temporary account.** This is much more likely to cause rejection than the vibes issue.

## Vibes category

The inability to select a vibes category should be recorded, but it does not automatically need to delay submission unless:

* it blocks a major advertised feature;
* it causes a blank or broken screen;
* or Apple is specifically instructed to test it.

Apple’s common review concerns include broken functionality and incomplete experiences, but a secondary filter that does not work is generally less serious than failed authentication or account deletion. ([Apple Developer][2])

For this release candidate, I would either:

1. confirm the vibes control is not needed in the reviewer path and document it as a post-approval fix, or
2. hide/disable it cleanly if tapping it visibly appears broken.

Do not build out the vocabulary system now.
