If a customer doesn't like a product
Generally, dissatisfaction with a product doesn't automatically make your platform liable. The key question is whether you're acting as a marketplace that connects buyers and sellers or as the seller of record.
If you're operating as a marketplace:
The business is usually responsible for the product.
The business sets its return and refund policies (within your platform rules and applicable law).
Your role is to facilitate the transaction and provide a dispute process.
However, you still have responsibilities, such as:
Processing payments correctly.
Providing accurate information about the transaction.
Handling fraud reports.
Removing businesses that repeatedly violate your policies.
Policies you'll want before launch
Every seller should agree to terms covering:
Product quality expectations.
Shipping and fulfillment requirements.
Return and refund policies.
Customer response time expectations.
Prohibited products.
Compliance with applicable laws.
Responsibility for taxes where applicable.
Indemnification (the business agrees to be responsible for claims arising from its products or conduct, to the extent allowed by law).
Customers should also agree to marketplace terms that explain:
Who the seller is.
How refunds are handled.
How disputes are resolved.
What Mapping with Melanin™ is responsible for—and what it is not.
Verification requirements
For sellers, I'd require:
Identity verification
Business registration (if applicable)
Tax information when required
Bank account verification
Contact information
Agreement to your seller policies
My recommendation for Mapping with Melanin™
I would phase this in:
Phase 1
Verified businesses only can sell.
Focus on products, services, and event tickets from trusted sellers.
Phase 2
Allow more businesses to apply to sell after completing verification.
Introduce seller ratings and performance metrics.
Phase 3
Offer additional benefits to top-performing sellers, such as lower marketplace fees, priority placement, and exclusive promotional opportunities.
This staged approach aligns well with your emphasis on trust and community.
