### First: what is the “original social media URL”?

It's much simpler than it sounds. **It's just the link you get when you press “Copy link” on a post.**

You do **not** need to find code. You do **not** need to know what an embed URL is. You do **not** need to download the video.

For example, you're on your phone and find an amazing Reel from a museum that you want displayed on its Mapping with Melanin page.

**Instagram:** Open the actual Reel/post → tap the **Share** icon (paper airplane) → tap **Copy link**. Instagram copies the address for you. Go back to Mapping with Melanin → Edit that museum/place → **Add Social Media Video/Post → Paste Link → Save.**

**TikTok:** Open the video → tap **Share** → **Copy link** → return to Mapping with Melanin → paste it.

**YouTube:** Open the video/Short → **Share → Copy link** → return → paste it.

**Facebook:** Open the individual post/video → **Share → Copy link** (when available) → return → paste it.

That's it. **“Copy link” gives you the URL Replit needs.**

### What Mapping with Melanin should do with that link

This is the part Replit should make easy for you and eventually for businesses/Cultural Ambassadors.

You paste something that looks like:

`instagram.com/reel/...`

Mapping with Melanin recognizes:

**This is Instagram → this is a Reel → store the original URL → display the supported Instagram embed/preview on this business/place profile.**

You shouldn't have to choose “embed,” convert the URL, or do anything technical.

I'd actually change the wording I previously gave Replit. **Don't call the field “Original Social Media Post URL.”** That's developer language.

Call it:

**Add from Social Media**

Then underneath:

> **Already posted it somewhere else? Paste the link here. No need to upload it again.**

THAT sounds like Mapping with Melanin and communicates one of your selling points.

### And your larger concept is important

You're not trying to convince a creator:

> “Stop posting on TikTok and upload all your videos to Mapping with Melanin.”

Quite the opposite.

A creator can continue building their Instagram/TikTok/YouTube presence. Mapping with Melanin becomes another **discovery layer** that helps people find that creator and their original work in a culturally relevant context.

Someone looking at a museum could eventually see an alumnus's TikTok about the museum, a Cultural Ambassador's Instagram Reel, the museum's own YouTube video, etc. The original creator/platform relationship remains intact rather than you asking everyone to recreate their work inside your platform. That's a meaningful part of your proposition.

### There is one limitation we should design around

Not **every** social post can necessarily be embedded. A private account, deleted post, age/region-restricted content, or a platform's embedding rules can prevent it.

So Mapping with Melanin needs graceful behavior:

**Paste link → Can embed? → Show embedded post.**

**Can't embed? → Preserve the link and show something like “View original post on Instagram.”**

It should **never** just show a broken box.

And I would have Replit preserve the **original URL regardless**, because that's your source and connection back to the creator.
