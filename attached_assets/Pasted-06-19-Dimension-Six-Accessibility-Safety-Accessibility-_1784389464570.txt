06.19 Dimension Six — Accessibility Safety
Accessibility Safety evaluates whether the physical, digital, operational, and communication environment can be used without creating preventable danger or exclusion.
It may include:
wheelchair access;
step-free entrance;
accessible restroom;
elevator availability;
safe pathway width;
seating;
service-animal access;
visual accommodations;
hearing accommodations;
sensory accommodations;
communication access;
accessible transportation;
emergency evacuation support;
accurate accessibility disclosures.
Unknown accessibility does not satisfy an explicit accessibility requirement.
Misrepresentation of accessibility is treated more seriously than missing information.
06.20 Dimension Seven — Family & Dependent Safety
Family & Dependent Safety evaluates whether conditions are suitable for children, elders, dependents, caregivers, or parent-managed minors when that context is explicitly active.
It may include:
age restrictions;
child-appropriate environment;
secure entry and exit;
crowding;
caregiver access;
restroom availability;
emergency procedures;
seating;
transportation;
environmental exposure;
event supervision;
verified family suitability;
accessibility for dependents.
The Engine must not assume that every family has the same needs.
06.21 Dimension Eight — Digital & Transaction Safety
Digital & Transaction Safety evaluates risks connected to online interaction, marketplaces, bookings, communication, payment, identity, and meetups.
It may include:
scams;
fraudulent listings;
impersonation;
account takeover;
payment fraud;
phishing;
fake business profiles;
identity misuse;
unsafe meetup requests;
off-platform payment pressure;
manipulated reviews;
fake opportunities;
predatory housing listings;
malicious links;
repeated cancellation patterns;
concealed commercial relationships.
06.21.1 Digital Concern Formula
Digital Concern =
  Fraud Signal Strength
× Identity Confidence Gap
× Transaction Exposure
× Pattern Corroboration
× User Harm Potential
× Immediacy
06.22 Standard Concern Score Formula
The baseline Concern Score is:
Concern Score =
  (Physical Safety × 0.22)
+ (Cultural & Discrimination Safety × 0.18)
+ (Identity-Related Safety × 0.12)
+ (Environmental Safety × 0.12)
+ (Health & Emergency Access Concern × 0.10)
+ (Accessibility Safety Concern × 0.10)
+ (Family & Dependent Safety Concern × 0.08)
+ (Digital & Transaction Safety Concern × 0.08)
Only applicable dimensions are included.
When one or more dimensions are NOT_APPLICABLE, the remaining weights are proportionally normalized unless an approved context profile provides a different rule.
