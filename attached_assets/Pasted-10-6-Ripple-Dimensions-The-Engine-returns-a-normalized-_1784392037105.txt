10.6 Ripple Dimensions
The Engine returns a normalized Ripple Score using eight dimensions.
Dimension	Weight
Direct Community Impact	20%
Downstream Opportunity Creation	18%
Relationship Expansion	15%
Community Knowledge Transfer	12%
Economic Circulation	12%
Long-Term Continuity	10%
Cross-Community Propagation	8%
Intergenerational Influence	5%
10.7 Dimension One — Direct Community Impact
Measures immediate verified outcomes directly resulting from the originating action.
Examples:
verified business visit;
event attendance;
successful referral;
completed mentorship;
scholarship awarded.
Formula:
Direct Impact =
Verified Outcomes
× Outcome Quality
× Attribution Confidence
10.8 Dimension Two — Downstream Opportunity Creation
Measures opportunities that exist because earlier opportunities occurred.
Examples:
hired employee later mentors another;
business expands after referrals;
scholarship recipient later creates scholarship;
entrepreneur hires local residents.
Second-order outcomes receive additional weighting.
10.9 Dimension Three — Relationship Expansion
Measures new relationships created through existing relationships.
Evaluates:
mentorship chains;
business introductions;
alumni networks;
community friendships;
collaborative partnerships;
recurring referrals.
The Engine measures network expansion—not audience growth.
10.10 Dimension Four — Community Knowledge Transfer
Measures how useful information spreads.
Examples:
historical education;
business recommendations;
travel guidance;
local safety information;
educational resources;
community traditions.
Knowledge must remain accurate to qualify.
10.11 Dimension Five — Economic Circulation
Measures how community resources circulate.
Includes:
local purchases;
referrals;
repeat business;
entrepreneur support;
hiring;
marketplace activity;
local investment.
Economic extraction receives negative adjustments.
10.12 Dimension Six — Long-Term Continuity
Measures whether Ripples continue.
Examples:
recurring annual event;
ongoing mentorship;
repeat community initiatives;
sustained volunteer programs.
One-time events receive lower continuity scores.
10.13 Dimension Seven — Cross-Community Propagation
Measures movement across:
neighborhoods;
cities;
regions;
institutions;
diaspora communities;
industries.
Cross-community influence increases propagation value.
10.14 Dimension Eight — Intergenerational Influence
Measures whether positive outcomes continue across generations.
Examples:
family mentorship;
alumni mentoring students;
preserved oral histories;
recurring scholarships;
institutional traditions.
Intergenerational Ripples receive the highest long-term weighting.
10.15 Ripple Levels
Community Ripple™ classifies propagation internally.
Level	Internal Meaning
Level 0	No measurable Ripple
Level 1	Direct Ripple
Level 2	Secondary Ripple
Level 3	Community Ripple
Level 4	Regional Ripple
Level 5	Legacy Ripple
These levels are internal operational classifications.
10.16 Ripple Formula
Ripple Score =
(Direct Community Impact × .20)
+(Downstream Opportunity × .18)
+(Relationship Expansion × .15)
+(Knowledge Transfer × .12)
+(Economic Circulation × .12)
+(Long-Term Continuity × .10)
+(Cross-Community Propagation × .08)
+(Intergenerational Influence × .05)
Normalized to 0–100.
10.17 Ripple Confidence
Confidence evaluates:
attribution certainty;
evidence independence;
propagation verification;
observation duration;
source diversity;
downstream confirmation.
Confidence Labels:
Score	Label
90–100	Verified
80–89	High
65–79	Moderate
45–64	Emerging
20–44	Low
0–19	Unknown
