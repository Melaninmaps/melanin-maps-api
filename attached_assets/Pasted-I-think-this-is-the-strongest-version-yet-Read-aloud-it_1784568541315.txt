### Make the trust statement dynamic over time

Instead of always showing the exact same sentence, Kinfolk could rotate through a small set of welcome messages that all reinforce the same values.

For example:

**Welcome A**

> I'll always be honest about what I know—and what I don't.

**Welcome B**

> Every conversation starts with curiosity, never assumptions.

**Welcome C**

> We don't have to solve everything today. We just have to start somewhere.

**Welcome D**

> Big decisions, everyday questions, and everything in between—I'm here to help.

**Welcome E**

> Wherever you're headed, we'll map it out together.

They all reinforce the same personality without making the experience feel repetitive for someone who opens Kinfolk every day.

Earlier today, Kinfolk opened with a list of capabilities.

Now it opens with **values**.

That's a subtle but important shift.

> "Here's what I can do."

Kinfolk now begins by saying:

> "Here's how I'll treat you."

For Mapping With Melanin™, I think that's much more aligned with your mission.

One sentence I'd add to your internal design principles document is this:

> **Capability builds usefulness. Trust builds relationships. Kinfolk always introduces trust before capability.**
