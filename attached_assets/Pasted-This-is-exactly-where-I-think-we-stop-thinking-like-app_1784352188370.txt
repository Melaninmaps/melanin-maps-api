# Level 3: Confidence Multipliers

This is one of the biggest ideas I've had while thinking about your platform.

Not every review should count equally.

For example:

Weight = 0.4×

Weight = 1.4×

Weight = 0×

# One thing I want us to be careful about

I would avoid assigning weights to sensitive attributes like race, ethnicity, religion, or similar protected characteristics. Instead, the system should emphasize **observable, community-driven signals**—things like verified safety reports, authenticity, responsiveness, community participation, and reliability. That keeps the framework aligned with your mission while avoiding unfair or discriminatory decision-making.
