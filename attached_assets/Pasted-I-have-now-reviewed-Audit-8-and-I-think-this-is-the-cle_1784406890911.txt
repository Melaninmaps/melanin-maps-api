# My review of the P0 findings

## 1. RevenueCat server verification

I agree this is the most critical commercial finding of the audit.

If an authenticated user can tell the server "I purchased Legacy Member" and the server simply believes it, then every paid tier can be bypassed. 

**I would elevate this to one of the highest-priority fixes across the entire platform.**

---

## 2. State written before payment completes

I completely agree.

The principle should be:

> **Payment creates entitlement. Intent to pay does not.**

Writing:

* memberType
* trialEndsAt

before Stripe confirms checkout creates a state where abandoned checkouts become successful upgrades. 

That violates one of our recurring constitutional principles:

> **The client may request. The server determines.**

---

## 3. Family seat purchase

This one concerns me because it affects honest customers.

The audit shows:

* customer pays;
* Stripe succeeds;
* webhook fires;
* no handler exists;
* feature never activates. 

This isn't simply a bug.

It's a trust issue.

Someone who paid for their family will believe the platform failed them.

---

## 4. Dual checkout systems

I think this is less about code duplication and more about institutional consistency.

A member should never receive:

* one trial on web;
* another trial on mobile;
* different redirect behavior;
* different subscription lifecycle.

The institution should have one subscription policy regardless of platform. 

---

## 5. Community Builder and Legacy Member

I agree this belongs in P0 because it's a **promise fulfillment** issue.

If those tiers appear in your tier architecture but cannot actually be purchased through the intended channels, members experience inconsistency.

## FD-8.3 — Community Builder & Legacy Member

My recommendation:

These should **not** be ordinary subscription tiers.

I think they should become **earned or invited institutional roles**.

For example:

### Community Builder™

Invitation or application.

Demonstrated contribution.

Community leadership.

Verified participation.

Perhaps organizations.

---

### Legacy Member™

Invitation.

Founding contributors.

Long-term stewardship.

Exceptional community impact.

Institutional ambassadors.

This actually aligns beautifully with your:

* Community Recognition System™
* Legacy & Learning Engine™
* Stewardship Engine™
* Governance Engine™

Instead of simply:

"$199/month"

Legacy becomes something someone earns.

I think that's much more powerful.

---

## FD-8.4 — Double billing

I would seal this now.

Rule:

A member may only have one active paid entitlement.

If:

Stripe exists

and

RevenueCat purchase occurs

the system should:

detect duplication;

inform the member;

offer migration or cancellation guidance;

never silently charge twice.

Commerce should never rely on members discovering duplicate billing.
