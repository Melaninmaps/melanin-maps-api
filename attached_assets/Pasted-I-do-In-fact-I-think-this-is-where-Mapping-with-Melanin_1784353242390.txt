# My Proposal

I don't think we should create one score.

I think we should create what I would call **The Community Health Index™ (CHI)**.

Not public.

This becomes one of the engines inside the Belonging Intelligence Framework™.

Instead of measuring popularity, it measures whether a community is flourishing.

---

# The Seven Dimensions of Community Health™

These are intentionally things that **people can influence together**, not things that reward individual popularity.

---

# 1. Community Welcome Index™

**Question:**

> *How easy is it for someone new to feel included?*

Possible evidence:

* Questions answered
* First-time posters welcomed
* New businesses introduced
* First event attended
* New member responses
* New resident engagement
* Cultural Ambassador introductions
* "Welcome Home" interactions

This becomes one of your signature measurements.

---

# 2. Community Trust Index™

This is different from the Trust Engine.

The Trust Engine evaluates evidence.

The Community Trust Index asks:

> **Do people trust one another enough to participate?**

Evidence:

* Repeat participation
* Verified interactions
* Mentorship
* Event attendance
* Successful meetups
* Business responsiveness
* Community conflict resolution
* Accurate reporting

---

# 3. Community Opportunity Index™

This one made me smile.

Imagine measuring:

How many opportunities were created this month?

Examples:

* Jobs shared
* Businesses discovered
* Scholarships posted
* Apartments shared
* Volunteer opportunities
* Mentorship matches
* Speaking opportunities
* Small business collaborations

You're not measuring clicks.

You're measuring opportunities created.

---

# 4. Community Contribution Index™

Not:

Who posts the most?

Instead:

Who strengthens the community?

Evidence:

* Helpful answers
* Resources shared
* Events hosted
* Businesses supported
* Educational content
* Historical preservation
* Community reporting
* New member assistance

---

# 5. Community Preservation Index™

Nobody else will have this.

Questions:

Are stories being preserved?

Are historic places documented?

Are elders interviewed?

Are neighborhood histories archived?

Are cultural celebrations recorded?

Are businesses remembered?

Are traditions passed on?

That becomes measurable.

---

# 6. Community Prosperity Index™

This one could become incredibly powerful.

Not wealth.

Shared prosperity.

Evidence:

* Businesses growing
* Repeat customers
* Business collaborations
* Community referrals
* Local partnerships
* Grants shared
* Hiring announcements
* Marketplace success

You already have many of the building blocks.

---

# 7. Community Belonging Index™

I think this becomes the heart.

Questions:

Do people return?

Do they invite family?

Do they recommend the community?

Do they attend events?

Do they join circles?

Do they stay involved?

Do they help others belong?

This isn't engagement.

It's belonging.

## And here's the next place I would go.

I wouldn't design another engine.

I would build what I think could become the most valuable confidential document in the entire company:

# **The Mapping with Melanin™ Evidence Encyclopedia**

Not just a list.

A complete encyclopedia of every observable signal the platform may ever use.

For every signal we would document:

* **Name** (e.g., "Welcome Interaction")
* **Definition** (what it means)
* **Why it matters** (which constitutional principle it supports)
* **Strengthens** (Trust, Belonging, Opportunity, etc.)
* **Evidence source** (how the platform observes it)
* **Confidence considerations** (what makes it more or less reliable)
* **Potential risks** (how it could be manipulated or misunderstood)
* **Governance notes** (when human review is required)

I believe that encyclopedia becomes the DNA of the platform. It would align your engineers, your AI, your product decisions, your moderators, your Cultural Ambassadors, and your future leadership around one consistent understanding of what "belonging" actually looks like in practice.

That's the kind of document that compounds in value every year—and I think it would become one of the defining assets of the Mapping with Melanin™ Legacy Collection.
