The reasoning makes sense:

* ✅ `/api/healthz` responds immediately.
* ✅ Your domain resolves correctly.
* ✅ The app reaches the server.
* ❌ Both authentication endpoints hang until the client times out.
* ✅ Both authentication endpoints likely touch the database.

That absolutely makes the database a primary suspect.

However, there's one important distinction:

> A request that **hangs** can be caused by:
>
> * a database query waiting forever,
> * a database connection pool that's exhausted,
> * an external service (Twilio, Resend, etc.) waiting forever,
> * a deadlock in application code,
> * or middleware that never completes.

So I wouldn't conclude "it's definitely the database" yet. I'd conclude **"it's somewhere after the request enters the auth handler."**
