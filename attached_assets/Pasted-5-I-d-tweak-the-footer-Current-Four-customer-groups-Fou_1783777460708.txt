The slide I think comes next
This is where I actually disagree with the traditional startup template.
Most pitch decks would jump to traction.
I wouldn't.
I think there's one slide that's still missing.
The Competitive Moat
Not competitors.
Your moat.
Something like:
Traditional Platforms	Mapping with Melanin™
Directory	✓ Community Intelligence Ecosystem
Reviews	✓ Reviews + Safety + Belonging
Search	✓ AI + Intentional Discovery™
Travel	✓ Travel + Relocation + Community
Business Listings	✓ Ongoing Engagement & Marketplace
Algorithms	✓ Community Intelligence + KinfolkAI™
Then end with one sentence:
Our advantage isn't one feature—it's the ecosystem created when trusted discovery, community, AI, businesses, and relocation work together on one platform.
Why I think this should come before Traction
Right now, your deck has done an excellent job answering:
What problem exists?
What are you building?
How does it grow?
How big is the opportunity?
How do you make money?
The next question in an investor's mind is:
"Why can't someone else build this?"
Answering that with a strong Competitive Moat slide before you move into traction and go-to-market will make the entire narrative feel complete. Then, when you show traction, investors are already thinking, "This is differentiated—and now they're proving they can execute." I think that's the strongest sequence for the story you're telling.
