I would—and I think I'd make it bigger than just "favorite influencers."

One of the things I've noticed throughout our conversations is that Mapping with Melanin™ isn't about celebrities. It's about **the people making a positive impact in their communities.** If you broaden the concept, it becomes much more aligned with your mission and much harder for competitors to copy.

I'd call the feature **Show Love** and make it a way for members to recognize people who deserve more visibility.

## Show Love ❤️

**"Celebrate the people making a difference in your community."**

Members could nominate:

* Creators & Influencers
* Small Businesses
* Local Artists
* Musicians
* Photographers
* Teachers
* Community Leaders
* Volunteers
* Nonprofit Organizations
* Mentors
* Coaches
* Local Events
* Restaurants they love
* Hidden Gems

The emphasis is on **recognition**, not popularity.

---

## How it Works

A member taps **Show Love** and answers a few prompts:

**Who are you recognizing?**

**What are they known for?**

* Great food
* Community service
* Educational content
* Travel
* Fitness
* Beauty
* Parenting
* Financial education
* Entrepreneurship
* Arts & Culture
* Local advocacy
* Other

**Why are you recommending them?**

Limit this to about **250–500 characters** so it stays thoughtful and easy to read.

Optionally:

> "Share a memorable experience."

---

## Community Recognition

Instead of Likes, I'd lean into your community-focused language.

Members can:

❤️ Show Love

🤝 I Support

⭐ Saved

📍 Visited

These feel more authentic than standard social media engagement.

---

## Recognition Levels

As more community members independently recommend someone, they could earn badges like:

🌱 Community Favorite

⭐ Local Favorite

💛 Trusted by the Community

🏆 Community Spotlight

These should be earned through unique supporters and moderation, not by paying for promotion.

---

## Monthly Community Spotlight

Each month you could feature:

* Creator Spotlight
* Business Spotlight
* Community Hero
* Mentor of the Month
* Hidden Gem
* Rising Creator

The selection could be based on a mix of community nominations, engagement quality, and editorial review rather than raw vote totals.

---

## For Influencers

When someone is nominated, they could receive a notification such as:

> **The community is showing you love!**
>
> 24 members recommended your content this month.

They could then claim a creator profile if they don't already have one.

---

## My Favorite Part

I would avoid turning this into a popularity contest.

Instead of showing:

> 4,327 votes

show things like:

> **Recommended by 47 community members**

or

> **Trusted by travelers in Philadelphia**

or

> **Helped 82 members discover local businesses**

Those metrics reinforce trust and impact rather than chasing followers.

## A Feature I Think Could Become a Signature Experience

Imagine every profile—whether it's a business owner, community member, or influencer—has a **"Why People Show Love"** section.

It would display short, authentic testimonials like:

> *"She introduced me to my favorite local bookstore."*

> *"His travel guides made my trip to New Orleans unforgettable."*

> *"They always highlight small businesses that deserve more attention."*

> *"This restaurant owner personally welcomed my family."*

Those stories create a level of authenticity that numbers alone can't convey.

I also think you should **link this to your Community Impact system**. If someone consistently receives genuine "Show Love" nominations, contributes helpful content, supports local businesses, and engages positively, that could increase their Community Impact score. It rewards the kind of participation that aligns with Mapping with Melanin™'s mission: building stronger, more connected communities through meaningful support.
