I actually think this could become one of the biggest growth engines in Mapping With Melanin™, but I would be very careful about **what** you reveal and **when** you reveal it.

My answer is:

**Yes, notify them.**
**No, don't notify them every single time someone searches.**

Those are two different things.

## I would think of it as an Opportunity Notification System.

The notification isn't saying:

> "Someone searched for you."

The notification is saying:

> **"Your community is looking for you."**

That's a very different feeling.

---

## For businesses that haven't joined

I would send **digest-style notifications**, not real-time alerts.

For example:

### Weekly (or monthly)

> **People are discovering your business.**
>
> Over the past week:
>
> • 18 people searched for your business
> • 9 viewed your community profile
> • 4 saved your business
> • 2 community members recommended you
>
> Claim your free profile to:
>
> • Respond to reviews
> • Add photos
> • Update hours
> • Receive community insights
> • See your complete analytics

Notice what's missing?

No user identities.

No names.

No personal information.

---

## Reviews

This is where I would be more conservative.

I would **not** immediately email a business every time someone leaves a review on an unclaimed profile.

Instead, I would send a summary.

For example:

> Your business received new community feedback this week.

Then:

> Claim your profile to view and respond.

This protects reviewers while still encouraging businesses to join.

---

## Searches

I actually would **not** tell them every search.

Searches fluctuate.

They're noisy.

Instead, I'd aggregate them.

Examples:

* 27 searches this month
* 84 profile views
* Trending upward
* Most searches came from travelers
* Most searches came from locals

That's genuinely useful.

---

## One thing I would absolutely build

The business gets:

That's much more powerful than:
