06.24 Safety Confidence Score™
The Safety Confidence Score measures how reliable the current assessment is.
06.24.1 Formula
Safety Confidence Score =
  Evidence Quality
× Evidence Volume
× Source Independence
× Corroboration Strength
× Context Specificity
× Recency
× Geographic Precision
The result is normalized to 0–100.
06.24.2 Confidence labels
Score	Label
90–100	Verified
80–89	High
65–79	Moderate
45–64	Emerging
20–44	Low
0–19	Unknown

06.27 Corroboration Strength
Corroboration measures agreement on material facts, not identical wording.
