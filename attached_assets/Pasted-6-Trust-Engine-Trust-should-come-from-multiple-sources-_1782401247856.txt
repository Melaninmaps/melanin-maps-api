I think Mapping with Melanin™ should stop thinking about itself as a collection of pages.

If I could recommend one final investment...
I would build a Recommendation Engine before building dozens of additional features.
Think of it as the brain behind the platform.

Instead of hardcoding:

"Show businesses."
The engine asks:
What is this user trying to accomplish?
What stage are they in?
What has helped similar users?
What trustworthy resources are relevant?
Then it recommends the next best action.
That single system can power search, AI, notifications, the marketplace, travel planning, relocation, health resources, and business discovery. In my opinion, it's the architectural investment that will make all of the individual features you've envisioned feel like one cohesive product rather than a collection of separate tools.
