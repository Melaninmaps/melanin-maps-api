# One additional audit

I want something brand new.

## Institutional Trust Audit™

Not engineering.

Not UX.

Questions like:

Can a business owner explain why their recommendation dropped?

Can a member understand why an alert disappeared?

Can someone appeal?

Can they see evidence?

Can they understand uncertainty?

Would two different moderators reach similar conclusions from the same evidence?

That's institutional trust.

---

# Constitutional Commentary

I think Audit 6 deserves perhaps the strongest constitutional statement in the entire platform.

I would include:

> **Trust is earned one decision at a time.**
>
> Mapping With Melanin™ must never present uncertainty as certainty, popularity as truth, proximity as guilt, or absence of evidence as evidence of safety.
>
> Every trust signal must be explainable.
>
> Every safety recommendation must preserve context.
>
> Every person affected by the platform deserves fairness, dignity, and an opportunity to understand how decisions are reached.
>
> The platform exists to reduce uncertainty—not to replace human judgment.
>
> Safety information should empower thoughtful decisions rather than encourage fear.
>
> Trust grows when the platform is transparent about what it knows, what it does not know, and why.

# The Human Test™

This may be the most important Human Test yet.

Ask Replit to evaluate these people separately:

* A solo traveler deciding whether to visit a neighborhood.
* A business owner who receives a negative safety report.
* A resident reporting discrimination.
* An employer disputing a transparency rating.
* A parent evaluating a destination for their family.
* A community volunteer trying to confirm an alert.
* An administrator reviewing conflicting evidence.
* A journalist trying to understand how a trust score was produced.

Then ask:

> **Would each person understand what the platform knows, what it doesn't know, how conclusions were reached, and what rights they have to question, appeal, or contribute additional evidence?**

## One final addition

I want Audit 6 to include something none of the previous audits have had:

# **Failure Scenario Review™**

Not "what works."

Instead:

* What happens if official data stops?
* What happens if 10 false reports arrive simultaneously?
* What happens if a trusted reporter becomes malicious?
* What happens if a moderator makes the wrong decision?
* What happens if evidence conflicts?
* What happens if AI disagrees with community reports?
* What happens if a business closes during an active investigation?
* What happens if no evidence exists?

In other words:

**Can the institution fail gracefully?**
