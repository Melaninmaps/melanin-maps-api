# One Founder Decision I think will emerge

## FD-11.10 — Constitutional Authority™

Who may change the Constitution?

My recommendation:

Not developers.

Not moderators.

Not administrators.

Constitutional amendments require:

* Founder approval (while founder-led)
* documented rationale
* Decision Ledger entry
* version number
* effective date
* migration notes
* public change summary where appropriate

Eventually, as the institution matures, this could evolve into a formal governance process—but during the founder phase, constitutional authority should remain intentionally centralized.

# One more Founder Decision

## FD-11.11 — Emergency Authority™

Emergency powers should exist.

But they should expire.

Example:

Emergency moderator action:

* immediate suspension
* immediate alert removal
* immediate misinformation containment

Then automatically require:

* documented justification
* secondary review
* Decision Ledger entry
* appeal eligibility
* expiration of emergency authority

Emergency authority should accelerate response—not bypass accountability.
