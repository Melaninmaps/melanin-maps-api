The Engine may not reinterpret a hard requirement as a soft preference merely to increase result volume.
05.11 Hard Filters and Soft Preferences
05.11.1 Hard filters
A candidate failing a hard filter is excluded or clearly separated.
Examples:
verified ownership required;
wheelchair accessibility required;
open now;
within specified distance;
event on specified date;
resource eligibility requirement;
no minors permitted;
online-only;
price ceiling;
verified provider only;
active opportunity deadline;
safety block.
05.11.2 Soft preferences
Soft preferences influence ranking but do not automatically exclude.
Examples:
locally rooted;
quiet atmosphere;
small business;
community favorite;
new or emerging;
family-friendly preferred;
Black historical significance;
public transit convenient;
low-cost preferred;
locally sourced;
women-led preferred where lawfully and appropriately supported;
Cultural Ambassador recommended.
The user must be told when results include options outside a soft preference because exact matches are limited.
05.12 Discovery Scoring Dimensions
Every eligible candidate receives a normalized Discovery Score from 0 to 100.
The baseline dimensions are:
Dimension	Baseline weight
Intent Relevance	22%
Context Fit	18%
Geographic Fit	12%
Temporal Fit	8%
Evidence Sufficiency	10%
Community Value	10%
Cultural Relevance	8%
Opportunity or Utility Value	7%
Exploration Value	5%
Total	100%
The BIF may adjust these weights by context.
05.13 Dimension One — Intent Relevance
Intent Relevance measures how directly the candidate satisfies the user’s stated objective.
05.13.1 Formula
Intent Relevance Score =
  Entity-Type Match
+ Category Match
+ Attribute Match
+ Actionability
+ Query Specificity Match
- Intent Conflict Penalty
Normalized to 0–100.
05.13.2 Intent match levels
Match level	Score range
Exact	90–100
Strong	80–89
Direct	70–79
Related	55–69
Exploratory	40–54
Weak	20–39
Irrelevant	0–19
05.13.3 Examples
Query:
“Black-owned pediatric dentist in Philadelphia.”

Exact:
Verified melanated diaspora-owned pediatric dentist in Philadelphia.

Strong:
Verified melanated diaspora-owned family dentist serving children.

Related:
Minority-owned general dentist in Philadelphia.

Weak:
General healthcare provider.

Irrelevant:
Restaurant or unrelated business.
05.14 Dimension Two — Context Fit
Context Fit measures suitability for the resolved primary and secondary contexts.
It incorporates:
user purpose;
audience;
travel conditions;
family status when explicitly provided;
relocation intent;
accessibility requirements;
business networking purpose;
cultural heritage purpose;
safety-sensitive conditions;
community-resource need.
05.14.1 Formula
Context Fit Score =
  Primary Context Match
+ Secondary Context Match
+ Modifier Satisfaction
+ Decision-Mode Suitability
- Context Conflict Penalty
05.15 Dimension Three — Geographic Fit
Geographic Fit measures the candidate’s practical relationship to the relevant location.
It may evaluate:
physical distance;
route deviation;
neighborhood match;
city match;
service area;
virtual availability;
transportation accessibility;
jurisdictional eligibility;
destination relevance;
local rootedness where relevant.
05.15.1 Geographic score bands
Condition	Baseline score
Exact requested location	95–100
Within preferred radius	85–94
Nearby and practical	70–84
Same city but outside preferred area	55–69
Regionally relevant	40–54
Remote but available online	Context-dependent
Geographically incompatible	0
Distance must not dominate when the user’s purpose is cultural heritage, relocation research, online resources, or route planning.
