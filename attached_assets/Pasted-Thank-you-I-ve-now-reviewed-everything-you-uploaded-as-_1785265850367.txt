# Before Replit starts implementing Builds 98–106, I would ask them to do one more thing

Have them produce a **Founder Intent Traceability Matrix**.

Not a technical traceability matrix—a philosophical one.

For every build and every major feature, answer these questions:

| Question                                   | Example                                         |
| ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Why does this exist?                       | What human problem is it solving?               |
| How does it increase trust?                | Explicitly describe the trust benefit.          |
| What founder principle does it support?    | Reference the governing principle.              |
| What could go wrong if implemented poorly? | Include community, legal, and ethical risks.    |
| How will we know it succeeded?             | Define measurable success beyond usage metrics. |

This document would become the bridge between your vision and the engineering roadmap, making it much harder for future contributors to unintentionally drift away from what makes Mapping with Melanin™ unique.

## My overall conclusion

I think Replit has done an excellent job translating your vision into an engineering roadmap. The sequencing, dependency analysis, and attention to security, moderation, privacy, and platform policy are much stronger than earlier versions.

Where I think the work is still incomplete is not in the engineering—it is in preserving the soul of the platform. Mapping with Melanin™ has always been about helping people find belonging, build trust, strengthen communities, and create opportunity. Those ideas should not be left to marketing copy or founder presentations; they should be treated as architectural requirements that guide every future decision.

If those founder principles are added as a governing layer above this roadmap, I would consider this not just a good implementation plan, but a durable blueprint that can support the platform as it grows far beyond its initial launch.
