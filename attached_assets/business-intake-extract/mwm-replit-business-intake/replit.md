# Community Business Intake — Permanent Replit Rule

Community members may submit a business through the website or source-attributed social-media links. Intake asks only for useful basics: business name, what it does, category, specialties, community tags, owner/founder information only if they choose to share it, city/state, and public contact/social links.

Every new submission begins as `pending_review`. Pending submissions must never appear in public Directory results, Map pins, local-search responses, Kinfolk recommendations, Community Vibes, public business pages, or mobile app content.

Only a founder/admin can approve, decline, or request more information. Approval calls the server-side canonical-business publisher, which creates/updates the official business record with a server-generated slug and valid location. Browser clients may never publish directly.

Use a single shareable form for social media and attribute it through `source` and `source_campaign`. Do not scrape social DMs or automatically publish social suggestions while the founder review process is being calibrated.
