05.59 Human Review Requirements
Human review is required when:
a candidate is blocked by disputed ownership;
severe safety or trust evidence conflicts with high relevance;
promotion eligibility is contested;
Hidden Gem status could materially affect reputation or visibility;
a rank changes by more than an approved threshold due to suspected manipulation;
duplicate consolidation may erase meaningful history;
a sponsored result repeatedly ranks above stronger organic results;
a low-confidence candidate would receive prominent placement;
a community alleges systemic suppression;
a business alleges discriminatory ranking;
a resource or opportunity may be fraudulent;
the Engine proposes a new ranking adjustment;
a protected or sensitive context may have influenced personalization;
a new candidate source is introduced;
a Governance hold exists.

The Discovery Engine™ must:
preserve user intent;
separate search, recommendation, promotion, and recognition;
prevent payment from creating eligibility;
limit popularity influence;
protect emerging and underexposed entities;
avoid fabricating confidence;
distinguish unknown from negative;
preserve relevant diversity;
prevent exact-match suppression;
protect hard accessibility and safety requirements;
avoid identity-based stereotyping;
preserve community voice;
maintain truthful ownership labels;
permit correction and appeal;
log meaningful ranking decisions;
prevent hidden commercial influence;
preserve historical and cultural records;
support geographic equity;
pass the Institution Test;
pass the Generational Test.
05.61 Institution Test Application
Before approving a discovery rule, Mapping With Melanin™ must ask:
Would we defend this as a fair and responsible way to determine what communities can find 25 years from now?
A rule fails the Institution Test when it:
makes payment equivalent to relevance;
permanently rewards incumbency;
hides qualified emerging entities;
relies on stereotypes;
cannot be audited;
cannot be explained;
erases community history;
produces concentrated visibility without review;
turns popularity into trust;
disadvantages communities because they have less data;
removes user choice;
creates undisclosed favoritism.
05.62 Generational Test Application
The Engine must ask:
Will future generations inherit a system that expands what they can discover, or one that quietly narrows the world to what was already visible?
A rule fails the Generational Test when it:
locks current market dominance into future ranking;
makes cultural discovery dependent on commercial reach;
suppresses future community definitions;
cannot be corrected under new leadership;
rewards extraction;
forgets historical entities;
treats low digital visibility as low value;
becomes unsafe when scaled;
cannot reproduce past decisions;
lacks stewardship.

05.64 Validation Scenarios
Scenario 1 — Exact verified business search
Request:
“Find verified Black-owned bookstores in Philadelphia.”

Expected:
Exact category and ownership filters
Trust verification required
Philadelphia geographic scope
Eligible exact matches rank first
Pending-verification candidates separated
Scenario 2 — Popular but irrelevant entity
Request:
“Find a quiet local café suitable for working.”

Candidate:
Highly popular nightclub serving coffee.

Expected:
Popularity does not overcome poor intent and context fit
Candidate ranks low or is excluded
Scenario 3 — Hidden Gem business
Evidence:
Low online visibility
Strong repeat customers
High community reciprocity
Verified operating history
Strong trust
No safety concerns

Expected:
Hidden Gem candidate
Exploration boost allowed
No automatic award
Scenario 4 — Paid but ineligible business
Evidence:
Business purchases sponsorship
Active unresolved safety hold

Expected:
No promotion
No ranking advantage
Sponsorship cannot override hold
Scenario 5 — Urgent resource need
Request:
“Food bank open today near me.”

Expected:
Availability and distance strongly weighted
Current capacity prioritized
Popularity minimized
Expired resources excluded
Scenario 6 — Family relocation
Request:
“We are considering moving to Atlanta with children.”

Expected:
Relocation and Family contexts
Discovery combines neighborhoods, resources, institutions,
community information, safety, and opportunity
No single popularity ranking
Scenario 7 — New business with limited evidence
Evidence:
Confirmed identity
Few reviews
Current hours
Relevant category
No negative evidence

Expected:
Search eligible
Emerging label
Recommendation limited by confidence
No automatic low-quality assumption
Scenario 8 — Repetitive results
Evidence:
Ten top-scoring restaurants concentrated in one neighborhood
Several similarly qualified alternatives elsewhere

Expected:
Relevant diversity re-ranking
No exact-match displacement
Rotation logged
Scenario 9 — Expired scholarship
Evidence:
High historical interest
Application deadline passed

Expected:
Excluded from active opportunity results
May remain in historical or learning context
Scenario 10 — Accessibility requirement
Request:
“Only show wheelchair-accessible salons.”

Expected:
Confirmed accessibility is a hard filter
Unknown does not satisfy requirement
No inference about user health
Scenario 11 — Cultural landmark search
Request:
“Important Black history sites in New Orleans.”

Expected:
Historical verification
Legacy & Learning integration
Popularity limited
Community stewardship considered
Scenario 12 — Search manipulation
Evidence:
Mass identical searches
Coordinated saves
Rapid visibility increase
Same network pattern

Expected:
Signals discounted
Rank frozen if necessary
Human review
No immediate punitive removal from one signal
05.65 Failure Conditions
If the Discovery Engine™ is unavailable:
exact named-entity retrieval may continue where safe;
broad personalized ranking must stop;
promotion must stop;
Hidden Gem surfacing must stop;
recommendation boosts must stop;
baseline category search may use deterministic fallback ordering;
safety and governance filters remain active;
the failure must be logged;
public language must not imply personalized recommendation.
Fallback output:
{
  "discovery_engine_status": "UNAVAILABLE",
  "search_mode": "BASIC_EXACT_OR_CATEGORY",
  "personalized_ranking_allowed": false,
  "recommendation_candidate": false,
  "promotion_candidate": false,
  "hidden_gem_detection_allowed": false,
  "human_review_required": true
}
