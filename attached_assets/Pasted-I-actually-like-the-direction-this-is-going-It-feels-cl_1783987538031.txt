I actually like the direction this is going. It feels cleaner and more premium than some of the earlier versions. That said, because I know the vision for **Mapping with Melanin™**, I think this slide is underselling what makes you different.

Here's what stands out:

### What's working

* The typography is strong and easy to read.
* The warm color palette feels trustworthy and premium.
* The checklist format makes the audience quickly identify themselves.
* Plenty of white space makes the slide feel polished.

### What I'd change

#### 1. The headline is too generic

"**Built for the moments that matter.**"

This could describe almost any travel app.

Your product is much more than that.

Consider something like:

* **Built for where life takes you next.**
* **Wherever life takes you, find your people first.**
* **From home to anywhere—know before you go.**
* **Travel. Relocate. Connect. Belong.**
* **Find your community wherever life leads.**

These immediately connect to your mission.

---

#### 2. Add people who never travel

One thing we've talked about repeatedly is making sure people understand this isn't just for relocation.

I'd adjust the list to something like:

✓ Moving to a new city

✓ Exploring your hometown

✓ Planning your next trip

✓ Looking for your community

✓ Supporting minority-owned businesses

✓ Finding trusted local recommendations

Notice how "Exploring your hometown" moves near the top. That tells people immediately this app is useful where they already live.

---

#### 3. Make the emotional payoff stronger

Current:

> If any of these sound like you—
> you're who we built this for.

I'd make it feel more personal:

> **Whether you're staying close to home or starting somewhere new, Mapping with Melanin™ helps you discover places, people, and businesses that make every community feel a little more like home.**

That reinforces belonging, which is really your brand.

---

#### 4. The phone should be doing more work

Right now the phone is mostly empty.

This is valuable real estate.

I'd show one of these:

* Map with safety overlay
* Business profile
* Community feed
* AI search
* Kinfolk Circles

Even a blurred version makes the slide feel alive.

---

#### 5. Add one subtle emotional sentence

Instead of ending with:

> you're who we built this for.

Maybe end with:

> **Because everyone deserves to know where they belong before they arrive.**

That line feels very "Mapping with Melanin™."

---

## One thing I would absolutely add

I'd include one bullet that no competitor can honestly claim:

✓ **Knowing before you go**

That's your entire platform distilled into four words.

---

### My suggested final version

**WHO IS IT FOR?**

# Wherever life takes you,

## find your community first.

✓ Moving to a new city

✓ Exploring your hometown

✓ Traveling somewhere unfamiliar

✓ Looking for community

✓ Supporting minority-owned businesses

✓ Finding trusted recommendations

✓ Knowing before you go

---

**Whether you're staying close to home or starting somewhere new, Mapping with Melanin™ helps you discover businesses, communities, and experiences that make every place feel more like home.**

With a stronger headline, a meaningful app screen in the phone mockup, and copy that emphasizes **belonging** instead of just **travel**, it becomes a **9.8/10** slide and aligns much more closely with the story you've been building throughout the deck.
