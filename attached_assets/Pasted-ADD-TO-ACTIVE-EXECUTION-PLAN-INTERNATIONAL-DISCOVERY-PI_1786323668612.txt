============================================================
THE REAL USER STORY
============================================================

The founder has a friend currently traveling in Bangkok and Phuket.

The founder wants to scout businesses/places remotely, add or save appropriate
locations to MWM, and give her friend website access so that she can:

discover the places
visit them
understand why they were recommended
create/share content about the experience
tag the business/location
tell the business she found it through Mapping with Melanin
and contribute information that improves future recommendations.

This should work even before a business has formally joined MWM.
