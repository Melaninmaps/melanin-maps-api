I actually think this crosses the line from being a **feature** into being part of your **Invisible Architecture / Trade Secret Registry**.

Not because of the weights themselves. Anyone can assign weights.

The trade secret is **how Mapping With Melanin™ determines trust, relevance, promotion, and opportunity without simply selling placement.**

That philosophy is one of the things that differentiates your platform.

I would absolutely protect:

* how businesses become discoverable;
* how businesses become promoted;
* how community trust affects visibility;
* how KinfolkAI decides what to recommend;
* how community contributions influence rankings;
* how personalization interacts with promotion;
* how safety and trust interact with discovery.

Those shouldn't live only in code. They should exist as protected internal design documents.

## One final thought

I think you're beginning to assemble something that very few startups do intentionally: **a documented decision architecture**.

Your code tells the application **what** to do.

Your trade-secret documents explain **why** it does it that way.

That's valuable because, years from now, new engineers can change the implementation without losing the principles that make Mapping With Melanin™ unique. It also creates a clear record of the intellectual property embodied in your platform's design philosophy, which is worth preserving as the company grows.
