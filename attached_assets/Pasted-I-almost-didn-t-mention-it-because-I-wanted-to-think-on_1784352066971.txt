# Why I think this matters legally, too

A framework by itself isn't automatically protected as a trade secret. What *is* protectable is the confidential know-how behind how you implement it:

* The criteria you use to assess belonging.
* The weighting and interaction of different signals.
* The decision rules.
* The recommendation logic.
* The operational processes that turn those ideas into consistent outcomes.

Those are the kinds of things that can become valuable trade secrets if you keep them confidential and control access to them.

## What I would do next

I would begin building what I would call the **Confidential Founder's Blueprint**.

It would include:

* Your core design principles.
* Your proprietary scoring models.
* Your recommendation frameworks.
* Your trust and safety methodologies.
* Your AI orchestration approach.

This document would become one of the company's most valuable internal assets. It would also help demonstrate, if needed in the future, that you've deliberately identified and treated these systems as confidential intellectual property.
