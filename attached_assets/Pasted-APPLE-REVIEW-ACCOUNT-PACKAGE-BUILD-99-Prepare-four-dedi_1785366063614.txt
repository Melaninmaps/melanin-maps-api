APPLE REVIEW ACCOUNT PACKAGE — BUILD 99

Prepare four dedicated App Review accounts, unless a role is not actually
available in the submitted iOS app.

1. COMMUNITY MEMBER REVIEW ACCOUNT
Provide an admitted member account with:
- Community Agreement completed
- populated profile and preferences
- Discover/map access
- demonstration businesses and business details
- cultural sites
- community feed
- events
- saved items
- one joined Circle
- resources/library
- Kinfolk only where safely implemented
- settings and in-app account deletion

2. BUSINESS OWNER REVIEW ACCOUNT
Provide an account connected to one clearly controlled demonstration
business with:
- business dashboard
- editable profile
- images, hours and contact details
- demonstration review and owner response
- events/offers and analytics only if included in Build 99
- no communications to real or unonboarded businesses
- no fabricated verification, ratings, reviews or safety claims

3. COMMUNITY AMBASSADOR REVIEW ACCOUNT
Show only Ambassador functions that are genuinely implemented:
- Ambassador role/profile state
- permitted contribution or curation tools
- nomination/recommendation workflow if live
- disclosure controls if live

Do not represent future Ambassador governance or Kinfolk influence as
complete.

4. ADMIN/MODERATOR REVIEW ACCOUNT
Provide this only if administration is available inside the submitted iOS
app. Show:
- moderation queue
- claims and verification review
- report handling
- waitlist/member management
- approve/reject actions

If admin is web-only, state that clearly in App Review Notes and do not
unnecessarily expose privileged production access.

PUBLIC/WAITLIST TEST
Also provide exact steps for Apple to test:
- new-user registration
- Community Agreement
- waitlist submission
- confirmation
- server-side prevention of unauthorized membership access

REVIEW-DATA REQUIREMENTS
- realistic and varied demonstration data
- no repeated placeholder image across most businesses
- no obviously fake universal five-star ratings
- no fabricated safety scores
- no false verified badges
- no real business outreach or notifications
- no action that depends on staff manually approving Apple during review
- no OTP or MFA unless the required code is provided and remains usable

For every account provide:
- username/email
- password
- role
- exact screens to review
- expected results
- any limitations
- confirmation credentials remain active throughout review

Confirm which of these experiences are actually in Build 99 and identify
anything that is future-state only.
