I actually think we've identified the third pillar of the platform.

After Community Members and Business Owners, **Cultural Ambassadors are not creators or influencers—they are community catalysts.**

That distinction matters because it changes how Kinfolk should interact with them.

Most platforms ask:

> "How many followers do you have?"

Mapping With Melanin™ should ask:

> **"How many lives have you helped enrich?"**

That is a very different success metric.

# I think we should recognize professionals differently

You asked an important question:

Should doctors, lawyers, educators, and other professionals have pages?

I think the answer is **yes**, but with a clear distinction.

Rather than treating "doctor" or "lawyer" as a creator category, treat them as **Professional Expertise** that can be displayed alongside their Ambassador identity.

For example:

* **Cultural Ambassador**

  * Focus: Family Wellness
  * Professional Expertise: Registered Nurse

Or:

* **Cultural Ambassador**

  * Focus: College Readiness
  * Professional Expertise: High School Counselor

Or:

* **Cultural Ambassador**

  * Focus: Homeownership
  * Professional Expertise: Real Estate Broker

That lets members understand the perspective someone brings without implying that everything they say is professional advice. Kinfolk should also distinguish clearly between general educational content and advice that requires a licensed professional relationship, using appropriate disclaimers where needed.

# The principle that ties it together

I think Cultural Ambassadors need their own constitution:

> **Kinfolk helps Cultural Ambassadors amplify their authentic impact, connect them with communities that benefit from their lived experience, and measure success by meaningful community outcomes—not by attention alone.**

That's consistent with everything you've built:

* Community Members find belonging.
* Business Owners grow through community.
* Cultural Ambassadors strengthen community.

Each role is different, but each one reinforces the same ecosystem rather than competing for attention. I think that's one of the strongest aspects of the platform you've designed.
