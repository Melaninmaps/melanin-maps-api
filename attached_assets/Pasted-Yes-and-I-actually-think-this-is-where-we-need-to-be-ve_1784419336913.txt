## Why I wouldn't test everything first

Imagine we spend two days documenting that:

* feature X fails,
* feature Y fails,
* feature Z fails...

...and then we immediately change them.

We've just documented problems that no longer exist.

The traceability matrix becomes much more valuable when it's verifying the **current** product, not preserving a snapshot of yesterday's defects.

Here's the workflow I'd propose:

### Workstream A — Implementation

### Workstream B — Verification

As each feature group is completed, Replit immediately adds it to the Traceability Matrix and runs the applicable tests.

Nothing is marked complete until it has evidence.

That creates a living verification system instead of a one-time testing project.
