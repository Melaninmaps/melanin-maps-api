distinguish fame from influence;
distinguish influence from impact;
distinguish impact from stewardship;
limit follower-count influence;
protect cultural complexity;
avoid identity essentialism;
prevent payment from creating authenticity;
preserve community voice;
require commercial transparency;
recognize emerging influence;

preserve attribution;
protect consent;
prevent cultural extraction;
preserve sensitive cultural knowledge;
maintain truthful ownership and affiliation;
allow correction;
preserve historical evidence;
support appeal;
maintain auditability;
comply with the Institution Test;
comply with the Generational Test.
07.53 Institution Test Application
Before approving a cultural influence rule, Mapping With Melanin™ must ask:
Would we defend this as a responsible institutional method for recognizing cultural influence 25 years from now?
A rule fails the Institution Test when it:
treats popularity as cultural authority;
reduces culture to demographics;
cannot distinguish authenticity from performance;
rewards extraction;
ignores attribution;
hides commercial influence;
disadvantages local or emerging voices;
creates permanent insiders;
cannot be appealed;
cannot be audited;
stereotypes communities;
assigns one person authority over an entire culture;
becomes exploitative at scale;
protects sponsors over community truth.
07.54 Generational Test Application
The Engine must ask:
Would future generations inherit a system that helps them preserve and shape culture, or one that permanently defines culture for them?
A rule fails the Generational Test when it:
freezes current cultural definitions;
excludes future forms of expression;
concentrates cultural authority;
erases local contributors;
rewards short-term visibility over legacy;
cannot survive leadership change;
uses community work without stewardship;
prevents future correction;
turns cultural memory into private commercial inventory;
makes technology the final authority on authenticity.

Evidence:
High follower count
High views
Low verified action
Limited community participation
No reciprocity evidence

Expected:
Reach may be strong

Follower count does not dominate
Partnership eligibility may remain limited

Evidence:
Small audience
Deep local knowledge
Strong business referrals

Verified outcomes

No penalty for limited reach
