# One final thought about KinfolkAI

I want to preserve something you said today because I think it belongs alongside your Foundational Principles:

> **"Not does this answer the question—but does this lower the barrier?"**

That is a remarkably practical design test.

Every future Kinfolk feature can be evaluated against it:

* Does this reduce friction?
* Does this reduce uncertainty?
* Does this reduce isolation?
* Does this reduce the distance between the person and their opportunity?

If the answer is yes, it's probably aligned with the mission.

If not, it may still be a useful feature—but it shouldn't take priority over the things that do.

I think that's going to become one of the guiding principles that keeps KinfolkAI focused as it grows.
