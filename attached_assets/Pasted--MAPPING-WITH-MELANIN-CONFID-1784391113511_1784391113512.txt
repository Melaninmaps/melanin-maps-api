08. COMMUNITY CATALYST MODEL™
Future-State Production Specification v1.0
Classification: Community Activation, Opportunity Creation, and Positive Action Intelligence Engine
Status: Review Mode — designed as the launch-state model
Confidentiality: Highest restriction; activation scoring, catalyst weighting, opportunity acceleration, intervention thresholds, momentum calculations, community activation logic, and progression methodology are confidential trade secrets and are not publicly disclosed.
08.1 Purpose
The Community Catalyst Model™ measures whether a person, business, organization, event, institution, resource, initiative, or network actively creates measurable positive movement within a community.
Unlike the Community Engine™, which measures the health of a community, the Community Catalyst Model™ measures what causes healthy communities to grow stronger.
It determines:
what actions generate meaningful participation;
which actions increase opportunity;
what creates lasting momentum;
which interventions strengthen belonging;
which people consistently help others participate;
which organizations accelerate community growth;
which businesses generate measurable local benefit;
which resources produce meaningful outcomes;
which efforts deserve amplification;
where small interventions may create disproportionately large community benefit.
The governing question is:
What action, person, organization, resource, or opportunity is most likely to create meaningful, measurable, and sustainable positive change within this community?
08.2 Foundational Principle
Communities rarely change because of one extraordinary event.
Communities change because ordinary people repeatedly create opportunities for one another.
Catalysts create movement.
Movement creates participation.
Participation creates trust.
Trust creates belonging.
Belonging creates stewardship.
Stewardship creates legacy.
The Community Catalyst Model™ therefore evaluates movement—not popularity.
08.3 Definition of a Community Catalyst
A Community Catalyst is any action, person, organization, business, institution, event, or resource that measurably increases one or more of the following:
participation;
opportunity;
connection;
collaboration;
visibility;
trust;
support;
economic circulation;
learning;
safety;
cultural preservation;
belonging.
Catalysts may be intentional or unintentional.
Small actions may produce significant downstream effects.
08.4 Evaluation Objects
The Model may evaluate:
Object	Examples
Individual	Member, mentor, Cultural Ambassador
Business	Local business creating community activity
Organization	Nonprofit, neighborhood association
Institution	HBCU, museum, school
Event	Festival, networking event, workshop
Resource	Scholarship, grant, food pantry
Opportunity	Job, internship, fellowship
Community Initiative	Neighborhood cleanup, fundraiser
Digital Action	Helpful post, discussion, recommendation
Collaboration	Multi-party partnership
Network	Alumni network, Kinfolk Circle
Each object receives an evaluation profile appropriate to its role.
08.5 Core Evaluation Dimensions
The Community Catalyst Model™ returns a normalized score from 0–100.
Dimension	Weight
Opportunity Creation	18%

Participation Growth	15%
Relationship Multiplication	12%
Resource Circulation	11%
Community Momentum	10%
Collaboration Quality	8%
Sustainability	6%
Replicability	4%
Total	100%
08.6 Dimension One — Opportunity Creation
Opportunity Creation measures how often an entity creates meaningful opportunities for others.
Examples include:
employment;
internships;
mentorship;
scholarships;
speaking opportunities;
vendor opportunities;
business referrals;
volunteer positions;
collaborations;
grant access;
educational opportunities;
introductions.
Formula:
Opportunity Creation =
Qualified Opportunities
× Accessibility
× Outcome Quality
× Community Relevance
