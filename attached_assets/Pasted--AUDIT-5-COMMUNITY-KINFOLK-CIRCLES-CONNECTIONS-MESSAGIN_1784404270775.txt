AUDIT EXPECTATION — READ BEFORE BEGINNING

This audit is not asking whether feeds, posts, groups, circles,
connections, messages, hashtags, requests, or social screens merely
exist.

A community capability should be marked COMPLETE only when real members
can safely complete the entire workflow from beginning to end, with:

- working frontend behavior;
- working backend behavior;
- correct database persistence;
- appropriate moderation and abuse protections;
- clear privacy and visibility rules;
- usable blocking, reporting, muting, and recovery controls;
- safe messaging and meetup behavior;
- accessible states;
- no fake, hardcoded, disconnected, or demo-only interactions;
- no feature that creates a relationship promise the platform cannot
  support;
- evidence supporting every conclusion.

The purpose of Mapping With Melanin™ is not simply to increase social
activity. It is to strengthen meaningful connection, reciprocity,
belonging, opportunity, trust, and community continuity.

Q. CONSTITUTIONAL COMMENTARY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Include this founder commentary exactly:

“Community is not measured by how much people post.

It is measured by whether people become meaningfully connected,
supported, visible, useful, and able to belong.

Mapping With Melanin™ must never confuse activity with relationship,
attention with care, or popularity with contribution.

A community feature should help people find one another, build trust,
share knowledge, circulate opportunity, and return with purpose.

Members must be able to participate without surrendering their privacy,
safety, dignity, or control.

Private spaces must remain private.

Requests for help must not become opportunities for exploitation.

Recognition must reflect meaningful contribution rather than volume,
status, influence, or coordinated engagement.

The platform succeeds when relationships continue beyond the screen,
communities become stronger, and people leave better supported than
when they arrived.”
