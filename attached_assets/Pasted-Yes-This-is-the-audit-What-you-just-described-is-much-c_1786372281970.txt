A few project-manager calls on top of that:

**Map, Library and the business/community page are the field-readiness core.** Kinfolk connects them, but those three surfaces need to work independently too. If Kinfolk vanished for an hour, a tester should still be able to find a business, understand it, save it, contribute to it and explore knowledge.

**Vibes/THE REAL and media are nonnegotiable before you start updating businesses today.** If you visit a location and know it is date-night friendly, Black-family friendly, great for solo travelers, judgment-free, excellent with natural hair, listens to patients, etc., you need to be able to capture that intelligence now rather than waiting for another redesign.

**For safety tests, Replit should not generate fake live police/ICE alerts visible to your actual tester community.** They should validate submission using a clearly isolated test/moderation state so we prove the workflow without creating false safety information.

And **15–30 testers is enough right now**. I would rather have 20 people who represent different real journeys than 100 people hitting the same broken search box. Once this audit passes, then expanding the pool becomes useful.

This is also the point where I would tell Replit: **stop optimizing for feature count. Optimize for completed journeys.** Your tour is four days away. A smaller set of completely connected journeys is far more valuable than another 40 partially wired screens.
