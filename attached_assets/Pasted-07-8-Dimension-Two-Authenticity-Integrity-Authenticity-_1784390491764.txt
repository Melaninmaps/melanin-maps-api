07.8 Dimension Two — Authenticity & Integrity
Authenticity & Integrity measures consistency between what an entity publicly represents and how it actually behaves.
It evaluates:
truthful self-presentation;
disclosure of financial relationships;
consistency across time;
accuracy of endorsements;
willingness to acknowledge limitations;
treatment of community members;
response to correction;
avoidance of fabricated engagement;
avoidance of false cultural authority;
consistency between public values and private conduct.
07.8.1 Authenticity Formula
Authenticity & Integrity Score =
  Representation Accuracy
+ Behavioral Consistency
+ Disclosure Quality
+ Correction Responsibility
+ Relationship Integrity
- Deception Penalties
07.8.2 Deception indicators
undisclosed sponsorship;
paid endorsement presented as organic;
fabricated community relationship;
purchased followers or engagement;
false claims of ownership;
misleading partnership claims;
altered testimonials;
staged community support;
impersonation;
using another person’s work without credit;
claiming outcomes that did not occur;
deleting disclosure after campaign completion.
07.9 Dimension Three — Community Connection
Community Connection measures whether influence is built through real relationships rather than distant observation or audience extraction.
It may evaluate:
repeat community engagement;
participation in local events;
community referrals;
collaboration;
responsiveness;
mentorship;
relationship continuity;
accessibility to community members;
engagement outside paid campaigns;
local trust;
connection across generations;
connection across diaspora communities.
07.9.1 Community Connection Formula
Community Connection Score =
  Relationship Depth
+ Repeat Participation
+ Community Accessibility
+ Collaborative Activity
+ Cross-Network Bridge Value
- Distance and Extraction Penalties
A large audience with little reciprocal community interaction may receive a low Community Connection Score.
07.10 Dimension Four — Resonance & Meaning
Resonance & Meaning measures whether the influence creates understanding, identification, emotional relevance, reflection, cultural recognition, or a stronger sense of belonging.
It may consider:
qualitative responses;
meaningful saves;
thoughtful comments;
personal testimony;
community discussion;
educational use;
emotional identification;
memory preservation;
repeat sharing with context;
audience reflection;
strengthened cultural pride;
increased curiosity or understanding.
07.10.1 Resonance Formula
Resonance & Meaning Score =
  Depth of Response
+ Relevance to Community Experience
+ Educational or Emotional Value
+ Repeat Meaningful Engagement
+ Narrative Persistence
- Superficial Engagement Penalty
Reaction volume alone does not establish resonance.
07.11 Dimension Five — Action & Conversion
Action & Conversion measures whether influence leads to meaningful behavior or outcomes.
Actions may include:
visiting a business;
attending an event;
applying for an opportunity;
donating;
volunteering;
saving a resource;
joining a Kinfolk Circle;
contacting an institution;
learning more;
sharing verified information;
booking a service;
referring another person;
participating in community initiatives;
supporting a creator;
preserving a story;
changing a decision based on useful information.
07.11.1 Action Formula
Action & Conversion Score =
  Qualified Actions
× Action Quality
× Attribution Confidence
× Outcome Relevance
× Completion Factor
Normalized to 0–100.
Clicks and views may support measurement but do not independently qualify as meaningful action.
