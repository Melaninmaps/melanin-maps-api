# I also have a thought...

As we've been building these decks over the past few days, I've noticed something about Mapping with Melanin™ that I don't think we've explicitly named before.

You're not really building a travel app.

You're not building a directory.

You're not even building a business platform.

You're building **social infrastructure**.

The product simply enables that.

The Community Builder Deck should lean into that identity. Every slide should leave someone thinking:

> "I want to be part of building this."

rather than

> "I should help promote this app."

That subtle difference transforms someone from a promoter into a true ambassador—and I think that's exactly the kind of community you're trying to create.
