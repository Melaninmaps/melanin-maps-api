1. Add "What We Never Do"
This is something we've talked about for months, and I think it deserves to be part of the design standards—not just this deck.
I'd add an eleventh section.
11. What We Never Do
Mapping With Melanin™ never communicates through:
Fear
Shame
Division
Corporate jargon
Artificial urgency
Overpromising
Manipulative marketing
Instead, we communicate through:
Hope
Trust
Authenticity
Curiosity
Belonging
Confidence
Community
This protects the brand voice long after this deck is finished.
2. Add "Audience First"
Every deck should adapt slightly depending on who is in the room.
For example:
Investor
↓
Focus on market, vision, scalability.
Business
↓
Focus on customers, storytelling, discovery.
Community
↓
Focus on belonging, safety, connection.
Volunteer
↓
Focus on mission.
Influencer
↓
Focus on impact.
The heart stays identical.
Only the emphasis changes.
I'd make this part of the Presentation Standards.
3. One sentence I would remove
This one.
For the first time, you have a place where your story is the product.
I know what you're trying to communicate...
...but I don't actually believe that's the best expression.
Businesses aren't the product.
Stories aren't products.
I'd rewrite it as:
For the first time, your story becomes part of how your community discovers you.
Or
For the first time, your story is as discoverable as your business.
That feels more authentic to your philosophy.

8. One final addition
This one is for us.
I'd add this at the very end of the standards.
The "Friend Test"
Before approving any slide, ask:
Would I say this exactly this way if I were sitting across from a friend over coffee?
If the answer is no...
Rewrite it.
I think this one sentence will keep every Zoom, keynote, roadshow, volunteer training, investor pitch, and onboarding session aligned with the authentic voice you've said you want.
