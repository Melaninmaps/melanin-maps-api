06.15 Dimension Two — Cultural & Discrimination Safety
Cultural & Discrimination Safety evaluates whether people may encounter unequal treatment, exclusion, hostility, stereotyping, cultural disrespect, discriminatory enforcement, or degrading treatment.
It may include:
racial discrimination;
ethnic discrimination;
nationality-based hostility;
religious intolerance;
microaggressions;
selective service denial;
discriminatory dress-code enforcement;
cultural mockery;
hostility toward dialect or language;
exclusion from participation;
biased complaint handling;
unequal pricing or access;
tokenization;
retaliation after reporting discrimination.
06.15.1 Cultural Concern Formula
Cultural Concern =
  Reported Harm Severity
× Evidence Credibility
× Pattern Strength
× Context Relevance
× Institutional Response Factor
× Recency
06.15.2 Institutional Response Factor
The way an entity responds may materially change current concern.
Response	Concern multiplier
Immediate accountability and verified correction	0.55
Meaningful response with monitoring	0.70
Partial or unclear response	0.90
No response	1.00
Dismissal or retaliation	1.20
Concealment or repeated denial despite evidence	1.35
The multiplier affects current concern but does not erase historical evidence.
06.16 Dimension Three — Identity-Related Safety
Identity-Related Safety evaluates whether a person’s stated or visible identity may materially affect how they are treated in the evaluated context.
The Engine may only use:
explicitly stated identity-related concern;
user-selected safety preference;
verified pattern relevant to the context;
current request language;
documented entity behavior;
approved profile preferences.
The Engine must not infer sensitive identity from:
name;
photograph;
neighborhood;
contact list;
browsing behavior;
voice;
clothing;
device;
social graph.
Identity-related dimensions may include:
race;
ethnicity;
nationality;
religion;
gender;
sexual orientation;
gender expression;
disability;
visible cultural expression;
language;
immigration-related vulnerability where explicitly relevant and lawfully handled.
A user’s identity must never be converted into an advertising segment.
06.17 Dimension Four — Environmental Safety
Environmental Safety evaluates temporary and durable hazards associated with natural, built, or operational conditions.
It may include:
extreme heat;
extreme cold;
flooding;
storms;
wildfire;
air quality;
smoke;
poor ventilation;
construction;
road closure;
power outage;
unsafe water;
crowding;
structural instability;
hazardous materials;
disaster conditions.
06.17.1 Environmental Concern Formula
Environmental Concern =
  Hazard Severity
× Geographic Relevance
× Time Relevance
× Exposure Probability
× Source Confidence
× Duration Factor
Official emergency sources may activate mandatory overrides.
06.18 Dimension Five — Health & Emergency Access
Health & Emergency Access evaluates whether a person can obtain appropriate help when a health or emergency need arises.
It may include:
hospital proximity;
urgent-care access;
pharmacy access;
emergency response;
AED availability;
first-aid readiness;
emergency exits;
event medical planning;
language access;
disability-aware response;
transportation to care;
crisis-resource availability.
This dimension may operate as:
a concern dimension when access is inadequate; and
a protective-capacity dimension when reliable help is available.
