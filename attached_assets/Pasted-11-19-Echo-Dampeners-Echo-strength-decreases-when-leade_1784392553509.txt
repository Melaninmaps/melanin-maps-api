11.19 Echo Dampeners
Echo strength decreases when:
leadership disappears without succession;
knowledge is undocumented;
community participation collapses;
institutions dissolve;
traditions cease;
resources become inaccessible.
Echo decline is gradual rather than binary.
11.20 Positive Echo Categories
Examples include:
Educational Echo;
Business Echo;
Cultural Echo;
Institutional Echo;
Leadership Echo;
Mentorship Echo;
Community Support Echo;
Legacy Echo;
Preservation Echo;
Opportunity Echo.
11.21 Relationship to Community Ripple™
Community Ripple™ measures propagation.
Community Echo™ measures persistence.
A Ripple may produce little Echo.
An Echo may exist after the Ripple has stopped expanding.
Both Engines inform the Legacy & Learning Engine™, but neither replaces it.
11.22 Historical Preservation Rules
The Engine values preservation of:
oral histories;
historical documents;
cultural traditions;
community archives;
institutional memory;
neighborhood identity.
Preservation without accessibility receives reduced value.
11.23 Community Adoption
The strongest Echoes occur when communities voluntarily adopt an idea.
Community adoption includes:
recurring participation;
independent organization;
decentralized leadership;
local ownership;
adaptation without losing core purpose.
11.24 Anti-Gaming Rules
Manipulation includes:
fabricated historical influence;
false legacy claims;
invented institutional partnerships;
fake succession;
artificial anniversary events.
Manipulation reduces confidence before reducing score.
11.25 Public Explanation Rules
Approved:
"This initiative continues benefiting the community."
"Community members have sustained this effort over time."
"Its influence continues through ongoing participation."
Prohibited:
"This organization permanently changed the community."
"This person created all future success."
"The algorithm guarantees lasting legacy."

11.28 Human Review Requirements
Human review is required when:
Generational Echo designation is proposed;
institutional adoption is disputed;
historical claims conflict;
manipulation is suspected;
Legacy progression is recommended.
11.29 Constitutional Rules
Community Echo™ must:
reward enduring benefit;
distinguish memory from mythology;
preserve attribution;
recognize community ownership;
avoid personality worship;
encourage succession;
remain auditable.
11.30 Institution Test
Every Echo rule must answer:
Would this still represent meaningful, responsible community influence twenty-five years from now?
