## My recommendation on the default region

For this Philadelphia-centered beta, choose **Option B: Philadelphia focus**.

That means:

* users who grant location access move to their actual area;
* users who deny or have not yet granted location see Philadelphia rather than a distant full-country view;
* the map immediately feels populated during this launch phase.

This should eventually become smarter—home city, selected city, last viewed city, or nearest cultural center—but that is a later enhancement. For RC2, Philadelphia is the better experience than an entire-US view.

Howard University is in Washington, D.C., so it would not appear in the initial Philadelphia viewport. A user should still be able to:

* search for Howard University;
* pan or navigate to Washington, D.C.;
* see its pin if it exists in production;
* tap the pin;
* open a useful detail experience.

## What a Howard University pin should show

Tapping it should not merely display the name. It should open a cultural-site profile containing, where verified:

* official name and location;

* official website;

* save/share actions;
* nearby businesses, events, and community activity;
