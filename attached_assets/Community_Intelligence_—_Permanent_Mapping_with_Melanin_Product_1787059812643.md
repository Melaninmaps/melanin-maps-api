# Community Intelligence — Permanent Mapping with Melanin Product Rule

Never use the product term **Community Safety**. The product term is **Community Intelligence**.

Community Intelligence means **community-sourced context for informed choices**. It can describe moderated shared experiences about arrival, access, atmosphere, local connection, practical conditions, business experience, events, and verified local resources.

## Prohibited inference

Mapping with Melanin must never imply or calculate that a location is unsafe because it has minority residents, is predominantly minority, has a particular race/ethnicity, is diverse, or lacks diversity. Demographic composition is not a safety signal and must not be an input to map pins, recommendations, rankings, labels, filters, Kinfolk responses, or neighborhood scores.

Search terms are not identity disclosure. Community Intelligence can respond to requested cultural context but must not turn it into a profile fact about the member.

## Language rules

Use: **Community Intelligence**, **Community-sourced context**, **Community-sourced insight**, **Shared local experience**, **Practical local context**, and **What community members have shared**.

Avoid: Community Safety, safety score, safe/unsafe neighborhood labels, diverse/less-diverse as a risk proxy, and blanket neighborhood judgments.

An approved, timely, source-attributed observation may describe a particular condition, such as accessibility, transit availability, business hours, or a member's arrival experience. It must identify its limits and never become a generalized judgment about residents or a neighborhood.

Emergency help remains accurately labeled as emergency help. It is separate from Community Intelligence and must point to official emergency services or trusted official alerts when appropriate.
