I remember the structure you are describing, even though I cannot retrieve the exact prior list word-for-word.

The important distinction was that **not every descriptor is a “vibe.”** We separated business language into categories so the app would not assign inappropriate tags. A law office, medical provider, accountant, daycare, or funeral home should never be labeled “Date Night,” “Romantic,” “Late Night,” or similar just because those tags exist globally.

The framework was essentially:

## 1. Experience or vibe tags

These describe how a place feels and should mainly apply to restaurants, nightlife, entertainment, travel, wellness, retail and gathering spaces.

Examples:

* Date Night
* Group Hangout
* Solo Vibes
* Bougie Treat
* Hood Classic
* Late Night
* Family Time
* Creative Scene
* Grown Folks Vibe
* Celebration Spot
* Block Party Energy
* Quiet Luxury
* First-Date Friendly
* Music & Movement

These should be filtered by business category.

## 2. Community and cultural identity tags

These describe the business’s relationship to the community, not whether it is romantic or entertaining.

Examples:

* Community Staple
* For the Culture
* Feels Like Family
* Home Away From Home
* Heritage Rich
* Diaspora Vibes
* Locally Loved
* Hidden Gem
* Neighborhood Favorite
* Generational Business
* Community Recommended
* Cultural Landmark
* Rooted in the Community
* Gives Back
* Family-Owned

These can apply more broadly, including to professional services.

## 3. Service-quality and trust descriptors

These are appropriate for lawyers, doctors, accountants, real estate professionals, contractors and other service providers.

Examples:

* Professional and Responsive
* Knowledgeable
* Patient and Thorough
* Clear Communication
* Trusted Advisor
* Community Trusted
* Welcoming Environment
* Respectful Service
* Culturally Aware
* Inclusive Service
* Reliable
* Transparent Pricing
* Family Friendly
* First-Time Client Friendly
* Accessible
* Bilingual Service
* Discreet and Confidential

For a law office, this is the category that should appear instead of “Date Night” or “Romantic.”

## 4. Practical-use tags

These explain when or why someone would use a business.

Examples:

* Good for Families
* Good for Groups
* Solo Friendly
* Budget Friendly
* Appointment Required
* Walk-Ins Welcome
* Quick Service
* Open Late
* Weekend Availability
* Remote Services
* Delivery Available
* Accessible Entrance
* Kid Friendly
* Work and Study
* Event Friendly
* Traveler Friendly

## 5. Food-specific descriptors

These should only appear for restaurants, cafés, bakeries and food businesses.

Examples:

* Soul Food
* Comfort Food
* Authentic Flavors
* Generous Portions
* Sunday Dinner Energy
* Auntie Approved
* Family Recipe
* Vegan Friendly
* Halal Options
* Caribbean Flavors
* African Cuisine
* Latino Flavors
* Southern Classic
* Brunch Favorite
* Sweet Treats

## 6. Safety and belonging indicators

These were distinct from vibe tags because they carry more serious meaning.

Examples:

* Welcoming Environment
* Community Trusted
* Solo Traveler Approved
* Family Safe
* LGBTQ+ Welcoming
* Disability Accessible
* Women Feel Safe Here
* Respectful Staff
* Culturally Respectful
* Verified Ownership
* Community Reviewed

These should be based on verified information or community feedback, not automatically assigned.

## The rule Replit should follow

Each business category should have an **allowed tag set**.

For example:

**Law firm**

* Community Trusted
* Clear Communication
* Knowledgeable
* Culturally Aware
* Welcoming Environment
* First-Time Client Friendly
* Bilingual Service

**Restaurant**

* Date Night
* Group Hangout
* Soul Food
* Family Time
* Late Night
* Hood Classic
* Bougie Treat
* Auntie Approved

**Hair salon or spa**

* Self-Care
* Beauty and Wellness
* Girls’ Day
* Relaxing
* Luxury Experience
* Community Staple

**Accountant or financial professional**

* Trusted Advisor
* Clear Communication
* Small-Business Friendly
* First-Time Client Friendly
* Transparent Pricing
* Community Trusted
