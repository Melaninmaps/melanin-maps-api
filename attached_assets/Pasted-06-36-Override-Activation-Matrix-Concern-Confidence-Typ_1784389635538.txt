06.36 Override Activation Matrix
Concern	Confidence	Typical treatment
90–100	Any	Immediate review; emergency rules if current
75–89	80+	Critical or High-Risk override candidate
75–89	45–79	Temporary hold and urgent review
75–89	Below 45	Precautionary review; no broad public claim
60–74	80+	High-Risk or context-specific restriction
60–74	45–79	Concern qualifier and monitoring
40–59	65+	Informational/contextual caution
Below 40	Any	Ordinarily no override unless mandatory rule applies
This matrix is guidance.
Mandatory official alerts and emergency requests may bypass score thresholds.
06.37 Alert Eligibility
Not every concern should create an alert.
An alert may be issued only when:
the information is time-sensitive;
the affected audience can be reasonably identified;
the information is actionable;
the evidence meets the applicable threshold;
disclosure will not create greater harm;
the language is approved;
privacy can be protected;
the alert has an expiration condition.
06.38 Alert Types
Alert type	Purpose
Emergency Alert	Immediate danger or official emergency
Local Safety Alert	Current geographic concern
Route Alert	Travel-path concern
Event Alert	Active event condition
Business Safety Notice	Current business-specific concern
Resource Alert	Resource closure or unsafe condition
Digital Fraud Alert	Scam or impersonation pattern
Informational Advisory	Lower-immediacy, actionable information
Update or Resolution	Condition improved, expired, or resolved
06.39 Alert Audience Rules
Alerts may be targeted by:
current location with permission;
saved business;
saved neighborhood;
active route;
event RSVP;
current trip;
followed topic;
affected marketplace interaction;
verified role;
selected safety preference.
Alerts must not expose the identity of a reporter unless explicit permission and lawful authorization exist.
06.40 Alert Language Rules
Approved alert language must distinguish:
confirmed fact;
credible report;
emerging information;
official notice;
unresolved concern;
precautionary guidance.
Approved examples:
“An official weather warning is active for this area.”
“Multiple recent reports describe similar concerns. Review details before visiting.”
“A safety report is under review. This notice is precautionary.”
“The event organizer has confirmed an entrance closure.”
“This listing has been temporarily held while identity concerns are reviewed.”
Prohibited examples:
“This place is dangerous.”
“Crime is high here” without defined evidence and context.
“People like you should not go.”
“The community says this business is racist” when the evidence does not support that categorical statement.
“You are safe here.”
“Nothing bad has happened.”
“Our algorithm predicts an attack.”
06.41 Alert Expiration
Every alert must include:
activation time;
expiration time or condition;
evidence source;
review owner;
update status;
resolution pathway.
Alerts must not remain active indefinitely because no one remembered to close them.
06.42 Recommendation Treatment
Safety outputs may produce one of six recommendation states.
State	Meaning
NORMAL	No special safety restriction
INFORMED	Relevant information should be shown
CAUTION	Recommendation permitted with context
RESTRICTED	Recommendation limited to defined contexts
HOLD	Recommendation paused pending review
BLOCKED	Recommendation not permitted
Search visibility is determined separately.
