# Melanin Maps™ MVP Requirements

## Objective

Build the first working version (MVP) of Melanin Maps™, a mobile-first platform that helps users discover businesses, events, opportunities, travel destinations, and communities through an interactive map experience.

---

## User Roles

### Consumer

Can:

* Create account
* Create profile
* Search businesses
* View businesses on map
* Save favorites
* Leave reviews
* RSVP to events
* Receive notifications

### Business

Can:

* Create business account
* Claim or create business listing
* Upload photos
* Add business information
* Respond to reviews
* Create events
* View basic analytics

### Administrator

Can:

* Manage users
* Manage businesses
* Approve verification requests
* Remove inappropriate content
* Review reports
* Manage memberships

---

## Primary Navigation

Bottom Navigation:

1. Home
2. Explore
3. Map
4. Events
5. Profile

---

## Home Screen

Display:

* Search bar
* Featured businesses
* Upcoming events
* Recommended businesses
* Trending locations
* Community announcements

---

## Explore Screen

Allow users to browse:

* Restaurants
* Beauty & Wellness
* Professional Services
* Retail
* Healthcare
* Real Estate
* Travel
* Entertainment

Include filters:

* Distance
* Rating
* Verified
* Open Now
* Category

---

## Map Screen

Interactive map displaying:

* Business locations
* Event locations
* User-selected categories
* Search results

Users can:

* Tap location pins
* Open business profiles
* Save locations
* Get directions

---

## Business Profile Screen

Display:

* Business name
* Photos

* Verification badges
* Directions button
* Save button

---

## User Profile Screen

Display:

* Profile photo
* Name
* Bio

* Reviews written

---

## Reviews

Users can:

* Leave reviews
* Rate 1–5 stars
* Upload photos
* Edit reviews

Businesses can:

* Respond to reviews

Administrators can:

* Remove inappropriate content

---

## Events

Businesses and administrators can create:

* Community events
* Networking events
* Business events

Display:

* Date
* Time
* Location
* Description
* RSVP button

---

## Business Verification

Status options:

* Verified Business
* Black-Owned Verified
* Women-Owned
* Veteran-Owned
* Wheelchair Accessible
* Family Friendly

Businesses submit verification requests.

Administrators approve or reject.

---

## Notifications

Users receive:

* Event reminders
* New reviews
* Business responses
* Safety alerts
* Platform announcements

---

## Admin Dashboard

Provide:

* User management
* Business management
* Verification requests
* Reported content
* Membership management
* Analytics overview

---

## Branding

Match branding from melaninmaps.com:

* Warm earth-tone colors
* Modern professional appearance
* Mobile-first layout
* Accessible design
* Consistent typography and logo usage

---

## Phase 1 MVP Only

Build only:

* User accounts
* Business accounts
* Interactive maps
* Search
* Business profiles
* Reviews
* Events
* Notifications
* Admin dashboard

Do not build yet:

* Travel booking
* Housing marketplace
* Messaging
* AI concierge
* Marketplace sales
* International support

These will be Phase 2 and beyond.
