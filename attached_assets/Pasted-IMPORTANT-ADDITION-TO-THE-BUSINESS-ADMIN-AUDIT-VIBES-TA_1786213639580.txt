## PRESERVE THE DIFFERENT TYPES OF LANGUAGE

Do NOT put everything into one generic "Tags" field.

We intentionally distinguish between different concepts.

### 1. CATEGORY / SUBCATEGORY

What the business IS.

Examples:

Restaurant
Attorney
Salon
Museum
Hotel
etc.

Use the actual canonical categories — these examples are illustrative only.

### 2. VIBES / EXPERIENCE

How an appropriate place or experience FEELS.

These are especially relevant for restaurants, nightlife, entertainment, hospitality, travel and similar experiences.

Do not force Vibes onto professional businesses when they do not make sense.

### 3. DISCOVERY / INTENT FILTERS

What someone is LOOKING FOR or WHY they are searching.

For example, someone may be looking for:

romantic/date night
chill/relaxing
turn up/nightlife
family-friendly
adult-oriented
solo-friendly
group-friendly
adventurous
etc.

These examples are illustrative.

Use the actual approved canonical vocabulary rather than creating another hard-coded list.

These discovery filters are functionally different from expressive Vibes even when there is occasional overlap.

### 4. PROFESSIONAL / SERVICE EXPERIENCE

For professionals and service providers, we developed more appropriate language because calling an attorney, accountant, realtor, therapist, contractor, etc. a "Vibe" often does not make sense.

These descriptors should communicate the kinds of things members actually want to know about working with that professional.

Use the Mapping with Melanin professional/service vocabulary already approved or supplied to you.

Do NOT substitute Manus suggestions.

### 5. COMMUNITY-REPORTED EXPERIENCE / QUICK REVIEW PREVIEW

Keep this separate from admin-assigned factual attributes.

If something such as:

"Explained everything clearly"

is intended to represent what actual customers/community members reported through Quick Review, an ADMIN should NOT manually assign it in a way that makes it appear to be community sentiment.

Clearly distinguish:

ADMIN-EDITABLE BUSINESS/DISCOVERY ATTRIBUTES

from

COMMUNITY-GENERATED REVIEW SIGNALS.
