## Slide 1

Replit says:

> It feels like arrival rather than promise.

But I don't think the solution is changing the copy.

I think it's changing the **lighting**.

Imagine...

The center of the slide is ever so slightly brighter than the edges.

Almost like dawn.

Not enough to notice consciously.

Enough to feel:

"I'm walking toward something."

Warmth embraces you.

## Slide 17

I completely agree about the lower third.

But...

I don't think we should fill it.

I think we should **earn** it.

There are two kinds of white space.

Dead space.

Sacred space.

Sacred space allows people to think.

Dead space looks unfinished.

The question isn't:

"Should something go there?"

The question is:

"Does the conclusion deserve room to breathe?"

That distinction matters enormously.

I'd mock up both.
