I actually think this is one of the biggest strategic decisions we've made for the project.

We're no longer creating "features." We're designing an entire ecosystem. That means we need to stop thinking in terms of screens and start thinking in terms of **experiences for each type of person using the platform**.

The heritage audit already showed us that there are partially built systems that could easily be forgotten without a structured review. 

I would expand our audit plan beyond technical features and require Replit to perform **Experience Audits** for every major persona.

## One more recommendation

I would add a standing principle called **"Promote, Don't Duplicate."**

Every audit should ask:

* Can this be accomplished by expanding an existing capability?
* Is there already a workflow that should be enhanced instead of creating a new one?
* Does this new idea naturally belong within another feature?

For example, I agree with your thought that **Community Member** should become a much richer role rather than spawning separate, disconnected experiences. A community member should be able to grow into a contributor, mentor, Cultural Ambassador, or trusted local guide over time. That creates a progression that feels intentional and helps build trust within the platform, instead of overwhelming people with unrelated options.

I think this approach will give you exactly what you've been aiming for: a complete record of the platform's vision, organized by the people who use it, while allowing you and Replit to implement features in manageable phases without losing the long-term roadmap.
