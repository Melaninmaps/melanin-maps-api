05.21 Dimension Nine — Exploration Value
Exploration Value allows the Engine to surface strong options beyond the most obvious or popular results.
It evaluates:
novelty to the user;
underexposure;
emerging quality;
local uniqueness;
category diversity;
geographic variety;
cultural significance;
Hidden Gem potential;
community relevance;
reduced prior visibility.
Exploration Value must never compensate for disqualifying safety or trust concerns.
05.22 Standard Discovery Score Formula
Discovery Score =
  (Intent Relevance × 0.22)
+ (Context Fit × 0.18)
+ (Geographic Fit × 0.12)
+ (Temporal Fit × 0.08)
+ (Evidence Sufficiency × 0.10)
+ (Community Value × 0.10)
+ (Cultural Relevance × 0.08)
+ (Opportunity or Utility Value × 0.07)
+ (Exploration Value × 0.05)
Example:
Intent Relevance:        94 × 0.22 = 20.68
Context Fit:             88 × 0.18 = 15.84
Geographic Fit:          82 × 0.12 =  9.84
Temporal Fit:            90 × 0.08 =  7.20
Evidence Sufficiency:    74 × 0.10 =  7.40
Community Value:         81 × 0.10 =  8.10
Cultural Relevance:      92 × 0.08 =  7.36
Utility Value:           84 × 0.07 =  5.88
Exploration Value:       65 × 0.05 =  3.25

Raw Discovery Score = 85.55
Rounded Discovery Score = 86
The Discovery Score is not the final Belonging Suitability Score.
It becomes one input to the BIF.
05.23 Discovery Confidence
The Engine returns a Discovery Score and a Discovery Confidence level.
05.23.1 Formula
Discovery Confidence =
  Query Confidence
× Candidate Identity Confidence
× Attribute Coverage
× Evidence Quality
× Availability Confidence
× Context Confidence
Normalized to 0–100.
05.23.2 Attribute Coverage Factor
Required attributes confirmed	Factor
100%	1.00
90–99%	0.95
75–89%	0.85
60–74%	0.70
40–59%	0.50
20–39%	0.30
Under 20%	0.10
Unknown optional attributes do not receive the same penalty as unknown required attributes.
05.24 Search Eligibility Thresholds
Discovery Score	Search treatment
85–100	High relevance
75–84	Strong relevance
65–74	Relevant
55–64	Supporting result
40–54	Exploratory result
25–39	Low relevance; ordinarily hidden behind expansion
1–24	Exclude from ordinary results
0	Ineligible
Search eligibility may be allowed at lower scores when:
exact named entity match exists;
the user explicitly requests broad exploration;
the market has very limited coverage;
the candidate is historically relevant;
the result is clearly labeled;
no hard filter is violated.
