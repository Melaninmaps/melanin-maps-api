# New Institutional Concept™

## **Stewardship Continuity™**

An institution should not depend upon one person remembering everything.

Every meaningful decision should be:

* documented;
* understandable;
* reproducible;
* reviewable;
* teachable.

This is how organizations outlive founders.

# Constitutional Commentary

I recommend including:

> **Institutions are remembered not only for the decisions they make, but for how they make them. Governance exists to protect fairness when emotions are high, consistency when circumstances change, and trust when outcomes are disputed. Every administrative action should leave behind enough evidence that a future steward can understand what happened, why it happened, and whether it should happen again. The goal of governance is not control—it is stewardship. Power should always be accountable, authority should always be reviewable, and every member should know that the institution is capable of correcting its own mistakes.**
