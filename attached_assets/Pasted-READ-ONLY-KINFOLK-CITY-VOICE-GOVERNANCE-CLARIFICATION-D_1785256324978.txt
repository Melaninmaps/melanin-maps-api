READ-ONLY KINFOLK CITY VOICE GOVERNANCE CLARIFICATION

Do not modify prompts, code, schemas, or user settings yet.

I want to confirm how the current Kinfolk city-voice system is designed
and preserve the following founder requirements for future state.

1. PRESCRIPTED VOICE IS A FOUNDATION, NOT THE FINAL MODEL

The existing city-specific vocabulary, phrases, directions language, and
cultural touchstones may remain as an approved baseline.

However, KinfolkAI must not treat any city's speech patterns as fixed,
complete, or permanently defined by a hardcoded list.

AAVE, regional Black language, diaspora language, slang, and cultural
expression evolve continuously.

Future Kinfolk city voices should be capable of learning from community
usage over time without simply copying individual users or reinforcing
stereotypes.

2. COMMUNITY-GROUNDED EVOLUTION

Document how a future learning model could responsibly use:

- user-submitted phrase suggestions;
- Cultural Ambassador contributions;
- resident and alumni videos;
- city archive stories;
- community posts;
- approved local terminology;
- corrections from members;
- emerging phrases and meanings;
- differences by age, neighborhood, profession, and context.

The system must distinguish:

- broadly recognized local language;
- temporary trends;
- individual speaking style;
- reclaimed language;
- harmful or derogatory language;
- professional versus casual context.

No phrase should become a city-wide default solely because a small number
of users used it.

3. CONSENT AND PRIVACY

Kinfolk must not silently learn a person's private speech patterns and
reuse them for other users.

Future learning must rely only on:

- content users knowingly submit for community learning;
- public or approved community contributions;
- anonymized, aggregated signals where appropriate;
- explicit correction or phrase-submission tools;
- Cultural Ambassador or moderator review.

Private chats must not automatically train the shared city voice.

Document what data is currently used and what future consent model would
be required.

4. PROFANITY MUST BE EXPLICITLY OPT-IN

Profanity must never be enabled by default.

Users must make a clear, affirmative choice before Kinfolk uses profanity
or explicit language.

Required future preference structure:

- No profanity — default
- Mild / occasional language
- Authentic unfiltered language — explicit opt-in only

The setting must be:

- visible;
- changeable at any time;
- separate from AAVE level;
- separate from city voice;
- separate from casual/formal tone;
- disabled automatically for minors and family/child modes;
- respected across text, audio, directions, and notifications.

Do not infer profanity preference from how a user speaks.

5. CONTEXTUAL SAFEGUARDS

Even with opt-in, Kinfolk must not use profanity in:

- emergencies or crisis responses;
- legal, medical, financial, or safety guidance;
- child or family mode;
- account, billing, moderation, or policy messages;
- professional introductions unless specifically requested and
  appropriate;
- content directed at another member without their consent.

Profanity must never be used as a substitute for cultural authenticity.

6. AAVE AND PROFANITY ARE NOT THE SAME THING

Make this a permanent language-governance rule:

AAVE is a complete, rule-governed language variety and must not be
equated with slang, profanity, broken English, or informality.

A member should be able to select:

- culturally aware language;
- regional or city voice;
- AAVE-informed phrasing;
- formal or professional tone;

without receiving profanity.

7. MEMBER CONTROL AND TRANSPARENCY

Future settings should let members see and control:

- City voice
- AAVE level
- Formal/casual tone
- Profanity level
- Voice/audio style
- Whether Kinfolk may use community-learned local expressions
- Whether the member wants to contribute language suggestions

Kinfolk should be able to explain:

- why a local phrase was used;
- whether it came from an approved city profile or community contribution;
- how the user can change the preference.

8. QUALITY AND MODERATION

Document a future review process for new city-language contributions:

- source or contributor type;
- city and neighborhood relevance;
- meaning and context;
- age appropriateness;
- professional versus casual use;
- whether the term is reclaimed, contested, or harmful;
- minimum community signal before adoption;
- Cultural Ambassador or moderator approval;
- expiration/review date for temporary slang;
- rollback process if a phrase causes harm.

9. REQUIRED READ-ONLY RESPONSE

Return:

1. Current city-voice implementation
2. Current AAVE implementation
3. Current profanity controls
4. Whether profanity is currently opt-in
5. Whether private chats currently influence shared voice behavior
6. Whether voiceMode is saved persistently
7. Existing minor/family safeguards
8. Existing moderation or governance
9. Gaps against the founder requirements above
10. Current-state versus future-state matrix
11. Exact source files and preference fields involved
12. Founder decisions required before implementation
