07.15 Dimension Nine — Reach & Accessibility
Reach & Accessibility measures whether culturally useful influence can reach relevant people in an understandable and accessible way.
It may include:
audience reach;
geographic reach;
platform reach;
language accessibility;
captioning;
transcript availability;
readability;
disability access;
cross-platform availability;
local reach;
diaspora reach;
audience relevance.
07.15.1 Reach Formula
Reach & Accessibility Score =
  Relevant Audience Reach
+ Access Quality
+ Geographic or Community Relevance
+ Communication Accessibility
+ Distribution Reliability
- Artificial Reach Penalty
07.15.2 Follower-count limitation
Follower count may contribute no more than:
10% of the Reach & Accessibility dimension
Because Reach & Accessibility is weighted at 5% of the baseline Cultural Influence Score, follower count ordinarily contributes no more than:
0.5% of the total Cultural Influence Score
An approved context-specific profile may increase the Reach dimension, but direct follower count may never contribute more than 10% of the total Cultural Influence Score under any profile.
07.16 Standard Cultural Influence Score Formula
Cultural Influence Score =
  (Cultural Grounding × 0.15)
+ (Authenticity & Integrity × 0.15)
+ (Community Connection × 0.14)
+ (Resonance & Meaning × 0.10)
+ (Action & Conversion × 0.12)
+ (Reciprocity & Shared Benefit × 0.13)
+ (Stewardship & Attribution × 0.09)
+ (Continuity & Reliability × 0.07)
+ (Reach & Accessibility × 0.05)
Example:
Cultural Grounding:              91 × 0.15 = 13.65
Authenticity & Integrity:        84 × 0.15 = 12.60
Community Connection:            88 × 0.14 = 12.32
Resonance & Meaning:             82 × 0.10 =  8.20
Action & Conversion:             76 × 0.12 =  9.12
Reciprocity & Shared Benefit:    89 × 0.13 = 11.57
Stewardship & Attribution:       93 × 0.09 =  8.37
Continuity & Reliability:        79 × 0.07 =  5.53
Reach & Accessibility:           58 × 0.05 =  2.90

Raw Cultural Influence Score = 84.26
Rounded Cultural Influence Score = 84
The Cultural Influence Score is not the final partnership, recommendation, promotion, or recognition decision.
It is an input to the Belonging Intelligence Framework™.
07.17 Cultural Influence Confidence
The Engine returns both:
Cultural Influence Score;
Cultural Influence Confidence.
07.17.1 Formula
Cultural Influence Confidence =
  Evidence Quality
× Evidence Volume
× Outcome Attribution
× Source Independence
× Time Coverage
× Community Representation
× Disclosure Confidence
Normalized to 0–100.
07.17.2 Confidence labels
Score	Label
90–100	Verified
80–89	High
65–79	Moderate
45–64	Emerging
20–44	Low
0–19	Unknown
