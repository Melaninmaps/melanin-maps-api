# New Permanent Metric™

Beginning with Audit 9, I would introduce:

## **Belonging Fulfillment Score™**

Separate from engineering.

Not:

"Did the event work?"

Instead:

**Did the platform help someone feel more connected afterward?**

Measured through signals like:

* repeat attendance;
* new community connections;
* business support;
* volunteer participation;
* Community Journey™ updates;
* Hidden Gem discoveries;
* Community Recognition earned;
* return visits.

That metric captures something most platforms never attempt to measure: **whether they helped create genuine community**.
