I have one recommendation to add that wasn't explicitly in either report: **make Build 99 a release candidate and freeze it.** Once this build passes all of your release gates, do not continue adding features to it. If another enhancement comes up, put it into Build 100. That discipline reduces the chance of introducing new regressions just before Apple review and gives you the best opportunity to get this submission approved.
