One addition I want to make to your onboarding requirement: because Kinfolk is meant to learn over time, **the onboarding should establish a starting point, not try to interrogate the user into a complete life profile**. Fifteen short questions is enough. Everything after that should develop naturally through saves, follows, searches, contributions and explicit updates.

And this audit should give us something we have not had yet: **visual production evidence tied to one clean user's entire lifecycle**. If Replit reports PASS on a step, there should be a screenshot showing the member actually saw it and a data check showing it persisted.

That will dramatically reduce the cycle we've been stuck in of “Replit says it exists → you open the website → it isn't there.”
