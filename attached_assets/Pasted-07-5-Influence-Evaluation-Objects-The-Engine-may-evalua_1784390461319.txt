07.5 Influence Evaluation Objects
The Engine may evaluate:
Object	Examples
Individual	Cultural Ambassador, creator, educator
Business	Culturally influential business or founder
Organization	Nonprofit, collective, association
Institution	HBCU, museum, archive, cultural center
Event	Festival, tour, lecture, gathering
Story	Article, video, oral history, documentary
Campaign	Business-support drive, community initiative
Partnership	Business–Ambassador collaboration
Network	Alumni group, diaspora community
Place	Landmark, neighborhood, cultural corridor
Program	Mentorship, fellowship, cultural education
Movement	Sustained community-led initiative
Each object type must use an approved interpretation profile.
07.6 Core Evaluation Dimensions
The Cultural Influence Engine™ returns a normalized score from 0 to 100 using nine dimensions.
Dimension	Baseline weight
Cultural Grounding	15%
Authenticity & Integrity	15%
Community Connection	14%
Resonance & Meaning	10%
Action & Conversion	12%
Reciprocity & Shared Benefit	13%
Stewardship & Attribution	9%
Continuity & Reliability	7%
Reach & Accessibility	5%
Total	100%
Follower count, raw views, and audience size are contained primarily within Reach & Accessibility and may not exceed the limits defined below.
07.7 Dimension One — Cultural Grounding
Cultural Grounding measures whether the influence is rooted in meaningful knowledge, lived connection, responsible study, verified history, community relationship, or accountable participation.
It evaluates:
relationship to the culture or community;
depth of knowledge;
historical accuracy;
local context;
cultural specificity;
community recognition;
language and terminology;
acknowledgment of complexity;
avoidance of stereotype;
appropriate sourcing;
relationship to institutions or tradition;
willingness to correct errors.
07.7.1 Cultural Grounding Formula
Cultural Grounding Score =
  Knowledge Depth
+ Context Accuracy
+ Community Relationship
+ Historical Responsibility
+ Cultural Specificity
- Misrepresentation Penalties
Normalized to 0–100.
07.7.2 Positive indicators
accurate historical references;
clear local context;
consultation with affected communities;
appropriate attribution;
acknowledgment of regional and diaspora differences;
demonstrated long-term connection;
culturally informed interpretation;
transparent limits of expertise;
correction of inaccuracies;
respectful use of language;
recognition by credible community institutions;
preservation of nuance.
07.7.3 Misrepresentation indicators
stereotypes;
invented cultural claims;
flattening distinct communities into one identity;
false historical statements;
claiming authority without basis;
exoticizing culture;
using cultural imagery without context;
erasing local contributors;
presenting opinion as community consensus;
appropriating community language for marketing;
refusing correction;
misleading ownership or identity claims.
