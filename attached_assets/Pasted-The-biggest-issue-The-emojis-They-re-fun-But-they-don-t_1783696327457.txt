The biggest issue
The emojis.
😊 They're fun.
But they don't match the premium visual language you've established.
Slides 1–4 feel like Apple.
This slide feels like PowerPoint.
That's not a criticism of you—it's just a shift in tone.
Instead
Use simple line icons.
Very thin.
Very modern.
Single color.
Gold.
Examples:
Discover → outline magnifying glass
Connect → two linked circles or handshake icon
Grow → upward arrow or sprouting leaf
Travel → compass or location pin (rather than an airplane, which narrows the meaning)
Belong → community or home icon (rather than a heart, which can imply romance or "liking")
Those icons will age much better and match the sophistication of the rest of the deck.
