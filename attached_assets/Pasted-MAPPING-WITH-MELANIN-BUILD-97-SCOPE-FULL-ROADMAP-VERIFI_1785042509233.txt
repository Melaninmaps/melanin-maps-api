1. CONFIRMED FOUNDER DECISIONS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Record these decisions as approved product direction:

A. COMMUNITY MEMBER

Community Member is the free base membership identity for every person
who registers.

A person does not need to pay, submit a selfie, or submit government ID
to become a Community Member.

B. VERIFIED COMMUNITY MEMBER

Eligibility to become a Verified Community Member will be included with
EVERY paid individual membership.

This is not limited to Navigator or any one named paid tier. The rule is:

- Free membership: Community Member
- Any active paid membership: eligible to complete Verified Community
  Member verification

Verification remains optional unless a future high-trust feature has a
separately approved requirement.

C. MULTIPLE ROLES

A person may simultaneously be:

- Community Member
- Verified Community Member
- Business Owner
- Verified Business Owner
- Cultural Ambassador
- Community Organization representative
- Mentor
- Contributor or Trusted Contributor

These are additive roles and designations on one account.

A Business Owner may be a Verified Community Member without being a
Verified Business Owner.

A Verified Business Owner may require separate ownership documentation.

Personal identity verification must never be treated as proof of minority
business ownership.

D. THIRD-PARTY FUTURE DIRECTION

The founder prefers a third-party identity-verification service for the
full-launch future state.

The objectives are:

- Lowest reasonable total cost at early-stage volume
- Strong user protection
- Provider-hosted capture
- Mapping With Melanin™ should not store raw selfie or government-ID
  images
- Explicit consent
- Clear deletion/redaction process
- Reliable iOS and Android experience
- One included verification for each eligible paid member
- Duplicate-charge protection
- Accurate badge meaning

Current recommendation for architectural planning:

Stripe Identity should be evaluated as the leading early-stage option
because of pay-as-you-go pricing and existing Stripe infrastructure.

Veriff should remain a future comparison option when verification volume
is high enough to justify a monthly minimum.

Do not select, contract, configure, or activate a vendor during this
request.
