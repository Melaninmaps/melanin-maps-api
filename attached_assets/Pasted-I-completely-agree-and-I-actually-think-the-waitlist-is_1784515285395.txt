I completely agree, and I actually think the waitlist is **more than a launch tool**—it's your first community management system.

One thing has become clear over the last week: your launch isn't "everyone gets in on day one." Your launch is **intentional**. That means the waitlist has to support strategy, not just collect emails.

Looking at Replit's revised Phase B, I'm happy to see they incorporated the major corrections about backups, rollback, atomic transactions, and preserving production data. Those are meaningful improvements. 

## Before Google Play goes live, I would want to be able to answer "yes" to every one of these questions:

### Public experience

* Can someone join the waitlist in under a minute?
* Do they immediately receive a confirmation that their spot was saved? (even if you don't send email yet, the UI should clearly confirm success)
* Can they safely submit twice without creating duplicate records?
* If they return later with the same email, what happens?

### Admin experience

You should be able to:

* Search by email.
* Search by city.
* Search by state.
* Search by business/community member.
* Filter by status.
* Filter by launch wave.
* See exactly when someone joined.
* See where they came from (website, tester, import, referral, etc.).
* Export the current waitlist.
* Import additional recovered records safely.
* View an audit trail of significant changes.

### City-by-city launch

This is the part I think will become one of your biggest competitive advantages.

Instead of:

> "Approve User"

I'd rather you think in terms of:

> **Activate Community**

For example:

**Atlanta**

* Community Members: 20 / 20
* Businesses: 5 / 5
* Cultural Ambassadors: 8 / 10
* Organizations: 2 / 2

Status:

**Ready to Launch**

Then you press one button.

That one action should:

* invite the selected members,
* invite the selected businesses,
* record the launch date,
* preserve everyone else on the waitlist for the next wave.

### A few additional admin capabilities I would add

Before launch, I would want:

* **Pause signups** (temporarily disable new waitlist submissions if needed)
* **City capacity settings** (configure targets like "20 members, 5 businesses")
* **Preview mode** (show exactly who would be selected before releasing a city)
* **Bulk communication** (send messages to only Atlanta, only businesses, only Wave 1, etc.)
* **Referral insights** (who is bringing in the most people and businesses)
* **Duplicate review queue** (flag, don't merge automatically)
* **Activity log** (every approval, import, export, and launch action is recorded)
