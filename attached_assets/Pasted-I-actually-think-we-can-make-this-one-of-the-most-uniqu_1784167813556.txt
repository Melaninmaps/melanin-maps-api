I actually think we can make this one of the most unique features of **Mapping with Melanin™.**

Rather than only listing historical sites, we can build a **Cultural Heritage Explorer** that allows users to discover landmarks, colleges, museums, neighborhoods, monuments, cultural districts, festivals, and heritage sites representing **all historically underrepresented communities** in the United States.

I'd recommend organizing them into categories so your app can grow over time.

---

## The metadata I'd store for every location

Instead of just a pin on the map, every place should include:

* Name
* Category
* Subcategory
* Ethnic/Cultural Community
* Description
* Historical significance
* Year established
* Address
* GPS coordinates
* Photos
* Website
* Hours
* Admission cost
* Accessibility information
* Family friendly
* Nearby minority-owned businesses
* Nearby restaurants
* Nearby events
* Audio guide availability
* Educational resources
* Verified source(s)

---

## One feature I'd strongly encourage

Rather than filtering by race alone, let users explore by **Community** or **Heritage**. For example:

* African American Heritage
* Native American Heritage
* Hispanic & Latino Heritage
* Asian American & Pacific Islander Heritage
* Middle Eastern & North African Heritage
* Jewish Heritage
* Caribbean Heritage
* African Diaspora
* LGBTQ+ History
* Women's History
* Disability History
* Immigrant History
* Multicultural Heritage

This aligns well with your vision of helping people discover and understand communities—whether they're traveling, relocating, or exploring their own hometown. It also creates a richer experience than a simple directory, because users can learn the stories, history, and cultural significance behind the places they visit.
