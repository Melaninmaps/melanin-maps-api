Absolutely. And this is exactly why building the spreadsheet before handing the system to Replit is useful—we're discovering that **“Beauty Supply” needs its own language**, not generic retail language.

For many Black women, the beauty supply store visit is extremely functional *and* personal. Sometimes you're browsing. Sometimes you need braiding hair tonight. Sometimes you're halfway through doing your daughter's hair and realize you're one pack short. Sometimes you're in a bonnet and sweats and absolutely do not want to be evaluated. 😭 The feedback choices should capture that reality.

I would separate **the feeling of the store (Vibes)** from **what the store actually delivered (What Stands Out?)**.

### Beauty Supply — VIBES

These describe the experience/energy rather than making a factual service claim.

| Vibe                              | What we mean                                                  |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |

| **Judgment-Free Beauty**          | No side-eye, hovering, or making you uncomfortable            |

A few of these are especially important because they communicate something a five-star rating never could. **“Come As You Are”** may be one of my favorites. It says something very specific to the experience without requiring a woman to explain why it matters.

And I think there is one particularly important distinction we should preserve:

**“Stocked & Ready”** = a **Vibe/perception** about the store.

**“They Always Have What I Need”** = a **community-earned What Stands Out signal** based on repeated experiences.

Eventually seeing:

**They Always Have What I Need · 87 Kinfolk**

is far more valuable than seeing ⭐ 4.7.

### There is another opportunity here

Beauty supply stores could eventually have **micro-signals** based on what someone is actually shopping for:

**BRAIDS:** Braiding Hair For Days · Every Color Was There · Edge Control Selection · Accessories Were Stocked

**NATURAL:** Natural Hair Section Is Serious · My Texture Was Covered · Black-Owned Brands Were There · Ingredients I Look For

**WIGS:** Good Wig Selection · Lace Options Were There · Wig Essentials In Stock · Helped Me Find The Right One

**BEAUTY:** My Shade Was There · Good Brand Variety · Lashes For Days · Nail Section Is Serious

That means a member searching for a beauty supply could eventually tell Kinfolk:

**“I need braiding hair.”**

And Kinfolk doesn't simply return the nearest beauty supply. It could eventually answer based on community intelligence:

**“Kinfolk consistently says this one has braiding hair, lots of colors, and usually has what they need.”**

That is the level of usefulness I think we're actually building toward.
