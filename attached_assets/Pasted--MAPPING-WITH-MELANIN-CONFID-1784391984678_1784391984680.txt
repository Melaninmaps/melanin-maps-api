10. COMMUNITY RIPPLE™
Future-State Production Specification v1.0
Classification: Network Impact, Downstream Effect, and Community Propagation Intelligence Engine
Status: Review Mode — designed as the launch-state model
Confidentiality: Highest restriction; Ripple propagation logic, downstream attribution methodology, network amplification calculations, intervention influence modeling, and propagation weighting are confidential trade secrets and are not publicly disclosed.
10.1 Purpose
The Community Ripple™ Engine measures how one meaningful action influences additional actions throughout the Mapping With Melanin™ ecosystem.
Unlike the Community Catalyst Model™, which measures whether an action creates positive movement, Community Ripple™ measures how far that movement travels.
It determines:
what actions create downstream effects;
how influence spreads through communities;
how opportunities multiply;
how relationships expand;
how knowledge propagates;
how trust transfers between people;
how local actions become regional impact;
how community support compounds over time;
which actions create sustained chains of benefit;
which interventions produce the greatest long-term effect.
The governing question is:
How many meaningful positive outcomes occurred because this original action happened?
10.2 Foundational Principle
Communities rarely change because of isolated actions.
Communities change because one action creates another.
A recommendation becomes a visit.
A visit becomes trust.
Trust becomes a conversation.
A conversation becomes mentorship.
Mentorship becomes employment.
Employment becomes stability.
Stability becomes stewardship.
Stewardship becomes legacy.
Community Ripple™ exists to measure these chains—not individual moments.
10.3 Definition of a Ripple
A Ripple is a measurable downstream outcome that would likely not have occurred without an originating action.
Ripples may be:
direct;
indirect;
delayed;
cumulative;
collaborative;
intergenerational.
Not every action produces a Ripple.
Not every Ripple has equal value.
