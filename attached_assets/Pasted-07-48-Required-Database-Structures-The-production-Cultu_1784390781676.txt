07.50 Human Review Requirements
Human review is required when:
cultural authenticity is materially disputed;
false identity or ownership claims are alleged;
cultural exploitation is alleged;
consent or attribution is disputed;
undisclosed commercial relationships are found;
a Cultural Ambassador partnership may create reputational risk;
a score changes by more than 20 points in 30 days;
manipulation is suspected;
community evidence is sharply divided;
sensitive cultural knowledge is involved;
a person is nominated for Legacy Ambassador status;
a high-profile entity receives a low integrity score;
an emerging creator alleges structural suppression;
sponsorship may be influencing visibility improperly;
a partnership involves minors;
a safety hold exists;
an appeal is filed;
a new evidence type is used;
public explanation could imply identity judgment;
Institution or Generational Test concerns arise.
07.51 Failure Conditions
If the Cultural Influence Engine™ is unavailable:
no Cultural Influence Score may be fabricated;
follower count may not be used as a substitute;
Cultural Ambassador partnership eligibility must be held;
cultural promotion boosts must stop;
recognition decisions must be held;
ordinary search may continue where otherwise eligible;
Trust, Safety, Community, and Discovery rules remain active;
the failure must be logged;
public language must not imply cultural validation.
Fallback output:
{
  "cultural_influence_engine_status": "UNAVAILABLE",

  "confidence_label": "Unknown",

  "follower_count_substitution_allowed": false,
  "human_review_required": true
}
