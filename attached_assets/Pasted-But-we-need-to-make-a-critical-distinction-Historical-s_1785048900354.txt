But we need to make a critical distinction:
Historical sundown-town designation is not the same as a claim that a community is presently unsafe or currently operates as a sundown town.
The Tougaloo database itself says it is incomplete and contains places that are confirmed, probable, or still being researched. Therefore, Build 97 should never place one generic warning marker on every listed town without showing the historical status, confidence, source, and present-day context.
What I recommend for Build 97
Add a map layer named:
Historical Sundown Towns
Not:
Dangerous Towns
Unsafe Towns
Current Sundown Towns
Avoid This Area
The layer can include:
State filter
Historical location marker
Name of town, city, county, or suburb
Historical designation status
Community or population historically excluded, when documented
Time period, when available
Evidence or confidence category
Brief historical explanation
Source attribution
“Historical designation—not a current safety rating”
Link to the original research source
Optional route-proximity notice only when a member intentionally enables it
Recently reported discrimination
This should be shown separately as:
Recent Community-Reported Experiences
It should not transform a historical designation into a present-day danger label.
For example:
Historical context
This community has been identified in historical research as a former or possible sundown town.
Recent community context
Mapping With Melanin™ received three approved reports describing racial discrimination in this area during the past 12 months.
Or:
No recent Mapping With Melanin™ reports are available. This does not confirm the absence or presence of discrimination.
That preserves the history without making an unsupported current claim.
The map should use separate evidence layers
Layer 1 — Historical record
Possible statuses:
Historically confirmed
Probable or strongly documented
Possible or under research
Historical record disputed or incomplete
Community has publicly acknowledged its history
Community has documented reconciliation or corrective initiatives
The scholarly geocoded dataset includes a confidence variable inherited from the historical registry, making it possible to avoid presenting every record with identical certainty.
Layer 2 — Recent verified public information
Examples:
Official civil-rights findings
Court decisions
Government reports
Verified public investigations
Credible reported incidents
Publicly documented policy changes
Each item needs a date and source. It should expire from “recent” status after an approved period while remaining in historical records where appropriate.
Layer 3 — Mapping With Melanin™ community reports
Examples:
Approved discrimination reports
Member-reported experiences
Day/night experience patterns
Reports of discriminatory policing
Business or lodging concerns
Reports of community support
These should be aggregated and anonymized. One report should never cause Kinfolk to label a town unsafe.
Safety language
Every detail card should include wording similar to:
Historical context
This location appears in historical research concerning sundown towns—communities that intentionally excluded Black people or other racial, ethnic, or religious groups through law, intimidation, custom, or violence. This designation describes documented history and is not, by itself, a statement about every current resident or a definitive assessment of present-day conditions.
And:
Current community information
Recent reports reflect submitted or publicly documented experiences and may be incomplete. Use this information as one source when planning—not as a guarantee of safety or danger.
Historically, sundown exclusions were not limited exclusively to Black Americans; some places excluded Chinese Americans, Jewish Americans, Mexican Americans, Native Americans, and other groups.
Should this be in Build 97?
Yes, but only as a carefully scoped historical layer.
Build 97 can reasonably include:
Historical Sundown Towns toggle
State-based browsing
Verified source attribution
Historical/confidence status
Map markers
Basic detail card
Clear disclaimer
Connection to recent approved MWM reports when data exists
Hide/show control
Performance testing to ensure the additional markers do not crash the Map
Build 97 should not attempt to include:
An automated present-day danger score
Live web scraping of allegations
Unmoderated reports displayed immediately
Predictive warnings based on demographics
Automatic “avoid this town” directions
A claim that every historically listed town remains a sundown town today
Community Health weighting formulas
Route avoidance without explicit user choice
AI-generated allegations about communities
This keeps the Build 97 addition educational and safety-conscious without turning it into a rushed present-day classification system.
