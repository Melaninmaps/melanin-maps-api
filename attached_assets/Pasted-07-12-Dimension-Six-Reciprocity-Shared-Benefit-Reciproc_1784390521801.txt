07.12 Dimension Six — Reciprocity & Shared Benefit
Reciprocity & Shared Benefit measures whether cultural influence returns value to the people, businesses, institutions, and communities that make the influence possible.
It evaluates:
economic return;
referrals;
opportunity creation;
attribution;
compensation;
visibility for others;
resource sharing;
mentorship;
local partnership;
community investment;
benefit distribution;
support after campaign completion.
07.12.1 Reciprocity Formula
Reciprocity & Shared Benefit Score =
  Economic Return
+ Opportunity Creation
+ Attribution and Recognition
+ Resource Return
+ Community Capacity Building
- Extraction Penalty
07.12.2 Positive indicators
direct traffic to business websites;
measurable customer referrals;
paid collaborations;
creator and community attribution;
reinvestment;
mentorship;
sharing useful resources;
highlighting overlooked businesses;
community-led partnership design;
continued support beyond launch;
transparent affiliate arrangements;
contribution to local institutions.
07.12.3 Extraction indicators
unpaid cultural labor;
taking community stories without consent;
visibility gained without returning value;
hiding partnership compensation;
promoting businesses without follow-through;
using community hardship for engagement;
directing traffic away from source businesses unnecessarily;
retaining all commercial benefit;
treating culture as aesthetic inventory;
abandoning the community after campaign completion.
07.13 Dimension Seven — Stewardship & Attribution
Stewardship & Attribution measures how responsibly cultural stories, history, knowledge, names, images, traditions, and community contributions are handled.
It evaluates:
source credit;
creator credit;
business credit;
historical sourcing;
consent;
contextual accuracy;
archival responsibility;
correction;
ownership respect;
cultural sensitivity;
future preservation;
protection against misuse.
07.13.1 Stewardship Formula
Stewardship & Attribution Score =
  Attribution Quality
+ Consent Quality
+ Historical Responsibility
+ Cultural Protection
+ Preservation Value
- Stewardship Violations
Severe unauthorized use may trigger Trust, Governance, or Legacy & Learning review.
07.14 Dimension Eight — Continuity & Reliability
Continuity & Reliability measures whether influence remains useful, responsible, and community-connected over time.
It evaluates:
consistent participation;
follow-through;
repeat support;
sustained accuracy;
long-term relationships;
reliable publication;
completion of commitments;
response during difficulty;
correction over time;
durability beyond trend cycles.
07.14.1 Continuity Formula
Continuity & Reliability Score =
  Sustained Participation
+ Commitment Completion
+ Relationship Persistence
+ Reliability
+ Long-Term Community Presence
- Abandonment Penalty
One viral moment cannot establish full continuity.
