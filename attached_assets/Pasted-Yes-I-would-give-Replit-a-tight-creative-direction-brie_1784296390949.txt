## Mapping with Melanin™ Deck Cleanup Instructions

Please review all Mapping with Melanin™ decks for consistency, clarity, and presentation quality.

This is a **cleanup and alignment pass**, not a full rewrite and not a redesign.

### Preserve the brand system

Keep the existing brand palette consistent across every deck:

* Warm brown: `#3B1F0E`
* Cream: `#FAF1E4`
* Gold: `#C9A84C`

Do not introduce new primary colors. Accent colors may only be used where they already support a specific feature, such as safety, alerts, or category identification.

Maintain:

* the current typography hierarchy;
* the logo treatment;
* the use of `™` where appropriate;
* the warm, polished, community-centered visual style;
* the established emoji language.

Emojis should feel intentional and mature—not playful, random, or excessive. Use them as visual anchors, not decoration.

### Core message that should appear across all decks

Every deck should consistently communicate that Mapping with Melanin™ is:

> A community-powered platform helping people discover businesses, understand places, find culturally meaningful experiences, and feel more confident wherever life takes them.

It should never be framed as only:

* a travel app;
* a business directory;
* a safety app;
* a social network;
* or an AI assistant.

The platform connects all of those experiences through one central promise:

> **Helping people feel like they belong—at home, in a new city, or abroad.**

### Story themes that should remain consistent

Please confirm that each deck supports these ideas in language appropriate to its audience:

#### Why we exist

People deserve more than generic recommendations and traditional crime statistics. They deserve lived experience, cultural context, trusted community insight, and a way to find people and places that help them feel seen.

#### More than directions

Most maps tell people where something is.

Mapping with Melanin™ helps people understand:

* what the experience may feel like;
* whether the community felt welcomed;
* where culture lives;
* which businesses deserve support;
* and where people may feel a deeper sense of connection.

#### Safety through lived experience

The safety layer should not be described as a promise that a place is safe.

Use language such as:

> Community insight helps people make more informed decisions based on the lived experiences of those who have already been there.

Avoid language that sounds like a guarantee.

#### Culturally meaningful discovery

The platform should highlight:

* minority-owned businesses;
* culturally significant places;
* heritage locations;
* local community experiences;
* and trusted recommendations shaped by culture and context.

#### Home and global use

The messaging must clearly show that this is not only for travelers or people relocating.

Use examples such as:

* discovering a new restaurant in your hometown;
* supporting a local entrepreneur;
* visiting family;
* moving to a new city;
* attending college;
* traveling for work;
* or exploring another country.

#### Community ownership

Users are not passive consumers.

They help shape the platform through:

* experiences;
* recommendations;
* safety insights;
* business additions;
* cultural knowledge;
* and community storytelling.

#### Business empowerment

Mapping with Melanin™ does not replace a business’s website, social media, or brand.

It helps direct people back to the business and allows owners to share:

* who they are;
* why they started;
* what makes them different;
* and how they serve their community.

#### Kinfolk AI

Do not describe Kinfolk as a generic chatbot.

Preferred framing:

> Kinfolk is a culturally aware guide that helps users navigate places, decisions, and experiences with greater context and confidence.

The strongest positioning language should reflect:

* warm;
* familiar;
* intelligent;
* grounded;
* protective without being alarming;
* culturally aware without becoming performative.

Preferred line:

> **Most AI tells you what exists. Kinfolk helps you understand what belongs.**

Also reinforce:

> Kinfolk is not built only to answer questions. It is built to help people navigate life, community, travel, and belonging.

#### Cultural Ambassadors

Use “Cultural Ambassadors” consistently instead of generic terms like influencers or content creators when referring to this program.

Define them as:

> Trusted voices who help introduce communities, businesses, stories, and experiences through authentic local perspective.

They should not appear as paid promoters only. They are bridge-builders between the platform and the communities they know.

### Deck-specific emphasis

#### Investor deck

Emphasize:

* the problem;
* the market opportunity;
* what makes the platform different;
* community-powered data;
* trust and safety;
* Kinfolk AI;
* business model;
* defensibility;
* growth potential;
* and why the team understands the problem.

Do not overload it with every feature.

#### Business deck

Emphasize:

* visibility without replacing the business’s own platform;
* direct traffic to websites and social media;
* authentic storytelling;
* community trust;
* customer discovery;
* promotions and events;
* cultural ambassador partnerships;
* and why joining early matters.

#### Community deck

Emphasize:

* belonging;
* local and global discovery;
* safety context;
* culture;
* community contribution;
* connection;
* Kinfolk;
* and how members help shape the platform.

#### Feature/experience deck

This deck should show the experience—not read like a product specification.

Organize slides around user outcomes such as:

* Know before you go
* See a city through your community
* Find your people
* Discover businesses with a story
* Walk through living history
* Travel like you already belong
* Build community wherever life takes you

### Cleanup tasks

Please review all decks for:

* duplicate slides;
* repeated wording;
* inconsistent capitalization;
* inconsistent use of Mapping with Melanin™ and Melanin Maps LLC;
* inconsistent references to Kinfolk, Kinfolk AI, and KinfolkAI™;
* outdated feature descriptions;
* old relocation-heavy language that makes the platform sound only for moving or travel;
* inconsistent color usage;
* inconsistent icon and emoji styles;
* crowded slides;
* overly small text;
* weak slide titles;
* unnecessary page numbers;
* inconsistent spacing and alignment;
* and claims that sound stronger than what the platform currently proves.

Do not remove slides solely because they are long. Flag them for later review if they contain important ideas.

### Accuracy rules

Do not claim a feature is live unless it is confirmed live.

Use one of these labels where needed:

* Available now
* In beta
* In development
* Planned
* Future vision

Do not describe:

* safety as guaranteed;
* businesses as verified unless they have completed verification;
* Kinfolk as fully autonomous;
* or features as patented, trademarked, or copyrighted unless confirmed by counsel or registration status.

### Final deliverable

Please provide:

1. A cleaned version of each deck.
2. A short list of slides that need founder review.
3. A list of duplicate or outdated slides.
4. A consistency report covering:

   * brand colors;
   * fonts;
   * logo usage;
   * emojis;
   * terminology;
   * feature status;
   * and messaging.
5. Do not make irreversible deletions. Preserve the original decks.
