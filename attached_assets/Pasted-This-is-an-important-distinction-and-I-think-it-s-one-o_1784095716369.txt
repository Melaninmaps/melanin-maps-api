This is an important distinction, and I think it's one of the places where Mapping with Melanin™ can differentiate itself.

The answer depends on **who you're partnering with** versus **who you're promoting to users.** Those don't have to be the same thing.

## Option 1: Minority-Owned Travel Partners (Most Aligned)

This is the approach that best fits your mission.

Examples include:

* Black-owned travel agencies
* Minority-owned tour operators
* Black-owned bed & breakfasts
* Indigenous-owned cultural tours
* Latino-owned excursion companies
* Minority-owned transportation companies
* Local cultural guides
* Minority-owned destination management companies

These businesses would receive the same treatment as every other promoted business on your platform.

That keeps your message simple:

> "Mapping with Melanin™ promotes minority-owned businesses. Our travel experiences are powered by minority-owned travel professionals whenever possible."

I think this should always be your first choice.

---

## Option 2: Strategic Partnerships (Not Promotions)

This is different.

Imagine Hyatt approached you.

Instead of saying:

> "Stay at Hyatt."

You might instead say:

> "Hyatt is providing discounted rates exclusively for Mapping with Melanin™ travelers."

The app would **not** feature Hyatt in searches as a promoted business if it isn't minority owned.

Instead, Hyatt becomes an infrastructure partner.

Think of them like:

* Stripe (payments)
* Google Maps
* Apple
* Amtrak
* Uber

They're helping power the experience rather than being highlighted because they're a featured business.

That's a very different relationship.

---

## Option 3: Community Choice

Another possibility is allowing users to add and review any business for informational purposes while keeping promotion reserved for minority-owned businesses.

For example:

* ✅ Minority-owned coffee shop → Featured and promoted
* ✅ Local hospital → Listed because it's useful
* ✅ Airport → Listed because it's necessary
* ✅ National hotel → Available for trip planning if users need it
* ❌ Not featured in "Support Minority Businesses"

This keeps your promise intact while making the app practical for real travel.

---

## My Recommendation

I actually think your platform should have **two standards** that are easy for users to understand:

### Featured Partners

Only minority-owned businesses.

These receive:

* Featured placement
* Promotions
* Spotlight articles
* Business of the Week
* Discovery boosts
* Marketing campaigns
* Recommendation priority

### Infrastructure & Travel Partners

Companies that help users travel but are **not promoted as featured businesses.**

Examples could include:

* Hotel chains
* Airlines
* Car rental companies
* Insurance providers
* Credit card companies
* Mapping providers
* Booking technology

Their role is to make travel easier, not to compete with the minority-owned businesses you're intentionally elevating.

---

## One Additional Idea

Since you've told me your long-term vision is to become the trusted platform for minority travelers, I would also create a certification or designation such as:

> **Community Ally Partner**

This would be reserved for larger companies that demonstrate a meaningful commitment to serving your community. They would **never** replace minority-owned businesses in discovery or featured placement, but they could provide tangible benefits like:

* Exclusive discounts for your members
* Scholarships
* Community grants
* Sponsorship of cultural events
* Funding for minority entrepreneurs
* Travel credits or rewards

That creates a way to work with major brands without compromising your commitment to promoting minority-owned businesses first.

I think this approach preserves the core promise of Mapping with Melanin™: **promotion is reserved for minority-owned businesses**, while strategic partnerships exist to improve the travel experience and invest back into the community. It also gives you flexibility as the company grows, because many large travel brands may want to support your mission even if they are not eligible for featured promotion.
