I actually love this idea because it reinforces something that's central to Mapping with Melanin™: **community is reciprocal.** Business owners aren't just asking for support—they're participating in it.

Rather than saying "put your money where your mouth is," I'd frame it as recognizing community engagement in a positive, authentic way.

Here are a few approaches:

### Simple & Clean

* **Supports Local Businesses**
* **Community Supporter**
* **Community Champion**
* **Shops Small**
* **Supports Minority-Owned Businesses**
* **Community First**
* **Local Advocate**

### More Meaningful

* **Supports the Businesses They Believe In**
* **Invests in Their Community**
* **Supporting the Community They Serve**
* **Building Community Together**
* **Championing Fellow Entrepreneurs**
* **Community Builder**
* **Supporting Businesses Beyond Their Own**

### Badge Ideas

These could appear on a business owner's profile:

🛍️ Community Supporter
🤝 Local Champion
🌍 Community Builder
💛 Gives Back
🏡 Shops Local
⭐ Community Ally

### Activity Examples

Instead of simply displaying purchases, you could show community-focused actions such as:

* Marcus has supported **18 local businesses** this year.
* Marcus has attended **6 community events**.
* Marcus has referred **12 businesses** to the community.
* Marcus has left **24 helpful reviews**.
* Marcus has welcomed **15 new members**.
* Marcus has volunteered **8 hours** with community organizations.

This shifts the focus from spending money to actively strengthening the community.

### My Favorite

Because your brand is about connection, I'd consider a metric called **Community Impact**.

For example:

> **Community Impact**
>
> Marcus doesn't just own a business—he actively supports the community around him.
>
> ✔ Supported 18 local businesses
> ✔ Attended 6 community events
> ✔ Referred 12 businesses
> ✔ Shared 34 helpful recommendations

This tells a richer story than a badge alone.

### One Feature I Think Would Be Unique

Create a **"Supports Others"** section on both business and community member profiles. It could include things like:

* Businesses they've visited
* Events they've attended
* Community initiatives they've participated in
* Referrals they've made
* Mentorship they've offered
* Charities or nonprofits they've supported (if they choose to share)

That reinforces a core message of Mapping with Melanin™:

> **The strongest communities are built by people who support one another—not just by businesses asking to be supported.**

It also creates a culture where being an active community member is recognized and celebrated alongside running a successful business.
