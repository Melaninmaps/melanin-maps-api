My recommendation
Transaction Amount	Platform Fee
Under $25	5%
$25–$250	8% (recommended standard)
Over $250	6% (or cap the fee)
I think 8% is the sweet spot.
Here's why:
It provides meaningful revenue for Mapping with Melanin™.
It's easier for businesses to absorb than double-digit commissions.
It leaves room for payment processing fees.
It aligns well with your mission of helping businesses grow rather than taking a large share of each sale.
