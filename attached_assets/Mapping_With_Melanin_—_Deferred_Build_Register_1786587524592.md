## FUT-001 — Licensed GIF-provider picker

Members may later select provider-licensed GIFs in permitted Community surfaces. The feature must use exactly one selected provider per picker, display required attribution, respect provider content/rating rules, avoid unlicensed upload/rehosting, use high/G safety filtering, preserve member visibility settings, and remain unavailable in Kinfolk chat, Library evidence, Safety, medical, legal, and urgent-alert surfaces. It does not authorize business reuse of member media.

## FUT-002 — Community-driven hyperlocal slang and scoped place aliases

Members may later propose and help validate local place names, neighborhood shorthand, historic names, and transliterations. Kinfolk can resolve an alias only within its approved city/state/country scope. The feature requires evidence thresholds or curator review, never assumes an alias applies in another city, never exposes member identities, and asks a concise clarification question when an unscoped local term remains ambiguous.

## Retrieval rule

When preparing a future-build report, include every row in this register, link the governing implementation document, state whether its trigger has been met, and identify any newer related specifications that affect it. Do not treat a deferred feature as approved for production until the founder explicitly prioritizes it and the feature’s independent acceptance gates pass.
