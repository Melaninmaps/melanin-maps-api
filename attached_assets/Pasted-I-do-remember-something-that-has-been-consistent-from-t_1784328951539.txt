## One thing I want us to protect

As this grows, we need to be careful not to promise features before they're ready. The vision you've outlined is powerful, but in the Foundations Book we should distinguish clearly between:

* **Our enduring mission and principles** (timeless).
* **Our current platform capabilities** (what exists today).
* **Our long-term vision** (what we aspire to build).

That distinction will keep the document inspiring without creating confusion about what is available now.
