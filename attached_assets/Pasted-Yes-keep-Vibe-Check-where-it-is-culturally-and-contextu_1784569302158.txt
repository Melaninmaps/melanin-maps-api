Yes — keep **Vibe Check** where it is culturally and contextually natural, and use a different label for professional and service-based businesses.

The mistake would be forcing one universal language system across every business. Mapping With Melanin™ should adapt the language to the business type.

## The rule for Replit

The platform should first identify the business’s **primary category**, then display the appropriate experience label and tag library.

A business should not receive every possible tag.

A law firm should never receive “Date Night.”
A nightclub should never receive “Estate Planning.”
A dentist should not receive “Romantic.”
A restaurant should not receive “Tax Preparation.”

Businesses with multiple categories can receive more than one relevant tag set, but only where the secondary category is verified.

## Keep Community Sayings separate

You are right that this is a different field.

**Structured category-specific field**

> Vibe Check / Best For / Care Focus / Community Role

This helps search, filtering, KinfolkAI, and recommendations.

**Community Sayings**

> “They make you feel like family.”
> “Ask for Ms. Renee.”
> “Worth the drive.”
> “They explain everything without rushing you.”
> “Get there before noon.”

This preserves authentic community language without turning every saying into a ranking tag.

## A clean classification model

| Business family                                        | User-facing label  |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------ |
| Dining, nightlife, entertainment, leisure, hospitality | **Vibe Check**     |
| Professional and essential services                    | **Best For**       |
| Healthcare and wellness                                | **Care Focus**     |
| Nonprofits, churches, community organizations          | **Community Role** |
| Retail and shopping                                    | **Shop For**       |
| Education and training                                 | **Learning Focus** |

This could become part of the **Adaptive Experience Classification™** system, while **Vibe Check** remains one of its most culturally recognizable expressions.

The guiding rule should be:

> **Use culturally natural language without forcing culturally specific language into contexts where it does not fit.**

That lets Mapping With Melanin™ keep its voice while still being useful and credible across every kind of business.
