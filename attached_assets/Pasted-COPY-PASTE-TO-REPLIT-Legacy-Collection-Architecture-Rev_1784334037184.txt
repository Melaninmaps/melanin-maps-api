# Legacy Collection Architecture — Review and Final Clarifications

**Status: Review Mode. No files should be renamed, moved, merged, or rewritten yet.**

The proposed **Volume → Chapter → Page** model correctly captures the long-term vision:

* **Mapping With Melanin™ Legacy Collection** is the official umbrella.
* **The Constitution** is Volume I.
* Presentations are chapters within the Presentation System.
* Individual presentation screens are pages.
* Pages may be reused across chapters without losing their approved source, history, or audience-specific variations.

This resolves the concern that the Experience Deck review was being treated as an isolated presentation project. It is one chapter within a much larger institutional collection.

Before implementation, please incorporate the following architectural clarifications.

---

## 1. Rename Volume V

The current structure places:

> Mapping With Melanin™ Legacy Collection
> → Volume V — Legacy Collection

That duplicates the name of the umbrella and may confuse future collaborators.

### Recommended name:

# Volume V — Legacy Editions

This volume contains the formal published expressions created from the broader Legacy Collection:

* Coffee table books
* City volumes
* Published documentary editions
* Letters to the Future
* Sounds of Home collections
* Historical publications
* Anniversary editions
* “To the Next Steward”

Alternative acceptable name:

# Volume V — Published Legacy

My preference is **Legacy Editions** because it can naturally support multiple books, films, albums, exhibitions, and future volumes.

---

## 2. Preserve Two Registry Levels

“Chapter Registry” should not fully replace the need to track individual pages.

The system needs both:

### Chapter Registry

Tracks complete presentation chapters:

* Experience Deck
* Investor Deck
* Business Deck
* Community Deck
* Zoom Deck
* Welcome Home Tour Deck
* Team Deck
* Features Deck

### Page Registry

Tracks every approved reusable page inside those chapters.

A page may appear:

* exactly as approved;
* as an audience adaptation;
* as a related concept;
* in multiple chapters;
* in print;
* in a video;
* on the website;
* in onboarding;
* or in a future documentary.

Recommended identifiers:

```text
MWM-EXP-P01-V1
MWM-INV-P05-V2
MWM-BIZ-P09-V1
```

The word **page** replaces “slide” in formal Legacy Collection language, but both Chapter Registry and Page Registry are necessary.

---

## 3. Add a Legacy Collection Index

The Legacy Collection needs a permanent top-level navigation document.

Recommended:

# The Legacy Collection Index & Manifest

It should identify:

* all volumes;
* chapters within each volume;
* page registries;
* current versions;
* approval status;
* confidentiality;
* document locations;
* owners;
* dependencies;
* related assets;
* last synchronization date.

This becomes the front door to the entire knowledge system.

The Legacy Collection is the library.

The Index is the catalog.

---

## 4. Clarify What Is Pulled Into Individual Presentations

Individual Zooms, investor meetings, business presentations, and community events should not require rebuilding content from scratch.

The Presentation System should support:

### Full Chapters

A complete approved deck created for one audience.

### Curated Chapter Editions

A shorter edition assembled from approved pages across multiple chapters.

### Individual Approved Pages

A single reusable page inserted into another chapter while retaining its source ID and history.

Example:

A Zoom presentation may pull:

* an opening page from the Experience chapter;
* a mission page from the Constitution;
* a business page from the Business chapter;
* a Founding Community page;
* the approved three-page closing sequence.

This is the intended system: approved pages can be curated into different audience journeys without losing governance.

---

## 5. The Constitution Does Not Replace the Legacy Collection

Official hierarchy:

```text
Mapping With Melanin™ Legacy Collection
│
├── Volume I — The Constitution
├── Volume II — Presentation System
├── Volume III — Living Archive
├── Volume IV — Community Atlas
```

“The Constitution” replaces the working title **Foundations Book**.

It does not replace the name of the full collection.

The **Mapping With Melanin™ Legacy Collection** remains the official umbrella and enduring institutional body of work.

## 7. Relationship to the Current Visual Work

The Experience Deck remains:

```text
Legacy Collection
→ Volume II: Presentation System
→ Experience Chapter
→ Pages 01–21
```

The current visual critique applies specifically to the **Experience Chapter**, but its approved design principles may later become global standards governing every presentation chapter.

Changes should not automatically propagate beyond the Experience Chapter.

The correct process remains:

1. Refine the Experience Chapter.
2. Approve its visual system.
3. Identify which visual decisions qualify as global standards.
4. Propose those additions to the Constitution and Creative OS.
5. Audit other presentation chapters.
6. Wait for cross-chapter authorization.
7. Apply only the approved scope.

```text
Legacy Collection

Volumes

Chapters
```
