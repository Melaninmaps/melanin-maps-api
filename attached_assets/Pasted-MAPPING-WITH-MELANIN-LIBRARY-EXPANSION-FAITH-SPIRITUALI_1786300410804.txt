MAPPING WITH MELANIN™
LIBRARY EXPANSION — FAITH, SPIRITUALITY & HOUSES OF FAITH
IMPLEMENTATION ADDENDUM

FOUNDER DECISION:
Faith, spirituality, religious history, and Houses of Faith must be incorporated
into the Mapping With Melanin Library architecture and Universal Search.

This is NOT simply a directory of churches.

Faith institutions are often cultural and community anchors. They may function as:

- places of worship
- historical landmarks
- cultural preservation centers
- immigrant/community gathering spaces
- food and mutual-aid resources
- youth and family centers
- educational institutions
- music and arts institutions
- civil-rights/community organizing spaces
- funeral/memorial spaces
- wedding/celebration spaces
- language-preservation communities
- volunteer organizations
- social-service providers

The architecture must represent this broader role.

==================================================
11. CULTURAL IDENTITY VS FAITH
==================================================

Do not equate ethnicity, nationality, race or diaspora identity with religion.

Examples:

Kenyan ≠ Christian
Arab ≠ Muslim
Indian ≠ Hindu
Jewish may describe religious, cultural and/or ethnic identity depending upon
context.

These entities should be relationally connected in the Library but stored as
distinct concepts.

This is important for both accuracy and inclusion.

==================================================
12. LGBTQIA+ INFORMATION
==================================================

MWM is inclusive, but do not infer whether a House of Faith is affirming or
non-affirming.

Only label:

LGBTQIA+ affirming
welcoming
inclusive ministry/programming
etc.

when supported by:

the institution's own published information,
a recognized denominational position where applicable,
or reliable documented evidence.

If unknown:

UNKNOWN / NOT DOCUMENTED

not "not affirming."

This distinction is important.

==================================================
13. COMMUNITY GOVERNANCE
==================================================

Faith communities are subject to the same MWM Community Governance &
Moderation rules.

A faith institution may hold religious beliefs that differ from another
member's beliefs.

That alone is not grounds for removal.

However:

active harassment
targeted hate
threats
dehumanization
calls for violence
discriminatory conduct on the MWM platform

remain governed by MWM policy.

Do not turn theological disagreement into a moderation violation automatically.

17. DISCOVERY SOURCE VS VERIFICATION SOURCE
==================================================

TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and community recommendations may be used to
DISCOVER a House of Faith.

Example founder workflow:

Search TikTok:
"Black Catholic churches Philadelphia"

Review:
official account
videos
community engagement
comments
events
community presence

Then verify the basic institution using stronger sources where available:

official institution website
official social profile
official denomination directory
recognized faith/community organization
government/interfaith directory
historic register/archive
reputable local reporting

TikTok comments alone must NOT make a House of Faith "verified."

19. KINFOLK
==================================================

Kinfolk should eventually understand faith as OPTIONAL context.

Examples:

"Find me a Black Catholic church while I'm in Philly."

"Is there a mosque near my hotel?"

"I want to visit historic Black churches."

"Find somewhere with gospel music."

"I'm Sikh and traveling to Philadelphia. Is there a gurdwara nearby?"

"Where can I volunteer through a faith organization?"

Kinfolk may use explicitly requested faith preferences.

DO NOT assume faith based on:

race
ethnicity
country
business ownership designation
family
location
previous unrelated searches.

Sensitive Library/context permissions still apply.

==================================================
20. LIBRARY FLYWHEEL
==================================================

Faith must participate in the same MWM flywheel.

Example:

Member searches:
Black Catholic churches Philadelphia

→ Universal Search finds institutions
→ member opens institution
→ member discovers Black Catholic history
→ Library offers Black Catholicism Book
→ Philadelphia history connects to relevant heritage
→ member watches community/archival media
→ member saves a House of Faith
→ Kinfolk may use that saved information only according to the member's
   privacy/context permissions
→ member visits
→ contributes story/photo/social video
→ institution receives additional community context
→ Library becomes richer
→ next member gets better discovery

The same architecture should work across other faith traditions.

Where can I find resources?
Where can I visit while traveling?

That is the purpose of Faith, Spirituality & Community inside the
Mapping With Melanin Library.
