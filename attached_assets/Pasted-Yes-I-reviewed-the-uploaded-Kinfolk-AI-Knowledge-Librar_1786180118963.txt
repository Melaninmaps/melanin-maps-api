One change I recommend making to the original specification itself: **“Reputable Sources” should become “Preferred Source Tiers.”** Kinfolk should not treat every listed website as equally authoritative.

For example:

**Tier 1 — Primary / Official:** CDC, NIH, FDA, HUD, CFPB, EEOC, DOJ, SBA, USCIS, state governments, courts, universities, peer-reviewed journals.

**Tier 2 — Recognized Professional / Institutional:** American Heart Association, ACOG, American Cancer Society, National Kidney Foundation, UNCF, professional associations.

**Tier 3 — Culturally Relevant Editorial / Community:** BlackDoctor.org, Essence, Blavity, diaspora publications, local community organizations.

That preserves the culturally relevant sources—which are essential to what makes Kinfolk different—while ensuring that **health, law, finance and safety claims are grounded first in authoritative information.** Your original specification already says Kinfolk should favor high-authority domains and whitelist trusted topic sources before expanding to the broader web, so this would strengthen the architecture rather than change its intent. 

I would also add one more field to every topic record: **“Ecosystem Connection.”** Your diaspora section already does this beautifully—for example connecting Caribbean topics to restaurants, hematologists and travel agents, and Vietnamese topics to Vietnamese-speaking physicians, salon suppliers and restaurants.   That should become standard across the entire Knowledge Library because it is where Kinfolk stops being “an article feed” and becomes part of the Mapping with Melanin ecosystem.

For example:

**Uterine Fibroids →** culturally competent OB-GYNs + doulas + support groups.
**First-Time Homebuying →** realtors + lenders + inspectors + attorneys.
**Natural Hair →** braiders + salons + dermatologists.
**Estate Planning →** attorneys + financial professionals.
**Moving to Houston →** neighborhoods + businesses + events + community groups + cultural ambassadors.

That last column may ultimately be one of the most valuable pieces of the whole system.
