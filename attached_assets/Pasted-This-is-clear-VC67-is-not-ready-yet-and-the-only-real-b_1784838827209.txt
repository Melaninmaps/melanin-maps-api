## What you should do now

In Google Play Console:

1. Open **Setup → App integrity**.
2. Find **App signing key certificate**.
3. Copy the **SHA-1 fingerprint** from that section—not the Upload key certificate.

Then in Google Cloud Console:

1. Open the project that owns the Android Maps key.
2. Go to **APIs & Services → Enabled APIs & services**.
3. Confirm **Maps SDK for Android** is enabled.
4. Go to **Billing** and confirm billing is active.
5. Go to **APIs & Services → Credentials**.
6. Open the Android Maps API key.
7. Under **Application restrictions**, choose **Android apps**.
8. Confirm or add:

   * Package name: `com.melaninmaps.app`
   * SHA-1: the **Play App Signing SHA-1**
9. Under **API restrictions**, restrict it to **Maps SDK for Android**.
10. Save.

Do not remove any existing SHA-1 entries yet. Add the Play App Signing SHA-1 alongside them until VC67 is tested.
