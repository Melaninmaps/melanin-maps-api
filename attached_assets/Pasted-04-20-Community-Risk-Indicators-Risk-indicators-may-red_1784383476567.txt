04.20 Community Risk Indicators
Risk indicators may reduce dimension scores or trigger review.
Examples include:
repeated gatekeeping;
exclusion of newcomers;
insider-only access;
harassment or retaliation;
extraction without return;
misleading community claims;
performative partnerships;
abandoned commitments;
suppression of criticism;
coordination to manipulate scores;
concentrated benefits;
cultural exploitation;
community displacement;
inaccessible resources;
public-facing diversity without internal influence;
unpaid labor expectations;
inconsistent treatment;
false urgency;
resource hoarding;
reputation management replacing accountability.
04.21 Exclusion Penalties
The following baseline penalties may apply.
Condition	Baseline penalty
Verified discriminatory exclusion	−30 to −60
Repeated newcomer gatekeeping	−10 to −25
Accessibility misrepresentation	−15 to −35
Hidden eligibility barriers	−10 to −20
Retaliation for legitimate feedback	−20 to −40
Community participation restricted for commercial reasons	−10 to −25
Insider-only benefit pattern	−10 to −30
Public inclusion claims contradicted by verified evidence	−15 to −35
Penalties may affect one dimension or the total Community Score.
Critical safety issues are routed to the Safety Intelligence Engine™ and may trigger an override rather than a standard penalty.
04.22 Extraction Penalties
Condition	Baseline penalty
Cultural use without consent or attribution	−15 to −35
Repeated unpaid community labor	−10 to −30
Hidden commercial use of community data	−25 to −50
Crisis profiteering	−25 to −60
Community visibility without shared benefit	−10 to −25
Partnership benefits concentrated outside community	−10 to −30
Misleading philanthropic claims	−15 to −40
Community story used after permission withdrawn	−25 to −50
A severe extraction finding may also reduce Trust, Stewardship, Cultural Influence, or Promotion eligibility.
04.23 Community Score Floors and Caps
Certain conditions limit the maximum score.
04.23.1 Baseline caps
Condition	Maximum Community Score
No direct community evidence	59
Only one evidence category	54
No reciprocity evidence	69
No voice or correction pathway	74
No continuity evidence	79
Active unresolved exclusion concern	64
Verified retaliation	49
Severe extraction finding	39
Active governance hold	0 for promotion
04.23.2 Score floors
No positive activity creates a mandatory score floor.
The Engine must remain capable of recognizing serious community harm despite high participation or visibility.
