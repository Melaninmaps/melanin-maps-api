For Melanin Maps™ / Mapping With Melanin™, I would choose tier names that feel aspirational, community-oriented, and aligned with exploration, discovery, and empowerment.

## Consumer Memberships

### 🧭 Explorer (Free)

**$0/month**

For users discovering the platform.

Includes:

* Business search
* Maps
* Reviews
* Events
* Community feed
* Basic recommendations

---

### 🌍 Navigator

**$7.99/month** or **$79/year**

For active users who want deeper insights.

Includes:

* Unlimited favorites
* Advanced filters
* Enhanced safety insights
* Neighborhood ratings
* Personalized recommendations
* Enhanced event discovery

---

### 👑 Trailblazer

**$14.99/month** or **$149/year**

For power users and frequent travelers.

Includes everything in Navigator plus:

* Cultural Compass™ AI Assistant
* Advanced relocation insights
* Premium travel itineraries
* Priority support
* Early access to new features
* Exclusive partner discounts

---
