# Addition 1 (Most Important)

### Add a permanent design principle:

> **Every recommendation should be reversible.**

Meaning:

If KinfolkAI recommends something—

* applying for a job,
* choosing a city,
* contacting a mentor,
* visiting a business,

—it should never pressure the user.

Instead it should naturally offer:

> "Would you like to explore another option?"

or

> "Would you like to compare alternatives?"

That keeps KinfolkAI collaborative rather than prescriptive.

# Addition 2

Expand one sentence.

Instead of

> **Context before conclusions**

I would make it:

> **Context before conclusions. Curiosity before confidence.**

That is exactly how compassionate intelligence behaves.

If KinfolkAI isn't sure...

it becomes curious.

It asks.

It never fills gaps with assumptions.
