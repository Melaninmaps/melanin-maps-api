For **Mapping with Melanin™**, I would define a business in a way that is broad enough to include traditional storefronts, mobile businesses, online businesses, and independent professionals, while still keeping the directory focused and trustworthy.

Here's a definition you could use throughout your platform:

> **A business is any legally operating organization, sole proprietor, nonprofit, independent professional, or entrepreneur that provides products, services, or experiences to the public or to other businesses. Businesses may operate from a physical location, online, from home (where permitted), or as a mobile service.**

### Businesses that would qualify

* Restaurants and food trucks
* Retail stores
* Salons and barbershops
* Healthcare providers
* Attorneys and accountants
* Real estate professionals
* Financial advisors
* Contractors and home services
* Photographers
* Event planners
* Tutors and coaches
* Fitness trainers
* Nonprofit organizations
* Online stores
* Freelancers and consultants
* Mobile businesses (food trucks, mobile detailing, mobile grooming, etc.)

### For your "Time in Business" badge

I recommend defining it as:

> **The length of time the business has continuously operated under the same ownership or registered business entity.**

If ownership changes significantly, the business would start over unless the new owner continues the legal business entity and its operations without interruption.

### For the "Current Location" badge

> **The length of time the business has continuously operated at its current physical address.**

This badge changes if the business relocates, while the **Time in Business** badge remains based on the overall age of the business.

### An additional badge worth considering

Many businesses are newer but have experienced owners. You could recognize that distinction with an optional badge such as:

* 👩🏾‍💼 **Experienced Owner** — Owner has 10+ years of experience in the industry.
* 🎓 **Industry Veteran** — Owner has 20+ years of professional experience.

This lets users distinguish between a newly opened business and an owner who has decades of expertise.

For Mapping with Melanin™, I'd recommend displaying these three pieces of information together:

* 🏢 **In Business:** 18 years
* 📍 **At This Location:** 4 years
* 👩🏾‍💼 **Owner Experience:** 22 years

That combination gives users a much richer understanding of the business than a single "Established" date.
