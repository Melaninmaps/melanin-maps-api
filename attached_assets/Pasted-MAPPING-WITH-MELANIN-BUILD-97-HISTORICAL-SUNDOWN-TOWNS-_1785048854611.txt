TARGETED READ-ONLY AUDIT + IMPLEMENTATION PLAN
NO IMPLEMENTATION UNTIL APPLE APPROVES BUILD 96
AUTHORIZATION PHRASE REMAINS: “Please implement.”

FOUNDER REQUIREMENT

Build 97 and the corresponding next Android testing build should include
historical sundown-town information on the Map.

This is being added because cultural discovery must not be separated from
historical and present-day safety context.

However, the platform must not:

- Suggest that minority communities are inherently violent
- Treat a historical designation as proof of present-day danger
- Label every resident of a community
- Present allegations as verified facts
- Use demographics as a proxy for safety
- Convert one report into a community-wide conclusion
- Tell members a location is guaranteed safe or unsafe

This request has two stages:

PHASE 1 — Read-only source, architecture, legal, content, and performance audit
PHASE 2 — Build 97 implementation proposal

Do not change code, database schemas, map settings, feature flags,
production data, build numbers, or Apple/Android submissions.

2. SOURCE AUDIT
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Investigate authoritative and research-supported sources, including:

A. Tougaloo College / History and Social Justice
   Sundown Towns Database

B. Historical Sundown Towns Linked to US Census Geographies
   national dataset published in Scientific Data in 2025

C. Original supporting records linked by those datasets

D. National Park Service civil-rights and Green Book historical
   materials where relevant

Determine:

1. Whether the source offers a downloadable dataset, API, CSV, spreadsheet,
   geospatial file, or state-by-state data.

2. Licensing or reuse terms.

3. Attribution requirements.

4. Whether automated import or scraping is permitted.

5. Available fields, including:
   - Place name
   - State
   - County
   - Coordinates or Census geography
   - Historical time period
   - Historically excluded population
   - Supporting evidence
   - Confidence or confirmation status
   - Source URL
   - Research comments
   - Current or recovering status, when sourced

6. Whether locations can be reliably geocoded.

7. Whether any source record lacks sufficient evidence for public display.

8. Whether records include:
   - Confirmed
   - Probable
   - Possible
   - Unconfirmed
   - Disputed
   - Recovering or transformed

Do not import data until licensing, attribution, completeness, and
technical compatibility are confirmed.

Do not describe a crowdsourced or probable record as confirmed.

4. REQUIRED BUILD 97 EXPERIENCE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Prepare the smallest stable implementation for:

A. MAP LAYER

A user-selectable toggle:

“Historical Sundown Towns”

Default behavior:

- Off by default unless founder later approves otherwise
- User deliberately enables it
- Visible legend explains that the markers reflect historical research
- Layer can be hidden independently of businesses and heritage places

B. STATE BROWSING

Members should be able to:

- Select a state
- Search a place by name
- View records within the current map area
- Return to the complete national map where technically appropriate

C. MAP MARKERS

Markers must visually distinguish confidence/status where source data
supports it.

Potential statuses:

- Historically confirmed
- Probable or strongly documented
- Possible or under research
- Disputed or incomplete
- Historical record with documented reconciliation work

Do not use identical warning treatment for records with different levels
of evidence.

D. DETAIL TILE

Each record should show, when available:

- Place name
- State and county
- Historical designation
- Historical period
- Community or group historically excluded
- Evidence/confidence status
- Short historical summary
- Source organization
- Source link
- Last source review date
- Disclaimer
- Present-day community information in a separate section

E. REQUIRED DISCLAIMER

Use founder-approved language based on:

“Historical context:
This location appears in historical research concerning sundown towns—
communities that intentionally excluded Black people or other racial,
ethnic, or religious groups through law, intimidation, custom, or
violence. This designation describes documented history and is not, by
itself, a statement about every current resident or a definitive
assessment of present-day conditions.”

“Current community information:
Recent reports reflect submitted or publicly documented experiences and
may be incomplete. Use this information as one source when planning—not
as a guarantee of safety or danger.”

Provide refined copy for founder approval before implementation.

5. RECENTLY REPORTED INFORMATION
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Historical records and recent reports must remain technically and
visually separate.

Create a proposed model for:

A. VERIFIED PUBLIC RECORD

Examples:

- Government civil-rights findings
- Court decisions
- Public agency investigations
- Credible published investigations
- Official policy or reconciliation statements

Required fields:

- Source
- Publication date
- Event date
- Location
- Summary
- Verification status
- Review date
- Expiration from “recent” presentation
- Permanent historical retention decision

B. MWM COMMUNITY-REPORTED EXPERIENCE

Examples:

- Approved discrimination report
- Discriminatory treatment
- Reported racial harassment
- Reported discriminatory policing
- Lodging, business, or travel experience
- Positive intervention or community support

Required controls:

- Anonymous or attributed submission
- Reporter identity never publicly exposed
- Moderation
- Duplicate detection
- Corroboration status
- Date range
- Aggregate display
- Minimum threshold before showing trends
- Ability to report urgent concerns privately
- Correction or appeal
- Expiration and re-evaluation
- No single report creating a community-wide label

C. PRESENTATION

Display separately:

HISTORICAL CONTEXT

and

RECENT COMMUNITY-REPORTED EXPERIENCES

Never combine them into one undifferentiated score.

For Build 97, determine whether current MWM report volume is sufficient
to display recent trends responsibly.

If not, include only the historical layer and preserve recent trend
integration as future state.

6. KINFOLKAI TREATMENT
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

For Build 97, KinfolkAI may explain sourced historical information.

It must not:

- Invent present-day safety conclusions
- Tell a member that a town is currently a sundown town without reliable,
  current evidence
- Infer danger from racial demographics
- State that a route is safe
- State that every resident holds discriminatory views
- Use inflammatory or sensational wording
- Recommend route avoidance by default
- Convert one member report into a universal claim

Potential response pattern:

“This town appears in historical sundown-town research. That designation
describes documented historical exclusion. Current conditions can differ,
and Mapping With Melanin™ does not yet have enough recent verified
community information to characterize the town today.”

KinfolkAI must distinguish:

- Historical research
- Verified current public information
- Aggregated MWM community reports
- Individual experience
- AI inference
- Unknown or insufficient evidence

9. COMMUNITY CHANGE AND RECONCILIATION
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Historical truth must be preserved, but the platform should also be able
to recognize documented present-day work.

Design optional fields for:

- Public acknowledgment of history
- Official apology
- Historical marker or museum
- Reconciliation commission
- Civil-rights programming
- Community-led restorative work
- Increased cultural organizations
- Verified inclusion initiatives
- Public documentation of change

These fields do not erase history.

They provide fuller context and allow communities doing meaningful,
documented work to have that work visible.

Do not accept promotional claims as proof without evidence.
