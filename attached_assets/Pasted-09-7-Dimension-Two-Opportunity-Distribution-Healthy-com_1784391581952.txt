09.7 Dimension Two — Opportunity Distribution
Healthy communities distribute opportunity.
Evaluates:
jobs;
internships;
entrepreneurship;
education;
grants;
scholarships;
networking;
business ownership;
upward mobility.
Concentrated opportunity receives lower scores than broadly distributed opportunity.
09.8 Dimension Three — Community Participation
Participation measures:
event attendance;
volunteerism;
local engagement;
Kinfolk Circles;
civic participation;
neighborhood activity;
recurring involvement.
Participation quality outweighs participation quantity.
09.9 Dimension Four — Resource Accessibility
Measures access to:
food;
healthcare;
transportation;
education;
housing;
financial resources;
emergency assistance;
legal resources;
cultural institutions.
Resources must be accessible—not merely available.
09.10 Dimension Five — Relationship Strength
Healthy communities have strong relationship networks.
Evaluates:
mentorship;
family support;
professional networks;
neighborhood familiarity;
collaboration;
reciprocity;
trust continuity.
Communities with isolated members receive lower scores.
09.11 Dimension Six — Cultural Vitality
Measures:
cultural preservation;
local traditions;
artistic activity;
historical education;
cultural institutions;
diverse representation;
intergenerational knowledge;
local storytelling.
Communities that preserve and celebrate culture score higher.
09.12 Dimension Seven — Resilience & Recovery
Measures how communities recover after disruption.
Examples:
natural disasters;
economic downturns;
business closures;
violence;
discrimination;
public health crises.
Recovery is measured by improvement—not perfection.
09.13 Dimension Eight — Leadership & Stewardship
Healthy communities cultivate leaders.
Measures:
mentorship;
succession planning;
volunteer leadership;
institutional cooperation;
accountability;
civic responsibility.
Leadership concentration receives lower scores than distributed leadership.
09.14 Dimension Nine — Future Readiness
Measures preparedness for future success.
Includes:
youth development;
education;
digital literacy;
entrepreneurship;
environmental planning;
institutional resilience;
long-term planning.
