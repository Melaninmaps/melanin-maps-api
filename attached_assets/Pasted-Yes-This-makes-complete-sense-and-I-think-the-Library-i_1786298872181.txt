# The source stack I want Replit to use

Rather than hand-picking one website and calling that “Kenya information,” I would seed every country with a **source portfolio**.

### Official country / government

Replit should locate each country's:

* national government portal
* ministry of tourism or official tourism board
* ministry of health
* ministry of culture/heritage where available
* national statistics agency
* immigration/visa authority where applicable
* investment/business authority
* national museum/archive where applicable

These should receive the highest authority weight for official facts, although government sources should **not be treated as the only source for politics, human rights, or contested history**.

### Global authoritative sources

These can cover essentially every country and give Replit a much more dependable baseline.

**African Union — country inventory / African regional identity**
[https://au.int/en/member_states/countryprofiles2](https://au.int/en/member_states/countryprofiles2)
The AU maintains the canonical 55-member-state structure that should seed the African country collection. ([African Union][1])

**United Nations Statistics M49 — standardized countries and codes**
[https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/)
Use this for country names, ISO-like identifiers, regional groupings and normalization. ([UNSD][2])

**World Health Organization — country profiles**
[https://www.who.int/countries](https://www.who.int/countries)
WHO maintains profiles across its member countries and is appropriate for vetted health data and national health information. ([World Health Organization][3])

**UNESCO — countries, heritage, culture and education**
[https://www.unesco.org/en/countries](https://www.unesco.org/en/countries)
This should be one of MWM's primary Library sources for cultural heritage, World Heritage, intangible culture, education and historic preservation. ([UNESCO][4])

**World Bank — economics/development**
[https://www.worldbank.org/en/country](https://www.worldbank.org/en/country)
Use for economic conditions, development indicators, reports and research.

**IMF — country economic profiles**
[https://www.imf.org/en/Countries](https://www.imf.org/en/Countries)
Use for macroeconomic indicators, country reports and financial/economic context.

**UNDP — development, governance and community programs**
[https://www.undp.org](https://www.undp.org)
Many countries have dedicated country sites, and these can also surface community organizations, development initiatives and opportunities.

**UN Tourism**
[https://www.unwto.org/](https://www.unwto.org/)
Useful as an international tourism authority, particularly for tourism statistics and tourism policy.

**UNHCR**
[https://www.unhcr.org/](https://www.unhcr.org/)
Use when refugee/displacement information is relevant.

**UNICEF**
[https://www.unicef.org/](https://www.unicef.org/)
Useful for children, families, education and public-health-related country context.

**ILO**
[https://www.ilo.org/](https://www.ilo.org/)
Employment, labor conditions, workforce and labor rights.

**FAO**
[https://www.fao.org/](https://www.fao.org/)
Agriculture, food systems and food security.

# That gives you an Authority Ladder

I would have Replit implement this conceptually:

| Subject                           | Strongest authority                                                             |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Medical                           | WHO, government health agencies, recognized medical institutions                |
| Legal                             | Government statutes/courts/bar associations; clear no-legal-advice boundary     |
| Current travel entry requirements | Government immigration/embassy                                                  |
| Safety advisory                   | Government/official sources + verified community intelligence clearly separated |
| Economy                           | World Bank, IMF, national statistical agencies                                  |
| History                           | UNESCO, archives, universities, museums, reputable historians                   |
| News                              | Multiple reputable news organizations                                           |
| Tourism                           | Official tourism authority + community traveler intelligence                    |
| Restaurant vibe                   | Community                                                                       |
| Hair/braiding experience          | Community + specialists                                                         |
| Entertainment                     | Local creators, venues, businesses, reputable entertainment sources             |
| Cultural practice                 | Cultural institutions + community voices                                        |
| Local slang                       | Community                                                                       |
| “Where should I eat?”             | MWM business intelligence + community                                           |
| “What does this medicine do?”     | Medical authority, not community popularity                                     |

That is what your sentence **“is this something that needs reputable medical/legal links or can community members jump in as authority?”** should become technically.

## I would add three things you did not explicitly mention

**Languages.** Kenya should connect to Swahili and other relevant languages; Haiti to Haitian Creole; Brazil to Portuguese; etc. Language becomes another travel/community bridge.

**Diaspora-at-home connections.** Kenya should not only mean *travel to Kenya*. It should connect to **Kenyan-owned restaurants in Philadelphia, Kenyan cultural organizations in DC, Kenyan festivals, creators, churches/community groups, and local businesses.** That allows the member to explore a culture before ever buying a plane ticket.

**“From here / connected to here / traveling here.”** A member might follow Kenya because it is their heritage, because their spouse is Kenyan, because they're planning travel, because they're studying it, or simply because they're curious. Kinfolk should not infer identity from following a country.

And that is the flywheel:

**Learn about Kenya → save Kenya → discover Kenyan food locally → follow a Kenyan Cultural Ambassador → watch their Nairobi video → save a Nairobi business → build a trip → receive relevant travel/safety information → travel → check in → contribute a video → enrich the Kenya Library for the next person.**

That is much more than a Library tab. **It is the knowledge layer connecting the rest of Mapping With Melanin.**
