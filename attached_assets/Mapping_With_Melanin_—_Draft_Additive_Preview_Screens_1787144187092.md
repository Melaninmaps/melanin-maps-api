# Mapping With Melanin — Draft Additive Preview Screens

**Status:** Owner-review draft only. No preview source, route, design, animation, deployment, or waitlist behavior has been changed.

## Purpose and placement

These two screens complete the approved 8/5 preview story without changing the protected five-card baseline. They should appear as **two additional, full-width phone-frame story panels** after the existing Cultural Ambassador panel and before the existing waitlist form. They are additive rather than replacements: the five existing cards, their copy, sequence, styling, animations, CTAs, and route must remain exactly as approved.[1]

Each new panel uses the existing dark-brown background, polished gold lines, cream typography, soft gold glow, phone frame, three dots, card-label treatment, and **3.5-second auto-cycle**. The visitor watches the system work; they do not need to type into a demo. The experience should feel like the current preview, not like a new product design.

> **Story completion:** The existing preview shows how community activity improves discovery, culture, business demand, and creator opportunities. These two panels show the missing link: Kinfolk gives a member useful guidance, and the useful, cited part of that work becomes easier for the next member to find in the Living Library.

| Additive panel | Existing story gap it closes | Visitor promise |
|---|---|---|
| **Living Library** | The preview does not currently show that community-relevant knowledge is saved, cited, and made easier to find over time. | “What we learn does not disappear after one conversation.” |
| **Kinfolk AI Journey** | The preview does not currently show Kinfolk helping an everyday member with a respectful response and an optional next step. | “Get guidance that understands the question and lets you choose what happens next.” |

**Headline:** **What We Learn, We Keep**  
**Supporting copy:** *A living library of community knowledge — helpful today, easier to find tomorrow.*

### Living Library content and safety guardrails

The demo must show **source markers** but must not invent clinical facts, personal diagnoses, or individual provider endorsements. The words “updated for you” refer to presentation and discovery context, not medical personalization. The finished product must retain the established diaspora-first research behavior, beginning with a Black-women lens whenever relevant, while presenting claims with appropriate citations and source context.

The preselected-topic set shown elsewhere in the Living Library should remain aligned with the platform’s foundational topics, including **Housing, Education, Trades, Health, History, and Business**. Each uses its own polished gold-outline topic icon, never a generic repeated feather.

**Supporting copy:** *Kinfolk gives clear guidance, understands what you may need next, and always lets you choose.*  
**CTA:** `This is me →`

The panel uses a **polished gold-outline conversation bubble with a small compass-point detail** as the Kinfolk marker. The gold feather remains a brand-level accent only; it is not the panel’s primary icon.

### Slide 7-A — Kinfolk understands the question

**Phone header:** `Kinfolk AI`  
**Eyebrow:** `Guidance that meets you where you are`

| Phone element | Exact copy |
|---|---|

| Kinfolk response title | `Let’s take this one step at a time.` |
| Kinfolk response | `Hair changes can have different causes. I can help you find a clear starting point, questions to consider, and community-informed options without overwhelming you.` |

### Kinfolk language and behavior guardrails

Kinfolk must conduct relevant web research with the permanent diaspora-first lens. It should recognize intent, provide useful information in a respectful and culturally aware tone, and present local professional or business connections as an **optional** next step. It must use the term **Community Intelligence** or **Community-Sourced** context rather than “Community Safety.” It must not claim diagnosis, guarantee an outcome, rank a professional as medically superior, or surface an unavailable or distant business as though it were local.

## Motion, interaction, and scope lock

| Requirement | Implementation direction |
|---|---|
| **Cycle** | Use the existing `3500ms` cycle and three-dot pattern. Both new panels must cycle independently, exactly as the five existing cards do. |
| **Visual continuity** | Reuse existing dark brown, gold, cream, muted text, phone frame, gold glow, soft fade-up, and slide-in motion. No redesign of the existing page. |
| **Visitor interaction** | The screen itself is a watch-only demo. Only the existing `This is me →` CTA is interactive; it selects the persona and scrolls to the existing waitlist. |
| **Waitlist integration** | Add only the two new values, `library` and `kinfolk`, to the existing selection-label map. Preserve the current endpoint, form fields, success state, UTM forwarding, and share behavior. |
| **Source scope** | Change only the explicit approved-preview artifact and the minimal existing selection-label code required for the two new panel IDs. Do not touch the React wrapper, preview route, five original cards, mobile artifact, API, workflow, deployment settings, or unrelated files. |
| **Terminology** | New text must use **Community Intelligence** and **Community-Sourced** language. Do not retrofit wording in the protected existing five-card baseline through this additive-screen request. Any legacy copy correction remains a separate, explicitly approved copy-only task. |

## Acceptance criteria for later implementation

The implementation is ready for owner approval only when the two panels appear below the current Cultural Ambassador panel, above the untouched waitlist; each contains exactly three non-interactive demonstrations on the 3.5-second cycle; all visitor-facing copy above is used without unsupported health or local-business claims; topic icons are polished gold-outline icons; and selecting either CTA passes `library` or `kinfolk` through the existing waitlist choice behavior.

No update should proceed without preserving the approved five-card artifact’s visual identity and without a scope-lock review confirming that only the named preview artifact and minimal selection map changed.
