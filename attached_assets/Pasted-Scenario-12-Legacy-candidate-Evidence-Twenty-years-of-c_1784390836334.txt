07.59 Final Governing Rule
The Cultural Influence Engine™ exists to ensure that Mapping With Melanin™ recognizes influence that is culturally grounded, community-connected, useful, reciprocal, and responsibly stewarded.
It must make room for people whose influence is deep but not famous.

It must recognize local trust, lived relationship, historical knowledge, mentorship, cultural preservation, and measurable community benefit.

It must prevent follower count, commercial reach, or visibility from becoming substitutes for authenticity.

It must protect culture from extraction without freezing culture in place.

It must permit disagreement, correction, growth, and new forms of cultural expression.

It must never claim that an algorithm can decide who is culturally authentic as a person.

It may only evaluate the evidence surrounding cultural work, conduct, relationship, impact, reciprocity, and stewardship.

Its final governing rule is:

Recognize influence by what it helps people understand, preserve, support, create, and pass forward—not merely by how many people were made to look.
