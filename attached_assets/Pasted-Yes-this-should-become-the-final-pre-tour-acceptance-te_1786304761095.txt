### The route-safety piece needs one correction

I would not have Kinfolk automatically say:

> “These are sundown towns you should avoid.”

Historical sundown-town designation alone is **not proof of current danger**.

Kinfolk should instead distinguish:

**Historical context**
“This location has documented sundown-town history.”

**Recent community information**
“MWM members have reported recent concerns here.”

**Current credible safety information**
“There is current information that may affect this stop/route.”

Then it may make a practical suggestion:

> “There is documented historical context along this route and recent community concerns in this area. I found an alternate stop 8 miles farther ahead that better matches your preferences. Want me to route you there instead?”

That's stronger and more defensible than simply creating a blacklist.
