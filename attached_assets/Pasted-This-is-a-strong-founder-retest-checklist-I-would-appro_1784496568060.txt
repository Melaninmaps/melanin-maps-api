This is a strong founder-retest checklist. I would approve it after four important corrections.

### 1. Internal uploads are required for testing

Change:

> Do NOT submit to App Store or Google Play until all items pass.

To:

> **Do not submit for public App Review, production release, or wider tester distribution until all required items pass. Internal TestFlight and Google Play internal-track uploads are permitted and required for founder testing.**

Otherwise, you would not be able to install and test the exact store binaries.

### 2. Remove “if implemented” from pass/fail requirements

Weather and “Take me there” cannot be mandatory PASS items while also saying “if implemented.” Put them into one of these categories:

* **Required for Community Beta 3**
* **Known limitation accepted for this beta**
* **Future capability**

Based on your expectations, I would require Replit to complete a KinfolkAI capability audit before deciding whether weather and contextual navigation block release.

### 3. Verify build numbers before triggering EAS

Build 90 / Android 64 may be correct, but Replit should verify them against:

* Expo EAS build history
* App Store Connect
* Google Play Console

Do not rely only on `app.json`, especially with `autoIncrement` enabled.

### 4. Add the test-business and timeout defects

These were discovered during your founder testing and should not disappear from the checklist:

#### TEST DATA

* [ ] Test businesses are labeled clearly in admin/testing views.
* [ ] Test businesses do not appear as genuine public or verified businesses.
* [ ] Test records are excluded from public recommendations, analytics, promotions, ratings and Hidden Gem eligibility.
* [ ] Test records created during founder testing are documented for cleanup.

#### ERROR AND TIMEOUT HANDLING

* [ ] Slow requests produce a clear loading state.
* [ ] Every loading indicator ends in success, retry or failure.
* [ ] Network timeout does not freeze the screen.
* [ ] Repeated taps do not create duplicate records.
* [ ] The user remains signed in after a timeout or server error.
