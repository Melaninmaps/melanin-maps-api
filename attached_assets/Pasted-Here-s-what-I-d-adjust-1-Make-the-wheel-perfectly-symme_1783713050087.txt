Here's what I'd adjust.
1. Make the wheel perfectly symmetrical
Right now, the labels are visually "pulling" the circle in different directions.
I would position them like the numbers on a clock:
                 Community

    Stronger                  Discovery
   Communities

 Business Growth      Recommendations
Everything should sit the same distance from the circle.
2. Move "Business Growth" out slightly
It's tucked inside the wheel while "Discovery" and "Recommendations" are outside.
I'd move Business Growth about 20–30 pixels farther from the arrow so all four labels feel equally weighted.
3. Move "Stronger Communities" closer to the arrow
At the moment it feels detached from the flow.
I'd shift it:
slightly to the right
slightly down
so it clearly reads as the next stage in the cycle.
4. Align the text with the direction of travel
One subtle thing I noticed:
Your eye naturally follows the arrows clockwise, but your labels don't always appear exactly where the arrow is taking you.
I'd let each arrow "point" directly to the next label.
Like this:
Community
      ↘
        Discovery
             ↘
          Recommendations
                ↘
         Business Growth
               ↘
      Stronger Communities
              ↺
That makes the motion almost effortless.
5. I would also make one wording change.
Instead of
Stronger Communities
I'd seriously consider
Stronger Community
Why?
Because the whole deck has been about building one community wherever someone goes.
Singular feels more like a movement.
It also balances nicely with:
Community
Discovery
Recommendations
Business Growth
Stronger Community
The symmetry is satisfying.
One more design thought
I would slightly reduce the size of the words.
Right now every stage has equal visual weight.
But they're not equally important.
I'd make:
Community — largest (it's the beginning)
Business Growth — second largest (it's the value created)
Discovery / Recommendations / Stronger Community — just a touch smaller.
That subtly tells investors where to focus without them realizing it.
