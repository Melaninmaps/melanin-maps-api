I actually think this is one of the most important stories Mapping With Melanin™ can tell—but it requires care.

The part I would be careful about is **not implying that one group "owns" culture or that all mainstream adoption is exploitative**. There are many examples where creators from historically marginalized communities have shaped trends and not received equal recognition or economic benefit, but it's best to frame your message around **recognition, attribution, and community support**, not opposition. That keeps the message inclusive, accurate, and difficult to dismiss.

I also think there's a subtle but powerful shift that aligns even more with your mission.

Instead of saying:

> "We built a platform for influencers."

Say:

> **"We built a platform for people whose influence already exists."**

That's a very different message.

You're not trying to manufacture influence.

You're helping people discover the people already creating it.
