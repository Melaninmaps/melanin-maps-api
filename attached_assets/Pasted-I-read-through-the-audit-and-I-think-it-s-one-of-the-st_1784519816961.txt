## 2. The audit actually found something I wanted fixed.

This is important.

Currently every business owner automatically receives business-owner context on every conversation.

That means someone asking:

> "Help me plan Saturday."

may unintentionally get business advice because KinfolkAI is guessing which context to use. 

Your instinct was right.

Instead of guessing, KinfolkAI should politely ask:

> "Are you asking about this for yourself or for your business?"

That feels much more human.

I would absolutely implement that.

## 3. They confirmed something we discussed earlier.

The weather examples...

Those cannot actually exist yet.

Not because they're bad ideas.

Because KinfolkAI literally doesn't receive personal event data or business event data yet.  

That means examples like:

> "You have a block party Saturday..."

or

> "Your picnic is Saturday..."

must wait until an events pipeline exists.

That is the correct engineering decision.

# 4. The biggest improvement I'd make

I would add one principle before implementation.

Right now the audit talks about:

> Personal

and

> Business

I think KinfolkAI should think in something broader:

## Active Context

Not just roles.

Context.

Examples:

* Personal
* Business
* Travel
* Relocation
* Event Planning
* Career
* Family
* Learning
* Wellness

Those contexts can overlap.

Someone asking:

> "Help me budget"

might be:

* budgeting for college
* budgeting for vacation
* budgeting for payroll
* budgeting for groceries

KinfolkAI shouldn't assume.

It should identify context first.

That becomes part of your Compassionate Intelligence philosophy.

# 5. One thing I would definitely add

I would add this rule to the Constitution.

> **Context before conclusions.**

KinfolkAI should determine what part of the user's life they're asking about before deciding how to help.

That one sentence will influence hundreds of future features.

# 8. One more addition I'd ask Replit for

I would add one final prompt rule.

I think this belongs permanently in KinfolkAI.

> **If more than one interpretation of the user's request is reasonable, never assume. Clarify first.**

Examples:

> "Help me budget."

↓

> "Are you thinking about your household budget, your business budget, or something else?"

---

> "Help me plan Saturday."

↓

> "Is this for yourself, your family, or your business?"

---

> "How should I prepare?"

↓

> "What are you preparing for?"

This sounds simple, but it dramatically increases trust because KinfolkAI stops making silent assumptions.
