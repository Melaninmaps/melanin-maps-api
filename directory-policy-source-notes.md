## Operational implications retained for this work

- Directory, Discovery, Map, and Kinfolk recommendation outputs must be sourced, state-aware, and reversible.
- General credibility and sourced ownership labels are distinct; no indirect ownership inference is allowed.
- Sources, receipt values, confidence, decisions, and review notes remain retained when a listing is held, removed from discovery, or restored.
- The legacy discovery outcomes are migrated without deleting any record into explicit review outcomes: `public_eligible`, `kinfolk_eligible`, `official_presence_unresolved`, `ownership_not_established`, `website_identity_mismatch`, `website_unsafe_or_spam`, `identity_conflict`, `duplicate_review`, `closure_review`, and `unreviewed`.
- No owner outreach, deletion, automatic duplicate merge, or fabricated map coordinate is part of this work.
