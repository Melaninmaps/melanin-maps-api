Welcome to Mapping With Melanin™ v1.0 — our first public release!

We're thrilled to launch the app that makes discovering and supporting Black-owned businesses easier than ever.

• Discover — Search and filter thousands of verified Black-owned businesses across the country
• Map — Full-screen interactive map with business pins and neighborhood safety overlays
• KinfolkAI™ — Your personalized AI travel guide for culturally informed recommendations
• Safety Surveys — Community-powered neighborhood safety reporting and real-time overlays
• Business Profiles — Rich profiles with photos, hours, reviews, and one-tap actions
• Favorites — Save your favorite places for quick offline access
• Membership — Join and earn points for community contributions
• Referrals — Invite friends and earn rewards via our early access waitlist
• Dark mode support

Thank you to our founding community for making this possible. Your support fuels the movement. Map Your Life. Connect Deeper.™
