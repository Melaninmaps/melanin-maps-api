# Screenshot Guide — Mapping With Melanin™ v1.0

Capture these 8 scenes in the iOS Simulator (iPhone 15 Pro, 6.7") and Android Emulator (Pixel 8 Pro).
Export at 1290×2796 (iPhone) and 1440×3120 (Android) — PNG format, no device frame for store listings.

---

## Scene 1 — Onboarding Welcome
**Screen:** `/onboarding` — first slide
**What to show:** Full welcome screen with the "Map Your Life. Connect Deeper.™" tagline and brand imagery
**Suggested caption:** "Built for the culture. Discover your world."

## Scene 2 — Home / Discover
**Screen:** Home tab with businesses loaded
**What to show:** Featured businesses cards, category filters (All, Food, Beauty, etc.), search bar active
**Suggested caption:** "Find Black-owned businesses near you — and everywhere you travel."

## Scene 3 — Business Profile
**Screen:** A restaurant or salon business detail page
**What to show:** Hero image, business name, category, rating, verified badge, Call/Save/Share buttons, and the map embed
**Suggested caption:** "Verified listings with real community ratings."

## Scene 4 — Map View
**Screen:** Map tab with business pins visible
**What to show:** Full-screen map with colored pins, neighborhood safety overlay active, pin callout open on a business
**Suggested caption:** "See safety ratings and businesses on an interactive map."

## Scene 5 — KinfolkAI™ Chat
**Screen:** Travel / KinfolkAI chat screen
**What to show:** A sample conversation asking "Plan a weekend in Atlanta for a Black traveler" with an AI response showing neighborhood recommendations and businesses
**Suggested caption:** "KinfolkAI™ plans your trip with community intelligence."

## Scene 6 — Safety Survey
**Screen:** Safety survey submission or results screen
**What to show:** Neighborhood safety sliders/ratings filled in, or a results card showing a neighborhood's community safety score
**Suggested caption:** "Real safety intel from people who look like you."

## Scene 7 — Referral & Waitlist
**Screen:** Referral section on profile or home
**What to show:** User's referral code, waitlist position badge, share button, referral count
**Suggested caption:** "Invite friends. Move up. Get early access sooner."

## Scene 8 — Profile & Membership
**Screen:** Profile tab showing saved places, membership tier, and settings
**What to show:** User avatar, name, membership badge (Founding Member or Community), saved businesses list
**Suggested caption:** "Your community hub — saves, membership, and more."

---

## Pro tips
- Use clean seed data — a business called "The Melanin Kitchen" in Atlanta with 4.8★ rating photographs well
- Ensure dark mode screenshots are included alongside light mode for the App Store preview gallery
- The Map scene is highest-impact — use a city with dense pins (Atlanta, DC, Houston)
- Submit 3 iPhone sizes: 6.7", 6.5", 5.5" for App Store; Google Play requires at least 2 screenshots
