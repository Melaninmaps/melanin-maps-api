# Screenshot Guide — Mapping With Melanin™ v1.0

Capture these 8 scenes in the iOS Simulator (iPhone 15 Pro Max, 6.7") and Android Emulator (Pixel 8 Pro) for store listings. Export at 1290×2796 (iPhone) and 1440×3120 (Android) — PNG format, no device frame for store listings.

Apple App Store requires at least 3 screenshots; Google Play requires at least 2. Aim for all 8 for maximum coverage.

---

## Scene 1 — Onboarding Welcome
**Screen path:** `/onboarding` (Launch app fresh, clear AsyncStorage or use a new simulator)
**What to show:** Full welcome screen with the Mapping With Melanin logo, hero imagery, "Map Your Life. Connect Deeper.™" tagline, and the "Get Started" CTA button.
**Suggested caption:** "Your guide to Black excellence — everywhere you go."

**Tips:** Make sure the status bar reads a clean time (e.g. 9:41 AM). Use light mode for this shot.

---

## Scene 2 — Home / Discover Feed
**Screen path:** Main app → Discover tab
**What to show:** The Discover screen with the search bar active, category filter pills visible (Restaurants, Salons, etc.), and at least 4–6 business cards visible with photos, names, and confidence scores.
**Suggested caption:** "Find Black-owned businesses near you — and everywhere you travel."

**Tips:** Scroll to a city with dense listings (e.g. Atlanta or Chicago) to populate the feed. Ensure at least one "Verified" badge is visible.

---

## Scene 3 — Business Profile
**Screen path:** Discover → tap a restaurant business card → Business Profile screen
**What to show:** A business profile with a hero photo, business name, category badge, star rating, verified badge, safety stats, and the call/share/save action buttons visible.
**Suggested caption:** "Verified listings with real community ratings."

**Tips:** Choose a business with a high-quality hero image and strong rating (4.5+). Ensure the Save (bookmark) button and Call button are both in frame.

---

## Scene 4 — Map View
**Screen path:** Main app → Map tab
**What to show:** Full-screen map centered on a city (Atlanta, NYC, or Chicago) with multiple colored business pins clustered and the neighborhood safety overlay visible. Pin callout open on a business or a bottom sheet preview partially visible.
**Suggested caption:** "See safety ratings and businesses on an interactive map."

**Tips:** Enable the safety overlay before screenshotting. Tap a cluster to expand it slightly so individual pins are distinguishable.

---

## Scene 5 — KinfolkAI™ Chat
**Screen path:** Main app → Travel / KinfolkAI tab → active chat session
**What to show:** An ongoing KinfolkAI™ conversation. Show a user message like "Plan a soul food weekend in Harlem" and an AI response with a structured itinerary, including business name callouts.
**Suggested caption:** "KinfolkAI™ plans your trip with community intelligence."

**Tips:** Stage a realistic multi-turn conversation before screenshotting. Use light or dark mode — dark mode looks especially striking here.

---

## Scene 6 — Safety Survey
**Screen path:** Main app → Safety tab → tap a neighborhood → Safety Survey form
**What to show:** The safety survey submission screen with a neighborhood name, the rating sliders or option selectors partially filled in, or a results card showing a neighborhood's community safety score.
**Suggested caption:** "Real safety intel from people who look like you."

**Tips:** Fill in several survey fields before capturing so the form looks active and in use. Show a recognizable neighborhood name.

---

## Scene 7 — Referral & Waitlist
**Screen path:** Profile tab → Referrals or Membership → Referral screen
**What to show:** The referral screen with the user's unique referral link, points balance, waitlist position badge, and the Share button prominent.
**Suggested caption:** "Invite your people — earn points every time they join."

**Tips:** Make sure the points balance shows a non-zero number (stage data if needed). The Share button should be clearly tappable.

---

## Scene 8 — Profile & Membership
**Screen path:** Profile tab → Membership screen (or Profile overview)
**What to show:** User avatar, name, membership badge (Founding Member or Community), points total, and saved businesses count.
**Suggested caption:** "Join the movement — unlock perks and support the community."

**Tips:** Use a profile with a photo, a visible membership badge, and a non-zero saved businesses count. Dark mode works well here.

---

## Export Checklist

| Platform | Required size | Format |
|---|---|---|
| App Store (iPhone 6.7") | 1290 × 2796 px | PNG or JPEG |
| App Store (iPad 12.9") | 2048 × 2732 px | PNG or JPEG (optional but recommended) |
| Google Play | 1080 × 1920 px min | PNG or JPEG |

- Export from Xcode Simulator: **File → New Screen Shot** (saves to Desktop)
- Export from Android Emulator: camera icon in the emulator toolbar
- Do not add device frames in the simulator — use a tool like Previewed.app or AppMockup to add them in post if desired for marketing materials
- Use clean seed data — a business called "The Melanin Kitchen" in Atlanta with 4.8★ rating photographs well
- Ensure dark mode screenshots are included alongside light mode for the App Store preview gallery
- Submit 3 iPhone sizes: 6.7", 6.5", 5.5" for App Store; Google Play requires at least 2 screenshots
