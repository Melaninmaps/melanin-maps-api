Mapping With Melanin™ — Community Discovery & Travel

Discover Black-owned businesses, get real community safety insights, and explore every city with confidence. Mapping With Melanin™ is the community-powered platform built by and for the culture.

DISCOVER BLACK-OWNED BUSINESSES
Find verified Black-owned restaurants, salons, boutiques, wellness centers, law firms, and more — near you or anywhere you're traveling. Every listing is community-verified with a confidence score so you always know what you're walking into.

COMMUNITY SAFETY INTELLIGENCE
Before you visit a neighborhood, see real safety ratings from people who look like you. Community members share their experiences so you can travel smarter, avoid unwelcoming spaces, and find places where you'll truly feel at home.

PLAN YOUR JOURNEY WITH KINFOLK AI™
Our AI travel assistant is trained on community knowledge. Ask KinfolkAI™ to build you a weekend itinerary, find Black-friendly neighborhoods in a new city, or suggest the best spots for a business dinner — and get personalized recommendations that actually reflect your experience.

SAFETY SURVEYS
Submit and read neighborhood safety surveys from community members across the country. Ratings cover everything from welcoming atmosphere and police presence to infrastructure and general safety — real intel from real people.

SAVE & ORGANIZE YOUR FAVORITES
Build your personal list of go-to spots and travel destinations. Access saved businesses instantly, even offline.

EVENTS & COMMUNITY
Stay connected with community events, local meetups, and cultural gatherings happening in your city and beyond.

FOR BUSINESS OWNERS
List your Black-owned business and connect with a community that wants to find you. Verification badges build trust and drive traffic to your door.
