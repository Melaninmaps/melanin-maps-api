# KinfolkAI Guided Demo — One Problem. Your Whole Day.

**Preview:** `preview-one-problem-whole-day.html`
**Purpose:** a 60–90 second founder-review demonstration of Kinfolk carrying one ordinary problem through the practical decisions it creates.

> **Core line:** “Life doesn’t happen one question at a time. Neither should your AI.”

## 75-second guided run of show

| Time | Viewer action | What they see | Point to make |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–10 sec | Open the page and read the opening prompt. | A member in the car asks: “My check-engine light came on, and I still need to get to work.” | This is an everyday life problem, not a feature demonstration. |
| 10–22 sec | Select **Hold the phone**, then tap the visibly member-sent **Review the dashboard photo with Kinfolk** attachment and **It’s flashing**. | The dashboard-photo state receives Kinfolk’s safe follow-up question and then reveals a cautious response. | Kinfolk asks what changes the decision. It does **not** diagnose the vehicle or say it is safe to drive. |
| 22–34 sec | Advance with **What might this cost?** | The conversation becomes an estimate explainer: diagnostic scan, repair path, approval questions. | A light does not produce a repair price. Kinfolk helps someone understand the questions to ask before agreeing to work. |
| 34–47 sec | Advance with **Find a mechanic I can trust** and choose a fictional preview shop. | Explicitly fictional, member-approved preview memory supplies the Black-owned-business preference, 2018 Honda Civic, and saved Home/Work places without a repeated question. | The question moves naturally from cost to trusted support. The shop cards are fictional records; ownership verification and Honda-service evidence appear only as fields that a live directory must substantiate. |
| 47–60 sec | Advance with **Use the selected shop & plan the next step**. | The selected shop, saved Work place, and an explicitly fictional authorized 9:00 AM calendar call create a proactive transit/rideshare handoff. | Kinfolk recognizes the next practical decision rather than waiting for the member to ask. It makes no arrival, fare, or route promise without a live integration. |
| 60–75 sec | Advance with **Help me handle the rest** and press **Copy draft**. | A concise work-update draft and breakfast discovery follow the car, repair, and commute context. | Kinfolk keeps the person moving through the day—not only the car problem. |

## Capability boundary report

| Category | What is shown | Status in this preview |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Connected conversation | One conversation carries the member’s earlier context into later questions. | **Product direction / source-informed**. |
| Member-approved memory and calendar context | Saved Home/Work places, Black-owned-business preference, a 2018 Honda Civic, and a 9:00 AM call appear in scenes 3–4. | **Clearly fictional preview data**; it represents the experience only after a member approves memory/calendar access. |
| Dashboard-photo interpretation | The warning-light image is reviewed and a follow-up is asked. | **Interactive simulation**; no actual photo upload, image diagnosis, or automotive advice service runs. |
| Repair estimate support | Diagnostic uncertainty and questions to ask are explained. | **Interactive simulation**; no current repair-price feed or vehicle diagnosis is used. |
| Preferred mechanic discovery | A map and verified-profile format retain ownership and vehicle preferences. | **Interactive simulation**; no named business, ownership status, availability, review, or appointment is invented. |
| Route, transit, and fare comparison | The repair stop becomes the starting point for a work commute. | **Interactive simulation**; no live Maps, transit, route, or fare data is requested. |
| Message draft and breakfast handoff | A draft can be copied locally and breakfast discovery follows the selected location. | **Interactive simulation**; nothing is sent, booked, or returned from a live provider. |

## Integration requirements for a live version

1. **Image / vehicle guidance:** a safety-reviewed image and diagnostic-information workflow that can provide non-diagnostic, source-backed guidance and escalate appropriately.
2. **Verified business directory:** confirmed business records, ownership designations, service specialties, hours, contact information, and user-selected location context.
3. **Memory and calendar:** member controls for saved preferences, vehicle information, places, retention, and granular calendar authorization; Kinfolk should use only data the member has approved.
4. **Maps and transit:** a routing provider with transparent origin/destination consent, transit availability, travel-time and fare handling, and clear data freshness.
5. **Messaging:** a member-approved copy / send handoff to an email or SMS integration; drafts must never send automatically.
6. **Safety rules:** clear medical, legal, financial, emergency, and mechanical-issue boundaries; an emergency path must never rely on generated advice.

## Interaction safeguards in the preview

- All actions are local to the browser session.
- No dashboard image is uploaded or retained.
- No diagnosis, pricing, business result, verification claim, availability, route, fare, message, appointment, or booking is real.
- The directory, map, route, and message-draft surfaces say when they are illustrative.
- Reduced-motion support, a skip link, visible keyboard focus, semantic headings, and reader-controlled story progression are included.
