## Safety-specific boundaries

### Member consent

- A member chooses what to share, with whom, for how long, and whether a check-in has a missed-check-in escalation path.
- Temporary location sharing is separate from sharing meeting details.
- The preview creates neither setting and does not contact anyone.

### Community reports

- A report draft is not a submission.
- The live product must give the member a clear privacy notice, a review of what will be shared, moderation expectations, and technical safeguards.
- The preview does **not** promise absolute anonymity.

### Alerts and trusted contacts

- A **credible serious-threat** notice may reach a separately opted-in trusted contact only as a **general-area event**; it must not reveal a member’s precise location.
- Routine police or ICE presence notifications remain personal to the member’s configured radius and do **not** automatically notify trusted contacts.
- A serious-threat preference, routine local notification, and missed check-in are three distinct consent workflows.

## Integration requirements before a live launch

1. **Approved memory and preferences:** granular consent, visibility, retention, editing, and deletion controls for member context.
2. **Verified context:** sourced business/place records, ownership and service evidence, employer facts, community-report attribution, and data freshness.
3. **Mobility:** a routing/transit provider that returns real routes, timing, fares, availability, and accessibility information only with member-selected locations.
4. **Trusted check-ins:** explicit recipient, scope, duration, channel, and escalation permissions; never send by default.
5. **Safety reports:** privacy review, moderation, audit trail, technical safeguards, clear limitations, and appropriate emergency escalation paths.
6. **Messaging and calendar:** member-approved integrations; drafts and event data must never be sent or accessed automatically.

## Prototype safeguards

- All five pages are isolated static founder-review previews.
- Preview content is bounded at the experience level. The supplied Mapping With Melanin community signals are concept content, not live analytics, a rating, verification, or independently measured claim; all other preview data remains non-live until a supported source is connected.
- No preview changes production behavior, live safety settings, memberships, authentication, mobile builds, or directory records.
