## The Two Ambassador Roles (finalized split)

### Cultural Ambassadors
Specialized role focused on **preserving and sharing community knowledge**.
- "Let me teach you about my community."
- Historians, educators, artists, neighborhood leaders, librarians, business owners, long-term residents, nonprofit leaders
- Multiple reviewers required per registry change — no single voice of a community
- Defined in detail in `kinfolk-constitution-decisions.md`

### Journey Ambassadors (new role)
People **a few chapters ahead** who walk alongside others through meaningful life chapters.
- "Let me walk with you through something I've already experienced."
- Not experts. Not influencers. People who have lived through what someone else is beginning.
- A sophomore saying: "I remember being terrified to leave home. Here's what my first semester at Cheyney was actually like."
- A bakery owner saying: "Here's what I wish someone had told me six months before I opened."
- A new mom saying: "You're probably exhausted. Let me show you the stroller-friendly places in my city."

**Why the distinction matters:** Two equally valuable forms of contribution:
- Cultural Ambassadors preserve community identity and knowledge over time
- Journey Ambassadors accompany individuals through personal milestones in real time

## Kinfolk as Opportunity AI

Kinfolk is not recommendation AI. Not content AI.

It is **Opportunity AI**.

A creator's morning briefing:

**Good Morning. Here's what your community needs today.**

> 📈 "Best museums in Cabo." 132 searches this week. Would you like to create a guide?
> 📈 "Traveling with toddlers." 84 searches. Based on your recent posts about becoming a mom, we think you're uniquely positioned to answer this.
> 📈 "Accessible beaches." 41 searches. Several families following your journey are planning vacations.

The creator isn't guessing. They're responding to real community needs.

Kinfolk also fills **knowledge gaps**:
> "We don't yet have someone covering accessible travel in Cabo. Would you be interested?"

And matches lived experience to community need:
> "Parents keep asking where to eat if their child has autism. We think you're uniquely positioned to help."

Not because they're famous. Because they have lived experience.

## The Trust Model (locked)

Kinfolk **never** says: "Make this video."

Instead:
- "Your community has been asking..."
- "People beginning this journey often look for..."
- "No pressure, but we think your experience could really help someone."
- "Would you like to create something? No thanks is always okay."

Kinfolk always explains **why** it is making a suggestion. Creators should feel like they're collaborating with Kinfolk to serve a community — not being manipulated by an invisible algorithm.

**Why:** Transparency is the trust foundation. Every suggestion has one purpose: helping someone else on their journey — not generating another click.

## Creator Profile as a Timeline

A creator's profile grows with them — not against them. TikTok punishes life changes (algorithm penalizes content-type shifts). MWM celebrates them.

Kinfolk says:
> "Congratulations. Would you like to begin sharing your Parenting Journey as well?"

Not Replace. Add.

Example timeline: ✈️ Travel → 🎓 Helped HBCU students → 💍 Wedding traditions → 👶 Parenting realities → 🧁 Opened a bakery → 🤝 Mentored entrepreneurs

The creator hasn't changed audiences — they've invited the community to grow alongside them.

## Four Creators, Same City — Different Stories

Same destination. Completely different Kinfolk guidance because the need is different.

Creator A (Luxury travel): "Your audience loves boutique hotels and museums."
Creator B (Young family): "Parents are asking about stroller-friendly attractions, splash pads, quiet restaurants, and nap-friendly itineraries."
Creator C (Black history): "Several users are searching for Cabo beyond the resorts. Consider highlighting Afro-Mexican history, local artisans, museums, and community-owned businesses."
Creator D (Food): "People following you have recently searched for seafood, family-owned restaurants, and cooking classes."

Same Cabo. Different stories. This is how AI shines — not writing scripts, but finding gaps.
