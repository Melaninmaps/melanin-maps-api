5. Enterprise Accountability — dashboard shows aggregate trends, not individual user data
6. Partnership Is Earned — no permanent status; consequence ladder exists
7. Recovery Matters — measurable improvement recognized without payment

## Key Rule
MWM does not solicit non-minority businesses. Enforced in code: admin.ts blackOwned gate.
Non-minority businesses may come to us; we do not go to them.

**Why:**
The founding mission is maximizing opportunities for minority-owned businesses.
Any commercial relationship that overrides community trust directly contradicts that mission.
The distinction between Promotion and Availability is what makes enterprise partnerships
constitutional rather than mission-compromising.

**How to apply:**
Any enterprise partnership conversation, promotional feature design, or KinfolkAI™
recommendation change must be reviewed against this charter before implementation.
