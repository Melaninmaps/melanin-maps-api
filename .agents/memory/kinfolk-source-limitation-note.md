---
name: Kinfolk source limitation note
description: Product rule for when Kinfolk may show a source caveat and how it must be presented.
---

Show the standardized source note only when a material factual, local, medical, legal, financial, or time-sensitive detail cannot be independently verified. It belongs in quiet, small attribution-style text at the bottom of the response, never inside the conversational answer.

Do not show it for ordinary conversation, community context, clearly framed suggestions, server-validated local listings, or answers with strong linked/cited support. Keep high-consequence safety guidance governed separately from this source-limitation rule.

**Why:** Repeated conversational caveats interrupt Kinfolk's voice and make well-supported or suggestion-oriented responses look unreliable. Members need a clear signal precisely when an unverified detail changes how they should use the information.

**How to apply:** Return a dedicated display field from the server after evaluating source support and intent, then render it after the response content and citations in every Kinfolk client. Do not append the wording to `reply`.
