## Notes for implementation
- Each of these communities requires the same evidentiary standard as the original 36-city registry and the current diaspora expansion
- Every profile must have community reviewers from within that specific community
- Jewish communities require particular sensitivity around terminology (Orthodox vs. Conservative vs. Reform vs. cultural Jewish identity; Ashkenazi vs. Sephardic vs. Mizrahi distinctions)
- Filipino community requires acknowledgment of the colonial history of the Philippines under both Spanish and US rule and its relationship to Filipino American identity
- Vietnamese community requires acknowledgment of the refugee experience and the generational divide between 1st-generation refugees and 2nd/3rd generation Vietnamese Americans
- Korean community requires acknowledgment of the 1992 Los Angeles Uprising context (Koreatown was a major target; the Sa-I-Gu community narrative)
- Chinese community requires acknowledgment of the Chinese Exclusion Act (1882) and the history of anti-Chinese violence in the US
- Japanese community requires acknowledgment of the Japanese American incarceration (WWII; Executive Order 9066) which directly shaped the current geographic distribution of Japanese Americans
- South Asian community requires acknowledgment of the Hindu-Muslim-Sikh distinctions and the post-9/11 hate crime context
