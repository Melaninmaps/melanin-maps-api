## Classification

**This is implementation memory — NOT constitutional.**

The Constitution answers: "What do we believe?"
This memory answers: "How will we execute?"

Keep that separation. The four testing phases, demo business strategy, and rollout order are operational and will evolve. They do not belong in the Constitution or Legacy Collection.

---

## Guiding Principle

Validate user behavior, not reveal the algorithm. Testers experience cause and effect — they never see weighting, formulas, or confidence models.

---

## Four Phases

**Phase 1 — Infrastructure First**
Auth stable · DB operations verified · verification flows work · event/contribution tracking operational · Railway confirmed. Platform must be technically solid before trust mechanics are tested.

**Phase 2 — Guided Testing via Demo Businesses**
Clearly labeled demo businesses ("Demo Business — For Testing Purposes" or "Community Demonstration Profile") serve as the teaching environment. Testers observe that meaningful participation — events, feedback, supporting others, challenges, conversations — positively influences a demo business. They learn the values. They do not learn the math.

**Phase 3 — Hidden Validation**
While testers interact with demo businesses, the platform quietly validates: whether weighting feels fair, whether any behaviors can artificially inflate reputation, whether contributors naturally understand what strengthens community, whether the model rewards meaningful participation over simple activity. This phase tests the philosophy as much as the software.

**Phase 4 — Public Launch**
Gradual trust signals instead of numerical scores:
- Verified Member
- Verified Business
- Community Contributor
- Community Supporter
- Trusted Host
- Active Community Business

No numerical scores until there is sufficient real-world data.

## What Stays Proprietary — Never Exposed

Weighting percentages · confidence calculations · fraud-detection signals · reputation formulas · decay models · anomaly detection · anti-gaming logic · ranking algorithms · recommendation priorities · Trust Engine calculations · Community Score calculations · Safety Confidence calculations · Business Confidence calculations · KinfolkAI recommendation logic.

---

## "Visible Outcomes, Invisible Logic"

People understand **why** something happened. They never need to know **exactly how** it was calculated.

Examples:
- "Your participation helped this business become more discoverable."
- "Your verified contributions strengthened community confidence."
- "Hosting community events increased your engagement profile."

**Why:** This is both a product philosophy and an IP protection strategy. It teaches values without exposing the proprietary engine.

**Applies to:** Trust Engine · KinfolkAI™ · Discovery · Recommendations · Moderation · Community ranking · Future AI models.

**Future graduation note:** This principle is currently stored as implementation memory. Over time it is a candidate to graduate into the Constitution under Principles, because it applies platform-wide — not just to the Trust Engine. Flag for consideration when the Constitution moves into the prose-writing phase.
