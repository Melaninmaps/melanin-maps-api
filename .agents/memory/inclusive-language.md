# Inclusive Language Rule

**Why:** The platform serves minorities and the melanated diaspora broadly — not exclusively Black people. This correction has been requested multiple times and must be applied to all slide decks and marketing copy every time content is written.

**Rule:** Never write copy that says "Black Americans," "Black communities," "for Black America," or "Black-owned businesses" as the primary audience descriptor in slides or marketing materials. Use inclusive alternatives instead.

**Preferred substitutions:**
- "Black Americans" → "minorities" or "the melanated diaspora"
- "Black communities" → "minority communities" or "melanated communities"
- "for Black America" → "for the melanated diaspora"
- "Black-owned businesses" → "minority-owned businesses" or "melanated businesses"
- "Black culture" → "melanated culture" or "minority culture"
- "Black wealth" → "minority wealth" or "melanated wealth"
- "traveling while Black" → context-appropriate inclusive phrasing

**How to apply:** On every new slide, every deck revision, and any marketing copy — review all audience descriptors before writing and apply these substitutions. Do not default to "Black" without checking this rule first.
