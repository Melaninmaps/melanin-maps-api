## Voice & Tone Rules

**The brand voice is: Warm · Confident · Hopeful · Community-first · Never corporate.**

| ✅ Say This | ❌ Not This |
|-------------|------------|
| "Welcome Home." | "User Onboarding Complete" |
| "Find Your People." | "Social Discovery" |
| "Community Insight" | "User Generated Data" |
| "Know before you go." | "Access safety data before travel." |

## Safety Language Rule

**Never guarantee safety. Always describe community insight and informed decision-making.**

| ✅ Say This | ❌ Not This |
|-------------|------------|
| "Community insight that helps you make informed decisions." | "Information that keeps you safe." |
| "Know what your community knows — before you arrive." | "Stay safe with real-time alerts." |
| "Community-powered awareness, not a police scanner." | "Safety guaranteed." |
