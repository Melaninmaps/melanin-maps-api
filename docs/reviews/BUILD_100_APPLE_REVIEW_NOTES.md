## WHAT THE APP DOES

Mapping With Melanin™ is a community platform for minority travelers,
entrepreneurs, and community members in their daily lives. Core features:

1. **Map** — Discover verified minority-owned businesses and cultural Heritage
   sites on a live map
2. **KinfolkAI™** — AI travel and life companion built for the minority
   community; ask it anything about local culture, businesses, or travel
3. **Community Feed** — Member posts, events, and local community updates
4. **Safety Hub** — Community-submitted safety insights and neighborhood context
5. **Heritage Explorer** — Cultural and historical sites across the United States
6. **Business Discovery** — Search and filter minority-owned businesses by
   category, city, and ownership type
7. **Circles** — Member-created groups for shared interests and safe meetups
8. **Library** — Cultural topics, knowledge articles, and community resources

## HOW TO USE THE TEST ACCOUNT

### Login
1. Open the app
2. Tap **"Sign In"**
3. Enter: `reviewer@melaninmaps.com` / `MapReview2026!`
4. Tap **Sign In** — you will land directly on the home/map screen

### Key Features to Review

**Map Tab (second tab):**
- Gold pins = verified minority-owned businesses
- Tap any pin → preview card → "View Business" for full detail
- Tap the **Heritage** button (book icon in the layer row) to activate
  the cultural Heritage layer
- Heritage pins show historical and cultural sites across the United States

**KinfolkAI (rotating star icon, bottom-right):**
- Ask: *"What minority-owned restaurants are near me in Philadelphia?"*
- Navigator tier has full AI access — no paywall will appear

**Community Tab:**
- Browse member posts, events, and local updates
- The test account has several posts already visible

**Safety Hub:**
- Community-submitted safety context and neighborhood insights
- Not algorithmic danger scores — community-sourced information

**Business Discovery:**
- Search tab → search by name, city, or category
- Filter by ownership type (Black-owned, Latino-owned, etc.)

### Account Deletion
Available at: **Profile → Settings → Account → Delete Account**
Deletion immediately revokes the session and removes all user data.

## WHY LOGIN IS REQUIRED

Every surface is member-generated and member-dependent:
- Community posts and safety reports carry member trust badges
- Business intelligence is shaped by member reviews and vouches
- KinfolkAI is personalized by the member's city, trust level, and history
- Content contributed by members is visible only to other members by design

This is structurally consistent with App Store-approved membership networks
(Nextdoor, Fishbowl, Raya). The gate is not cosmetic — it is the platform's
primary safety function.
