## WHAT THE APP DOES

Mapping With Melanin™ is a community platform for minority travelers,
entrepreneurs, and community members. Core features:

1. **Map** — Discover verified minority-owned businesses and cultural
   Heritage sites on a live map
2. **KinfolkAI™** — AI travel and life companion built for the minority
   community
3. **Community Feed** — Member posts, events, and local updates
4. **Business Dashboard** — Tools for minority business owners
5. **Heritage Explorer** — Cultural, historical, and educational sites
   across the United States

## HOW TO REACH THE HERITAGE MAP FEATURE (Build 98 — new in this build)

The Heritage layer is on the **Map tab** (second tab, map-pin icon).

**Step-by-step:**
1. Log in with the test account above
2. Tap the **Map** tab (second icon in the tab bar)
3. On the map, tap the **Heritage** button (book-open icon, appears in the
   layer control row at the top of the map alongside "Safety Heat")
4. The Heritage layer is now active — colored circular pins appear on the map
   for cultural sites across the United States
5. To filter to Historical Sundown Towns specifically:
   - Scroll the horizontal chip row (below the layer controls) to find
     **"Sundown Towns"**
   - Tap it — the map zooms to show only the 15 documented Historical
     Sundown Town pins
6. Tap any pin to open a preview card at the bottom of the screen
7. Tap **"View Site"** on the preview card to open the full detail screen
8. The detail screen shows:
   - Place name and location
   - Historical era
   - "HISTORICAL RECORD ONLY" disclaimer (visible in the About section)
   - Full historical description
   - Source attribution (labeled "Source:")
   - "Visit Official Website" button linking to the documented source

**To switch back to business pins:** Tap "Heritage" again to toggle off the
layer. Business pins (gold circles) are always visible behind the Heritage
layer.

## ABOUT THE HISTORICAL SUNDOWN TOWNS FEATURE

This is a **historical reference layer only**. It documents places where
racial exclusion policies were enforced in the United States during the
20th century, sourced from peer-reviewed academic research (James W. Loewen,
"Sundown Towns," 2005; Tougaloo College NSF Database; NAACP; U.S. Department
of Justice records; state historical societies).

**What the feature does NOT do:**
- It does not provide a current safety rating or danger score for any location
- It does not warn users away from any present-day community
- It does not make any claim about the current character of listed towns

Every entry carries the disclaimer: *"HISTORICAL RECORD ONLY — These policies
are no longer legally enforceable and do not reflect the current character of
this community."*

This feature is consistent with established public historical archives
(Smithsonian, EJI National Memorial, National Park Service) that document
this period of American history for educational purposes.
