# HBCU Roster Source Notes

## Published 107-school roster

The Hundred-Seven publishes a state-by-state roster headed **“There are 107 colleges in the United States that are identified by the US Department of Education as Historically Black Colleges and Universities (HBCUs). Of those 107, three are currently closed.”** [The Hundred-Seven — HBCU Listing](https://www.thehundred-seven.org/hbculist.html) (retrieved 2026-09-23).

The source identifies the three closed institutions as Concordia University-Alabama (closed 2018), Lewis College of Business (closed 2013), and Saint Paul’s College (closed 2013). The roster also includes specialized/graduate schools and seminaries, including Miles School of Law, Carver College, Johnson C. Smith Theological Seminary, Southern University Law Center, Hood Theological Seminary, and Payne Theological Seminary.

### Implementation use

This source is used only to reconcile the published 107-school HBCU roster in the MWM Cultural Heritage catalog. The HBCU tab must query exact `heritage_category = 'HBCU'` records only; it must not use loose keyword matching against unrelated heritage sites or events. Source-derived history and official institutional links remain separately visible on individual HBCU detail pages.
