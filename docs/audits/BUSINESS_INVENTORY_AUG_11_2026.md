## Data Quality Notes

1. **Ownership designations:** Only 249 of 2,194 businesses (11%) have `black-owned` in their `ownership_designations` column. This is a data gap, not a representation gap — most community-seeded businesses lack structured ownership data.

2. **Verification rate:** 32 of 2,194 businesses (1.5%) are verified. Verification is manual and admin-driven; the pipeline exists but hasn't run at scale.

3. **Category normalization:** Two near-duplicate categories exist — `Community Organizations` and `Community & Organizations` (3 records). Minor cleanup needed.

4. **Country normalization:** 752 records have `country = 'US'` and 1,304 have `country = 'USA'`. Both refer to US; query treats them as equivalent.

5. **Reference-only businesses (104 total):** These are community entries without owner claims, primarily international and historical/cultural sites. They display a "Community Reference" badge.

6. **Safety data:** As of August 11, 2026, the `business_safety_submissions` table is newly created. All displayed safety stats (`safety_rating`, `would_return_alone`, `recommendation_rate`) on businesses are either 0 or manually set. The safety survey onSubmit is now wired — community data will begin populating on first user submissions.

7. **Confidence scores:** All `confidence_score` values on businesses currently reflect pre-launch estimates. The dynamic computation formula is now live and will recompute on each new safety submission.
