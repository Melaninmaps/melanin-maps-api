## Founder Intent

Business Owners, Cultural Ambassadors, Community Organization representatives,
mentors, and professionals are also Community Members.

Their profiles should overlap enough to create meaningful community
understanding, trust, and impact — but not in ways that expose private
searches, saves, attendance, identity, location, family information,
anonymous reports, or other sensitive activity.

The platform must help people find safe and affirming places while recognizing
that the world does not yet consistently protect people who are publicly
identified with every community, event, belief, health condition, or
life circumstance.

**Visibility must therefore be:**
  Deliberate
  Granular
  Contextual
  Reversible
  Private by default for sensitive activity
  Technically enforced
  Never required for platform access

---

## The Deeper Principle — Permanent

> You are trying to create a world where people can be seen, supported, and
> connected without forcing them to expose themselves before the world is
> safe enough to hold that information responsibly.

## Section 1 — One Account, Separate Role Contexts

One account may hold:
  Community Member
  Verified Community Member
  Business Owner
  Verified Business Owner
  Cultural Ambassador
  Community Organization representative
  Mentor
  Contributor
  Trusted Contributor
  Areas of Expertise

Each role has its own visibility boundaries.

### A — Community Member Profile Context
May show, only when the member chooses:
  Interests
  Communities supported
  Public reviews and recommendations
  Volunteer activity
  Charitable events
  Community contributions
  Mentorship availability
  Cities or neighborhoods they are comfortable publicly associating with
  Impact milestones

### B — Business Profile Context
May show:
  Business services
  Ownership designations
  Vibes
  Accessibility
  Events
  Charitable partnerships
  Community involvement
  Business-owner introduction
  Business goals
  Public business milestones
  Verified ownership status

### C — Cultural Ambassador Profile Context
  (To be documented)

### D — Community Organization Profile Context
  (To be documented)

### E — Professional Expertise Display
  (To be documented)

### F — Private KinfolkAI Context
May include (private unless explicitly shared):
  Search history
  Saves
  Event attendance
  Life Journeys
  Location history (if enabled by member)
  LGBTQIA+ interests
  Faith interests
  Health-related interests
  Family context
  Relocation plans
  Private Circle activity

This information must not become public profile content unless the member
affirmatively chooses to share a specific item.

### G — Kinfolk Circle Context
  Visible only to Circle members the person joined with
  Not visible on public profile without explicit choice

### H — Anonymous Contribution Context
  Technically separated from all public-profile activity
  Not linkable to the member's identity by any other member, business, or
  recommendation system
  Not included in public impact display without explicit separate choice

**Rule:** A role may use shared identity fields such as name or profile image
only when the member has approved that relationship.

**Rule:** Do not automatically expose private Community Member behavior on a
Business Owner, Ambassador, professional, or organization profile.

## Section 2 — Profile Linking Control

A member must choose whether public profiles are linked.

Potential controls:
  "Show that I own this business"
  "Show this business on my Community Member profile"
  "Show my Community Member profile from my business page"
  "Show my Cultural Ambassador role"
  "Show my professional expertise"
  "Keep these profiles publicly separate"

Document required for each control:
  Default setting (always private until changed)
  Effects of the choice
  Search visibility impact
  Whether historical activity becomes linked
  How unlinking works
  What remains visible after unlinking
  How verified statuses are displayed separately

**Rule:** Do not force profile linking as a condition of business ownership,
verification, promotion, or membership.

**Rule:** Changing a visibility setting must not automatically publish
past activity.

## Section 3 — Community Activity Visibility Model

Three visibility levels — applied to every type of activity.

### A — Share by Default
Only for activity the member knowingly creates as public:
  Public reviews
  Public recommendations
  Public event submissions
  Public posts
  Public business updates
  Public resources submitted
  Public volunteer opportunities submitted

### B — Ask Me Each Time
  Event attendance
  Charitable-event participation
  Volunteer milestones
  Mentorship milestones
  Businesses supported
  Community impact totals
  Relocation reflections
  Local-guide activity
  Cultural-event participation
  Professional-community activity

Correct interaction model (see Section 4 for full example):
  Present a specific activity
  Offer granular choices (share total, share one item, share category,
    save as private milestone, do not share, never ask for this type)
  Default choice: do not share

### C — Always Private by Default
Members may choose to share a specific item from this list, but no broad
setting may automatically publish future sensitive activity.

  Search history
  Saved places
  KinfolkAI conversations
  Private messages
  Kinfolk Circle activity
  Anonymous employer reports
  Anonymous safety reports
  Precise location
  Verification documents
  Child-related information
  Health-related searches
  Legal-service searches
  Financial-hardship activity
  Religious activity
  Sexual-orientation-related activity
  Political activity
  Immigration-related activity
  Domestic-violence resources
  Crisis-resource use

## Section 4 — Sensitive Event and Identity Standard

### The LGBTQIA+ Event Example (Canonical)

KinfolkAI may use search or attendance privately to improve that member's
experience when the member has consented to personalization.

KinfolkAI must NOT infer:
  Sexual orientation
  Gender identity
  Relationship status
  Political beliefs
  The reason for attendance

KinfolkAI must NOT automatically publish:
  "Marcus attended 3 LGBTQIA+ events."

Correct interaction model:
  KinfolkAI asks privately:
  "You attended several community events this month. Would you like to
   share any of that activity?"

  Granular options presented:
    Share only the total number of community events
    Share one selected event
    Share the event category
    Share the organization supported
    Save this as a private milestone only to me
    Do not share
    Never ask me to share this type of activity

Public language if member chooses to share at category level:
  "Marcus supported local LGBTQIA+ community organizations this month."

Public language if member selects a specific event:
  "Marcus attended the Phoenix Pride Community Resource Fair."

Public language if member shares participation without event details:
  "Marcus supported 4 community events this month."

The member controls the level of detail. Always.

### Apply the Same Standard to All Sensitive Categories
  Faith events
  Political events
  Health-support events
  Legal clinics
  Recovery or crisis groups
  Immigration events
  Disability-support events
  Domestic-violence resources
  Child and family events

## Section 5 — Public Impact Without Sensitive Exposure

Design options that allow meaningful public participation without revealing
unnecessary detail.

Approved example language:
  "Marcus supported 4 community events this month."
  "Marcus volunteered with local organizations."
  "Marcus helped welcome new Community Members."
  "Marcus contributed information that helped others."
  "Johnson Family Bakery supported two neighborhood food drives."

Members may choose whether to name:
  The event
  The organization
  The category
  The location
  The date
  The business involved
  Their role

**Rule:** Do not publish a precise timeline that could reconstruct a
member's movements.

## Section 6 — Business Owner as Community Member

### A — Business Activity
Activity performed by or officially on behalf of the business.

May display:
  Charitable partnerships
  Community events hosted
  Volunteer work (business)
  Local hiring
  Scholarships
  Donations
  Community milestones
  Public business milestones

### B — Owner Personal Activity
Activity performed by the owner as a private Community Member.

May display on the owner's Community Member profile only:
  Mentorship
  Personal founder story
  Personal community impact
  Communities and neighborhoods they choose to associate with publicly

**Rule:** Do not automatically convert personal attendance or searches into
business-profile information.

**Rule:** Do not let business staff or co-owners access the owner's private
Community Member activity through the business dashboard.

**Rule:** Personal searches, saved businesses, anonymous reports, Circle
memberships, family information, and precise location must never appear
on the business profile or be accessible to business co-managers.

### The Profile Linking Example (Canonical)

Business profile:
  "Johnson Family Bakery supports youth literacy programs and hosted two
   neighborhood food drives this year."

Owner's Community Member profile:
  "Marcus is a Community Member, mentor, and volunteer."

Linked only when Marcus explicitly chooses:
  "Show me publicly as the owner of Johnson Family Bakery."

The system must never automatically reveal:
  Which events the owner attends personally
  Their private searches
  Their saved businesses
  Their Circle memberships
  Their family information
  Their political, religious, health, or identity interests
  Their precise whereabouts
  Anonymous reviews or safety reports they authored

## Section 7 — Anonymous Contribution Safeguards

Anonymous employer, safety, discrimination, and other protected contributions
must remain technically separated from public-profile activity — permanently.

KinfolkAI may privately provide impact feedback:
  "Your anonymous workplace insight helped Community Members make more
   informed decisions."

It must NOT:
  Add the submission to the public profile
  Reveal authorship to the employer
  Reveal authorship to another member
  Include identifying timing or details
  Link the anonymous report to public mentorship activity
  Suggest publicly that the member authored the report
  Use the public profile to imply authorship

KinfolkAI may separately ask privately:
  "Your insight has helped others. Would you like to explore mentoring in
   your field? This would not reveal or connect you to your anonymous
   submission."

Technical requirements (document before implementation):
  Database separation between anonymous reports and public-profile tables
  Analytics must not join anonymous report records to member identity
  Logging must not include member identity in anonymous report event logs
  Recommendation system must not use anonymous report metadata to surface
    the member publicly

## Section 8 — KinfolkAI Personalization vs. Disclosure

Strict separation required between:

  Information KinfolkAI may use privately for the member
  Information the member has chosen to display publicly
  Information shared inside a specific Kinfolk Circle
  Information available to a business
  Information available to administrators
  Information used only as an aggregated community signal

**Constitutional Principle (verbatim):**
  "A member's private activity may improve their own experience, but it
   must not become someone else's information without explicit permission."

Consent rules:
  Silence does not count as consent
  Account creation does not count as consent
  Event attendance does not count as consent
  Search behavior does not count as consent
  A prior unrelated sharing choice does not count as consent for future
    sensitive activity

Document how consent is stored and enforced technically —
not only in UI preferences.

## Section 9 — Child and Teen Controls

### Under 13
  Search history private
  Saves private
  Event attendance private by default
  Precise location never public
  No public movement timeline
  No adult discovery based on child activity
  No direct adult contact outside approved structures
  Strongest default profile limits
  Parent-managed sharing where legally required

### Ages 13–15
  All of the above plus:
  No sensitive-event categories publicly suggested for sharing
  Anonymous reports remain anonymous from parents where law, safety,
    and product policy require appropriate confidentiality
    (subject to legal review)

### Ages 16–17
  Teen agency preserved within approved safety boundaries
  Stronger defaults than adults
  Sensitive-category defaults: always private

### Adults
  Standard platform defaults as documented in this standard

**Action required before implementation:**
  Legal review of COPPA, FERPA, and applicable state child-privacy law
  Policy review of parental access vs. teen confidentiality in
    health, safety, and identity-related categories
  Age-verification approach defined

## Section 11 — The 10 Non-Negotiable Safety Rules

These apply regardless of feature, build, or configuration:

  1. No sensitive inference.
     KinfolkAI does not infer sexuality, religion, health, immigration
     status, political views, disability, or family status from searches,
     attendance, names, photographs, or location.

  2. No automatic publication.
     Private behavior never becomes profile activity by default.

  3. Specific consent, not blanket consent.
     "Share my community activity" is too broad for sensitive activity.
     Members choose the item and level of detail.

  4. No retroactive exposure.
     Changing a visibility setting must not automatically publish
     past activity.

  5. No cross-role leakage.
     Personal searches and attendance do not appear in a business
     dashboard or business profile.

  6. No location trail.
     Public event participation must not allow reconstruction of a
     member's movements.

  7. Minor protections.
     Children and teens receive stricter defaults, parent-managed
     controls where appropriate, and no public attendance history.

  8. Anonymous means technically separated.
     Anonymous reports are excluded from public-profile attribution and
     ordinary recommendation explanations. Separation is enforced at the
     database, analytics, logging, and recommendation layers — not only
     in the UI.

  9. Easy withdrawal.
     A member can remove shared activity later without deleting their
     account.

  10. Safety over growth.
      A recommendation, community connection, or engagement opportunity
      must be suppressed when disclosure could create meaningful risk.

### Constitutional Principle 10 — Visibility as Choice
  "Kinfolk helps members express community belonging without requiring
   disclosure of sensitive identity or activity. Visibility is chosen,
   contextual, reversible, and never the price of participation."

### Constitutional Principle 11 — Private Activity as Personal Property
  "A member's private activity may improve their own experience, but it
   must not become someone else's information without explicit permission."

## Pre-Build Checklist — Before Any Privacy-Related Feature Ships

  Have the role contexts for this feature been defined?
  Is the default private for any sensitive category?
  Is the consent specific (item and level) rather than blanket?
  Does the feature create any cross-role leakage?
  Can past activity remain private when settings change?
  Is anonymous activity technically separated from the profile layer?
  Does the feature create a location trail?
  Are minor protections applied if the feature is accessible to under-18?
  Is safety-over-growth applied — would any disclosure create risk?
  Has the purposeful collection gate (CIC Principle 9) been applied to
    every data field this feature introduces?
