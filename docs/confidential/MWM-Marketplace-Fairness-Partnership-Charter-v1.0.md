## PART II — THE SEVEN GOVERNING PRINCIPLES

### Principle 1 — Mission First

The mission statement in Section 1.1 is not aspirational language.
It is an operational constraint. Every recommendation, partnership agreement,
promotional placement, and business policy is evaluated against it.

**Application:**
- A minority-owned business with a lower raw score may appear above a non-minority partner
  when the mission requires prioritizing minority-owned discovery.
- A non-minority partner with excellent community ratings may be recommended
  when no minority-owned alternative exists — because the second half of the mission
  requires users to confidently meet their needs.
- Neither half of the mission overrides the other. Both halves are permanent.

---

### Principle 2 — Promotion ≠ Availability

**This distinction is constitutional.**

A non-minority-owned business may be:
- Available (present in search results and on the map)
- Discoverable (appearing in filtered searches)
- Recommended when necessary (when no minority-owned alternative meets the user's need)

Without receiving:
- The same promotional treatment as a verified minority-owned business
- Featured, campaign, or premium placement
- Mission-category endorsement
- Equal weight in KinfolkAI™ recommendation sequences

**Operational Example — Metro Area:**
> User searches: "Hotels in Atlanta"
> Verified minority-owned hotels exist within search area.
> Result: Minority-owned hotels appear first, ordered by community trust signals.
> Partner hotels appear afterward if relevant to the user's expressed preferences.

**Operational Example — Rural or Underserved Area:**
> User searches: "Hotels in rural Arizona"
> No verified minority-owned hotels exist within the search area.
> Result: KinfolkAI™ may respond:
> "I couldn't find a verified minority-owned hotel within your preferred area.
>  Here are highly rated alternatives that meet your accessibility, safety, and travel preferences."
> This fulfills the user's need without misrepresenting the platform's mission.

**What this principle prohibits:**
- Paid placement that overrides community trust rankings
- Sponsored content that appears indistinguishable from organic minority-owned recommendations
- Any label, badge, or visual treatment that implies mission endorsement of a non-minority partner

---

### Principle 3 — Recommendations Happen in Layers

Rather than a single ranking score, KinfolkAI™ follows a layered decision sequence.
The exact implementation of this sequence and its weighting logic is proprietary.

**The Decision Sequence (public summary form):**

```
Layer 1: Does a verified minority-owned option exist that meets the need?
Layer 2: Is it trustworthy? (meets safety and community trust minimums)
Layer 3: Is it reasonably accessible? (proximity, hours, capacity)
Layer 4: Does it satisfy the user's active filters and preferences?
Layer 5: If no — broaden the search radius or relax one filter at a time.
Layer 6: If still no — include trusted partners or other businesses,
         clearly labeled as alternatives, not as primary mission recommendations.
```

**Constitutional Rule:**
The weighting inside each layer and the threshold values between layers
are proprietary trade secrets (TS-MF-003). They are never disclosed publicly.
The sequence itself — not the weights — may be described in public communications
as the platform's stated values in practice.

### Principle 4 — National Brands Are Not One Business

**Each location of a national or enterprise brand is evaluated independently.**

A national brand's enterprise relationship with Mapping With Melanin™
does not transfer trust, ratings, safety scores, or promotional eligibility
from one property to another.

**The Hierarchy:**

```
[Enterprise Account]  (parent company — holds the partnership agreement)
       |
       ├── [Location A]  (independently rated, independently scored)
       ├── [Location B]  (independently rated, independently scored)
       ├── [Location C]  (independently rated, independently scored)
       └── [Location D]  (independently rated, independently scored)
```

**What the Enterprise Account holds:**
- The partnership agreement and its terms
- The enterprise accountability dashboard (aggregate trend view across locations)
- Billing and administrative contact
- Enterprise-level appeals and dispute pathway

**What each Location holds:**
- Its own community ratings
- Its own safety reports and scores
- Its own discrimination reports
- Its own accessibility ratings
- Its own owner/management response record
- Its own promotional eligibility status
- Its own engagement score

**Constitutional Rule:**
KinfolkAI™ recommends the **location**, not the brand.
A strong enterprise reputation does not suppress accurate location-level signals.
A weak enterprise reputation does not suppress a genuinely strong location-level record.

### Principle 5 — Enterprise Accountability

**Enterprise partners receive accountability visibility, not immunity.**

The enterprise accountability dashboard provides the corporate team with
aggregate trend data across all participating locations. This visibility
serves the platform's mission by giving enterprises the intelligence they
need to address underperforming properties.

**Dashboard Format (summary — implementation detail is proprietary):**

```
Example: Enterprise Partnership Overview

Overall Partnership Health Score: [calculated]

Regional breakdown:
  Northeast Region:   [score range] [trend indicator]
  Southeast Region:   [score range] [trend indicator]
  Mountain West:      [score range] [trend indicator]
  Midwest:            [score range] [trend indicator]
```

**What enterprise partners see:**
- Aggregate community scores across participating locations
- Regional and national trend lines
- Areas showing measurable improvement or decline
- General categories of community feedback themes (never individual user data)

**What enterprise partners do NOT see:**
- Individual community member identities
- Individual review text before it appears publicly
- Safety report details not yet publicly available
- User search history, profiles, or private data
- Unmoderated content of any kind

---

### Principle 6 — Partnership Is Earned, Not Permanent

**No enterprise receives permanent partner status.**

Partners are continuously evaluated against defined standards. Failure to meet
those standards results in graduated consequences up to and including
loss of promotional benefits or suspension of the partnership.

**Partnership Continuation Standards (categories — specific thresholds are proprietary):**
- Community trust signals (aggregate location scores)
- Responsiveness to community feedback
- Safety record at participating locations
- No unresolved serious community concerns
- Policy compliance
- Verified location information accuracy

**Consequence Ladder:**
```
Sustained performance     → Full partnership benefits maintained
Declining performance     → Advisory notice issued to enterprise
Persistent underperformance → Promotional placement suspended (discovery preserved)
Serious unresolved concern → Partnership review initiated
Constitutional violation  → Partnership suspended, admin review required
```

**This is not punitive.**
It is the maintenance of the integrity that makes the partnership meaningful.
A partner designation that cannot be lost is an endorsement, not a partnership.

---

### Principle 7 — Recovery Matters

**Businesses and enterprise partners have a path to improve.**

If a location or enterprise shows measurable corrective improvement,
the platform must be capable of recognizing and reflecting that improvement.

**Public acknowledgment format (when applicable):**
> "This location has shown measurable improvement in community trust
>  over the past [period] following corrective actions."

**What recovery requires:**
- Verified, sustained improvement in community signals (not a single positive review)
- Resolution of any flagged concerns
- Compliance confirmation where applicable
- Passage of a minimum review period (specific duration is proprietary)

**What recovery does not require:**
- Payment
- Premium subscription
- Relationship with a sales team

Recovery is open to all businesses on the platform,
regardless of their commercial relationship with Mapping With Melanin™.

## PART III — THE TWO NAMED TRADE SECRET PRINCIPLES

These two named principles are designated for eventual public disclosure
in their summary form only. The full implementation logic remains confidential.

### TS-MF-001: The Marketplace Fairness Principle™

> **Partnerships increase opportunity — they do not override community trust.**

**Classification:** Trade Secret — Summary may be published
**Cross-reference:** Invisible Architecture Spec #32 (Constitutional Ecosystem Framework™)
**Application:** Any commercial relationship between Mapping With Melanin™ and any
enterprise partner is explicitly subordinate to community trust signals.
A partner may purchase promotional placement. A partner may not purchase
a better community rating, a safer safety score, or a KinfolkAI™ recommendation
that does not reflect the location's actual community record.

---

### TS-MF-002: The Local Truth Principle™

> **Enterprise relationships never supersede the lived experience of a specific location.**

**Classification:** Trade Secret — Summary may be published
**Cross-reference:** Invisible Architecture Spec #32, Spec #06 (Safety Intelligence Engine™)
**Application:** The lived experience of community members at a specific location —
including safety reports, discrimination reports, accessibility ratings, and community
trust signals — is always evaluated independently at that location.
A national enterprise relationship does not dilute, suppress, or override those signals
at any individual property.

## PART IV — KINFOLKAI™ RECOMMENDATION PHILOSOPHY FOR NON-MINORITY BUSINESSES

### 4.1 The Standard

KinfolkAI™ is not recommending or avoiding a business because of its commercial
relationship with Mapping With Melanin™.
It is using location-specific, community-verified information.
That is the standard that builds trust.

### 4.2 Approved Language Models

**When a minority-owned alternative exists:**
KinfolkAI™ leads with the minority-owned option without commentary
on the existence of non-minority alternatives unless the user asks.

**When no minority-owned alternative exists:**
> "There aren't currently any verified minority-owned [category] that match your search.
>  Among other available options, [location] has consistently strong community
>  [safety/trust/accessibility] feedback."

**When a partner location has active concerns:**
> "Although [brand] is one of our partners, recent community reports for this specific
>  location indicate [concern category, not full detail]. Here are nearby alternatives
>  with stronger recent community feedback."

**What KinfolkAI™ never says:**
- "Our partner [brand]..." as a promotional introduction
- "[Brand] is featured because they support our mission" (unless independently true)
- Recommendations based on commercial relationship without community-signal support

### 4.3 The Constitutional Constraint

From Invisible Architecture Spec #20 (KinfolkAI™ Architecture):
"Trust is the product — not conversation, not judgment."

Commercial relationships are inputs to availability. They are not inputs to trust.

## PART V — NON-MINORITY BUSINESS ENGAGEMENT POLICY

This section supersedes any prior informal understanding on this topic.

### 5.1 Outbound Engagement — Non-Minority Businesses

**MWM does not solicit non-minority-owned businesses.**
This is a constitutional posture, not a preference.

- No outreach emails, notifications, or digests are sent to non-minority-owned businesses.
- No automated triggers contact non-minority-owned businesses.
- No admin-initiated outreach is permitted for non-minority-owned businesses.
  (Enforced at code level: `admin.ts` blocks the outreach route for any business
   where `blackOwned = false`.)

### 5.2 Inbound Engagement — What Non-Minority Businesses May Do

Non-minority-owned businesses may:
- View MWM's website on their own initiative.
- See that MWM community members visited their business page (via their own web analytics
  — MWM referral traffic appears in their own Google Analytics as mappingwithmelanin.com).
- Access their MWM listing page analytics IF they independently discover MWM,
  initiate a claim, and are approved by an admin.
- Contact MWM at info@mappingwithmelanin.com on their own initiative.
- Inquire about the Sponsored Enterprise Partnership program on their own initiative.

### 5.3 Sponsored Enterprise Partnership — Inquiry Path

MWM waits for enterprise partners to come to us.
The inquiry path must be:
- Self-service (a known URL that MWM never links to from outbound communications)
- Clearly labeled as an inbound inquiry form
- Never referenced in any email, notification, or push communication to a
  non-minority-owned business that has not already established a commercial relationship
