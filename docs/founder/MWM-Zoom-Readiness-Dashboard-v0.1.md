## Critical Path — Must Complete Before Zoom

These four items are 🔴 and block a credible Zoom:

### 1. Demo Flow
What exactly will be shown, on what device, in what order.
Suggested sequence based on 20 founding member experiences:
1. Fresh install → onboarding → Welcome Home Experience™ (Wave 4-A must be complete OR scripted around)
2. Business search in their city
3. Map view with safety overlay
4. KinfolkAI — one live conversation
5. Community feed
6. Profile and saved places
7. Membership gate (brief)

### 2. Known Limitations Document
A one-page document that names what is not yet complete — framed with confidence, not apology.
Suggested sections:
- What is fully available in this build
- What is actively being improved during the founding beta
- What is on the roadmap (not a promise, a direction)

This document prevents disappointment and builds trust. Testers who know the limitations in advance become collaborators, not critics.

### 3. "Available Today vs. Coming During Beta" Handout
One page. Two columns. Specific.
Column A: What they can do right now in the app
Column B: What is being added during their beta period
No column C (roadmap) in the handout — that is the Zoom conversation, not a printed promise.

### 4. Live Demo Backup Plan
What happens if technology fails during the Zoom. Prepare this before the meeting — not during it.

| Failure | Backup |
|---|---|
| Internet fails | Switch to pre-recorded walkthrough video — plays without internet |
| Login fails | Have a second device already logged in and ready |
| Map fails to load | Have screenshots of the map with real business pins |
| Push notification fails | Show notification screenshot; explain what triggers it |
| App crashes | Have screenshots of every key screen in a separate folder |

**The rule:** The meeting never stops because of technology. The product exists whether or not the demo cooperates.  
**Prepare:** Record a 3–5 minute walkthrough video of the full demo flow before Zoom day. This video serves as backup and can also be shared with testers who miss the call.

## Alignment Rule

Every claim made in the Zoom — every feature named, every experience described — must appear in one of three categories:

1. **Available in the exact tester build** (iOS 1.1.5 Build 84 / Android versionCode 60 + any waves implemented before Zoom)
2. **Being added during the founding beta** (waves in progress, honest timeline)
3. **Longer-term institutional vision** (clearly labeled as vision, not promise)

No category 4. No vague "coming soon." Every promise has a location.
