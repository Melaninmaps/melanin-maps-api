## The Propagation Principle

When a chapter in Volume I (The Constitution) changes — for example, the Mission is rewritten — the Chapter Registry identifies every presentation in Volume II that references that chapter and prompts:

> "Would you like to update every presentation that references the approved Mission chapter?"

This is the propagation system. One source of truth. Many expressions.

---

## On the Word "Pages"

These are not slides. They are pages.

Sometimes a page is presented on a screen.
Sometimes it is printed.
Sometimes it is narrated in the documentary.
Sometimes it is in a coffee table book.
Sometimes it is on the website.
Sometimes it is part of onboarding.

It is still the same page. The medium changes. The story doesn't.

---

## On "Slide Registry" → "Chapter Registry"

Every page is part of a chapter.
Every chapter belongs to a volume.
Every volume belongs to the Legacy Collection.

The registry tracks chapters, not slides.
