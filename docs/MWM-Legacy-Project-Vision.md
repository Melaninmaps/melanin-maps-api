## Legacy Spots — Platform Feature Vision

Not just a starred favorite restaurant. A place with memory.

Examples:
- "My grandmother met my grandfather here."
- "This church helped my family after Hurricane Katrina."
- "This mural honors our neighborhood."
- "This barber shop has been here since 1968."
- "My father brought me here every Saturday."

These aren't reviews. They're memories. The map becomes a living cultural archive created by the people who know those places best.

*Note: Legacy Spots is a long-term vision feature. It is not currently built and should not be described as available functionality.*

## Critical Distinction — What Must Be Protected

As this vision grows, the Foundations Book must clearly distinguish between:

1. **Our Enduring Mission and Principles** — Timeless. Does not change with the product.
2. **Our Current Platform Capabilities** — What exists today. Accurate and honest.
3. **Our Long-Term Vision** — What we aspire to build. Inspiring but clearly marked as future.

This distinction keeps the document inspiring without creating confusion about what is available now. It also protects the company from overpromising to community members, investors, partners, or press.

## The Word That Fits

Not "Bible."

**Legacy.**

Technology changes. Apps change. AI changes.

But a legacy is something that outlives the technology.

The Mapping With Melanin™ Legacy Collection — twenty years from now — will not be remembered for how the app worked. It will be remembered for the communities it helped connect, the stories it preserved, the music it elevated, and the sense of belonging it fostered.
