## The Idea

KinfolkAI should notice opportunities and surface them to members — not wait to be asked.

This is the difference between a search tool and a steward. A steward is paying attention even when you're not looking.

---

## The Advisor's Examples (Exact)

> "Three businesses you love are participating in a neighborhood cleanup this Saturday."

> "You mentioned you're trying to start a business. Two grant deadlines open next week."

> "You recently visited two HBCUs. An alumni networking event is happening nearby."

> "You marked mentoring as important. A verified business owner is offering office hours this Thursday."

Each example has the same structure:
1. KinfolkAI knows something about the member (saved businesses, stated goal, recent activity, onboarding answer)
2. Something in the platform environment is newly relevant to that knowledge
3. KinfolkAI surfaces it proactively — not as a generic push notification, but as a contextual notice

---

## What This Requires

**Member context (inputs):**
- Saved and favorited businesses
- Goals set during onboarding (FSR-019, FSR-025)
- Recent activity (visited places, completed surveys, posts)
- Life stage (FSR-020)
- Contribution intent from onboarding

**Platform signals (triggers):**
- New events from businesses the member has saved
- Grant/opportunity deadlines in the Opportunity Center
- Nearby alumni or networking events
- Business owners offering mentorship in the member's interest area
- Neighborhood safety changes in saved areas

**Delivery mechanism:**
- Could be in-app (KinfolkAI tab, notification center, or a "Kinfolk Notices" feed section)
- Could be push notification for time-sensitive items (grant deadline, this Saturday)
- Should not be a generic notification flood — contextual and relevant only

---

## The Stewardship Distinction

Stewardship is different from notification spam because:
- It is based on *known member intent* (what they said they care about)
- It surfaces opportunities the member would recognize as relevant
- It does not sell or promote — it connects
- It has memory: it doesn't surface the same opportunity twice

---

## Relationship to Existing Platform

The notification system (8 categories, quiet hours) already exists on the infrastructure side. The gap is the intelligence that decides what to surface and when.

This requires:
- FSR-019 (conversational onboarding to gather intent)
- FSR-025 (goals/dreams system to maintain member aspirations)
- KinfolkAI reframing as intelligence layer (FSR-018)

---

## Founder Decisions Required

1. Should KinfolkAI surface proactive opportunity notices?
2. Where do these notices appear — inside the KinfolkAI tab, in the notification center, as push notifications, or all three?
3. How does a member control the frequency and type of proactive notices?
4. Should these notices be labeled "Kinfolk noticed..." or given a different voice?
5. What is the minimum member context needed before stewardship notices can start? (e.g., must have set at least one goal, or been active for 7 days)
