## The Idea

When a business joins the platform, the questions they answer should go beyond category and location. The platform should ask businesses not just what they sell, but what community they're trying to build.

---

## The Advisor's Proposed Business Questions

Not: "What category are you?"

Instead:

- What makes your business special?
- Who do you serve best?
- What community do you hope to strengthen?
- What values drive your business?
- How do you give back?
- Would you like volunteers?
- Would you like mentors?
- Would you mentor others?
- Are internships available?
- Do you hire locally?
- Do you host events?
- Can people learn from you?
- Are you family friendly?
- Do you offer accessibility accommodations?
- What languages do you serve?
- What cultures are reflected in your business?

---

## What This Changes

**Current model:** Business listing = category + location + contact info + hours + photos.

**Proposed model:** Business profile = identity + community role + contribution capacity + accessibility + cultural expression.

A business that answers these questions becomes a community asset, not just a listing. KinfolkAI can surface it in context:
- "You mentioned you're interested in mentorship — this business owner offers office hours monthly."
- "This restaurant is family-friendly and serves three languages."
- "This business hires locally and offers internships."

---

## Relationship to Existing Platform

The business listing table already has:
- Category, location, hours, contact
- Photos, hero images
- Safety stats
- Verification status

What does not exist:
- Community impact fields (who they serve, how they give back)
- Contribution fields (volunteers, mentors, internships)
- Cultural expression fields (languages, cultures)
- Accessibility field beyond the basic accessibility flag

## Founder Decisions Required

1. Should the business profile be expanded to include community contribution questions?
2. Are these the right questions, or should the list be shorter/different for launch?
3. Should businesses answer these during listing, or in a separate "community profile" section after listing?
4. How do these answers surface on the business card and business detail page?
5. Should community contribution answers affect business ranking or discovery?
