## The Idea

KinfolkAI should not primarily be an AI assistant. It should become the intelligence layer of the entire platform.

The question shifts from:
> "What can AI answer?"

To:
> "How can Kinfolk help people belong?"

Everything else grows from that.

---

## What This Changes

**Current framing:** KinfolkAI is a chat interface where members ask questions and receive answers about businesses, travel, safety, and local recommendations.

**Proposed framing:** KinfolkAI is the intelligence layer that:
- Understands where a member is in their life stage
- Knows what they're hoping to find (belonging, opportunity, community)
- Knows where they're trying to go (their goals and dreams)
- Proactively surfaces opportunities, not just answers
- Evolves alongside the member as their life changes
- Connects members to each other, not just to places

The distinction is: **stewardship versus search.**

---

## Relationship to Existing Platform

This reframe would affect every area of the platform:
- Onboarding feeds KinfolkAI with richer member context (FSR-019)
- Member goals feed KinfolkAI with proactive context (FSR-025)
- Life stages feed KinfolkAI with evolving context (FSR-020)
- Ecosystems model feeds KinfolkAI with connection maps (FSR-021)
- Businesses' community profiles feed KinfolkAI with opportunity data (FSR-023)
- Cultural Ambassadors become KinfolkAI collaborators, not just another user tier

---

## Founder Decisions Required

1. Should KinfolkAI be repositioned as the intelligence layer — meaning it is present across the platform surface, not confined to a chat tab?
2. What does "KinfolkAI as intelligence" look like when a member is on the map screen? The discover screen? The community feed?
3. Does this reframe require a product naming or positioning decision? ("KinfolkAI" as a feature name vs. "Kinfolk" as the platform's intelligence)
4. What is the release sequence — does the intelligence layer launch for all members at once, or does it deepen as members share more context?
