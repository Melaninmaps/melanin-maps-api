## The Idea

Community organizations should become a distinct entity type on the platform — not listed alongside businesses, not treated as content — but treated as connectors. They are how members find each other, find opportunities, and find belonging.

---

## The Advisor's Profile Questions for Community Organizations

When a community organization joins the platform, they should be asked:

- Who do you serve?
- How can members participate?
- What resources do you provide?
- Who should contact you?
- How can businesses partner with you?
- What recurring events do you host?
- Are volunteers needed?
- Do you mentor youth?
- Do you provide emergency support?

---

## What Makes Community Organizations Different From Businesses

| Businesses | Community Organizations |
|-----------|------------------------|
| Commercial purpose | Community purpose |
| Serve customers | Serve members |
| Provide products/services | Provide belonging, support, resources |
| KinfolkAI recommends them | KinfolkAI connects members TO them |
| Discovered by search | Discovered by context (life situation) |

---

## Relationship to Existing Platform

The platform currently has:
- Business listings (businesses table)
- Community spaces (community-spaces table)
- Community verified badge (community-verified.tsx)
- Events (event listings)
- Opportunity center (jobs, mentorship)

Community organizations may overlap with some of these but are a distinct concept: they are structured institutions (churches, nonprofits, civic organizations, professional associations, alumni chapters, mutual aid networks) whose primary purpose is connecting and supporting members.

---

## What This Enables

With community organizations as formal platform entities:
- KinfolkAI can surface "An organization in your area provides emergency relocation support" when a member says "I'm starting over"
- Members relocating to a new city can be connected to the organizations that welcome newcomers
- Business owners can be connected to chambers and professional associations
- Students can be connected to alumni chapters and mentorship programs

This is a core component of the ecosystems model (FSR-021).

---

## Founder Decisions Required

1. Should Community Organizations be a distinct platform entity type, separate from businesses?
2. What is the onboarding flow for a community organization that joins the platform?
3. Who manages their profile — the organization itself, a Cultural Ambassador, or both?
4. How are community organizations discovered — by KinfolkAI surfacing them, by a dedicated browse section, or both?
5. Are community organizations monetized differently from businesses (listing fees, promoted placement)?
