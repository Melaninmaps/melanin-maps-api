## The Idea

The Cultural Ambassador application (FSR-003) should include a specific set of questions that capture what makes an Ambassador irreplaceable — their specific knowledge, their community connections, and their willingness to give.

These are not screening questions. They are the foundation of what KinfolkAI knows about each Ambassador and how it connects them to members who need them.

---

## The Advisor's Cultural Ambassador Questions

When a member applies to become a Cultural Ambassador:

- Why do you want to become a Cultural Ambassador?
- Which communities do you represent?
- What stories deserve to be preserved?
- What places do visitors misunderstand?
- What traditions should never disappear?
- How do you hope to help newcomers?
- Would you mentor?
- Would you review historical submissions?
- Would you welcome relocated families?
- Would you speak at events?

---

## Why These Questions Matter

Each question unlocks a specific platform capability:

| Question | What It Enables |
|---------|----------------|
| "Which communities do you represent?" | KinfolkAI routes cultural questions to the right Ambassador |
| "What places do visitors misunderstand?" | Seeds the heritage correction/nuance system (FSR-009) |
| "What traditions should never disappear?" | Seeds Living Legacy Stories (FSR-001, FSR-008) |
| "How do you hope to help newcomers?" | Enables FSR-022 (Community Organization Connector) for Ambassador-guided relocation |
| "Would you mentor?" | Connects to FSR-005 (Structured Mentorship) |
| "Would you review historical submissions?" | Enables peer review of community-submitted heritage content |
| "Would you welcome relocated families?" | Creates a "Welcome Committee" feature within the Ambassador program |
| "Would you speak at events?" | Enables speaker directory within the events system |

---

## The Advisor's Description

> "I think this is where Kinfolk becomes magical."

The Ambassador is not just a power user with a badge. They are a person with specific, irreplaceable knowledge who has agreed to share it. KinfolkAI knows what each Ambassador knows and when to connect a member to them.

Example: A member relocating to DC mentions they're looking for community around go-go music. KinfolkAI knows which Cultural Ambassadors in DC have identified go-go culture as a tradition that should never disappear, and can surface a connection.

---

## Relationship to Existing Platform

FSR-003 (Cultural Ambassador Program) is already PARTIALLY BUILT. This FSR adds the specific activation questions to the existing program.

The trust level system (trustLevel 4 = Community Ambassador, manually granted by admin) is the current Ambassador infrastructure. These questions would become the narrative layer on top of that infrastructure.
