## The Idea

Most apps create feeds. Mapping With Melanin™ should create ecosystems.

A feed delivers content passively. An ecosystem understands a member's situation and connects them to the full web of what they need — without them having to search.

---

## What Makes an Ecosystem Different From a Feed

| Feed | Ecosystem |
|------|----------|
| Surfaces content you might like | Understands what you need |
| Reactive — you ask, it delivers | Proactive — it notices and offers |
| One-dimensional (content type) | Multi-dimensional (needs, goals, connections) |
| Grows by adding more content | Grows by understanding connections better |
| Same experience for everyone | Personalized to life situation |

---

## Platform Capabilities That Already Support This

The platform has the infrastructure to build ecosystems rather than feeds:
- Business categories (what kind of business)
- User identity flags (isBusinessOwner, isCommunityOrganizer)
- Smart pathways (KinfolkAI query templates)
- Life journeys (travel and goal tracking)
- Events (local happening awareness)
- Community feed (social layer)
- Kinfolk Circles (group connections)
- Opportunity center (jobs, mentorship)
- Marketplace (community listings)
- Topic library (knowledge)

The gap is: these capabilities are not connected by the intelligence layer. KinfolkAI currently handles chat. It does not weave these together proactively.

---

## What "Ecosystem" Means Architecturally

An ecosystem model means:
1. Member context (life stage, goals, situation) is known and maintained
2. Platform capabilities are mapped by what context they serve
3. KinfolkAI acts as the connector — surfacing the right capability at the right moment
4. Members don't need to know which tab has what they need

This is FSR-018 (KinfolkAI as Intelligence Layer) expressed architecturally.

---

## Founder Decisions Required

1. Is "ecosystems vs. feeds" the right frame for how the platform presents information?
2. What is the minimum context needed to activate an ecosystem for a new member?
3. Which community types (coffee shop owner, student, retiree, new parent) are highest priority for ecosystem mapping?
4. Does the Discover tab become the ecosystem surface, or does KinfolkAI become the entry point for ecosystem connections?
