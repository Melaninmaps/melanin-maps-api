# MAPPING WITH MELANIN™
## LIBRARY / CULTURE & ROOTS — SOURCE, TRUST & PROVENANCE FRAMEWORK

**STATUS:** Companion to Culture & Roots / Diaspora Discovery Spec. Founder-approved direction.  
**GATE:** Do not begin mass web ingestion until the trusted-source registry and provenance model are approved. Audit required first (see Section 41).

---

## PURPOSE

Culture & Roots should not simply aggregate whatever appears on the internet.

The Library and Kinfolk should pull from trusted sources appropriate to the TYPE of information being presented.

Every retrieved item should preserve:
- source_name · source_url · publisher / organization
- country / region · content_type · topic
- published_at · updated_at (where available) · retrieved_at
- language · source_tier · verification_status
- author (where available) · original_source (where syndicated)
- geographic_scope · community / culture tags
- citation / attribution data

Users should be able to see where important factual information came from.

---

## SOURCE PRINCIPLE

Use the strongest available source for the claim.

**PRIMARY / OFFICIAL** sources are preferred for:
laws · government policy · elections · public-health guidance · official statistics · travel requirements · immigration rules · heritage designations · government services · official tourism information · emergency information

**REPUTABLE JOURNALISM** is appropriate for:
breaking news · investigations · political developments · social developments · current events · business developments · entertainment news

**COMMUNITY / CULTURAL** sources are appropriate for:
local culture · community language · food traditions · neighborhood context · music scenes · festivals · creator perspectives · "What Locals Are Saying"

They should not replace authoritative sources for medical, legal, safety, or government facts.

---

## 1. COUNTRY / GEOGRAPHY / REGIONAL IDENTITY

**Preferred sources:** African Union · United Nations · official national governments · official national statistical agencies · recognized regional organizations

**AFRICA:** Use the African Union for the canonical 55-member-state Culture & Roots framework. Do not create country lists from blogs or commercial travel sites.

Also support official regional organizations: ECOWAS · East African Community · SADC · COMESA · IGAD · Arab Maghreb Union

Country naming should preserve aliases/synonyms for search while retaining one canonical geographic ID.

---

## 2. NEWS / CURRENT EVENTS

Use a multi-source model.

**Preferred high-trust international sources:** Reuters · Associated Press · BBC · Agence France-Presse · Al Jazeera · other established news organizations with professional editorial standards

Also prioritize reputable COUNTRY-BASED and REGIONAL journalism. The goal is NOT to tell African, Caribbean, Asian, Latin American or other diaspora stories only through U.S. or European outlets.

For each country/community, build an approved local/regional news-source list.

Where possible: LOCAL/REGIONAL SOURCE + INTERNATIONAL VERIFICATION SOURCE should be preferred for consequential stories.

**News should store:** headline · publisher · author · published date/time · updated date/time · country · topic · source URL · verification status

**Kinfolk should distinguish:** reported fact · analysis · opinion · editorial · unverified claim

Do not present opinion as verified news.

---

## 3. POLITICS / GOVERNMENT / ELECTIONS

**Preferred sources:** African Union · national government websites · national parliaments · official election commissions · constitutional courts / official judiciary sources · United Nations · regional political bodies

For U.S. diaspora communities: appropriate federal/state/local government sources.

For public sentiment / attitudes: Afrobarometer · reputable polling/research organizations · academic survey projects · national statistical/research bodies

**IMPORTANT:** Political information should distinguish: official government statement · opposition statement · independent reporting · public-opinion data · analysis

Kinfolk must not treat a government press release as neutral independent reporting.

---

## 4. SOCIAL / COMMUNITY CONDITIONS

**Preferred sources:** UNDP · UN Women · UNICEF · IOM · UNHCR · World Bank · national statistical agencies · African Development Bank · Afrobarometer · credible universities/research institutions · recognized civil-society organizations

Afrobarometer is especially useful for measured public opinion rather than assumptions about what "Africans think."

---

## 5. MEDICAL / HEALTH

**HIGH-STAKES SOURCE RULE.** Medical and public-health content must prioritize authoritative sources.

**AFRICA:** World Health Organization · WHO Regional Office for Africa · Africa CDC · national Ministries of Health · national public-health agencies

**Other:** CDC · NIH · peer-reviewed medical research · recognized medical associations · major academic medical centers

Use Africa CDC for Africa-specific public-health surveillance. Use WHO for international/country health guidance.

**Do NOT use:** influencer videos · general blogs · social posts · commercial wellness sites as the source of medical claims. They may appear as PERSONAL EXPERIENCE content only and must be labeled accordingly.

Health content should display: source · publication/update date · country/region applicability · medical-information disclaimer where appropriate

Kinfolk should never convert community experience into medical advice.

---

## 6. WOMEN'S HEALTH / MATERNAL HEALTH / FERTILITY

Use: WHO · Africa CDC where applicable · national health ministries · CDC / NIH where relevant · ACOG or equivalent recognized professional medical bodies · peer-reviewed medical literature · recognized local medical associations

Community stories may supplement—but NEVER replace—medical guidance.

---

## 7. CULTURE / HERITAGE / HISTORY

**Preferred sources:** UNESCO · UNESCO World Heritage Centre · national archives · national museums · national heritage authorities · universities · peer-reviewed scholarship · recognized museums · Smithsonian institutions · Library of Congress · historical societies · community archives · oral-history projects with documented provenance

History should NOT rely solely on tourism summaries.

When history is contested or interpreted differently: preserve the source · identify the perspective · allow multiple reputable sources. Do not silently flatten historical disagreement into one sentence.

---

## 8. BLACK / AFRICAN-AMERICAN HISTORY IN THE UNITED STATES

**Preferred sources:** Smithsonian National Museum of African American History and Culture · Library of Congress · National Archives · National Park Service · HBCU archives · state/local archives · Black historical societies · university archives · recognized museums · primary-source collections

For local history, community archives and oral histories are valuable but should preserve attribution.

---

## 9. INDIGENOUS HISTORY / CULTURE

**Highest priority: the Nation / Tribe / People themselves.**

Use: official tribal/nation websites · tribal cultural centers · tribal museums · tribal historic preservation offices · recognized Indigenous organizations · academic/archival sources developed with the community

Do not allow a generic external website to override how a people describes itself.

---

## 10. TOURISM / TRAVEL

**Preferred sources:** UN Tourism · official national tourism boards · official state/province tourism agencies · official city tourism organizations · national parks / heritage authorities · transportation authorities · airport/rail authority sites · recognized cultural institutions

Also allow reputable travel journalism for experience, neighborhood feel, itineraries, food, culture, reviews — but clearly distinguish editorial recommendations from official travel requirements.

---

## 11. ENTRY REQUIREMENTS / VISAS / PASSPORTS

ONLY use authoritative sources as the primary source:
- destination country's immigration ministry
- embassy / consulate
- official government visa portal
- U.S. State Department for U.S. passport/traveler context
- other relevant home-country foreign ministries

Commercial visa sites may be supplemental but should never override official government requirements. Show "last checked" date.

---

## 12. TRAVEL SAFETY

Use multiple layers:

**OFFICIAL:** national/local authorities · government travel advisories · embassy/consular alerts · public-health authorities

**MAPPING WITH MELANIN:** verified/moderated community safety reports · sundown-town historical layer · community incident patterns · THE REAL / community experience where relevant

**LOCAL:** reputable local journalism · recognized civil-rights/community organizations

> Do not treat one government advisory as the complete truth about the lived experience of minority travelers. Likewise, do not turn one community report into a factual declaration that a place is dangerous.

**Kinfolk should distinguish:** official advisory · historical context · verified community pattern · individual report

---

## 13. TOURISM + CULTURAL EXPERIENCE

The Library should show both:

**OFFICIAL TOURISM:** major sites · parks · museums · transport · visitor requirements · official events

**COMMUNITY TOURISM:** Black-owned tours · Indigenous-led tours · local cultural guides · diaspora food experiences · community festivals · heritage experiences · local creator recommendations

This is where Mapping With Melanin adds value beyond a standard tourism board.

---

## 14. ENTERTAINMENT

**Possible authoritative/high-quality sources:** official artist websites · official venue sites · official promoter/festival sites · ticketing/venue event feeds · record labels · film studios/distributors · official film festivals · music charts/trade publications · reputable entertainment journalism

For African/diaspora culture, prioritize reputable local and diaspora entertainment publications in addition to global outlets.

Do not let celebrity gossip or unsourced social posts become verified Library facts.

---

## 15. MUSIC

**Preferred:** artist's official source · record label · official venue/festival · recognized chart/trade source · music-industry organizations · reputable music journalism · local music/culture publications

User/creator videos may be embedded as community discovery but should preserve the original source/platform attribution.

---

## 16. FILM / TV

**Preferred:** official studio/network/streamer information · film festival · national film boards · recognized industry publications · reputable entertainment journalism

Community reviews and creators may supplement official information.

---

## 17. FOOD / CUISINE

Use multiple source types:
- business's official menu/site/social
- community reviews · cultural institutions
- recognized culinary historians · cookbooks / documented culinary scholarship
- tourism/culture authorities · local food journalism

Do NOT define a culture's cuisine from a single restaurant or influencer. Allow community terminology and regional variation.

---

## 18. BUSINESS INFORMATION

**Preferred source order:**
1. verified business-provided information
2. official business website
3. verified social-media account
4. public business/government registry where appropriate
5. Mapping With Melanin admin/community submission
6. reputable third-party directory as supplemental only

Store the ORIGINAL source. Community-added data should preserve: contributor · date · source URL · moderation status

---

## 19. BUSINESS OWNERSHIP / IDENTITY

Never infer race, ethnicity, nationality, gender identity, LGBTQIA+ identity, disability, religion, or Indigenous identity from: name · photo · neighborhood · language · business category

Use: self-identification · verified business submission · approved documentation where required · recognized certification · founder/admin verified source

Community suggestions may trigger verification but should not automatically establish identity.

---

## 20. ECONOMIC / BUSINESS / FINANCIAL CONTEXT

**Preferred:** World Bank · African Development Bank · IMF · national central banks · national statistical agencies · ministries of finance · official economic agencies · UNDP · reputable financial journalism

Use official/statistical sources for: GDP · inflation · employment · population · trade · development indicators. Use journalism for explanation/current developments.

---

## 21. POPULATION / DEMOGRAPHICS / STATISTICS

**Preferred:** national census/statistics agency · UN Statistics · World Bank · African Development Bank · UN Population Division · recognized official demographic sources

Always attach: year · geographic definition · source. Do not show stale statistics as current without the year.

---

## 22. EDUCATION

**Preferred:** national ministries of education · universities · accrediting bodies · UNESCO · UNICEF · official school/university sites

For HBCUs: institutional archives · official HBCU sites · Department of Education where relevant · recognized HBCU historical organizations

---

## 23. LANGUAGES

**Preferred:** official government sources · UNESCO · universities/linguistic institutes · recognized language academies · community cultural institutions

Africa is highly multilingual. Do not assign one language to a country and imply that everyone there speaks it.

Store: country · language · official status if applicable · regional/community use · source

---

## 24. RELIGION / SPIRITUALITY

**Preferred:** the faith institution itself · recognized denominational/religious organizations · academic religious studies sources · historical archives

Do not infer religion from country or ethnicity. Community-specific traditional/ancestral spiritual practices should use respectful community/academic sources.

---

## 25. LGBTQIA+ INFORMATION

**For LAW / LEGAL STATUS:** official legislation/courts/government · recognized human-rights organizations · reputable journalism

**For COMMUNITY RESOURCES:** verified LGBTQIA+ organizations · community centers · local organizations · verified businesses/events

Kinfolk must separate: law · safety advisory · community experience · cultural attitude. Do not use stereotypes about an entire country's people.

---

## 26. HUMAN RIGHTS / DISCRIMINATION

**Preferred:** United Nations human-rights bodies · African Commission on Human and Peoples' Rights · recognized national human-rights institutions · reputable civil-rights organizations · credible investigative journalism · court/government records

Serious allegations should be multi-source verified where possible.

---

## 27. MIGRATION / IMMIGRATION / DIASPORA

**Preferred:** IOM · UNHCR · official immigration authorities · national census/statistics agencies · government immigration resources · credible diaspora organizations · universities/research organizations

---

## 28. CLIMATE / WEATHER / ENVIRONMENT

**Preferred:** national meteorological agencies · World Meteorological Organization · UN environmental agencies · official disaster-management authorities · authoritative real-time weather API for live/current weather

Do not rely on static articles for current severe-weather alerts.

---

## 29. EVENTS / FESTIVALS

**Preferred order:** official event organizer · venue · city/community organization · official festival page · verified social account · local reputable event journalism

**Store:** event date · start/end time · location · coordinates · organizer · source URL · last verified

Expired events should not appear as upcoming.

---

## 30. LOCAL CULTURAL LANGUAGE / "WHAT LOCALS ARE SAYING"

Sources may include: Mapping With Melanin member contributions · approved local creators · community organizations · local media · verified repeated community usage

Kinfolk may identify candidate local phrases.

**DO NOT label a phrase "locals say" until there is sufficient community validation.**

Example: "jawn" (Philadelphia) may be recognized as local vocabulary, but Kinfolk should not force it into every Philadelphia interaction or imply every Philadelphian uses it.

---

## 31. SOCIAL MEDIA

Social platforms are DISCOVERY / ORIGINAL-CREATOR sources. They should NOT automatically be treated as fact-verification sources.

**Good uses:** creator video · business post · festival announcement · local cultural perspective · community story · artist announcement

For consequential factual claims: verify against a stronger source.

**Always retain:** original post URL · creator · platform · date · business/place/event linkage · moderation status

---

## 32. WIKIPEDIA / GENERAL REFERENCE SITES

May be used for: discovery · finding terminology · finding possible primary sources

Should NOT normally be the final authoritative source for high-stakes or contested information. Kinfolk/library ingestion should attempt to trace important claims back to the primary or recognized authoritative source.

---

## 33. SOURCE TRUST LEVELS

### TIER 1 — AUTHORITATIVE / PRIMARY
government · AU · UN agencies · WHO · Africa CDC · official statistics · official institution · court/law · official business/event/artist source

### TIER 2 — VERIFIED PROFESSIONAL
Reuters/AP/etc. · major reputable journalism · peer-reviewed research · universities · recognized museums · recognized NGOs/research organizations

### TIER 3 — COMMUNITY / SPECIALIST
local cultural publication · community organization · verified creator · oral history · community-contributed Mapping With Melanin content

### TIER 4 — DISCOVERY ONLY
general directories · aggregators · unverified social posts · general blogs

Tier 4 content should trigger additional verification before consequential claims are surfaced.

---

## 34. TOPIC-SPECIFIC MINIMUM SOURCE RULES

| Topic | Minimum Source Required |
|---|---|
| MEDICAL | Tier 1 / peer-reviewed Tier 2 required |
| LEGAL | official law/court/government primary source required where possible |
| POLITICAL OFFICEHOLDER / ELECTION RESULT | official election/government source + reputable reporting |
| BREAKING NEWS | at least one reputable professional source; multi-source for consequential claims |
| HISTORY | recognized archive/museum/academic/primary source |
| TOURISM | official tourism/destination source for logistics; community/editorial may supplement experience |
| SAFETY | official + moderated community/contextual sources; clearly label the type |
| BUSINESS OWNERSHIP | self-identified/verified — never inferred |

---

## 35. FRESHNESS / UPDATE RULES

**VERY FRESH (periodically refresh):** breaking news · weather · travel advisories · entry requirements · elections · events · public-health alerts · business hours

**MODERATELY FRESH:** tourism information · government programs · economic indicators · community organizations

**HISTORICAL (do not refresh merely for recency):** archives · heritage · historical context

Store: last_verified_at · source_updated_at (where available)

---

## 36. CONFLICTING SOURCES

Do not silently choose whichever source Kinfolk finds first.

If reputable sources conflict: show the uncertainty · prefer the relevant primary source for official facts · retain multiple perspectives where interpretation is disputed

Example: Government says X. Independent reporting says Y. Kinfolk should identify those as different sources/perspectives.

---

## 37. BIAS / LOCAL VOICE RULE

Do not build Culture & Roots primarily from U.S./European descriptions of other communities.

For every region/country, actively seek: local institutions · local journalism · local cultural organizations · local scholars · local creators · official national sources — alongside international verification sources.

Mapping With Melanin should help communities see themselves represented, not merely described from outside.
