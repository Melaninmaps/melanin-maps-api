## DELTA ADDITIONS — BUSINESS VOCABULARY
### Travel Agents, Accountants, Insurance, Staffing, Events, Service Businesses

**STATUS:** Founder-approved vocabulary additions.  
**GATE:** These are ADDITIONS to the existing master vocabulary. Preserve all existing Vibes, THE REAL tags, What Stands Out? tags, counts, IDs, and historical data.

**General Rule for All Business Types:**
Every category/subcategory vocabulary should answer four distinct questions:
1. **WHAT ARE THEY?** → Category → Subcategory → Specialty → Services
2. **WHAT DOES IT FEEL LIKE?** → The Vibe (only where experiential Vibe makes sense)
3. **CAN I TRUST THEM WITH SOMETHING THAT MATTERS?** → THE REAL (for professionals/service providers/high-trust interactions)
4. **WHAT DID THE COMMUNITY QUICKLY NOTICE?** → What Stands Out?

Language should capture what a person would actually tell their family, group chat, neighbor, or friend. Do not infer user identity to decide which language appears. Eligibility comes from business Category + Subcategory.
