# Mapping With Melanin™ — Feature Constitution

**Effective:** July 26, 2026
**Status:** Structure created. Sections populated progressively after each area's experience audit is complete.
**Purpose:** One authoritative specification per major platform area. No guessing. No contradictory prompts. No lost conversations. Every audit, implementation wave, and future enhancement points back to this document.

---

## Governance Rule

A section of the Feature Constitution is only considered authoritative once:
1. The experience audit for that area has been completed (Phase 1)
2. The architecture review has been completed (Phase 2)
3. The founder has explicitly approved the specification ("Please implement." or "This section is approved.")

Until then, the section exists as a placeholder. A placeholder is not a specification.
