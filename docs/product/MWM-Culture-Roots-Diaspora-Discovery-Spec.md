# MAPPING WITH MELANIN™
## LIBRARY ADDENDUM — CULTURE & ROOTS / DIASPORA DISCOVERY

**STATUS:** Founder-approved direction for specification.  
**GATE:** Do not implement until current Library/search taxonomy is audited against this document.

---

## THE HEART OF THIS FEATURE

The Mapping With Melanin Library should allow members to explore:

- Where I am from
- Where my family is from
- My culture
- My roots
- My diaspora
- Communities I want to learn about
- Communities I want to support

This is NOT limited to business ownership.

It should connect members to culturally relevant:
- businesses
- heritage sites
- museums
- historical places
- HBCU/educational content
- events / festivals
- food / music / art
- travel information
- community organizations
- faith institutions where applicable
- language resources
- stories / local creators / community videos
- articles/resources
- Kinfolk recommendations

**USER-FACING NAME:** CULTURE & ROOTS

**Suggested prompt:** "Explore where you're from, what you grew up around, or a culture you want to know more about."

---

## SEARCH ARCHITECTURE

Allow users to search/browse by:

```
REGION
→ COUNTRY / TERRITORY
→ CULTURE / COMMUNITY
→ LANGUAGE (where useful)
→ DIASPORA / LOCAL COMMUNITY
→ TOPIC
```

**Example:**
- Africa → Ethiopia → Ethiopian → Amharic / Oromo / Tigrinya → Ethiopian diaspora → Food / businesses / history / travel / events / community

**Example:**
- Caribbean → Jamaica → Jamaican → Jamaican diaspora → Restaurants / businesses / music / events / history / travel

**Example:**
- Latin America / Caribbean → Cuba → Cuban → Afro-Cuban → Food / music / history / businesses / travel

Do not force users to choose only one root/community. Allow multiple Culture & Roots selections.

## TOP-LEVEL CULTURE / DIASPORA GROUPS

These should be broad discovery umbrellas, NOT replacements for specific country/community identities.

### 1. AFRICAN & AFRICAN DIASPORA
- Continental African
- African American / Black American
- Afro-Caribbean
- Afro-Latino / Afro-Latin
- African immigrant diaspora
- African-descended communities globally

### 2. CARIBBEAN
- English-speaking Caribbean
- French/French-Creole Caribbean
- Spanish-speaking Caribbean
- Dutch Caribbean
- Afro-Caribbean
- Indo-Caribbean
- other Caribbean identities as the taxonomy expands

### 3. LATINO / HISPANIC / LATIN AMERICAN
- Mexican / Puerto Rican / Dominican / Cuban
- Central American / South American
- Afro-Latino / Indigenous Latin American
- other country-specific identities

> **IMPORTANT:** Do not force "Latino" and "Hispanic" to mean exactly the same thing. Country/community-specific identity should be selectable wherever possible.

### 4. INDIGENOUS / NATIVE COMMUNITIES
- Native American / American Indian
- Alaska Native
- Native Hawaiian
- Indigenous Caribbean / Indigenous Latin American
- Indigenous African communities where culturally appropriate
- First Nations / other Indigenous communities where future geography supports them

> Do not flatten tribal/nation identities into one generic Indigenous label where specific nation/community data exists.

### 5. ASIAN & ASIAN DIASPORA
- East Asian / South Asian / Southeast Asian / Central Asian
- Asian American
- country/community-specific identities

### 6. MIDDLE EASTERN / NORTH AFRICAN (MENA)
- North African / Arab / Middle Eastern
- Amazigh / Berber where appropriate
- country/community-specific identities

> **IMPORTANT:** North African countries must ALSO remain available under AFRICA. Do not force a user to choose between "African" and "MENA."

### 7. PACIFIC ISLANDER / OCEANIAN
- Native Hawaiian / Samoan / Chamorro / Tongan / Fijian
- other Pacific Island communities

### 8. IMMIGRANT / REFUGEE / NEW AMERICAN COMMUNITIES
- Immigrant-owned / immigrant community
- Refugee community / First-generation / Second-generation / diaspora
- newcomer resources

These are contextual/community descriptors and should not erase country or cultural roots.

### 9. LGBTQIA+ COMMUNITIES
- LGBTQIA+ community / LGBTQIA+-owned businesses / LGBTQIA+-friendly spaces/resources

LGBTQIA+ is not an ethnicity or nationality and should remain a separate identity/community facet that can intersect with every Culture & Roots selection.

### 10. WOMEN / GENDER-FOCUSED COMMUNITY DISCOVERY
- Women-owned / Women-centered resources
- culturally specific women-centered spaces where applicable

Intersects with Culture & Roots rather than replacing it.

### 11. DISABILITY COMMUNITY
- Disability-owned / Accessibility-focused / disability community resources

### 12. VETERAN / MILITARY COMMUNITY
- Veteran-owned / Military family/community resources

### 13. FAITH / SPIRITUAL COMMUNITY
OPTIONAL intersecting discovery facet, not a presumed identity:
- Christian / Muslim / Jewish / Hindu / Buddhist
- traditional / ancestral spiritual practices
- other approved faith/spiritual communities

> Never infer faith from nationality or ethnicity.

## KINFOLK CONNECTION

Kinfolk should be able to understand explicit statements such as:
- "I'm Ethiopian."
- "My family is from Ghana."
- "I'm Puerto Rican and Dominican."
- "I want to reconnect with my Nigerian roots."
- "I want to learn more about Haiti."
- "Show me Jamaican events near me."
- "Find Cuban-owned businesses."

The user should control whether that becomes saved profile context.

**Suggested prompt after relevant interaction:**
> "Would you like Kinfolk to remember that this culture/community matters to you?"
> - YES
> - NOT NOW

Do not automatically infer roots from: name · appearance · location · language · business searches · friends/family · or other proxy data.
