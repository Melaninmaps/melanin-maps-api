# Mapping With Melanin Endorsement System

> **Source of truth:** `UPDATEDVIBESCATEGORYforbusinesses-1.xlsx`, governed 2026-09-24.

Quick Reviews are one-tap, praise-only Community Feedback. They are selected by business category and subcategory, never inferred from a member’s identity. The canonical machine-readable catalog is `data/endorsement_tags_seed.json`.

## Safeguards

- Negative concerns, discrimination reports, and safety issues use private report/safety flows rather than endorsement tags.
- A business owner cannot award themselves member endorsements.
- Adaptive wording requires an explicit member preference and continues to roll up to one underlying tag count.
- Existing selected feedback remains retained even if a later workbook revision changes which choices are offered to new members.
