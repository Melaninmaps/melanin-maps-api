## 1. Business Classification Tiers

### Tier 1: Minority-Owned Businesses (PRIMARY)
- Always shown FIRST in search results and recommendations
- Full profile access, community reviews, Kinfolk AI recommendations
- Eligible for flash deals, creator partnerships, demand intelligence

### Tier 2: Allied Businesses (PASSIVE)
- Non-minority-owned businesses verified as safe spaces by the community
- **NEVER promoted above a minority-owned alternative in the same category**
- **NEVER contacted or alerted when first added** — remain passive until sufficient engagement
- Partnership pathway only after 500+ click-throughs from MWM
- Subject to immediate deprioritization if discrimination is reported

## 2. Safety Reporting — Two Types (CRITICAL DISTINCTION)

### Type A: Community Safety Concerns (Visitor/Customer Experience)
- Reporter has TRANSIENT relationship (visited once and left)
- Aggregation threshold: **2 independent reports within 60 days**
- Time delay: **7 days** before visibility
- Affects public-facing trust score
- Community sees: "Community safety concern reported"

### Type B: Unfair Labor & Workplace Practices (Employee Experience)
- Reporter has ONGOING daily relationship — extreme retaliation risk
- Aggregation threshold: **3+ independent reports over 90+ days**
- Time delay: **30-60 days** before any action
- Does NOT affect public-facing trust score initially
- Private warning to job-searching members only
- **Small team protection:** if fewer than 10 employees, requires 5+ reports

### Separation of Scores
1. **Visitor Safety Score** — Type A reports. Visible to all. Affects search ranking.
2. **Employer Trust Score** — Type B reports. Visible ONLY to job-seekers or Kinfolk queries about employment. Does NOT affect visibility as a place to visit/shop.

### Protection Mechanisms
1. Anonymous aggregation threshold — no action on a single report
2. Time-delayed visibility (7 days Type A / 30-60 days Type B)
3. Geographic fuzzing for Type B (brand level, not individual location)
4. No individual attribution — NEVER reveal report count, who filed, or metadata
5. Reporter identity stored as one-way hash
6. Retaliation detection — flags accounts reported shortly after they filed safety reports
7. Team size protection for Type B (small teams need higher thresholds)

## 3. Legal Protection Framework

- **Platform vs. Publisher** (Section 230): Never make editorial judgments. "Community members have shared concerns" — not "This business discriminates"
- **Graduated Response System:**
  - Level 1: No visible change (collecting data)
  - Level 2: Subtle deprioritization (invisible to business)
  - Level 3: "Community concerns noted" badge (business notified, can respond)
  - Level 4: Removed from recommendations but still searchable
  - Level 5: Delisted — requires founder/admin approval + human review
- **Human Review Gate:** Levels 3-5 require founder approval. No automated delisting.

## 5. Four-Tier Community Marketplace Model

### 1. Verified Business
Established, formally registered entities (LLC + permits). Primary search placement.

### 2. Community Entrepreneur
Informal businesses (home chefs, braiders, bead makers). 
- **ALWAYS FREE** — never charge individuals pursuing their craft
- Not in main business search by default
- Can upgrade to Verified Business when ready
- All history carries forward on upgrade

### 3. Community Professional
Minority individual working at a non-minority business.
- **ALWAYS FREE**
- The PERSON is listed, not the host business
- Endorsements follow the PERSON, not the location
- Host business NEVER notified

### 4. External Reference
Community-added, business hasn't claimed profile yet.

```
Community Professional → Community Entrepreneur → Verified Business
     (employed)              (side hustle)           (own shop)
         └── All history carries forward at each transition ──┘
```

Kinfolk celebrates milestones, never gates them.

## 7. Business Profile Card — Content Hierarchy (4 Layers)

1. **Layer 1 — The Business's Own Story** (personal content uploaded to MWM)
2. **Layer 2 — Community Voice** (aggregated endorsements, "1,001 people said...")
3. **Layer 3 — Cultural Ambassador Content** (creator videos/posts)
4. **Layer 4 — Outbound Link** (Book, Visit, See More — transaction happens on their turf)

> **Key Principle: MWM makes the introduction. The business owns the transaction.**

## 8. Direct Commerce (Verified Businesses Only)

| Commerce Type | Safety Integration |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Service Booking | Kinfolk reminder + Circle location sharing |
| Experience/Excursion | Itinerary shared with safety contacts |
| Event Tickets | Attendee connections; venue safety tips |
| Reservations | Travel plan integration; area safety context |

Transaction fee applies ONLY to verified businesses who opt in. Community Entrepreneurs ALWAYS retain option to link out to their own payment methods (Venmo, CashApp, etc.).

## 9. Kinfolk AI Talent Pipeline

- Trigger: Community Entrepreneur reaches 40+ reviews at 4.5+ stars
- Kinfolk prompts nearby relevant businesses: "A community member has exceptional reviews for Caribbean cuisine — looking for kitchen talent?"
- Kinfolk prompts the entrepreneur simultaneously
- **Cost: FREE for both sides** — organic ecosystem benefit

## 10. Accountability Without Surveillance

- Location data: used ONLY when explicitly granted, for stated purposes
- Search history: feeds recommendations, but user can view and delete
- Kinfolk AI explains its reasoning
- Users can correct Kinfolk's assumptions
- All data collection is permission-based with clear value proposition
