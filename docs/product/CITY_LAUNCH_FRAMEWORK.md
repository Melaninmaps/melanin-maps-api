## The Four-Section Launch Checklist

### Section 1 — PRE-LAUNCH

These are platform readiness gates. No city opens to members until all 11 are confirmed.

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Businesses Seeded** | Minimum viable business directory for the city — curated, vetted, geolocated |
| **Cultural Sites** | At least one cultural heritage site on the map (museums, landmarks, murals, HBCUs) |
| **Historical Sites** | Historical context sites loaded — neighborhoods, sundown-era markers, civil rights sites |
| **Community Resources** | Local nonprofits, legal aid, health clinics, shelters, food banks mapped |
| **Events** | Seed events loaded or connected to a local feed |
| **City Imagery** | Hero imagery for the city landing page — photographer-sourced, community-approved |
| **Moderation Review** | All seeded content reviewed for accuracy, sensitivity, and community standards |
| **Kinfolk City Context** | KinfolkAI city briefing written and tested — history, neighborhoods, culture, language |
| **Search Validation** | Search returns correct results for the 10 most common city-specific queries |
| **Map Validation** | All businesses and cultural sites render correctly on the map at all zoom levels |
| **Analytics Enabled** | City-specific analytics tracking active before first member approved |

---

### Section 2 — COMMUNITY

People who make the city real. These are recruitment targets, not platform features.

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Founding Members** | First cohort of community members approved — ideally neighbors, not strangers |
| **Founding Businesses** | 3–5 businesses who have committed to MWM before launch |
| **Ambassadors** | At least one Cultural Ambassador identified and onboarded |
| **Creators** | Content creators or photographers who will document the launch |
| **Volunteers** | People who will moderate, welcome newcomers, and seed conversations |
| **Local Organizations** | Community orgs, churches, civic groups, or institutions connected |

---

### Section 3 — MARKETING

How the city finds out. Executed in this order — never reversed.

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **City Landing Page** | Live at mappingwithmelanin.com/[city] — hero image, waitlist form, city context |
| **Launch Announcement** | Founder-approved copy ready — email, social, and press version |
| **Social Assets** | City-branded graphics for Instagram, X, and LinkedIn — no generic assets |
| **Founder Interview Prompts** | 10 questions about this city ready for founder-led content |
| **Local Press Checklist** | Local Black newspapers, radio stations, podcasts, and blogs identified |
| **City Hashtags** | Primary hashtag and 3–5 supporting hashtags defined and reserved |
| **Referral Campaign** | City-specific referral incentive active in the platform |

---

### Section 4 — OPERATIONS

How we keep it running without breaking anything.

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Feature Flags** | City-specific feature flags set in the platform (waitlist gating, rollout %) |
| **Rollout Percentage** | Initial approval rate set — start at 10–20%, scale weekly |
| **Monitoring** | Railway alerting configured for city-specific error spikes |
| **Crash Dashboard** | Sentry or equivalent filter active for city traffic |
| **Waitlist Activation** | Waitlist open and accepting registrations |
| **Rollback Plan** | Documented — who does what if something breaks on launch day |

## Launch Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Planning** | City is on the roadmap. No public-facing work yet. |
| **Pre-Launch** | Checklist in progress. Waitlist may be open. No members approved. |
| **Soft Launch** | Founding member cohort approved. Limited access. Monitoring closely. |
| **Live** | Full rollout active. Waitlist approval rate at target. |
| **Paused** | Launch halted. Documented reason required before resuming. |

## Operational Playbook — City Launch Day

### T-30 days
- Begin seeding businesses and cultural sites
- Recruit founding members and ambassadors from community network
- Publish city landing page in stealth mode (no indexing)

### T-14 days
- Complete all Pre-Launch checklist items
- Test KinfolkAI city context with 20 realistic queries
- Run map validation across all business and cultural site pins
- Photograph city content or source from community photographers

### T-7 days
- Complete all Community section checklist items
- Brief founding members on what to expect
- Finalize all Marketing section assets
- Set feature flags and rollout percentage
- Run operations checklist

### T-1 day
- Final moderation sweep of all seeded content
- Confirm founder-approved launch announcement is ready
- Confirm rollback plan is documented and accessible

### Launch Day
- Approve founding member cohort (first 20–50 members)
- Publish launch announcement across all channels
- Monitor Railway logs and crash dashboard continuously for first 4 hours
- Founder check-in at hour 1, 4, and 24

### T+7 days
- First community health check — member activity, business engagement, post volume
- Identify any content gaps surfaced by real member usage
- Scale rollout percentage based on stability and demand

## What Makes a City Ready

A city is ready when the community is real before the platform opens — not when the database is seeded. The checklist clears technical gates. The community section clears the human gate.

**No city goes live without at least one founding member who asked us to open it.**
