# Mapping With Melanin Vibe Taxonomy

> **Source of truth:** `UPDATEDVIBESCATEGORYforbusinesses-1.xlsx`, governed 2026-09-24.

Vibes describe atmosphere, visit type, or energy. They are never ownership claims, quality scores, or professional-service evaluations. The file in `data/vibe_tags.json` preserves the exact workbook wording and helper text.

## Routing

- **The Vibe** appears only for workbook-approved atmosphere categories and subcategories.
- **The Real** replaces atmosphere labels for professional/trust-sensitive service categories and, per founder direction, every Education & Learning listing plus Children & Family → Childcare only.
- **Community Feedback** uses the separate praise-only Quick Reviews vocabulary. Concerns stay in the existing private report and safety flows.
- Existing selections are retained; this catalog adds the approved vocabulary without deleting historical feedback.
