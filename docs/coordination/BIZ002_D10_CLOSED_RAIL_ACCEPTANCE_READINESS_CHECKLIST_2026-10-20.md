## 4. Closed-rail acceptance sequence

This is an ordering checklist, not authorization to perform any step. A later step cannot be treated as passed because an earlier decision is blank.

1. **Commercial decision packet complete:** The Founder and accountable business commerce/operations owner complete every row in Section 3 with unambiguous plan vocabulary, benefit boundaries, included/add-on treatment, price mapping, ranking/placement disclosure, and operating owner.
2. **Legal/operations disposition complete:** The appropriate owners approve the terms, tax/merchant posture, cancellation/refund/reversal/rollback rules, dispute ownership, and support/fulfillment process. This checklist does not supply those decisions.
3. **Source reconciliation and controlled implementation review:** A separately authorized owner maps approved decisions to the canonical entitlement/catalog and delivery surfaces. Conflicting price or benefit copy must be resolved before any rail is considered.
4. **No-charge, no-provider staging readiness review:** Only after separate authorization, a controlled staging plan may be reviewed. It must exclude payment-provider activation, checkout, charge, subscription, purchase, and provider account/trial creation unless separately approved.
5. **Authenticated acceptance evidence:** Authorized representative roles exercise the agreed no-charge flows, including eligibility, promised deliverables, transparent placement disclosure, error/recovery, support handoff, and rollback/reversal representation. Capture role, build/candidate identity, environment, observed outcome, and errors.
6. **Founder rail-opening decision:** A separate explicit decision may consider opening an identified payment rail only after all prior decisions and evidence are complete. Nothing in this document opens a rail.
