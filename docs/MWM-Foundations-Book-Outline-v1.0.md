Do not build, modify, generate, commit, deploy, or replace any existing materials until the product owner explicitly authorizes implementation.

---

## Purpose

The Mapping With Melanin™ Foundations Book is the permanent, living record of:
- Why the company exists
- How the brand communicates
- How products and features are developed
- How designers, developers, contractors, volunteers, and AI partners work with the company
- How the community contributes
- How the launch, tour, documentary, and cultural history evolve
- How the company protects its identity as it grows

This is the internal "Bible" for Mapping With Melanin™ — a continuously updated brand, product, culture, development, and historical archive.

---

## Format (Five Connected Forms)

1. **Master Foundations Book** — Full internal version. PDF and editable presentation. Contains all standards, policies, operating principles, and historical documentation.
2. **Presentation Version** — Polished visual version for team members, partners, contractors, and selected stakeholders. Shorter and easier to present.
3. **Video Version** — Narrated visual version including founder voice, community footage, city footage, music, interviews, and app demonstrations.
4. **Documentary Archive** — Long-term collection of approved photos, interviews, stories, music, video footage, and tour documentation for the official documentary.
5. **Public Heritage Version** — Carefully selected public-facing version that tells the company story without revealing confidential development, financial, legal, or trade-secret information.

---

## Structural Rule

The complete Foundations Book does NOT automatically appear in every presentation.

- The **Master Deck** contains the complete Foundations Book.
- Individual decks include a short final page: "Built From Our Foundations" / "Our Storytelling Standards" / "The Mapping With Melanin™ Foundation"
- That page may link internally to the full Foundations Book when appropriate.
- Selected standards may appear as appendices for team, designer, developer, volunteer, and ambassador presentations.
- The Foundations Book is the source of truth. Other decks inherit from it.

## Build Phases

**Phase One — Written Source of Truth**
Create complete written content first. Do not begin with visual slides. Every section reviewed and approved as text before design begins.

**Phase Two — Visual System**
After written content is approved: define section templates, apply approved colors and typography, apply custom gold outlined icons, define photography layouts, define video placeholders, define archival captions, create version and date markers.

**Phase Three — Master Book**
Build the complete internal version.

**Phase Four — Audience Editions**
Create condensed versions for: designers and developers, Cultural Ambassadors, volunteers, businesses, investors, community members, documentary collaborators.

**Phase Five — Living Archive**
Add approved tour photography, member stories, city pages, business stories, artist submissions, interviews, launch milestones, community impact, press, documentary updates.
