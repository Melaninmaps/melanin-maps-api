## Governing Principle

> The platform's geographic availability and the Welcome Home Tour schedule are intentionally independent. Tour-specific experiences are activated only by founder authorization and are never tied automatically to supported cities or future roadmap locations. This protects founder safety, preserves editorial control, and ensures authentic community engagement.

---

## The Core Distinction

A city can be **platform-ready** long before it is **tour-active**. These are two separate and independent states.

### Example

**Atlanta — Platform Ready:**
- KinfolkAI™ understands Atlanta
- Cultural landmarks available and searchable
- Historically and culturally significant locations pre-built
- Safety features active
- Businesses can join
- Community members can participate
- "Shout Your City" and city-specific features functional
- 20+ cities including KinfolkAI cultural data, safety intelligence, and curated locations are live

**Atlanta — Tour Active:**
- Welcome Home Tour banner
- Community meetups
- Founder events
- Documentary filming
- City-specific tour challenges
- Tour-exclusive content
- Cultural Ambassador spotlights
- Live tour storytelling

When the tour moves to New Orleans:
- Atlanta remains fully functional as a platform city
- Atlanta's Tour Mode quietly ends
- New Orleans becomes the active Tour City

## Two Independent Status Systems

### Platform Status

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Not Available | City not yet on the platform |
| Early Access | City added, limited data and features |
| Active | Full platform features, businesses, safety intelligence |
| Mature | Deep community data, high engagement, full KinfolkAI cultural context |

### Tour Status

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Not Scheduled | No tour plans (default for all cities) |
| Upcoming | Private/internal only until authorized by founder |
| Live | Tour actively happening in this city |
| Completed | Tour concluded, transitioning to Living Archive |
| Legacy Archive | Permanently archived — part of the institutional record |

**These statuses are never automatically linked.**

---

## Four City Types

| Type | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Platform City | Exists on Mapping With Melanin™ |
| Featured City | Editorial spotlight — curated content and attention |
| Tour City | Active or planned Welcome Home Tour stop |
| Legacy City | Tour completed, permanently archived |

These are four different concepts. Separating them provides maximum editorial and safety flexibility.

---

## Tour Activation Safeguards

### Delayed Activation
- Spend the day in a city filming and meeting businesses
- Activate Tour content that evening or the following morning
- Never activate in real-time as you arrive

### Gradual Deactivation
- Tour content remains active briefly after departure
- Captures photos, stories, and community engagement
- Then transitions to Completed and Living Archive

**The app celebrates where you've been — not where you are.**
