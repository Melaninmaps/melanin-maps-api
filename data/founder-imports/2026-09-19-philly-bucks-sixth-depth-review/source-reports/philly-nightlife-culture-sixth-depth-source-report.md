## Corpus exclusion and deduplication

Before retaining or holding a row, the process indexed **320** JSONL records from **19** required corpus files in these exact 2026-09-18 directories: `philadelphia`, `philadelphia-deep-pass`, every `philly-city-*-depth` directory, and every `pa-suburbs-*-depth` directory. The final corpus set contained 19 JSONL files across ten folders.

The exact method first lowercased and stripped punctuation from `name`, `city`, and `state`, then rejected any exact normalized `(name, city, state)` match. It also normalized every non-null `website`, `instagramUrl`, `facebookUrl`, `tiktokUrl`, and `socialSourceUrl` to a lowercase hostname after stripping protocol, `www`, path, query, and trailing slash. A row was rejected if any normalized official-destination hostname matched an indexed prior destination. The same checks ran across the newly built candidates and held records to prevent internal duplicates. The resulting counts were **0 normalized name/city/state collisions** and **0 normalized official-destination collisions** with the prior corpus; there were **0 internal normalized name/city/state collisions**.

This domain-level destination check is intentionally conservative. It can exclude a distinct program that shares a parent organization’s official domain, but reduces the risk of replaying an existing official customer destination.

## Review constraints and known limitations

Several nightlife records were routed to `regulated_review` because their reviewed official pages explicitly describe alcohol or food-and-drink service, or because they are food-service venues. This routing is procedural, not a statement that a license exists or is current. The records only state a regulated-profession note where source wording supports the service category.

World Cafe Live is source-backed through Visit Philadelphia’s official visitor guide, which supplied the address, official domain, and service summary. A direct fetch of the official domain produced no extractable text, so its details should be reconfirmed from the official site before any downstream action. Mural Arts Philadelphia’s reviewed official pages establish genuine tour and ticketing services but did not expose a street-number address; it is retained solely as a routed community resource and must not produce a pin from this intake.

Solar Myth and The Ave Live remain held because official pages did not provide a street-number address. The Photographic Society of Philadelphia remains held because the reviewed official pages lacked both a physical address and a clear current enrollment destination. Phila Photo Arts remains held because the official site is a blog without a demonstrated customer service offering or address. All unknown fields are JSON `null`, never the string `"null"`.
