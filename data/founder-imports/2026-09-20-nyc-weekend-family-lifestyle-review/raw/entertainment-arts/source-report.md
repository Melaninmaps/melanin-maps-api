## Deduplication decisions

Records were deduplicated on normalized **name + street address/city** before writing. The following decisions are particularly material:

| Potential overlap | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Snug Harbor Cultural Center & Botanical Garden / Staten Island Museum | Retained both | They are separate visitor destinations on the same campus; the museum is Building A and Snug Harbor is the broader culture park. |
| Apollo Historic Theater / Apollo Stages at The Victoria | Retained one active candidate and held the other | They are distinct numbered venues. The source marks the Historic Theater temporarily closed, while it lists current events at The Victoria. |
| Brooklyn Museum / Brooklyn Botanic Garden / Brooklyn Children’s Museum | Retained all | Separate institutions with distinct numbered street addresses. |
| Museum of the Moving Image / Socrates Sculpture Park / Queens Museum | Retained MoMI and Socrates; held Queens Museum | Separate Queens destinations; Queens Museum lacked a public numbered street address in the opened official evidence. |
| El Museo del Barrio / Museum of the City of New York | Retained both | Distinct named museums with separate numbered Fifth Avenue addresses. |

## Limitations and handling notes

The research used official customer-facing, institutional, Smithsonian, or municipal sources that were actually opened. Several official URLs produced no extractable content, an error, an obsolete 404 page, or a domain-for-sale page; these are recorded in the ledger and were not used as sole evidence. Intersection-only or park-building-only locations were held rather than converted into a made-up street number. Fotografiska New York was held because its own page says the NYC chapter concluded in 2024. Citi Field was held because the opened official transportation page did not provide a numbered address. `sourceStatus` describes evidence disposition, not a claim that records are published or live.
