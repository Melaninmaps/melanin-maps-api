# NYC Faith, Community, and Calm Social/Dining Research Ledger

## Review summary

This review produced **18 submitted candidates** and **4 held candidates**. The submitted file is a source-backed weekend inventory spanning faith and worship, public cultural or community destinations, and publicly described Venezuelan and Mexican dining. It covers Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, and Staten Island. It does not claim that any venue is live in an application database, does not publish records, and does not use coordinates or geocoding.

All source URLs in the ledger below were opened and read. Official customer, visitor, or institutional pages were preferred. The site addresses, contact details, public descriptions, and social profiles in the JSONL rows are limited to what those pages displayed. Current schedules, admission, access arrangements, and service availability should be reconfirmed with the listed organization.

## Limitations and review boundaries

The research does not infer race, ethnicity, sexuality, religion, gender, price, owner identity, safety, accessibility, operating hours, or visitor profile beyond explicit source text. In particular, the dining records are cuisine-based; no Black-owned listing is asserted because no ownership evidence was collected that met the requested threshold. The three official pages that lacked a numbered address and the two sources that were unreadable or unusable are held rather than force-fit into the submitted inventory. No medical, legal, or other regulated service is submitted.

**No-publication statement.** These files are source-backed candidates for protected review only. No application database was written, no publication endpoint was called, no live-record status is claimed, and no coordinates or geocoding were created or used.
