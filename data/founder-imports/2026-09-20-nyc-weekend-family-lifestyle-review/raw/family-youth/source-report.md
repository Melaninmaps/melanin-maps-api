# NYC Family & Youth Weekend Lifestyle Directory — Source Report

## Scope and review status

This source-backed research covers **one NYC directory category only**: age-14-friendly weekend activities, gaming/anime-compatible outings, recreation, family entertainment, and daytime experiences. The reviewed inventory favors official customer-facing and institutional visitor pages across all five boroughs. It is prepared for protected review only. **No application database was written, no publication endpoint was called, and no record is claimed to be live.** No coordinates were collected, created, inferred, or included.

## Deduplication decisions

Records were normalized conceptually by lowercased venue name plus street address and city before output. No submitted candidates shared the same normalized name/address pair. The two pages used for Kick Axe are deliberately **one** submitted venue record, not duplicates: the activity page substantiates age and offerings, while the official contact page substantiates the street address. The Bronx Zoo and Queens Zoo are distinct destinations with distinct names and addresses. No separate location-only entries for the New York Transit Museum’s Grand Central gallery/store or 2 Broadway shop were included, because the submitted record is the primary Brooklyn museum experience.

## Limitations and safeguards

The inventory is a selective, high-yield weekend/daytime research set rather than a claim of comprehensive NYC coverage. URLs, offerings, event calendars, admission terms, and access requirements can change; reviewers should revisit primary sources before any later use. Some sources used a general campus or institution address rather than a separate attraction suite, and such use is stated only where the source itself presents that address. Queens Museum was held rather than supplied with an inferred street number. Social links are recorded only where they were linked on the source opened. No ownership, race, ethnicity, sexuality, religion, gender, price, accessibility, safety, operating-hours, patron-identity, or cultural-affiliation inference was made. The report intentionally does not rate affordability or safety and does not profile an audience.

**No-publication statement:** These files are protected-review research artifacts only. They have not been sent to an application database, map, publication system, or live directory.
