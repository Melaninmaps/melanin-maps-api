# NYC Beauty & Wellness Directory Research — Source Report

**Scope.** This source-backed research covers one directory category: beauty, natural-hair/locs, nail care, wellness, and fitness options across the five New York City boroughs. The review intentionally mixes commercial customer destinations with clearly identified community resources and one explicitly mapless online service. It does not infer owner identity, racial or cultural affiliation, price, accessibility, safety, availability, operating hours, or medical suitability. Explicit ownership designations appear only where an opened source made that statement.

**Outcome.** **17 candidates** are submitted for protected review and **6 plausible leads are held**. The submitted file is a research artifact only; no database was written, no publication endpoint was called, and no record is claimed to be live. No coordinates were collected, created, inferred, or geocoded.

## Deduplication decisions

Records were deduplicated on normalized name plus street address/city. Hair & Co BKLYN is retained twice because the opened official page lists **two different numbered Brooklyn locations**, with independently useful phone numbers. Brooklyn Strength’s historical addresses were not submitted because its official page describes the present service as virtual; only the mapless online-business record remains. The Vagaro URL for She & Her did not produce a readable listing and is not a separate record. LA Fitness locations are separate businesses by different street address/club page. No candidate is duplicated in the submitted/held files.

## Limitations and review notes

This is a conservative discovery set, not a current-operating-status audit. Source pages can change after review; organizations with “final season,” construction/presale, membership, appointment, or customer-platform conditions should be rechecked before any downstream use. The Brooklyn Wellness Club’s site says it is in its final season at 704 Dekalb. Fitness Factory Harlem is held because its own site says opening/classes are coming soon. Queens Natural Hair Stylist is included only with its public-directory caveat because Fresha says the business is unaffiliated. Bronx House, House of Hair, and the attempted Nadia Vassell contact page were not promoted because their opened sources could not provide sufficient readable evidence.
