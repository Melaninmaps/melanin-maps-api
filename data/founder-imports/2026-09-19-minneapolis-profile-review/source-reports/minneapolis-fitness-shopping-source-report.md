# Minneapolis–St. Paul Fitness and Shopping Research Report

**Category:** Minneapolis fitness and shopping
**Research status:** Source-backed discovery package for review, not a production directory import
**Candidate records:** 15
**Held records:** 8

## Scope and result

This package researches one Minneapolis–St. Paul metro category: **boutique fitness, yoga, Pilates, wellness memberships, fashion, jewelry, gifts, and self-care shopping**. It includes Minneapolis, Saint Paul, and clearly metro municipalities only. The stated voluntary discovery preference was used solely to prioritize relevant fitness, beauty, gifts, fashion, and cultural-shopping leads. No further identity, health, financial, language, accessibility, quality, price, licensing, ownership, or service facts were inferred from that preference, business names, or imagery.

The candidate file contains **15 customer-facing destinations** with an official website or official customer destination and a credible Black, Latino/Latine, or diaspora-related lead source. Most physical-business addresses were confirmed by the official site. Where a credible tourism or directory source supplied the numbered address while the official site supported an in-store location but did not surface that number, the limitation is stated in the row notes. Lions Gym is set to `regulated_review` because its public listing says it offers chiropractic care and physiotherapy; this package makes no credential or licensing finding about those services.

## Search sources opened and used

The principal Black-business discovery sources were the Minnesota Black-Owned Business Directory’s sports-and-fitness category and individual business pages, plus Meet Minneapolis’s Black-owned boutiques and shops guide. The directory describes itself as a Minnesota Black-business directory created to strengthen the state’s Black community; its individual records identify Black owners for 612 Jungle, Lions Gym, and The F.I.T. Lab. [1] [2] [3] [4] Meet Minneapolis explicitly frames its guide as Black-owned Minneapolis boutiques and shops and supplied the shopping leads that were then checked against customer-facing sites. [5]

Visit Saint Paul’s Black-owned-business page supplied Saint Paul leads and numbered addresses for Black Garnet Books and Lip Esteem. [6] Latino/Latine and diaspora-related leads were sourced through Meet Minneapolis’s Latin American shopping guide, Mercado Central’s customer site, and the MN Latinos Directory. The city guide describes the Twin Cities Latino/Latine business context and lists Los Vaqueros, Mercado Central, and related shopping destinations. [7] Mercado Central’s site says it fosters business development for Latinos in Minneapolis. [8] MN Latinos describes Plaza Mexico as a Hispanic mall and Primitiva’s founder as Colombian; neither claim was converted into an unsupported ownership claim. [9] [10]

The following official customer destinations were opened to confirm physical or online availability, addresses where displayed, phone numbers where displayed, services, product categories, and official social links: 612 Jungle, 57FIT, Lions Gym, The F.I.T. Lab, Kobi Co., Black Garnet Books, Bouquets by Carolyn, Top 2 Bottom, 36 Lyn, Los Vaqueros, Mercado Central, Rocky Robinson, Embellish ALC, Style a Go-Go, and Lip Esteem. ## Dedupe and data-quality checks

Dedupe checks were performed within this category using normalized business name, official-domain comparison, phone comparison, and numbered-address comparison when available. No accepted candidate duplicates another accepted candidate. The fitness check identified the current **The F.I.T. Lab** Saint Paul footer address as different from an older directory address; the official-site address was retained and the discrepancy is disclosed in the candidate note. The same check identified two different official-site addresses for **Fit 1st Running**; that business is held with no chosen address. **Plaza Mexico** and **Mercado Central** are retained as separate multi-merchant shopping destinations because their names, addresses, and customer sites differ.

## Held leads and limitations

Eight leads are deliberately in `held-fitness-shopping.jsonl`, not candidates. 5bulouz has a Black-owned city-guide lead but no current numbered official address surfaced. Fit 1st Running has conflicting official addresses. Sheer Treasures, Mystic Healing Stones, and Angie’s Hats lack a sufficiently complete physical or online classification on the pages inspected. Primitiva has an MN Latinos physical listing while its official site is an e-commerce site without a physical-location confirmation; it is held rather than forced into either record type. Pretty Girls Boutique’s official page could not be extracted for review. Style a Go-Go is described by the city guide as online and mobile, so it is not clearly online-only and has no verified physical address.

This is a research-only artifact. **No production database, API, map pin, geocoding, deployment, authentication, users, sessions, payments, or waitlist records were changed.** No coordinates were generated or stored.
