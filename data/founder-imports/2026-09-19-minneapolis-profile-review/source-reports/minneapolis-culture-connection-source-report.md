# Minneapolis culture and connection — research report

## Scope and method

This research package covers **only Minneapolis and Saint Paul** for the category **“Minneapolis culture and connection.”** It emphasizes diaspora cultural places, galleries and museums, performance, Black and Latino business organizations, professional-networking resources, and community-facing resources. The stated voluntary discovery preference was used only to prioritize potentially useful culture, connection, dining, wellness-adjacent, entertainment, and professional-life categories; no additional identity, health, financial, ownership, quality, accessibility, price, language, licensing, or service claims were inferred.

Every accepted record has an opened public source and a customer-facing official destination. Cultural and community organizations were accepted where their official page or an independent civic/destination source explicitly supported a Black, African American, Somali, Hmong, Latino/Latine, Jamaican, Kenyan, or comparable diaspora/community connection. Physical businesses additionally required a current numbered address on the official site. Social fields appear only where the official page linked to that destination. Unknown values are `null` in JSONL.

## Results

The candidate file contains **19 accepted records**: **six cultural places, eight community resources, and five physical businesses**. The held file contains **6 leads** that did not clear the address, current-destination, or official-destination bar. No online-only business was accepted, and no licensed or regulated provider was entered. The one CLUES record represents the explicit Latino Art Gallery and arts-programming destination, rather than clinical services.

## Dedupe and scope checks

A manual dedupe check normalized names, official root domains, and numbered street addresses within this category. It found **no duplicate accepted entity or duplicate physical destination**. The two types of location-specific deconfliction were deliberate: Afro Deli is limited to its official Stadium Village Minneapolis location; Pimento’s temporarily closed locations were excluded. CLUES is represented once as its distinct **Latino Art Gallery** cultural destination rather than duplicated as a generalized community-service record. Similar subject matter is not treated as a duplicate when organizations are separate entities with different official addresses and missions, such as the Minnesota Black Chamber, Black Business Network, Urban League Twin Cities, and Minnesota Black Collective Foundation.

All candidate addresses are in Minneapolis or Saint Paul. The State Fair Afro Deli location in Falcon Heights was excluded because it is not in the requested city scope and is seasonal. No other priority city, Philadelphia, or international location was researched or included.

## Limitations and holds

The held file is intentional rather than a secondary candidate list. The Minnesota Latino Museum describes a future museum location rather than a current public museum; Teatro del Pueblo’s opened official site did not publish a fixed venue address; AICS’s opened official pages described Twin Cities services but did not publish a numbered target-city address; and the African Development Center city-event lead did not supply an official customer-facing destination. MAAMCC was held because its opened page lacked a numbered address and included unrelated third-party advertising-style content. Phyllis Wheatley Community Center remained held because a public state-directory lead was not enough without a verified customer-facing official destination.

Listings and official pages can change after this research pass. Address, hours, availability, accessibility, pricing, licensing, language, capacity, quality, and all offerings should be rechecked before any public-facing use. The package does not make ownership claims beyond exact source-backed designations; for example, Capri’s record describes a source-backed Black cultural context but leaves `ownershipDesignations` as `null`.

## Research-only status

This was **research only**. **No production DB, API, map pin, geocoding, deployment, authentication, users, sessions, payments, or waitlist records were changed.** No coordinates were created or included.
