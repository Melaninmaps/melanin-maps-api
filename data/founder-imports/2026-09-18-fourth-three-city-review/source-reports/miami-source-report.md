# Miami–Fort Lauderdale Source-Pass Report

## Scope and handling

This is **research-only** work for Mapping with Melanin’s Miami–Fort Lauderdale, Florida pass. Nothing in this pass was staged, published, submitted, deployed, or written to an external system. The three files in this folder are local research artifacts only.

The pass prioritized an official regional tourism guide, official local-government pages, chamber/community institution sites, a Black-business directory, and first-party business or organization destinations. Each candidate has a public HTTP(S) source URL and a first-party website or a specifically linked official social destination. Commercial businesses, community resources, and cultural places were deliberately separated. Identity and ownership wording is retained only where the cited publisher directly supplied it; chamber affiliation was not treated as ownership certification.

## Results

**21 candidates** were retained and **5 records** were held for manual review. The retained candidate set has no exact normalized duplicate against the existing local city JSONL research under `/home/ubuntu/directory-research-wave-2026-09-18/`, using normalized name + city + state + address. Both JSONL outputs were parsed after writing.

## Held records

The five held records are preserved rather than promoted because an official domain showed unrelated content or a platform/reactivation screen, a linked destination conflicted with the named business, or a physical business lacked a numbered street address without being expressly established as online-only. These are not negative findings and require current manual confirmation before potential promotion.

## Limitations

This was a bounded, source-backed discovery pass, not a certification or operational audit. Directory and tourism-guide records can age; current hours, prices, availability, accessibility, service area, inventory, licensing, health outcomes, and operating status were not inferred. Several official domains were unavailable, unresolved, redirected to unrelated content, or did not yield extractable content; those conditions are either documented in the relevant retained notes without claiming closure or placed in the held file when they created material destination uncertainty. Public chamber membership or appearance in a community directory was used only as a source basis for inclusion, not as proof of ownership or protected identity. No coordinates, pins, directions, or other location geometry were created.
