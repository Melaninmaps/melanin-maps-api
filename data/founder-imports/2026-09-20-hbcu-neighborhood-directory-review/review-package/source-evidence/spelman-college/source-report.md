# Spelman College neighborhood business research

## Scope and contract

This research covers physical, everyday-life businesses near **Spelman College in Atlanta, Georgia**. It is research-only and does not assert publication, ingestion, or database status. The contract permits 0–3 candidates and requires a current HTTPS official destination, a numbered physical address supported by a primary or authoritative source, and explicit ownership evidence where an ownership designation is used.

## Eligible candidates

Two candidates passed all required checks: Busy Bee Cafe and Paschal's. Both are food businesses with current official HTTPS websites and numbered Atlanta addresses. Both have explicit Black-ownership evidence from a reputable independent source (Resy's 2020 Atlanta feature on Black-owned restaurants), supplemented by official business history and ownership information where available.

## Limitations

The sources establish business identity, address, destination, category, and explicit ownership evidence, but this report does not independently measure walking distance from Spelman College. “Nearby” is based on the businesses' location in the Atlanta University Center/Westside area and the research brief; distance-sensitive downstream review should verify proximity. No unsupported hours, pricing, accessibility, licensing, menu, or service claims were added beyond what the official pages explicitly show. Paschal's ownership evidence is historical/brand-contextual in the cited authoritative sources; the record uses only the explicit Black-owned designation from Resy's feature and does not infer ownership from names or neighborhood.
