# Source report — University of the District of Columbia, Washington, DC

## Scope and method

This research followed the supplied HBCU Neighborhood Directory Candidate Contract. I researched nearby physical, everyday-life businesses and required an HTTPS official destination, a numbered address supported by an official or authoritative source, and explicit ownership evidence before promoting a record to `candidates.jsonl`. No ingestion, publication, or database access was attempted.

## Count reconciliation

The research produced **1 eligible candidate** and **2 held candidates**, for **3 total researched leads**. The candidate count is within the contract maximum of three. Held records are retained because they are plausible but fail at least one strict promotion condition: missing explicit ownership evidence (Sankofa) or ambiguous current official destination/address (Flavorture).

## Limitations

“Nearby” was interpreted at the surrounding Washington, DC neighborhood level rather than as a measured walking-radius result; no unsupported distance, hours, pricing, accessibility, licensing, menu, or service claims were added. Ownership was not inferred from names, founders, neighborhood, imagery, cuisine, or community reputation. The source pages were accessed on 2026-09-20; business locations and operating status can change, so a later health check is required before any review-pipeline use.
