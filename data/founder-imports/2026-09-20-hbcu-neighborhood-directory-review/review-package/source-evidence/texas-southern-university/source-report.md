# Texas Southern University — source report

## Scope and method

This research covers a maximum of three nearby, everyday-life physical businesses around Texas Southern University in Houston, Texas. The contract requires a current official HTTPS destination, a numbered physical address supported by the business or an authoritative source, and explicit ownership evidence where an ownership designation is included. Research only; no ingestion, publication, or database access was attempted.

## Eligible candidates

Two candidates passed the validation threshold and appear in `candidates.jsonl`:

1. **Lucille's** — The official website provides 5512 La Branch Street, Houston, TX 77004 and describes the Southern-cuisine restaurant and its co-founders. Southern Foodways Alliance's Gravy identifies Chris Williams as chef-owner and states that he opened the restaurant with his brother Ben; the article explicitly discusses Williams's Black Texan history. Sources: [official website](https://www.lucilleshouston.net/) and [Southern Foodways Alliance](https://www.southernfoodways.org/the-fine-legacy-of-lucilles/).
2. **The Breakfast Klub** — The official website provides 3711 Travis at Alabama, Houston, TX 77002, phone 713-528-8561, food-service details, and official social links. Its Marcus Davis page identifies him as the entrepreneurial leader and Chief Steward of the parent company. The Houston Chronicle identifies Marcus Davis as owner and The Breakfast Klub as Black-owned. Sources: [official website](https://thebreakfastklub.com/), [Marcus Davis page](https://thebreakfastklub.com/about-tbk/marcus-davis/), and [Houston Chronicle](https://www.houstonchronicle.com/entertainment/dining/article/The-Breakfast-Klub-among-top-Black-owned-16828919.php).

## Held lead

One plausible lead appears in `held-candidates.jsonl`: **Crumbville, TX**, formerly at 3409 Emancipation Avenue. Public sources identify Ella Russell as owner and describe the bakery as Black woman-owned, but the business's own Instagram reports that the storefront closed May 24, 2025 and shifted toward online/pop-up activity. It is therefore held rather than presented as a current physical candidate.

## Limitations

No distance calculation or geocoding was performed, consistent with the contract's prohibition on latitude/longitude fields. Current hours, menus, accessibility, licensing, and service details were not generalized beyond what the official destinations state. Ownership was not inferred from names, location, cuisine, imagery, or reputation. Search-result snippets were treated as discovery only; candidate records rely on fetched official or authoritative pages. The Houston Chronicle ownership URL may be paywalled for some readers, but its search result and cited article identify Marcus Davis as owner and The Breakfast Klub as Black-owned; the official site independently confirms Davis's leadership and the address.
