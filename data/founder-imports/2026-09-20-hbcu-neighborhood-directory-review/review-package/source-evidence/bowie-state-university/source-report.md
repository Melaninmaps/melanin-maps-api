## Limitations and policy notes

The official Milk & Honey page verifies the current HTTPS destination, numbered address, phone, restaurant category, and social links. The ownership designation comes from Black Restaurant Weeks, an authoritative specialty directory whose page explicitly labels the restaurant Black-owned; no ownership was inferred from cuisine, names, imagery, or neighborhood.

Ruby's official page verifies its current destination and address, while Northwestern Mutual provides explicit named-owner evidence. Because the reviewed sources do not state a qualifying ownership designation, Ruby's is held rather than returned as a candidate. B.B.U. Kitchen's official page verifies destination, address, phone, and category, but the reviewed evidence does not explicitly establish ownership. Search-result/social language was not upgraded into qualifying evidence. Hours, pricing, accessibility, licensing, menus, and proximity measurements were not added beyond what sources directly state. All records are marked `research_only` and are not claims that listings are live.
