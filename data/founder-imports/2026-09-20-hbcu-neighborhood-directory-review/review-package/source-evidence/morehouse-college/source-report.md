## Scope and method

This is research-only work for **Morehouse College, Atlanta, Georgia**. I reviewed official business destinations first and then cross-checked ownership with authoritative editorial or institutional sources. No ingestion, publication, or database access was attempted.

## Limitations

The contract does not define a precise radius for “nearby”; the accepted businesses are in the Morehouse/Atlanta University Center and adjacent Westside/Castleberry Hill area rather than being presented with unsupported distance claims. Address and destination checks reflect the pages fetched during this research pass. Ownership designations are limited to what sources explicitly support; no demographic inference was used. Hours, prices, accessibility, licensing, menus, and service claims were not added unless directly reflected as basic category context, and no claim of live publication is made.
