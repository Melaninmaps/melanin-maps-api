## Limitations

The contract requires an official HTTPS destination, a numbered physical address, and explicit ownership evidence. Search-result snippets and third-party directory labels were not used as sole evidence. No ownership designation was inferred from names, neighborhood, cuisine, imagery, or community reputation. Hours, accessibility, licensing, pricing, and other service claims were not added unless directly supported; none are needed for the candidate decision. This report makes no claim that any listing is live or published.
