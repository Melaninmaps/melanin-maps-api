# Harrisburg Reentry, Education, Employment, and Support — Source Report

## Review status

This research produced **24 protected-review candidates** and **5 held leads** for the specified Harrisburg scope. Every accepted record is grounded in a public page that was opened and inspected on 2026-09-19. The accepted records use `pending_review`; held records use `needs_research`. The files are a research queue for ordinary local discovery, not publication, eligibility determination, referral, availability statement, or a claim that any service fits a particular person.

The accepted set concentrates on official government, nonprofit, workforce, education, legal-aid, faith-service, and library sources. It includes reentry navigation, identification and benefit offices, adult education, job training and apprenticeship pathways, employment navigation, housing and basic-needs navigation, legal aid, behavioral-health and recovery navigation, and public libraries. Clinical and legal-service entries were routed to `regulated_review`. No ownership or cultural designation was inferred, and every accepted record therefore has empty ownership-designation and ownership-evidence arrays.

## Query approach and inclusion rule

Research began with broad local queries for **Harrisburg reentry, adult education, workforce training, benefits and identification, housing navigation, legal aid, recovery, behavioral-health access, and libraries**. Results were narrowed to official provider/customer sites, Commonwealth or Dauphin County pages, public institutions, recognized nonprofits, faith organizations with service pages, and library authorities. Search-result snippets were used only to discover pages; source facts in the output were extracted only after the linked pages were opened.

A physical accepted record required both a numbered public street address and a provider/customer website or official public destination. Local deduplication used normalized **name + city + state + address**. Service terms reproduce or closely normalize service labels published by the source. The notes record only source-supported context and repeatedly preserve uncertainty around eligibility, capacity, enrollment, and program fit.

## Held leads and named limitations

**Held leads.** Five leads are intentionally kept outside the candidate file. The Center for Employment Opportunities local page was not sufficiently extractable for local facts. RASE Project’s own site says it permanently closed. The County’s Reentry Coalition page lacks a qualifying public address or customer destination. The state Apprenticeship and Training Office page lacks a qualifying local street address. Penn State Harrisburg Continuing Education has a published Middletown address and is therefore outside this Harrisburg-city batch. These decisions prevent addressless, closed, unsupported, or out-of-scope entries from appearing as ordinary discovery candidates.

**Evidence limits.** The directory does not verify current hours, openings, waitlists, eligibility, pricing, insurance, funding, transportation, accessibility, language availability, or whether a program will serve any particular person. It does not label any place safe, sober, trouble-free, culturally affiliated, Black-owned, or medically/legally suitable. It does not infer criminal history from users. The behavioral-health/recovery descriptions are source-based navigation labels only; clinical service candidates remain protected-review records and require independent review before any future presentation.

**Geographic limit.** This batch is limited to records with a published Harrisburg, Pennsylvania physical address. Nearby or regional programs were not promoted merely because they serve the broader capital region. Statewide web resources without the required local address were held rather than treated as online businesses because they are public programs, not customer-facing online businesses.
