# Philadelphia Caregiver, Cancer, Recovery, Grief, and Faith Support: Source Report

**Scope:** Philadelphia caregiver, prostate-cancer information/support, cancer navigation, grief/bereavement, behavioral-health and substance-use recovery, diet/nutrition, older-adult, faith/community, and family support. **Retrieval date:** 2026-09-19. This is a protected-review research set for later ordinary local discovery. It is not a service referral, eligibility determination, availability statement, clinical recommendation, or publication action.

## Results

The accepted set contains **20 candidates**. Every accepted physical record has a numbered public street address and an official website. The held file contains **8 leads** that need resolution rather than inclusion. Three official service pages without a published numbered service address were moved from the accepted set to the held file during final validation; they must not be staged as physical listings or map pins until an official address is confirmed. All accepted records have `sourceStatus: pending_review`; all held records have `sourceStatus: needs_research`.

## Query and screening approach

Research began with broad public-web queries combining Philadelphia with caregiver, cancer navigation, prostate cancer, bereavement, recovery, older-adult, food/nutrition, family, and faith support. Each potential source was then opened and inspected. Priority was given to official provider, nonprofit, health-system, City, federal government, and faith-community sites. The record text retains only facts published by the cited page, such as a service description, contact method, and address. Sources and candidates were locally deduplicated on normalized name, city, state, and address. Repeated, unresolved, missing-address, and blocked-source leads were placed in the held file.

## Held leads and limitations

**Eight leads were held.** Maternity Care Coalition was supported by its official service description and phone, but its inspected contact page did not show a street address. Project HOME described services but no numbered address was captured on the page inspected. Unity Recovery’s official Philadelphia page was blocked by verification and search results showed conflicting addresses, so it remains unfit pending direct official confirmation. A repeated Maternity Care Coalition lead was held to prevent duplication. The City Faith-Based and Interfaith Affairs office publishes contact information but not a street address on its department page. Philly HEALs, Penn Medicine Bereavement Services, and the Mayor’s Commission on Aging were also held during final validation because the inspected official service pages did not publish a numbered Philadelphia service address; their inclusion does not establish a physical service location.

The review does not establish current capacity, enrollment, insurance acceptance, price, language access, hours, clinical suitability, professional licensure, safety, sobriety, cultural affiliation, or ownership. `regulatedProfession: true` only flags records that describe regulated clinical or legal service contexts; it is not a licensure verification. No ownership designation was assigned because this research did not find source-specific evidence that supports one. Record inclusion does not mean any resource is available to a particular person.
