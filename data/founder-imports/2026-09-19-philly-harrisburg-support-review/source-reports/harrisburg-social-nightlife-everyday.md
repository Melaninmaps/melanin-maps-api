## Query and verification approach

Research was scoped to **Harrisburg, Pennsylvania** and used broad discovery queries for official arts and museums, family recreation, live music and nightlife, civic events, public markets, libraries/community programming, and public venues. Local destination organizations were used for discovery only where appropriate. Each retained source was opened and inspected rather than relying on search-result snippets.

The evidence preference was: official provider/operator pages first; City of Harrisburg and Commonwealth of Pennsylvania pages for municipal and public venues; recognized local destination organizations only as discovery or secondary corroboration. For each accepted physical entry, the evidence check required a numbered address and an official website or official social destination. Names were normalized and checked locally by name, city, state, and address. No same-name/same-address accepted collision remained.

## Held leads and limitations

**Pennsylvania State Capitol**, **City Island**, and **Harrisburg Senators / FNB Field** are held because the specific official pages inspected did not supply a numbered public street address for the lead. **State Museum of Pennsylvania** is held because its official homepage explicitly says the building is closed for renovation. It may be reconsidered after a current public in-person opening status is confirmed. **Sturges Speakeasy** is held because the local destination listing supplied location and phone information but the separately inspected apparent web domain did not identify the Harrisburg venue as its official customer destination. **Harrisburg Mural Trail by Sprocket Mural Works** is held because the inspected destination listing did not establish an official customer website or official social destination.

Hours, ticket availability, event schedules, admission, accessibility, alcohol service, age restrictions, and operating status can change and were not treated as promises. The records do not infer ownership designation, Black or diaspora affiliation, cultural affiliation beyond an organization’s own published description, safety, affordability, or individual fit. Ownership arrays are empty because no ownership designation was added without explicit source support. No clinical, legal, or other regulated-profession resource was included in this social/cultural scope, so all `regulatedProfession` values are `false`.
