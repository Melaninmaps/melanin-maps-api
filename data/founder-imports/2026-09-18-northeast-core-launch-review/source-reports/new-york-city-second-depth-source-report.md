# New York City Second-Depth: Research Source Report

**Scope and result.** This is a **review-only research package** for the five boroughs of New York City. It contains **16 retained candidates** and **16 held candidates**. Retained records meet the package test: a named candidate, category, public listing-level source, inspected official customer-facing site or official customer destination, and a publicly supported numbered street address without an unresolved conflict. The retained set deliberately is not padded with restaurant leads.

The retained set spans **seven everyday-need categories**. It includes arts and culture, faith and community, beauty and personal care, retail, childcare and family education, professional services, and fitness and recreation. It uses listing-level records from five final source families: NYC Tourism + Conventions, Downtown Brooklyn Partnership, Little Caribbean NYC, NoHo NY/Meatpacking BID, and East Brooklyn BID. Additional official and civic source families were inspected for discovery and disposition, including Myrtle Avenue Brooklyn Partnership, Shop Your City/NYC SBS, Harlem 125th Street BID, Brooklyn Chamber, Bronx Chamber, and Jamaica Center BID.

## Source-family findings and dispositions

**New York City Tourism + Conventions** neighborhood itinerary pages were inspected as listing-level records. The Jamaica page supplied retained VP Records, DeVore Dance Center, Greater Allen A.M.E. Cathedral of New York, and King Manor Museum. The Crown Heights page supplied retained Colors Effect. The same source family supplied held leads where a numbered location was absent or an official customer destination could not be read: Afrikan Poetry Theatre, Marché Rue Dix, Martine’s Dream, and The Pop-Up Shop BK.

**Downtown Brooklyn Partnership** was inspected at full-directory and individual-listing level. It supplied retained [Salon]718, Brooklyn Paramount, Brooklyn Tabernacle, Wright & Goebel Wine & Spirits, and Brooklyn Prospect Downtown Elementary School. It supplied held Flossy Pet Pawlor and Brooklyn Total Fitness due to address conflicts, YAY Scrubs due to an unrelated official domain, and Brooklyn Renaissance Art because the official destination could not be read.

**Little Caribbean NYC** supplied retained Nostrand Health Food Store & Juice Bar, Granru Market, and Fleur De Lis Beauty. Cultcha Non Stop was held because the supplied official Instagram destination displayed a login wall. **NoHo NY / Meatpacking BID** supplied retained New York Biltong and AYON Studio; AARMY was held because no numbered address was exposed in the inspected source and official destination. **East Brooklyn BID** supplied retained Sofive and held Trey Whitfield School and Filco Carting due to address conflict and acquisition/current-location uncertainty. **Myrtle Avenue Brooklyn Partnership** supplied only held leads in this pass, including Doo’s Barber Shop, Klevor Liquor, Ray’s Barber Shop, and Voodo Fé Gallery, when the customer destination or address test was not met.

## Dedupe method

Every JSONL record under `/home/ubuntu/directory-research-wave-2026-09-18/` was read before output, excluding this output folder. Both prior candidate and held files were included. Proposed candidates were compared on normalized **name + city + state + country + address**. Normalization lowercased strings and removed punctuation and spacing. A conservative same-name screen also checked normalized name in the same city/state/country. No exact normalized package duplicate was emitted; uncertain same-name records were not merged.

## Research-only boundary

This package is for review only. It makes no assertion that a candidate is open, available, safe, accessible, affordable, licensed, credentialed, or of any particular quality. It performs no geocoding and includes no coordinates, map pins, external database/API writes, deployment, publication, payment, account, authentication, password, waitlist, or other operational change. Ownership and cultural-designation statements are retained only when expressly attributed in `ownershipEvidence`.
