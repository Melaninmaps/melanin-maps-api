# Los Angeles County Academic & Culture Directory Research

**Scope and result.** This is a research-only directory-research wave for **Los Angeles County, California**. It identifies **54 candidates** across universities, adult education, libraries, bookshops, museums, and literary/cultural programs. It also keeps **6 leads** in a separate held file because the required official-destination or numbered-address verification was not complete. The selection uses the requested subject areas and does not make personal inferences or ratings.

## What the candidate file contains

The candidate file contains 54 distinct records: **25 physical businesses or institutions**, **16 cultural places**, and **13 community resources**. Every candidate has an official customer-facing destination opened in this research wave. Physical records have a numbered street address. The only current-location exception addressed directly in notes is Kinokuniya: the tourism source describes a Little Tokyo location while the retailer’s official notice directs visitors to its temporary The BLOC shop; the record uses the official current location rather than the older directory address.

The municipal and county sources support the library and public-program records. The tourism and association directories support museum, bookshop, and university discovery. Each corresponding official destination was also opened before the record was retained. The LACCD campus records rely on the district’s official customer-facing college directory, which supplies each campus address, official web link, and noncredit-application route.

## Evidence and record rules

Candidate records use the required 23 fields in the exact requested order. **`sourceUrl`** identifies the public directory, civic source, or official source used for the record. **`website`** is the opened official customer-facing destination. Unknown fields use literal JSON `null`; no coordinates were collected. `servicesSearchTerms` are neutral, semicolon-separated discovery terms supported by opened sources.

Designations are conservative. The Malik Books record carries **Black-owned** only because its official website explicitly self-describes that way. The Book Soup record does not receive an LGBTQ+ designation merely because a tourism guide describes an LGBTQ book selection. The Conservancy record notes that its official site contains an explicitly titled LGBTQ historic-places resource, without assigning an LGBTQ+ designation to the organization. These distinctions follow the instruction to record LGBTQ+ or ownership information only when explicitly source-attributed.

## Within-category duplicate checks

Bookshop candidates were deduplicated by physical storefront and business identity. Skylight’s main shop is retained while its nearby Arts Annex is not a separate record. The Kinokuniya record uses the retailer’s currently published temporary location rather than retaining both the old and new storefront as duplicates. Tía Chucha’s unified bookstore and cultural center is represented once as a community resource.

Cultural places were deduplicated by visitor site. Getty Center and Getty Villa are retained separately because they have separate addresses and official visitor destinations. MOCA is represented by the Grand Avenue site; the official plan-your-visit page reported the Geffen Contemporary temporarily closed for installation, so it was not separately added. The four LA County Library Cultural Resource Centers are distinct named programs at different library addresses and are therefore retained separately.

## Held leads and limitations

The held file contains Children’s Book World, Sejong Bookstore, Museum of Latin American Art, USC Pacific Asia Museum, La Brea Tar Pits, and Museum of African American Art. These were not promoted because the research wave did not complete the required official customer-facing destination or direct numbered-address verification. Holding them avoids treating an unverified, stale, conflicting, source-only, or addressless lead as a candidate.

This research validates web-published information only as read in this wave. Hours, admission conditions, schedules, relocations, and program availability can change; the official destination is provided for current checking. It does not assess quality, luxury level, safety, eligibility, ownership beyond explicitly cited designations, or the suitability of any business or program for a particular person.

## Operational boundary

**Research-only.** No production database or API write, publication, map pin, geocode, deployment, native build, authentication, user/session change, waitlist change, or payment change was performed.
