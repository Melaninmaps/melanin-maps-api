# Los Angeles County LGBTQ+ Community Directory Research

**Research-only directory wave.** This category covers publicly designated LGBTQ+ community organizations, explicitly LGBTQ+-oriented social venues, cultural spaces, professional networks, resources, and events in Los Angeles County. The completed candidate file contains **25** distinct records. The held file contains **5** leads that did not meet this wave’s address or official-destination verification standard.

## Scope and attribution rules

The geography is restricted to **Los Angeles County**. West Hollywood, Long Beach, Burbank, and Los Angeles entries were retained because their source-supported addresses are within the county. No coordinates were collected. The requester’s voluntary context was used only to prioritize useful types of public-facing records—social venues, live performance and cultural spaces, professional networks, and community events. It was not used to infer anything about any person or organization.

An LGBTQ+ designation, gay-bar designation, LGBTQ+ ownership statement, or similar descriptor appears only where a source attributes it. For example, Revolver’s ownership designation comes from Revolver’s official site, Beaches’ LGBTQ+ focus from Beaches’ official site, and several venue designations from Visit West Hollywood’s tourism guide. Records do not assert ownership, quality, eligibility, safety, accessibility beyond a source statement, or licensing unless a source expressly provides that information. The Trans Wellness Center is intentionally routed to `regulated_review`; its listing is not a credentialing, quality, licensing, or eligibility finding.

Each candidate has an opened official customer-facing destination in `website`. The `sourceUrl` is the opened municipal, county, tourism, university/library, or official public source supporting the row; `notes` records material address and identity limits. Physical social venues have a numbered street address. Clearly network-based Out Professionals is recorded as `online_business` with `address: null`; event organizers use documented event or mailing locations without presenting a mailing address as an event site.

## Candidate coverage and duplicate check

The 25 candidates comprise nine community resources and networks, including two Pride organizations; one online professional network; eight cultural places; one regulated-review resource; and six social venues. The count treats a venue as distinct only when it has a different named public destination and function. For example, the Los Angeles LGBT Center is retained as a multiservice organization while Renberg Theatre is retained as a separately named performance venue with its own events and location. ONE Gallery, ONE Archives, and Mazer are distinct gallery/archive destinations even though ONE Gallery and Mazer share a building address. No identical organization-and-location duplicate remains.

## Held leads and limitations

Five leads are held rather than converted to candidates. Black Lesbians United has a County source and an opened official site, but not a supported numbered local address. Pomona Pride Center and Project Q were County-directory leads whose official destinations were not opened in this wave. Men’s Health Foundation appears in the City directory but its official destination could not be extracted and it is a healthcare lead requiring direct regulated-provider review. Tom of Finland Foundation’s purported official URL resolved to a server directory, so its customer-facing official destination could not be verified. These held records are intentionally not production candidates.

This is a bounded web-research snapshot. Event dates, hours, programs, admission, availability, and contacts can change. The database records do not verify legal ownership beyond explicit source language and do not make any determination about who should use a venue, organization, event, or service.

## Operational boundary

**Research-only; no production database/API write, publication, map pin, geocode, deployment, native build, auth/user/session/waitlist/payment change.**
