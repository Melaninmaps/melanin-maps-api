# Los Angeles Live Music, Performing Arts, and Cultural Event Venues

**Research category:** Live music, performing arts, and cultural event venues. This is one directory category for **Los Angeles, California** records. Candidate locations were limited to Los Angeles city addresses that are plainly in Los Angeles County. The research covers jazz, live music, performing arts, cultural programming, event venues, nightlife-adjacent music venues, and one online cultural-calendar service.

## Result

The resulting candidate file contains **22 distinct, evidence-backed records**. It includes six jazz or live-music venues, five museums/cultural organizations with documented music programming, six major performing-arts or event destinations, three public/community presenting resources, one entertainment district, and one online events calendar. Nine leads are retained in the held file because the published evidence does not meet the address and official-destination verification rules.

## Evidence and attribution rules

Each candidate was supported by a credible public directory, tourism, chamber, university, museum, or official civic/cultural source, and its customer-facing official destination was separately opened. Discover Los Angeles is used as the official tourism source for its jazz and outdoor-music guides and calendar. DTLA Alliance is used as the downtown directory source. Institutional and venue sites were then opened to verify current event, program, contact, or visitor information. The official destination is identified in each JSONL record’s `website`, while the public evidence page is identified in `sourceUrl`.

No ownership, identity, LGBTQ+, or designation claim was inferred. The only recorded designations are explicitly attributed: Sam First describes itself as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and BMO Stadium states that LAFC owns and operates it. The research does not make claims about user fit, safety, quality, luxury, eligibility, audience identity, ownership beyond those explicit records, or current availability. Search terms are neutral discovery terms drawn from the cited programming descriptions.

The record for **Discover Los Angeles Events Calendar** is intentionally classified as `online_business` and has `address: null`; it is a digital discovery service, not a physical venue. The record for **MAC·LA** uses the tourism guide’s numbered address because the current official site identifies the bandshell by intersection only. This reconciliation is noted in the record rather than silently converting the intersection into an address.

## Within-category duplicate check

I compared normalized names, URLs, and street addresses within this single category before writing the files. There are **no duplicate candidate names or duplicate candidate URLs**. Program series were not added as separate venue records when their host venue is already included: Jazz at LACMA is represented by LACMA, and the Music Center calendar is represented by The Music Center. The Music Center, Walt Disney Concert Hall, and REDCAT are retained as separate candidates because their sources identify distinct named venues/destinations with different customer-facing destinations and physical addresses or venue functions. L.A. LIVE is retained as an entertainment-district destination rather than repeated as each of its unverified individual venues.

## Held leads and limitations

The Baked Potato, The World Stage, Somerville, and General Lee’s have credible tourism evidence and an opened official site, but no readable official page supplied a numbered address. The Orpheum Theatre’s purported official site could not be opened because the extractor could not resolve its hostname. Regent Theater returned no extractable content. The Belasco page was too sparse to confirm operational details. The Novo and Peacock Theater official sites confirmed programming but did not provide a venue-specific numbered address; the L.A. LIVE district address was deliberately not substituted. These nine leads remain in `held-live-music-events.jsonl` pending direct official verification.

Live event calendars change. This work records evidence observed from the opened pages and should not be read as a promise that a specific show, artist, menu, ticket, reservation, operating hour, or program will remain available. No coordinates were collected or generated.

## Research-only status

This is **research only**. There was **no production database/API write, publication, map pin, geocode, deployment, native build, auth/user/session/waitlist/payment change**. No directory entry was published and no external system was changed.
