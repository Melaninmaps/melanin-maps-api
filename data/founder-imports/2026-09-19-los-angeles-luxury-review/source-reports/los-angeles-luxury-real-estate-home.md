# Los Angeles County Directory Research — Real Estate & Home

**Scope.** This is a single, research-only directory category for **real-estate and home services**: residential real estate, housing navigation, design, restoration, home improvement, property support, and practical household services. Records are limited to **Los Angeles County, California** locations or clearly countywide online/public resources. The voluntary profile supplied in the assignment was used only to prioritize useful home-related categories; it was not used to infer a person’s or a business’s identity, sexual orientation, marital status, health, wealth, occupation, language, preferences, quality, luxury level, safety, ownership, or eligibility.

## Results

The candidate file contains **16 distinct records** and the held file contains **6 leads** that were not promoted because of address conflicts, missing numbered addresses, or a stale/parked official destination. The selected set spans one real-estate professional, architecture/interior design, restoration and home improvement, two online housing-navigation resources, and four public/community housing supports.

## Duplicate and scope checks

A normalized name, website, phone, and address check was completed **within this category** before writing the records. No candidate duplicates another candidate by a shared customer-facing destination, phone, or exact physical address. The two municipal/County housing-search resources are retained as separate online services because one is the City’s Access Housing LA registry and the other is the Countywide Housing Resource Center. LAHD Handyworker and LAHD’s online registry were retained as distinct programs rather than duplicate businesses. The Los Angeles LGBT Center is a community resource record for its **Senior Housing Navigation** service, not an inference about any user or a general claim of service fit.

The held file also guards against inaccurate duplication: Adamm’s, DiBara, K.C. Restoration, Clouston Sash and Door, Kelley Construction, and Zelda Design were excluded rather than merged or padded. In particular, Adamm’s and DiBara had address conflicts between the directory and official destination; Clouston’s linked official domain was parked; K.C. Restoration and Kelley Construction lacked an official, source-supported numbered address; and Zelda Design’s source listing lacked a numbered address.

## Source-attribution and data-handling rules

Every candidate has a credible public source plus an official customer-facing destination opened. The `sourceUrl` is the public directory, municipal/county referral, or official program page supporting inclusion; `website` is the official destination opened for the candidate. A physical business is included only where a source supports a numbered Los Angeles County address. Clearly online housing-search services use `online_business` and literal JSON `null` for address. `community_resource` is used for municipal, County, and nonprofit navigation/support. `regulated_review` is a cautionary routing label, not a claim that credentials have been verified; license numbers and terms such as “licensed” appear only when source-reported, and records expressly note that no independent current-status validation was performed.

Ownership fields are `null` unless an ownership/designation is explicitly stated by the source. The only populated ownership designation is Tashman’s official-site statement that it is family-owned. The LGBTQ+ wording in the Los Angeles LGBT Center record is explicitly attributed to that organization’s official service page and is not an ownership designation, user inference, or eligibility conclusion. Chamber directory membership is not treated as Black ownership or any other ownership/designation claim.

## Limitations

This is a source-backed discovery set, not a complete market census, ranking, recommendation, quality assessment, license verification, availability check, price comparison, safety assessment, or eligibility determination. Public directory listings and official pages can change; contact a provider or program directly before relying on its hours, address, services, prices, licensing, coverage area, or requirements. The City Handyworker and all housing programs may have source-described restrictions, funding limits, or waiting lists; the research does not apply those criteria to any individual. No coordinates were collected or created.
