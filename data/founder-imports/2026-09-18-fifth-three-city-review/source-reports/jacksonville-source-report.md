# Jacksonville and Nearby Northeast Florida — Source-Pass Report

## Scope and method

This source-backed research pass covers Jacksonville and nearby Northeast Florida references found in official tourism/editorial guides, the Jacksonville Black Chamber of Commerce directory, official business and organization websites, and official cultural or government destinations. Each retained entry has a public evidence URL and a customer-facing official website or official social destination. The pass deliberately mixes retail, food, home services, health review, cultural places, and community resources rather than expanding restaurant entries alone. Chamber membership was used only as an inclusion basis; it was not treated as an ownership certification.

A feasible exact duplicate check compared normalized name + city + state + address against all pre-existing `*.jsonl` files under `/home/ubuntu/directory-research-wave-2026-09-18/`, excluding this output directory. No exact retained-candidate duplicate was found. Both JSONL outputs were parsed line-by-line after writing.

## Source use and limitations

Visit Jacksonville’s Black-Owned Shops and Black-Owned Jacksonville Restaurants guides are publisher designations, not independent ownership certifications. Those guide designations were preserved only in the ownership fields for businesses actually named by the guide. The JBCC directory was treated as a public chamber-membership directory: it supports inclusion and any facts in its individual listing, but it does not establish ownership or identity. Direct business and organization statements were limited to their stated text.

Several inspected official sites lacked a numbered street address, a verifiable official customer destination, or sufficient detail for regulated services. Those records are in `held-candidates.jsonl` with a machine-readable `hold_reason`; no coordinates, pin, directions data, operating-status conclusion, licensing conclusion, health-outcome claim, price, hours, accessibility, language, or availability inference was added. The NPS Kingsley visitor page provided a mailing address, while Visit Jacksonville provided the public site address retained for the cultural-place record.

**Research-only status:** These results are research-only. Nothing was staged, published, deployed, or written to any external system; no repository, application, database, API, account, user, authentication setting, or deployment was modified.
