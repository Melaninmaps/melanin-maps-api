The retained set deliberately avoids a restaurant-heavy result: only two of the 12 candidates are food and drink businesses.

The shared local-research corpus under `/home/ubuntu/directory-research-wave-2026-09-18/` was parsed before the output was written. It contained **2,729 JSONL records** with no parse errors. A normalized exact comparison of **name + city + state + address** found **no duplicate** for any retained or held record. Normalization case-folded, removed diacritics, and removed punctuation and whitespace; it did not perform fuzzy matching.

## Source support for retained candidates

The Cleveland Bucket List describes itself as a Cleveland-focused guide that showcases local businesses and makers. Its page titled **Black Owned Businesses in Cleveland** supplied the public publisher designation for the commercial records that cite it; that basis is preserved as a publisher description rather than an ownership certification. [1] [2] The candidates’ first-party websites were separately opened to verify a customer-facing destination and, where required, a numbered street address.

**Blank Canvas CLE, The Sweet Fix Bakery, Balance Cheer and Gymnastics, Extraordinaire Photos, Jasmine Rene Studio, The Makeup Institute, Black Box Fix, Village of Healing Center, and Perfect Pineapple Wraps** appear in the editorial directory. [2] The first-party sites respectively support their public destination and current details. Blank Canvas publishes gallery/shop information at 2174 Lee Road. [3] The Sweet Fix publishes its address and ordering/catering links. [4] Balance publishes a current Warrensville Heights address, public classes, and official social links; its official address is used because it differs from the directory’s older address. [5] Extraordinaire publishes a Cleveland studio and public photography booking information. [6] [7] Jasmine Rene publishes a shop, appointment route, Cleveland Heights location, and official social links. [8] [9] The Makeup Institute publishes a Lyndhurst address, customer salon services, programs, and official social links; it is retained only as **regulated_review**. [10] Black Box Fix publishes the Legacy Village address and customer ordering link. [11] Village of Healing publishes appointment contact, public health-service descriptions, Cleveland and Euclid locations, and official social destinations; it is retained only as **regulated_review**. [12] Perfect Pineapple Wraps is classified as **online_business** because its first-party site is a public ecommerce destination and describes the brand as rooted in Cleveland, but does not supply a verified public storefront. [13]

**Julia De Burgos Cultural Arts Center** is directly supported by its official website, which states its mission to promote Latino heritage and publishes a numbered Cleveland address and public cultural program links. [14] **Spanish American Committee** is directly supported by its official public-service site and contact page, which describe services for Latino and immigrant families and publish its Cleveland office address. [15] [16] **African American Cultural Garden** is directly supported by its official association site, which states the garden’s cultural and educational purpose and names 890 Martin Luther King Jr. Drive. [17]

## Controls and limitations

This is **research-only** work. The records were **not staged**, **not published**, **not deployed**, and no external writes were made. No repository, application code, database, public API, users, accounts, authentication, login, passwords, waitlist, or deployment configuration was modified.

The publisher list is a useful community-oriented source basis but is not an ownership certification. It is therefore recorded as a direct publisher designation only. First-party sites were used for public destinations and published contact/location details, not to infer any protected trait, language capability, service availability, price, hours, accessibility, licensing, health outcome, coordinates, pins, or directions. Health and beauty-service records are explicitly routed to **regulated_review** rather than presented as vetted providers. Several older directory entries conflicted with first-party information or had nonfunctional/insufficient destinations; those entries were held instead of promoted. No coordinates, pins, or directions data were created.
