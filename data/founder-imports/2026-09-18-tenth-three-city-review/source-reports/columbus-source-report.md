# Mapping with Melanin — Columbus and Central Ohio source pass

## Scope and method

This is a **research-only directory wave** for Columbus and nearby Central Ohio communities in Ohio. It was **not staged, not published, and not sent to any application, database, public API, map, deployment, account, or user system**. No coordinates, pins, routing, directions, or operational-status determinations were created.

I used public directories and official customer-facing websites or official social pages. A record was retained only when a directory supplied inclusion context and the reviewed evidence established a specific official customer/public destination and a non-conflicting numbered street address for a physical entity. Designations below retain the publisher’s or business’s wording; they are not independently certified or inferred. Chamber listing is treated as a source basis for inclusion, not an ownership certification.

A feasible exact duplicate scan was completed before writing the output. It compared normalized `name + city + state + address` against 58 existing local-city JSONL files beneath `/home/ubuntu/directory-research-wave-2026-09-18/` (excluding this new Columbus output). The seven retained keys were unique; all scanned JSONL rows parsed without error.

## Retained-record support summary

Art of Republic is retained as a cultural place because the Short North Alliance’s Black-owned + led directory provides its inclusion context and street address, while its official Facebook Page identifies it as an art gallery at the same address. Dress for Success Columbus and Stonewall Columbus are retained as community resources because the directory directly describes each as Black-led and the corresponding official sites supply current public program/resource destinations; the directory supplies their numbered locations. Drunch Eatery + Bar, Ransom Supply, and Sole Classics are retained physical businesses because their respective official sites confirm customer destinations and match the directory addresses. GFE Trailers is retained because the public Columbus Chamber designation directory supplies its address and inclusion context and its official site confirms the rental-service destination and contact channel.

## Holds and limitations

Six leads are held rather than promoted: Coles Plants has conflicting official-social street numbers; Gallerie by CFA lacks a verified customer-specific official destination; Jerky’s Jamaican and LuxWear Boutique have parked listed domains; PIVOT has conflicting street numbers and explicit wind-down language; and Vibe Venue has conflicting ZIP codes. These are **not negative findings about the organizations**; they are evidence-quality controls.

This was a limited, one-pass review, not a comprehensive census of Central Ohio. Directory entries and websites can change after inspection. The research does not infer protected traits, ownership beyond stated source designations, language ability, accessibility, pricing, hours, availability, licensing, health outcomes, or operational status. No regulated service was retained in this pass. Phone numbers, addresses, descriptions, and social links were copied only when directly displayed by an inspected source; nulls mean that the relevant detail was not needed or was not responsibly established here.
