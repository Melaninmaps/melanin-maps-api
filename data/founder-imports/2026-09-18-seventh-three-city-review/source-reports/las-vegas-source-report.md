# Mapping with Melanin — Las Vegas & Southern Nevada Source Report

## Scope and method

This is **one research-only source-backed directory wave** for Las Vegas and nearby Southern Nevada communities, Nevada. The pass prioritized official local-government and tourism material, first-party customer/public-service websites, and community institutions. Each retained commercial or cultural-place row has a numbered street address and a specific official customer-facing website; the one mapless community resource is explicitly retained under the community-resource exception. Publisher designations are reproduced only where the inspected publisher or organization supplied them. Chamber membership or chamber identity is not treated as certification of an individual member business.

A feasible exact duplicate check was run on normalized **name + city + state + address** against `22` other local city `candidates.jsonl` files under `/home/ubuntu/directory-research-wave-2026-09-18/`. It found **0 matching retained rows**. All `13` candidate JSONL lines and `8` held-candidate JSONL lines were parsed successfully after writing. Existing-file parse errors encountered during the comparison: **0**.
