## Retention rationale

Retained commercial candidates pair a directly published designation from a reputable editorial source with an inspected official customer destination and a numbered address. Retained cultural places and community resources use first-party institutional, government, public tourism, or reliable editorial sources. Community resources are organization-level entries only; no provider-level claims about health, legal, tax, behavioral-health, licensing, appointments, eligibility, or outcomes are asserted.

Chambers are retained based on their own public mission and current customer/public destination. **Chamber membership is not treated as an ownership certification of individual members.** The Denver Latino Commission is mapless because the City provides an explicit city, a current public-service destination, public meetings, and liaison contact; it is not represented as a physical venue.

## Holds and limitations

Eight records were held: The Hair & Image Studio requires regulated-service credential/license review; Tao of Boxing’s official domain could not be verified; Black Travel Box lacked a directly supplied specific local city; Little Sistas Treats lacked a numbered public address; LCAC was described as opening Fall 2026 and lacked a numbered address; Corazón Printing lacked a specific locality, address, and verified official destination; Jeffery B Jewelers had conflicting postal codes; and Burgess Services displayed conflicting phone details. Held records are research-only and are not recommendations or claims of current operation.

This focused pass prioritized confirmed everyday-life, cultural, civic, business-support, and community-service categories rather than inflating restaurant count. Sources may change after inspection. No protected trait, ownership beyond directly published designation, language capability, service, price, hour, availability, accessibility, licensing, health outcome, coordinate, or operating status was inferred.
