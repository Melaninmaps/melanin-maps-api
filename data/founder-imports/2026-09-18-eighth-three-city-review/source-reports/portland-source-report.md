Records use public editorial, chamber, local-government, or first-party organization/business sources. No traits, services, licensing, operational status, hours, price, availability, access needs, directions, or coordinates have been inferred.

**Research-only status.** This work is **research only**. It has **not been staged, published, deployed, or written to any application, database, public API, account, or repository**. No pins, coordinates, or directions data were created.

## Limitations

Publisher designations have been transcribed only where the publisher or organization directly supplied them. In particular, **Hispanic Metropolitan Chamber membership is an inclusion basis only and is not treated as an ownership certification** unless the individual member page says ‘Latino-owned.’ Official sites were used to establish public destinations and contact details, not to infer current operational status, availability, price, language, accessibility, licensing, or service outcomes. The underlying editorial and chamber pages may change after this research pass; the report records what was publicly displayed when inspected. A malformed/failed attempt to reach `https://www.blackunitedfund.org/` was not treated as an inspected or supporting source because its hostname could not be resolved by the extraction tool.
