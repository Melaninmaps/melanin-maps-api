# Pennsylvania Philadelphia Suburbs — Fourth-Depth Research Report
## Scope and boundary
This is a **research-only, review-only** pass for Bucks, Chester, Delaware, and Montgomery counties and nearby Pennsylvania Philadelphia suburbs. Philadelphia city was excluded. No geocoding, coordinates, map-pin use, production or database writes, deployments, account changes, authentication changes, payment changes, or availability/quality/safety/accessibility judgments were performed. ## Dedupe method
Every JSONL record under `/home/ubuntu/directory-research-wave-2026-09-18`, excluding this output folder, was parsed into a conservative comparison index. The primary comparison key normalized **name + city + state + country + address** by lowercasing and stripping non-alphanumeric characters. A separate same-name screen normalized **name + city + state + country**; it was used to stop rather than merge uncertain matches. Three Glen Mills child-care records were exact conservative same-name/address matches in `existing-tri-state-candidates.jsonl` and are held rather than re-added. No uncertain records were merged.
