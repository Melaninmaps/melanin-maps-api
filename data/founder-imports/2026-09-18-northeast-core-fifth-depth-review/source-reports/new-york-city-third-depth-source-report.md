# New York City Third-Depth: Review-Only Research Report

**Result:** 28 verified review candidates and 16 held records. This is a research-only output for the five New York City boroughs. It contains no coordinates, map pins, production writes, deployment actions, or account, payment, waitlist, or authentication changes.

## Scope and research boundary

The geography is limited to **Bronx, Brooklyn, Manhattan, Queens, and Staten Island**. I read municipal BID coverage and then prioritized listing-level evidence from BID and corridor directories. A candidate required a public numbered address, a listing-level source URL, and an independently opened official customer website or official business-social destination. Regulated-facing categories were routed to `regulated_review`; nonprofit and cultural records were routed to `community_resource` or `cultural_place`. Facts in records are limited to source wording and direct official-site content.

## Deduplication method

Every JSONL file below `/home/ubuntu/directory-research-wave-2026-09-18/` was read except this output folder: **102 files** and **3267 records**. For every proposed record, I compared normalized `name + city + state + country + address`, then applied a conservative same-name screen on normalized `name + city + state + country`. Any match would be excluded rather than merged. The input parse reported **0 invalid prior lines**. The emitted JSONL files were then parsed again and checked against the required field set.
