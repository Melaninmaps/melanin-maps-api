# Delaware Second-Depth — Research Source Report

## Scope and method

This **research-only, review-only** pass covered Delaware statewide with emphasis on Wilmington and New Castle County, and also examined Kent- and Sussex-area material. It did not geocode records, collect coordinates, write to any external directory/database, publish results, change accounts, or make customer-facing changes. This is a screened research package, not a recommendation or verification of ownership, licensure, availability, price, quality, accessibility, safety, or hours.

I inspected public directory/listing records first and then inspected a business’s official customer-facing website or official social destination before retaining a row. Physical commercial rows were retained only when a numbered street address was publicly supported and had no unresolved conflict. Explicitly mobile businesses with no public physical location were recorded as `online_business` with `address: null`. Health, finance, engineering, and cosmetology-related leads were routed to `regulated_review` or held instead of being represented as credentialed.

## Deduplication

Before emission, every potential row in this pass was compared against **all existing JSONL research files under** `/home/ubuntu/directory-research-wave-2026-09-18/`, excluding only this output folder. The comparison normalizes case, punctuation, whitespace, and common street abbreviations and checks the composite of name + city + state + country + address. A conservative normalized-name screen is also run across all records, without merging uncertain same-name results. Existing-package rows are never re-added. For example, E Squared Cigar Sports Bar, Huxley & Hiro, The Juice Joint-related prior package lead, African Fusion Restaurant, and other already-present Delaware research were not re-emitted. The machine-readable dedupe results are saved in the job workspace at `/home/ubuntu/jobs/job_CRP3YhKu_a3/dedupe-results.json`.

## Data boundaries and caveats

Directory-source identity or ownership statements are stored only as attributed directory evidence in `ownershipEvidence`; they are not independently verified. Social links are supplied only where an inspected official site or individual listing directly exposed them. No latitudes, longitudes, map pins, or geocoding are present. `null` means not publicly established in this pass, not that a fact does not exist. Held rows are intentionally retained for review rather than treated as verified directory candidates.
