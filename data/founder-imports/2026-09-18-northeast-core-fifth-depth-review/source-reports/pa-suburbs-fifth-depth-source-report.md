# Pennsylvania Philadelphia-Area Suburbs — Fifth-Depth Research Evidence Ledger

## Scope and result

This **review-only research package** covers Pennsylvania suburbs in the Philadelphia region. It includes suburban locations in Bucks, Chester, Delaware, and Montgomery Counties, and **excludes Philadelphia city**. The package retains **8 candidates** and holds **7 leads** when the required public listing source, official customer or organization destination, physical-address eligibility, and routing evidence could not all be supported without an unresolved conflict. It does not pad the count.

The retained records come from two fresh high-yield source families: an explicitly public **diaspora-community business directory** maintained by CAIR-Philadelphia and an **official municipal directory** maintained by East Goshen Township. The CAIR page states that inclusion is not endorsement. Its entries supplied outward customer links, address data, and service descriptions; the records preserve no unquoted ownership or identity designation. The East Goshen page identifies itself as a business directory for retail stores, shops, and offices in the Township. [1] [2]

## Duplicate control and validation

Before output, every accepted and held row was screened against all raw candidate records in the four designated prior passes: `pa-suburbs-high-yield`, `pa-suburbs-second-depth`, `pa-suburbs-third-depth`, and `pa-suburbs-fourth-depth`. The screen normalizes Unicode, lowercases, and removes non-alphanumeric characters. It compares normalized `name + city + state + country + address` and also conservatively screens normalized same-name records. It also compares the newly emitted candidate and held files to one another. **No accepted or held record has a detected normalized full-key or same-name collision in the four-pass raw candidate corpus.** No uncertain match was merged.

The JSONL validator checked all 15 lines for the required 23 labels and sequential `sourceRow` values. It confirmed no extra coordinate fields, no key named latitude or longitude, and no string value equal to `"null"`. All unknown values are JSON `null`. The output directory contains exactly the three requested deliverables.

## Limitations and research-only boundary

This pass uses only public pages reached through two public directory systems and the businesses’ or organizations’ stated customer destinations. A source link does not establish present eligibility, quality, safety, price, accessibility, language, culture, ownership, professional credential, current availability, or endorsement. Some official destinations were insufficient to corroborate a numbered physical location and were held rather than promoted. No closure claim is based on any web response.

This is a **protected, review-only research package**. It has **not been published**, deployed, geocoded, map-pinned, written to a production database or API, or used to change application code, accounts, authentication, passwords, sessions, waitlists, memberships, payment, or any live system.
