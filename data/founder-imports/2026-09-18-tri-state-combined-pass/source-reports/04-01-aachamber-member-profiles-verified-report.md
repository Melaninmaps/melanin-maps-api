# African American Chamber Member-Profile Collection Report

## Result

This source pass retained **126** research candidates from the public [African American Chamber of Commerce of PA, NJ & DE active member directory][1]. Every retained row has an individual public GrowthZone profile URL, a numbered street address, a published category or service, and a direct customer-facing website. The collection contains **99 ordinary business records** and **27 regulated-review records**. The regulated-review classification covers childcare, health, legal, tax, insurance, and similar services that require an additional authority or licensing review before any public directory publication.

The retained locations are **113 in Pennsylvania, 9 in New Jersey, and 4 in Delaware**. They have no ownership designations because membership in an African American Chamber does not, by itself, verify a business owner’s identity or ownership category.

## Coverage and exclusion controls

The collection discovered **384 public member profiles** through the active directory’s alphabetic and category pages. It held or excluded **258 profile records**. The principal reasons were a missing category, a location outside the Philadelphia tri-state scope, an exact existing same-business/same-location record, no direct customer destination, a nonphysical or generic address, or no eligible physical/online evidence. The crawl also applied a normalized comparison so that one additional name variation, *Yung’ster Joos*, did not become a second candidate.

## Evidence boundary

Each row is **research-only**. It is not a finding that a business is currently operating, licensed, owned by a specific person or group, accessible, available, or suitable for a particular need. No row has been staged to a database, published to the website or mobile app, placed on a map, or supplied to live Kinfolk recommendations. The consolidated tri-state package must still remove duplicates, test customer-facing destinations, reconcile against the existing database, validate locations, and route regulated services through the appropriate reviewer queue.
