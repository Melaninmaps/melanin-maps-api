# Philadelphia Black Business Guides — Evidence Report

## Scope and method

This pass searched public Philadelphia Black-business guides and civic/community sources, then retained only individually attributable listings with a published Philadelphia street location and an official customer-facing website or social destination. The primary extraction source was [Visit Philadelphia’s guide](https://www.visitphilly.com/articles/philadelphia/black-owned-shops-and-boutiques-in-philadelphia/), whose introduction identifies the entries as Black-owned shopping destinations. Each retained record preserves the exact guide URL and the official destination linked from the individual entry. No database or API was queried.

## Sources searched

| Source | Result | Limitation |
|---|---:|---|
| Visit Philadelphia, A Guide to Black-Owned Shops & Boutiques in Philadelphia | 57 retained from guide | Guide includes Greater Philadelphia entries; Lansdale, Ardmore, Upper Darby, and West Chester records were excluded from this Philadelphia-only pass. |
| City of Philadelphia, Celebrate National Black Business Month | contextual source | Points readers to Visit Philadelphia, Free Library, Campus Philly, chambers, and corridor directories; not itself an individually attributable business list. |
| Beech Community Services Black Business Directory | contextual source | 2021 PDF directory was identified, but accessible page did not expose individual listings with official customer destinations in extractable HTML; no unsupported records added. |
| African American Chamber of Commerce directory | contextual source | Membership directory is a useful follow-up, but chamber membership alone is not ownership evidence and listings were not imported without independently attributable destinations and published locations. |

## Counts and exclusions

**Retained candidates:** 57. **Excluded:** 6 guide entries outside Philadelphia or without a qualifying official destination. Excluded guide names: The Black Reserve Bookstore, Kylie Rose Fashion, Abiyah Naturals, Girls Auto Clinic Repair Center, QueenStylista's Mane Artistry, FarmerJawn Agriculture.

## Evidence and caveats

The guide’s editorial framing is the ownership evidence; this file does not infer ownership from names, neighborhoods, or chamber membership. Official destinations are first-party domains or the business’s linked Instagram/Facebook page. Google Maps links were used only as the guide’s location corroboration and are not used as official customer destinations. Health, beauty, spa, skin, waxing, and hair entries are marked `regulated_review` conservatively for later verification; no license, insurance, availability, hours, or service claim is inferred. The source is an editorial guide and may be stale; this is research expansion, not a claim that any candidate is currently live.
