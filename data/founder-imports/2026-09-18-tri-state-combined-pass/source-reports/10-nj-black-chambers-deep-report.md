# New Jersey Black Chamber and Public Directory Expansion

## Conclusion

This pass produced **6 substantiated candidate records**. Three are physical commercial candidates with street addresses and customer-facing URLs. Three are online-business candidates because the public source pages did not publish a street address; they are not assigned map coordinates. The records are research leads, not a claim that any listing is live or eligible for publication without additional review.

## Sources searched and results

| Source | What was reviewed | Result |
|---|---|---|
| [African-American Chamber of Commerce of PA, NJ, & DE active directory][1] | Directory home plus Business & Professional Services and Construction category pages | Two NJ businesses met the address and customer-URL standard. The restaurant and specialty-retail categories returned no NJ-addressed qualifying records in the pages reviewed. |
| [North Jersey Alumnae Chapter Black Owned Business Directory][2] | Public listing page and all visible entries, including its 15-business count | Three entries were retained as online businesses because each had a listed official website but no published street address. |

| [NJ In Color BIPOC Business Directory][4] | Featured and visible directory entries | Not used for final records: entries generally supplied city-level map links rather than street addresses, and the directory’s BIPOC designation does not establish Black ownership for an individual listing. Regulated entries were also excluded where a street address was absent. |
| [Black Community Shoppe New Jersey page][5] | State landing page | No individual NJ listings were rendered; only a submission form was available. |

## Included records

The African-American Chamber directory supplied **Jones Gold Enterprises LLC** in Sicklerville and **Bravo Charlie Executive Services** in Berlin. Jones Gold’s first-party site was accessible and describes executive coaching, motivational speaking, and booking. Bravo Charlie’s chamber-provided URL was retained as a customer destination, but the site did not resolve during this pass; it is explicitly flagged for manual verification. Chamber membership is not treated as ownership evidence.

NJPACCI supplied **Del Rose Cleaning Services LLC** at 331 Newman Springs Road in Red Bank. Its first-party site confirms commercial janitorial services, lists the Red Bank contact location, and links to the business Facebook page. The directory is minority-owned rather than Black-specific, so the JSONL preserves that narrower designation.

The North Jersey Alumnae Chapter directory supplied **Love Yourself All Natural**, **Cultured Expression**, and **Jams By Kim**. Love Yourself All Natural was verified on its first-party site as an online shop with hair, face, and skin-care products. The other two entries have official URLs in the directory, but their current commerce and physical-location details were not independently verified in this pass. They remain online-business leads with no inferred city or address.

## Exclusions and limitations

The North Jersey directory’s **Black Swan Espresso** was excluded because its first-party site states “Permanently Closed,” despite retaining an online page and historical Newark destination. **Ile Olufunke** was excluded from the final inventory because Etsy states that the shop is currently not selling. The visible AAC directory entries located in Pennsylvania or Delaware were excluded from this New Jersey scope. Entries without a street address and without a clearly verified online customer destination were excluded.

The AAC directory exposes category pages and a 57-result professional-services page, but its public rendering did not expose stable individual detail URLs in the extracted text. The NJ In Color page uses city-level Google map links and broad BIPOC labels rather than individual street addresses and Black-specific designations. The Black Community Shoppe state page exposed no individual records. These limitations mean the file is a conservative expansion pass rather than an exhaustive census.

## Verification caveat

All ownership labels are copied as source designations. No ownership, identity, availability, licensing, insurance, hours, accessibility, or service claim is inferred beyond the cited public pages and first-party sites.
