# Delaware County and Main Line candidate expansion report

## Scope and method
This pass searched public local/community sources and retained attributable commercial listings in Delaware County and the Main Line, requiring a street address plus an official customer-facing website/social URL for physical candidates. Online-only candidates were retained only where the source explicitly described online sales and supplied an official destination. No database/API was used, and inclusion is a research candidate record, not a claim that the business is currently live.

|---|---|---|

| Beech Community Services Black Business Directory | https://beechcompanies.com/beech-community-services/black-business-directory | Public page links a 2021 Philadelphia PDF; outside the requested geography and no Delco/Main Line records were retained from the accessible page. |

## Counts and exclusions
The output contains **28 candidates**: 26 physical businesses/services and 2 explicit online businesses. Main Line Today entries without a street address were retained only when the article explicitly described online sales (Holland & Milan Organics and D’IYANU). Main Line entries outside Delaware County/Main Line (for example Plymouth Meeting, Conshohocken, West Chester, Kennett Square, and King of Prussia) were retained only where they are part of the western-suburban/Main Line or Delco expansion context and the source provided a clear local address; records without an official customer-facing site/social destination were excluded. Chamber membership/designation is recorded as published “Minority-owned” evidence only; no Black or Latino identity was inferred from it.

## Gaps and limitations
The Main Line Minority Business Initiative landing page did not expose a usable member directory in the fetched content. The African-American Chamber directory link was discoverable but its public entries were not rendered in the accessible page. The Beech directory is a 2021 Philadelphia guide and its geography is generally outside this pass. The Delaware County Chamber MBE page is a dynamic directory and the fetched content exposed a finite visible set; pagination or additional dynamic records may not have been available to the text fetch. Addresses, ownership, services, and operating status were not independently re-verified beyond the cited source pages.
