# Evidence Report: Germantown and Mount Airy Businesses

## Scope and method

This pass targeted Germantown, Mount Airy, and nearby Northwest Philadelphia using the starting source **Visit Philadelphia’s “A Guide to Black-Owned Shops & Boutiques in Philadelphia”**, supplemented by official customer-facing business sites and the Go Mt. Airy/Mt. Airy CDC directory landing pages. Exact listing URLs are preserved in each JSONL record. No database, API, or publication was used.

## Included candidates

| Candidate | Area | Evidence outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Uncle Bobbie’s Coffee & Books | Germantown | Strong inclusion: guide identifies the Germantown location and owner; official site confirms address, phone, cafe, bookstore, events, and social links. |
| Mount Airy Candle Co. | Northwest Philadelphia / Mount Airy | Strong inclusion: guide names owner and physical studio/retail lounge; official site confirms candles, fragrance, body care, and studio experiences. |
| The Movement Philly | Mount Airy | Strong inclusion: guide describes Black-culture-inspired products; official Facebook business page confirms address, phone, Black-made brands, and apparel category. |
| Multiverse | Chestnut Hill / nearby Northwest Philadelphia | Manual review: guide and official site confirm a qualifying local retail business, but reviewed sources do not explicitly establish Black/Latino/diaspora ownership. |
| Between Friends Boutique | Chestnut Hill / nearby Northwest Philadelphia | Manual review: guide confirms founders, address, and customer website, but does not explicitly designate the store as Black/Latino/diaspora-owned. |

## Source accessibility and exclusions

The Mt. Airy CDC business-services page and Go Mt. Airy homepage were accessible and confirm that a member business directory exists, but the directory’s dynamic membership records were not reliably exposed in the fetched page and therefore were not converted into candidates without business-level pages and customer destinations. Visit Philadelphia’s page was accessible and supplied exact listing details for the primary entries, but its “Where” links point to Google Maps; those generic map URLs were not used as business customer destinations. Instead, each physical candidate includes an official website or official business Facebook page.

No houses of worship were included as businesses. No childcare, health, legal, finance, or other regulated-service records were found with sufficiently complete business-specific evidence in this pass; consequently, no regulated-review record was fabricated. Ownership, language, hours, accessibility, licensing, insurance, availability, and services were not inferred beyond the cited published evidence. Multiverse and Between Friends Boutique are retained only as `manual_review` candidates so that the qualifying ownership question can be resolved by a later reviewer.
