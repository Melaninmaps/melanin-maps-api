# Philadelphia Black Small-Business Guides — Evidence Report

## Scope and method

This pass researched credible Philadelphia-area guides from local organizations and commerce publications, prioritizing a recent local-publication guide with explicit Black-ownership framing and customer-facing destination links. The primary source was [The Philadelphia Citizen’s “20+ Black-Owned Businesses That Do Good”](https://thephiladelphiacitizen.org/black-owned-businesses-philadelphia/), published December 5, 2025. The article explicitly presents the businesses as Black-owned and supplies street addresses for physical entries or marks online businesses as “Online.”

The candidate file contains 19 records. Each physical record has a published street address and an official customer-facing website or business social destination. Online candidates have null addresses and are not assigned map pins. Girls Auto Clinic is marked `regulated_review` because automotive repair is a regulated/high-risk service category for review.

## Other guide/source checks

[Visit Philadelphia’s “A Guide to Black-Owned Shops & Boutiques in Greater Philadelphia”](https://www.visitphilly.com/articles/philadelphia/black-owned-shops-and-boutiques-in-philadelphia/) was accessible and last updated July 28, 2026. It contains many overlapping candidates and official links, but this pass avoids duplicating that guide’s broad set where the Citizen guide already provided a recent, independently published record. Visit Philadelphia also links to the [African American Chamber directory](https://membership.aachamber.com/list); chamber membership alone was not treated as proof of ownership and no chamber-directory records were imported here.

[Beech Community Services’ Black Business Directory](https://beechcompanies.com/beech-community-services/black-business-directory) was accessible only as a landing page for a “2021 Edition” PDF. The underlying directory was not imported because its age and current business-destination verification limits could create stale records. [PIDC’s 2025 resource roundup](https://pidcphila.com/blog/resources-for-black-business-owners/) was useful as a source map and links Visit Philly/AACC, but it is a resources article rather than a business listing, so it generated no direct candidate rows. The [Philadelphia Sun guide/article](https://philasun.com/local/how-to-support-philadelphia-businesses-during-national-black-business-month-and-all-year-long/) was accessible and contains additional overlapping 2023 listings; those were excluded from this pass when the Citizen/Visit Philly evidence was stronger or when a customer-facing destination could not be verified without relying on generic directory pages.

## Exclusions and limitations

No Google, Yelp, generic directory home page, chamber account, or personal LinkedIn profile was used as a customer destination. No ownership, identity, language, hours, accessibility, license, insurance, availability, or services were inferred beyond the cited guide text. A source guide is editorial and may not establish current operating status; all records should receive normal downstream verification before publication.
