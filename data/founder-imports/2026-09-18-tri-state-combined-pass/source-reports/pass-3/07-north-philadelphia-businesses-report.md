# North Philadelphia Businesses — Evidence Report

## Scope and method
This pass researched the requested North Philadelphia scope: Black/Latino/diaspora consumer businesses and services, using a visitor guide, a public economic-development article, and official customer-facing business sites. A candidate was included only when the source supplied a physical street address in the North Philadelphia area (including Brewerytown, Lehigh Avenue, North Broad, and the Rising Sun/5th Street corridor) and a business-specific website or social destination. Ownership labels are reproduced only where the source explicitly published them.

## Sources reviewed

| Source | Accessibility and use |
|---|---|

The article has a long page and some entries lack addresses; only Tierra Colombiana was included after official-site address confirmation. |

## Candidate evidence notes
The JSONL contains seven candidates. Barkley’s BBQ, Butter’s Soul Food To Go, Caribbean Feast Restaurant, Cray Taste, and SOUTH are supported by Visit Philadelphia’s Black-owned restaurant guide. Tierra Colombiana is supported by PIDC’s Hispanic/Latino business feature and its official contact page. Down North Pizza is supported directly by its official website. Caribbean Feast’s phone number was found in the official-site search result/business contact ecosystem and should be rechecked before publication; the official site itself confirms the street address and service offering.

## Exclusions and limits
No generic Google results, directory home pages, chamber accounts, LinkedIn profiles, or shared directory social accounts were used as business customer destinations. Houses of worship were not identified in the reviewed pages; none were classified as businesses. Regulated services were not included in this pass, so no regulated-review records were created. The source material is editorial and directory-like rather than a current licensing or ownership registry; opening status, hours, accessibility, insurance, licenses, language, and service availability were not inferred. The sources may be stale or may omit businesses, and the HACE directory could not be extracted because of client-side loading.
