# West Philadelphia Businesses — Source Pass 06

## Scope and conclusion

This pass targets West Philadelphia-specific business and corridor sources rather than generic city-wide lists. The strongest current candidates are **Hakim's Bookstore & Gift Shop**, **Blk Ivy Thrift**, and **48th Street Grille**. Three additional records are retained for manual review because the evidence documents a former storefront, a conflicting or possibly closed address, or an announced brick-and-mortar closure.

The West Philadelphia Corridor Collaborative describes a hyperlocal entrepreneurship ecosystem representing businesses across multiple West Philadelphia commercial corridors, including Lancaster Avenue, Baltimore Avenue, 52nd Street, Woodland Avenue, and others [1]. Its 52nd Street directory page was accessible, but the directory itself is marked “Coming Soon” and did not expose a usable business listing set [2]. The Visit Philadelphia retail-corridor guide specifically identifies University City and Baltimore Avenue as West Philadelphia destinations and links to the Baltimore Avenue Business Association directory [3]; that directory was not extractable in this environment, so it was not used as sole evidence for a candidate.

## Accessibility limits and exclusions

The TECCDC 52nd Street page was accessible but provided no actual directory entries. The Baltimore Avenue Business Association directory page returned no extractable content. Those limits prevented systematic extraction from two of the most directly relevant West Philadelphia corridor sources. Generic city-wide directories, generic Google or map pages, chamber homepages, and shared directory social accounts were excluded as business customer destinations.

No ownership identity, language, hours, accessibility, license, insurance, or service was inferred. Ownership designations are included only where the cited source explicitly supplied them. Regulated-profession flags are false for the retained records because none is presented as a health, legal, finance, childcare, or similar regulated professional service.
