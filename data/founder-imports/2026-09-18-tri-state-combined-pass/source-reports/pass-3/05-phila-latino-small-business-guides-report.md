# Philadelphia Latino Small-Business Guides — Evidence Report

**Pass:** 3
**Scope:** Philadelphia-area Latino-owned small businesses, commercial-corridor resources, and business associations beyond previously represented sources.
**Candidate rows:** 12

## Method and evidence

This pass used two accessible, recent guide sources and one official organization site. Visit Philadelphia’s guide explicitly says it was compiled with help from the Philadelphia Hispanic Chamber of Commerce and the Association of Mexican Business Owners of Philadelphia, and supplies customer-facing destinations plus street addresses for the retail and service businesses selected here. Impacto Media’s resource guide is dated March 7, 2026 and supplies organization descriptions, addresses, phones, and websites. The official GPHCC site independently confirms its business-development mission, current programming, social links, and contact block.

All physical business rows include a published street address and a business-specific website or customer-facing social URL. The association/resource rows are classified as `community_resource`; they are not treated as proof of ownership for member businesses. Ownership designations are only recorded where the source states Puerto Rican, Colombian, Mexican, Ecuadorian, Latino, or equivalent identity/ownership evidence. No language, hours, accessibility, license, insurance, availability, or services beyond the cited guide wording were inferred.

## Sources

1. [Visit Philadelphia — A Guide to Latino-Owned Shops & Boutiques in Greater Philadelphia](https://www.visitphilly.com/articles/philadelphia/latino-owned-shops-boutiques-in-greater-philadelphia/). Accessible; includes exact “Where” addresses and customer destinations for the selected businesses.
2. [Impacto Media — Resource guide for the Latino community in Philadelphia](https://www.impactomedia.com/region/avisos-a-la-comunidad/resource-guide-for-the-latino-community-in-philadelphia/). Accessible; dated 2026-03-07; includes organization descriptions and contact blocks.
3. [Greater Philadelphia Hispanic Chamber of Commerce](https://www.philahispanicchamber.org/). Accessible official site; confirms mission, events, social links, and 141 E. Hunting Park Ave. contact block.
4. [City of Philadelphia — Association of Mexican Business Owners partner spotlight](https://www.phila.gov/2026-02-05-partner-spotlight-the-association-of-mexican-business-owners-of-philadelphia-at-christmas-village/). Accessible and recent, but not used for a physical candidate because it does not publish a street address for AEM; it corroborates AEM’s small-business support role.

## Accessibility limits and exclusions

The Visit Philadelphia page exposes Google Maps links in its “Where” fields, but this inventory preserves the article URL and does not use generic Google pages as customer destinations. The Impacto guide lists some organizations as virtual or without a street address; those were excluded where a physical candidate record could not meet the required address/customer-destination standard. Named restaurants and regulated health/therapy/spa providers in the PIDC guide were not imported in this pass: restaurant entries lacked a published street address in the fetched excerpt, while health, physical therapy, and medical-esthetic services require `regulated_review` and separate verification. Generic chamber/directory pages, LinkedIn profiles, shared directory social accounts, and unsupported commercial-corridor abstractions were excluded.
