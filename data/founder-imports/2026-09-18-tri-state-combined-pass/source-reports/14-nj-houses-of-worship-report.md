# New Jersey Houses of Worship — Evidence Report

## Scope and method
This source pass searched public faith/community directories for New Jersey houses of worship serving Black, Latino, diaspora, and broader communities. Records were retained only where the directory supplied a physical address, city/state, category, source URL, and a customer-facing first-party website. Houses of worship are classified as `cultural_place`; no regulated-service claims were made. This is research expansion, not a claim that any listing is currently live.

## Output counts
The candidate file contains **11 records**: 10 from faith directories and 1 from the community chamber directory. All are `cultural_place`, physical listings, and `regulatedProfession: false`.

## Exclusions and gaps
The Assemblies of God page listed several additional New Jersey congregations without websites (for example, Christian Mission Juan 3:16, Iglesia Cristiana Liberacion en Cristo, and Community Christian Church); they were excluded under the requirement for an official customer-facing destination. The Cape May County directory contains many more houses of worship, but most entries have only an address and phone, or partial/undated web text, so they were not retained. ABCNJ and the Episcopal Diocese pages are useful discovery sources but generally require additional first-party verification of addresses and customer URLs. Social URLs were not published on the retained source pages and were left null.

## Evidence caveats
The Black-Churches directory states that its address/contact information is gathered from Google Maps and uses a city page label of “Black Churches”; the candidate records preserve that public characterization without treating it as independently verified ownership evidence. The Assemblies of God and chamber directories are organizational/community listings; names such as “Catedral” or “Iglesia” are recorded as published naming/language signals only. No ownership, identity, hours, accessibility, licensing, insurance, availability, or service claims were inferred.
