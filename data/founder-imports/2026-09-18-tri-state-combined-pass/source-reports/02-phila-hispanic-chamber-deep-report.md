# Philadelphia Hispanic Chamber Deepening Pass 2

## Scope and method
This pass began with the [Greater Philadelphia Hispanic Chamber membership directory](https://www.philahispanicchamber.org/membership-directory), then checked first-party member websites and official social profiles for customer-facing destinations, addresses, and published service descriptions. Public restaurant/property listing results were used only to corroborate an address where the first-party page did not expose it; no database or API was used.

## Results
The pass produced **9 candidate records**: 7 physical commercial/community listings and 1 online/service-area business, with the remaining count represented by 8? Actually records are: Casa Mexico, El Merkury, Puyero, El Limon Lansdale, El Sarape Blue Bell, La Llamita Vegana, Mila's Pup Tienda, Tierra Colombiana, and Unides Para Servir Norristown (**9 total**). Categories include restaurants/food service (6), retail (1), online catering (1), and community resource (1).

All nine names were visible in the chamber directory. Physical records include a street address, city/state, category, chamber source URL, and an official website or social destination. La Llamita Vegana is intentionally `online_business` because its official site describes pop-ups, markets, special events, and private catering across the Philadelphia area rather than a fixed storefront. Houses of worship were not included. No candidate was marked as Hispanic/Latino-owned based solely on chamber membership. The only ownership designation recorded is La Llamita Vegana's own published “woman and Latinx owned” statement.

## Exclusions and gaps
The chamber directory exposes many additional member names but often hides member details behind “More Info” and does not provide a usable official customer destination in the public listing. Those names were excluded rather than inferred. Regulated services (legal, accounting, tax, insurance, financial, health, childcare) were not promoted into ordinary business candidates; they require a separate regulated-review workflow. El Merkury's fetched contact page did not display its street address, so the address/phone were corroborated from a public restaurant listing while its official website/socials remained the customer destination. Mila's website did not resolve during this pass; its official Instagram supplied the customer-facing destination and the address came from the accessible public profile/search result. Chamber membership and directory presence are not claims that any business is currently live, open, or accepting customers.
