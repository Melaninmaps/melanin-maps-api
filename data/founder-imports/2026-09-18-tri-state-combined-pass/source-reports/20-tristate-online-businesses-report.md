## Exclusions and non-inclusions

**ARTMEETSCHAOS** was not included: public first-party material showed “Available in-store & online,” and its customer-service page published an Edgewater, New Jersey mailing address. That does not satisfy the no-physical-storefront/explicit-online-only scope. **Art Meets Chaos** was therefore excluded rather than mislabeled as online-only.

Several search results were generic directories, national brand lists, third-party retailers, social snippets without a reliable state basis, or businesses with physical storefronts. They were not included. Delaware search results did not yield a candidate with all of: explicit online-only language, credible state basis, ownership evidence, and an official customer destination. Latino/diaspora results were likewise not included unless the tri-state location and explicit online-only basis could be substantiated.
