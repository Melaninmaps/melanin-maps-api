# Southeastern Pennsylvania Houses of Worship — Evidence Report

## Scope and method
This pass searched public faith/community directories and first-party congregation sites for Black, Latino/Spanish, Haitian, African/diaspora, and other community-serving houses of worship in Philadelphia and nearby Pennsylvania suburbs. Records are classified as `cultural_place`; no commercial-business classification was used. This is research expansion, not a claim that any congregation is currently live.

| [Eastern PA UMC African American list](https://www.epaumc.org/connectional-ministries/advocacy/racial-ethnic/african-american/list-of-african-american-um-churches-in-the-eastern-pa-conference/) | Regional list of predominantly African American or African-descent congregations | Reviewed; names generally lacked street addresses and official congregation URLs, so not imported |

## Counts
Ten candidate records were retained in the JSONL file. All ten are physical houses of worship and all are `cultural_place`. There are no online-only records, regulated-review records, commercial businesses, or manual-review records in this pass.

## Exclusions and limitations
Generic search-result pages, Yelp pages, Google Maps pages, and directory home pages were not used as official customer destinations. The Black-Churches directory states that its contact information is gathered from Google Maps; therefore its linked first-party websites were retained where available, while the directory remained the evidence source for the identity classification and address. Philadelphia Baptist Association entries were retained even when no official website was surfaced because the task scope permits credible faith directories for houses of worship; those rows have `website: null` and should receive later first-party verification. The First Spanish listing publishes “Hancock and York Streets” rather than a numbered street address; this is preserved exactly as published rather than inferred. The Eastern PA UMC list was not imported because it supplies city names but not sufficient congregation-level street-address/official-link evidence for this pass.

## Caveat
Directory data can become stale, and several records have no surfaced first-party website. Confirm current address, worship status, contact details, and any desired language/community descriptors before public map publication.
