# Central New Jersey Mercer, Middlesex, Somerset, and Monmouth Source Pass

## Scope and method
This pass searched public Central New Jersey chamber and community-directory leads for consumer-facing Black/Latino businesses and services in Mercer, Middlesex, Somerset, and Monmouth Counties. The primary usable source was the [MyCentralJersey Central Jersey Black-owned business list](https://www.mycentraljersey.com/story/news/local/how-we-live/2020/06/05/where-support-black-owned-businesses-central-jersey/3151371001/), which names businesses in Somerset and Middlesex Counties and links many first-party sites. Additional public checks used official business sites and public county/community listings for current address and phone details. No database or API was queried.

## Sources searched

| Source | Result |
|---|---|
| [Mercer County Chambers of Commerce](https://www.mercercounty.org/work/economic-development-sustainability/chambers-of-commerce) | Chamber lead page; member directories were largely dynamic and did not expose substantiated ownership plus customer destinations in fetch output.
| [Princeton Mercer Regional Chamber directory](https://business.princetonmercerchamber.org/member-directory) | Dynamic directory shell only in accessible text; not used for candidate rows.
| [MyCentralJersey list](https://www.mycentraljersey.com/story/news/local/how-we-live/2020/06/05/where-support-black-owned-businesses-central-jersey/3151371001/) | Main evidence source; 20+ named Black-owned businesses, with town and first-party links for Somerset and Middlesex.
| [SJM Black-Owned Business Directory](https://www.socialjusticematters.org/blackownedbusinessdirectory) | Category index only in accessible text; individual entries were not exposed, so no rows were taken from it.
| [BuyBlack New Brunswick directory](https://www.buyblack.org/new-jersey/new-brunswick) | Public listings had incomplete addresses and generally lacked official customer destinations; not used for rows except as a discovery lead.
| [Middlesex County Culture: Above Art Studios](https://middlesexcountyculture.com/organization/above-art-studios/) | Address/phone corroboration for Above Art Studios.

## Results
The JSONL contains **10 substantiated candidates**: 8 physical commercial businesses, 1 online-only business, and 1 cultural place. County distribution is Somerset 5, Middlesex 5, Mercer 0, and Monmouth 0. All ownership designations are explicitly attributed to the MyCentralJersey article and should not be treated as independently verified ownership certification.

Excluded records included businesses for which the source did not publish a street address (Performing Arts Academy and Venue 518), entries with insufficient official customer destinations, and Seed Burger and Spice Isle because public results indicate they are closed or status-uncertain. Health/childcare/education-type services were not added without sufficient address evidence; regulated review is appropriate for any later additions in those sectors.

## Gaps and limitations
Mercer and Monmouth produced no final rows under the evidence policy. The accessible chamber directories were dynamic shells or member lists without sufficient ownership evidence, and the public community directories did not expose complete listing details. The MyCentralJersey article is dated 2020 and explicitly calls itself a starting list; several official sites show later address/status changes. Every candidate therefore requires a current-status and location refresh before publication.

This is research expansion, not a claim that any business is currently live, licensed, insured, available, or still Black-owned.
