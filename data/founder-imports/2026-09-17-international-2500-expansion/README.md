# International 2,500-candidate source-backed business expansion

This is an additive public-source research batch for 48 international markets. The stated count is a target, not a reason to include uncertain listings. Candidates require a current public street address, an attributable source URL, and an official website or official public social destination. International search must use relevant local languages as well as English. Identity and ownership labels require a source; no label may be inferred from a city, name, image, neighborhood, or category. Online-only and mobile services remain separate from physical map-pin candidates. A source address is not a map pin: address-backed coordinates are added only through the guarded staging geocoding workflow after review.
