# Richmond, Virginia Everyday-Living Research Source Report

## Scope and publication boundary

This is a **research-only** Richmond, Virginia and source-supported nearby-metro sweep completed on September 20, 2026. It created no database records, listings, map pins, geocodes, or production changes. The accompanying JSONL files contain **no coordinates**. Each candidate has a numbered street address and was checked against a current official customer, government, or organization destination before inclusion. Nearby places appear only where the official source itself identifies a Richmond-area/metro location, including Midlothian, Glen Allen, and Richmond International Airport.

## Coverage achieved

The inventory covers Black-owned food where an attribution is directly supported by BLK RVA, including budget-to-higher-price examples from official menus; Mexican/Hispanic food options without inferring owner identity; grocery/market food; museums, Black history, performing arts, live music, gardens, and family attractions; youth ballet, gymnastics, and martial arts; natural-hair, barber, nail, and spa options; gym and community fitness; Veterans health, community primary/dental/behavioral care, behavioral-health crisis support; legal aid and consumer financial services routed as `regulated_review`; employment and youth workforce resources; food, housing, diaper, immigrant/refugee, translation, and financial-pressure supports; dry cleaning and laundry; auto repair/towing; airport and visitor-trip planning.

## Specific evidence notes

The food entries retain price context only where the official menu/order page published it. Mama J’s official menu shows examples from a $2 corn muffin to a $24 crab-cake entrée. Lillie Pearl’s official Toast ordering page shows examples from $11 baked mac and cheese to $32 braised short ribs. La Milpa’s official Clover ordering page shows examples from $1.50 churros to $20.99 fajitas. Planet Fitness and 5th Avenue Cleaners similarly preserve published price examples with the applicable page caveats rather than making generic affordability claims. The Children’s Museum page states its qualifying Museums for All admission arrangement, and the VA, Daily Planet, RBHA, Feed More, and Commonwealth Catholic Charities entries capture the stated access or crisis/resource routes without offering personal advice.

Regulated care, legal aid, and credit-union records are intentionally typed `regulated_review`. Inclusion means a current official destination and physical address were found; it does **not** represent a licensing determination, professional endorsement, clinical assessment, financial-product recommendation, legal representation guarantee, or eligibility decision.

## Holds, duplicates, and overlap risk

The sole held lead is **Croaker’s Spot**. Its official homepage was read, but the page did not disclose a numbered address, city, phone, or substantive current customer details. It is therefore deliberately kept out of the candidate inventory.

No existing Richmond research files were present when this sweep began, so no record-level overlap could be reconciled against prior Richmond candidates. There is nevertheless a meaningful **source-path overlap risk** for Mama J’s Kitchen, Lillie Pearl, Ms. Girlee’s Kitchen, and Soul N’ Vinegar because both BLK RVA and Richmond Black Restaurant Experience serve as regional Black-food discovery pathways. These names were included once per verified physical location, with the official customer page as the core candidate evidence. Mama J’s Kitchen and Mama J’s Market are retained as separate candidates because their official site gives distinct numbered Richmond locations and distinct restaurant versus market functions. Multi-location brands were not duplicated beyond the specifically sourced branch.

## Limitations

This is a broad, time-bounded everyday-life inventory rather than a census. Search discovery was supplemented with official pages, but dynamic pages, menu prices, hours, availability, event schedules, pricing, membership offers, program capacity, accessibility accommodations, insurance, professional status, and eligibility can change. The official websites were not used to infer racial, ethnic, language, gender, or other ownership identity; Hispanic food options are included by their explicitly described cuisine, not assumed ownership. No generic chains were added except Planet Fitness where its official local page provided a concrete metro fitness and published-price option; no vague service areas, unnumbered physical entries, duplicate listings, map-pin URLs, or coordinates are present.
