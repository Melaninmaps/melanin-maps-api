# Harrisburg, PA Everyday-Living Research Source Report

## Research boundary and reconciliation

This is **research only**. No database was accessed or altered; no record was staged, published, geocoded, mapped, pinned, suppressed, or sent to a production directory. The two JSONL files contain **no coordinates** and use the contract’s 23-field schema.

| File | Count | Reconciliation |
|---|---:|---|
| `candidates.jsonl` | 48 | 48 evidence-backed destinations with a numbered address in Harrisburg or a source-supported nearby destination |
| `held-candidates.jsonl` | 6 | 6 plausible leads held for incomplete/ambiguous current location or inactive status |
| Total researched records | 54 | 48 + 6 |

All candidate rows have a numbered street address, city, state, current official customer/government/agency destination, and a `multi-city-harrisburg-` source row identifier. Clinical, legal, pharmacy, financial-counseling, and other regulated destinations were routed to `regulated_review`; the research does not assert licensure or endorse a provider.

## Coverage achieved

The inventory covers **Black diaspora food and Black-owned options where a local tourism directory explicitly attributes that designation and the official customer destination was read**; **Hispanic/Latin food for every profile without inferring owner identity**; health, dental, behavioral-health, pharmacy, HIV-related support, health access; reentry, job search, beauty training and workforce pathways; legal aid and credit/debt counseling; housing, home repairs, food insecurity and veteran support; natural hair, barbers, nail leads, gyms and youth fitness; dry cleaning and laundry; auto repair and 24-hour towing; museums, science, theater, live music and nightlife; ballet, dance and gymnastics; transit and nearby airport travel context.

Food records include source-stated price examples where the official menu exposed them: Porter’s House lists whiting from $3.00 and oxtail from $18.00–$28.00; Marie’s Kitchen lists Haitian spaghetti $14.00–$14.50 and red snapper $35.00–$36.30; El Sol lists taco platters at $17.00–$20.00. These are source observations, not standardized price classifications. Avoid inferring food ownership, language, cuisine authenticity beyond each cited destination’s own statement, accessibility, hours, or availability beyond notes and sources.

## Overlap risk and limitations

No pre-existing city candidate files were present in the assigned multi-city sweep when this city research began, so direct candidate-level overlap could not be reconciled. **Future overlap checks are warranted** for broad regional institutions and cross-city destinations: Harrisburg International Airport (Middletown), Pennsylvania Regional Ballet (Enola), CCCS/Advantage CCS (Lemoyne), and Latin Mambo (Steelton). Within this city set, the Harrisburg Area Food Pantry and Camp Curtin YMCA intentionally share an address but are distinct organizations/services; their shared-location relationship is stated in the YMCA source.

This is a time-bounded web research sweep, not an exhaustive directory. Official websites may change hours, prices, menus, eligibility, addresses, capacity, service availability, and program status. Search snippets were never used as evidence. Some legitimate prospects were held where the official site did not publish the necessary current physical details; other categories (notably a fully verified nail/spa candidate and an active HACC campus record) remain incomplete rather than padded. No coordinates, map pins, or fabricated location evidence were created.
