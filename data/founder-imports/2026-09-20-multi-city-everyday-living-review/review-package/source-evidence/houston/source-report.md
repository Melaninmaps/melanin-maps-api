# Houston, TX — Everyday-Living Research Source Report

## Scope and method

This research-only sweep covers **Houston, Texas** plus two source-supported visitor/day-trip destinations: **Space Center Houston** (within Houston) and **Moody Gardens in Galveston**. It prioritizes single-adult social and cultural options, nurses and families needing health/practical support, parents of children 0–12, amateur cooks, beauty and spa needs, fitness, repair, and difficult-day resources. Each published physical candidate has a numbered address and an official customer, government, university, or organization destination that was read before inclusion. Discovery used credible civic/tourism, community, and subject-matter sources, followed by the official page for each record; search-result snippets were never used as final evidence.

The resulting files are **research-only**. They have not been connected to a database, submitted to a publishing system, geocoded, or used to create map pins. They contain **no coordinates**.

## Overlap and duplicate-risk review

No pre-existing Houston inventory was present in the assigned city folder at the beginning of this task. The deliberately distinct Museum District records (Lucille’s, Children’s Museum Houston, HMAAC, MFAH, Houston Zoo) and downtown records (Discovery Green, Xochi) have geographic proximity but are different named destinations. All input records use distinct names and published addresses. The HHD Northside center is the single public-clinic record used rather than separately duplicating its dental program. Potential future overlap risk exists if another city/research wave independently adds major regional destinations such as Houston Zoo, MFAH, Space Center Houston, or Moody Gardens; exact name/address reconciliation should occur in the governed downstream review process.

## Limitations

This is an evidence-constrained sweep, not an exhaustive directory. Current hours, capacity, prices, availability, eligibility, insurance, licensing, and age policies can change; users should confirm directly before travel or booking. Price references appear only when an official page visibly published them. The research does not infer racial, ethnic, language, gender, or other ownership identity from names, neighborhoods, photos, reviews, or third-party listings. Regulated services are marked `regulated_review`; this sweep makes no clinical, legal, or financial endorsement. There is no verified published candidate for towing/car sales in this pass, and the active jazz/ballet leads remain held rather than padded into candidates.
