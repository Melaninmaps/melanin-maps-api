## Overlap risk and duplicate control

- **Le Moyne’s Chophouse / The Admiral:** Le Moyne’s operates at the Admiral’s 251 Government Street address but is retained as a separate dining destination because each has a separate official customer site, name, category, service scope and phone. They are an intentional co-location, not duplicate records.
- **Villa Del Rey:** The Du Rhu Drive and Schillinger Road records are separate addresses published by the same official site, so they are distinct location candidates rather than duplicates.
- **Mobile arts/history network:** Mobile Museum of Art, History Museum of Mobile, Africatown Heritage House and Exploreum are separate institutions/sites even though tourism and municipal sources may co-list them. Africatown Heritage House’s exhibition is explicitly operated by the History Museum; it is nevertheless a separate physical visitor destination at 2465 Winbush Street.
- **City gymnastics / NAMI event venue:** City gymnastics is at 1711 Hillcrest Road. NAMI’s site mentions Medal of Honor Park at the same address for its walk, but NAMI has no current customer-facing physical street address on its own site; therefore the NAMI record is an online service, avoiding a false duplicate or map pin.
- **Existing city research:** No pre-existing Mobile candidate inventory was supplied to this task, so cross-wave duplicate comparison could not be performed. IDs, complete addresses and source URLs are supplied for later controlled reconciliation.

## Limitations and follow-up boundaries

This is a broad, source-backed sweep, not a completeness or quality ranking. Search snippets were never used as evidence for a candidate. Where a live source was blocked by a cookie/security gate (Mobile Museum of Art and Mobile County Health Department behavioral page), only accessible official governmental or direct official sources were used; inaccessible pages did not become candidates solely from snippets. The inventory does not claim Black, Hispanic, or other ownership/identity except Cozy Brown, Great Day Latte, The Breakfast Spot and Box Owt, for which Visit Mobile’s guide directly provided the relevant Black-owned designation. No restaurant ownership, price tier, language ability, accessibility, license, availability, age policy or insurance acceptance was inferred. Published prices, hours, classes, admissions, eligibility and dispatch claims are time-sensitive and must be rechecked on the linked source before any use.

The research boundary remains strict: these JSONL files are source research only, have no coordinates or map pins, and have not been connected to or used to alter any production directory or database.
