## Coverage achieved

This inventory covers Black-owned and family-owned food where expressly attributed; Jamaican, Caribbean, Southern, and Mexican/Tex-Mex food; primary care, dental, behavioral health, veterans health, and counseling; legal and financial referral leads; natural-hair, barber, nail, and yoga options; children’s museum, gymnastics, ballet, zoo, art/science, art, music, and museum destinations; nearby Port Allen, Baker, and Darrow day-trip contexts; work and community-college resources; housing-stability/day services; grocery and dry cleaning; auto sales, repair, towing, and municipal pest-control support.

Multiple budget signals are source-exact rather than inferred: Royal Taste of Jamaica lists $3.50–$35 items; Bullfish lists examples from $5 sides to $75 surf-and-turf; Tacos del Cartel lists $3.50–$32 items; Knock Knock lists $15 visitor admission and free entry for ages 0–23 months; Baton Rouge Zoo lists $7 children’s and $10 adult/teen admission; LSU Museum of Art lists $5 adult, free child and qualifying-access options; Houmas House lists tours from $20/$35. Prices and availability may change; source pages should be consulted before travel or purchase.

## Evidence notes and designation handling

Visit Baton Rouge’s [Black-Owned Restaurants guide](https://www.visitbatonrouge.com/restaurants/black-owned-restaurants/) is an established tourism discovery source and explicitly identifies Chicken Shack, Zeeland Street, Bullfish Bistro, and Royal Taste of Jamaica in its Black-owned restaurant coverage. Each of those entries was then validated against its official customer website. The `ownershipDesignations` field is blank everywhere else unless the organization itself directly states “family-owned”; no owner demographic identity, language, accessibility condition, price tier, licensing, or service claim was inferred. Tre’s Street Kitchen is included as **family-owned** only because its official site uses that exact designation; that is not a racial or ethnic designation.

Health care facilities, counseling, lawyer referral, and credit-union records are marked `regulated_review`. The report does not evaluate clinician, counselor, lawyer, or financial-provider qualifications, eligibility, insurance, fees, licensure, or suitability. The VA source was treated as a government destination, but veterans should confirm eligibility; the lawyer referral service says it is telephone/online only with no walk-ins; financial terms must be verified directly with Pelican.

The wider-area destinations are source-supported rather than assumed: BREC’s zoo is in **Baker**, West Baton Rouge Museum is in **Port Allen**, and Houmas House says it is between Baton Rouge and New Orleans in **Darrow**. Houmas House’s official page frames its historic experience around plantation-owner life; the record flags this context rather than sanitizing or independently characterizing that history.

## Limitations and overlap risk

This is a bounded, current-web sweep—not a complete business census. Official sites can change locations, hours, menus, accessibility, prices, availability, age rules, and eligibility without notice. Some official sources omit a phone number, exact price, or direct booking page; such values are null or omitted rather than inferred. Food coverage is strongest where official menu pages exposed live prices; neither every Black-owned food destination nor every Hispanic/Latin restaurant in the metro is claimed to be enumerated. “Hispanic food” is covered as cuisine/restaurant options for all profiles, not as an asserted owner identity.

The notable locations in this research may recur in other research paths or city sweeps, especially statewide/government resources (VA, Louisiana Department of Health, CareerOneStop), Visit Baton Rouge-discovered Black-owned restaurants, BRCC, BREC properties, and Port Allen/Darrow day-trip places. No existing Baton Rouge research files were present in the assigned city folder at the start of this task, but later duplicate reconciliation should check names and street addresses against all city folders and any central staging process.
