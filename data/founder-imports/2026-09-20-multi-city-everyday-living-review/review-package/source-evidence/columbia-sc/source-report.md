# Columbia, South Carolina Everyday-Life Research Sweep

**Research status:** Research only. This inventory is not connected to a database, has not been published or geocoded, and contains **no coordinates**. It is a source-backed research handoff for later review, not a public listing.

## Result and count reconciliation

The final inventory contains **40 candidates** in `candidates.jsonl` and **7 held leads** in `held-candidates.jsonl`, for **47 researched records**. Every candidate has a numbered street address in Columbia or a source-supported nearby destination (West Columbia or Hopkins) and an official customer, government, or institution destination that was read before inclusion. Medical, behavioral-health, hospital, VA, and legal-aid entries are deliberately routed as `regulated_review` rather than presumed ready for publication.

## Scope and evidence practice

The sweep prioritizes Columbia and immediately relevant nearby places only where the official source supported the location: Three Rivers Behavioral Health in West Columbia and Congaree National Park in Hopkins are retained as nearby destinations. Food coverage intentionally includes Hispanic options regardless of presumed user profile. Big Bull’s official destination directly calls the restaurant Black- and women-owned; that is the only ownership designation made in the candidate file. [6] No ownership, language access, price tier, accessibility status, licensing, or age suitability was inferred when the official source did not make the point.

The evidence set is dominated by official business, government, hospital, museum, park, and organization pages. Discovery also used credible tourism, municipal, and editorial paths, but discovery snippets and third-party listings were not used as final candidate evidence. Fields reflect what the linked official source states; service availability, pricing, eligibility, hours, insurance, reservations, and clinical appropriateness can change and require direct confirmation.

## Held candidates and why they were not promoted

Seven plausible leads remain held rather than placed in the candidate inventory. Railroad BBQ’s official page confirmed family meal ordering but did not supply a numbered street address on the page read. Chayz Lounge has an official destination but current operational status was uncertain in the research window. La Isla Bonita’s official social presence did not yield a read, current official page with numbered address. Acercamiento Hispano’s official domain did not yield readable official customer information. Tripp’s Fine Cleaners confirmed Columbia service but not a numbered Columbia storefront on the page read. Cassell Brothers’ official Columbia page confirmed service coverage but was a service-area page rather than evidence of a numbered physical business location. The official Prisma Health Baptist Hospital page supplied an intersection-style location, rather than a numbered address. [41] [42] [43] [44] [45] [46] [47]

These held records are not negative findings. They are deliberately excluded from candidates until an official customer destination establishes the missing identity, current status, or physical address. Prisma Health Baptist Hospital is also held because its official page’s intersection-style address is not a qualifying numbered street address.

## Overlap and limitations

No completed sibling city candidate file was present in the assigned sweep directory during final quality control, so no record-level overlap with existing city research could be reconciled. Several prominent destinations can appear through multiple discovery paths—especially museums, parks, and regional resources—but this inventory has one record per final physical destination and cites the official destination rather than duplicating source paths.

This is a broad but time-bounded inventory, not a claim that every Columbia provider or business has been found. Direct owner identity was not inferred. Individual doctors, dentists, therapists, lawyers, and counselors were not added; institutional regulated destinations are marked for governed review. No dry-cleaner storefront or physical home-services office was promoted because the reviewed official pages did not supply a qualifying numbered address. Faith, detailed nightlife, and individual regulated professional categories were not padded with unverified results.
