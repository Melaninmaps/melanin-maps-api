# Allentown / Lehigh Valley: College Access, Tutoring, Careers, Basketball, and Care-Workforce Education

**Research date:** September 19, 2026
**Prepared by:** Manus AI

## Result

This research pass produced **22 candidate records** and **15 held records** for everyday discovery in Allentown, Bethlehem, Easton, and the clearly identified Lehigh Valley service area. The candidate set emphasizes direct, practical routes to FAFSA and college-financial-aid help, academic support, employment transition, nurse and care-workforce education, public basketball facilities, youth leagues, and school athletic programs. It includes one source-supported Hispanic-serving nonprofit record, Hispanic Center Lehigh Valley. Its designation is explicit in the source materials; no demographic, ownership, language, price, hours, credential, availability, quality, accessibility, or safety attribute was inferred for any other record. [10] [11]

The candidate JSONL retains only destinations with a source-supported numbered address and an opened official customer-facing page. Several potentially useful leads are deliberately held when current dates, a verified local destination, or a numbered address were absent. This makes the final candidate set smaller but more actionable.

## Source review and verification

The research opened the sources below. Candidate records use an opened official destination for the organization, facility, school, college, or program, plus a public chamber or municipal directory only when it provides independent directory/designation or contact support. The `sourceUrl` field points to the primary evidentiary source for each record, while the report references retain the source inventory.

1. **Lehigh Carbon Community College.** The official Donley Center page verifies the downtown Allentown campus, numbered address, contact details, tutoring/education-support services, noncredit career pathways and ASPIRA transition support. The official FAFSA page verifies LCCC assistance and Donley Center workshop format. [1] [2]
2. **College financial-aid offices.** Lehigh’s official Financial Aid page publishes counseling, FAFSA information and Bethlehem contact details. Penn State Lehigh Valley’s official page publishes the Financial Aid Specialist, FAFSA support categories and Center Valley location. [3] [4]
3. **Learning resources.** Allentown Public Library lists Brainfuse test preparation, eLearning and homework help. JEI lists its local address, contact path and diagnostic-test process. The Literacy Center lists ESL, GED and workforce-development options, its nonprofit designation and Allentown location. [5] [6] [7]
4. **Career and community resources.** PA CareerLink Lehigh Valley publishes job seeker services, youth programs, English/Spanish service access and its Allentown location. Hispanic Center Lehigh Valley’s official site states its 501(c)(3) status and service to Hispanic and non-Hispanic communities; the opened Greater Lehigh Valley Chamber listing verifies its address, phone and mission language. Lincoln Tech lists Allentown programs and student services. [8] [9] [10] [11] [12]
5. **Nursing and health-care pathways.** LCCC’s Practical Nursing page explicitly names Donley Center and Pennsylvania State Board of Nursing approval. St. Luke’s School of Nursing, Falcon, BAVTS and St. Luke’s Training Programs publish their respective local destinations and education/training descriptions. The Sacred Heart location page verifies the Allentown address used only for training entries explicitly tied to Sacred Heart. [13] [14] [15] [16] [17] [18]
6. **Basketball and athletics.** City of Allentown sources document Roosevelt Park courts, youth athletic listings and Biddy Basketball details. East Side Youth Center, Allentown PAL, Lehigh Valley Steel and the City of Easton publish program/facility material. The City’s athletic directory, paired with opened official school pages, supports the three charter-school athletics records. [19] [20] [21] [22] [23] [24] [25] [26] [27]

## Dedupe checks

A within-category dedupe check was completed before writing the files. Records were compared by normalized organization/facility name, address, official domain, telephone number and program relationship. **No duplicate candidate record remains.** LCCC’s all-purpose Donley Center and its Practical Nursing program are intentionally separate because one is a broad college-access/tutoring/FAFSA destination and the other is an explicitly Pennsylvania State Board of Nursing-approved program at that same location. East Side Youth Center and City Biddy Basketball were also reviewed separately: the former is a standing nonprofit facility/program organization, whereas the latter is a municipal program with dates and location still listed as forthcoming; Biddy Basketball is held, not added as a duplicate candidate.

## Held leads and limitations

The held JSONL contains **15 records**. Nine leads have a municipal directory entry but lack a numbered physical address and/or a separately opened official customer-facing destination. Several other leads are held for a dated, already-past event or announcement: Northampton FAFSA sessions were for the 2024–25 form, a BAPL homework-help event had already happened, YouthBuild’s available source was a historic launch article, and the Cultural & Linguistic Workforce Development Center source was a 2023 announcement. LVHN’s student-career page is current but has no single fixed program street address, and Community Options’ STEP page is a 2009 article without a verified local intake location. These items are not rejected as unhelpful; they are reserved for a current-program and local-destination verification pass. [28] [29] [30] [31] [32] [33]

The research does not assume eligibility, admission, enrollment, schedule, cost, session availability, language availability, accessibility, licensure beyond explicit source wording, or quality. Several candidate sources publish time-sensitive details, especially training vacancies and court reservations; those must be checked directly before a tester visits or applies. No coordinates were created.

## Scope and implementation statement

This is **research only**. There was **no production database/API write, publication, map pin, geocode, deployment, native build, auth/user/session/waitlist/payment change**. The deliverables are local JSONL and Markdown research artifacts only.
