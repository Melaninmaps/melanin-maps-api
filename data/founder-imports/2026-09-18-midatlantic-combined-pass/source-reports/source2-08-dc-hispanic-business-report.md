# Source report: 08-dc-hispanic-business

## Source used

**Greater Washington Hispanic Chamber of Commerce In the Biz Small Business Directory (SBTA catalog, Aug. 31, 2017)**. Exact source URL: [https://www.gwhcc.org/uploads/1/2/9/4/129415236/sbta_catalog_final_aug312017.pdf](https://www.gwhcc.org/uploads/1/2/9/4/129415236/sbta_catalog_final_aug312017.pdf). The GWHCC resources page ([https://www.gwhcc.org/resources.html](https://www.gwhcc.org/resources.html)) describes this as a directory highlighting Washington, D.C. businesses that participated in the GWHCC Small Business Technical Assistance Program, and links to the public catalog. The GWHCC Business Network landing page ([https://www.gwhccbiz.com/](https://www.gwhccbiz.com/)) was also inspected; it exposes a quarterly spotlight but not a public paginated member directory. The catalog is a static 28-page PDF; no pagination controls or hidden pages were present.

## Collection method and pages inspected

I opened the GWHCC resources page and downloaded/opened the linked PDF. I inspected the catalog’s beauty section (PDF p. 10), food section (pp. 13–15), retail section (pp. 19–20), services section (pp. 21–27), and appendix (p. 28). I then opened the direct official destinations for each retained candidate: the Lady Clipper, Dominican Image Hair Salon, La Prima Catering, BNG Consulting, W.S. Jenks, and SunnyNite. Those destinations were reachable at collection time and were used to confirm a current customer-facing web presence and published services; catalog pages supply addresses where the official site did not repeat them. I also inspected Dapper Guru and Mario Rojas destinations; both were parked domains for sale and were held. Taqueria Los Compadres’ inspected domain resolved to unrelated generic news content rather than a business destination, so it was not treated as a candidate and was not counted as a held business row because the source identity could not be safely reconciled.

## Results

| Output | Count |
|---|---:|
| Candidate rows | 6 |
| Held rows | 2 |

The six candidate rows each have a specific public catalog source URL, a reachable direct official website or official social destination, and a numbered address from the public catalog. One row (BNG Consulting) has a catalog ZIP/address discrepancy: the catalog says 20876 while the current official site says 20879; this is explicitly flagged for review and the catalog value is retained. No ownership designation was inferred from the chamber’s Hispanic/Latino focus. The Lady Clipper designation is retained only because its own official site states “black woman-owned.”

## Limitations

This is a historical 2017 GWHCC program catalog rather than a current verified membership roster. It does not establish present operation, ownership identity, or destination suitability beyond the inspected public pages. The public GWHCC member directory is described as member-portal-only, and the public GWHCC Business Network page did not expose a complete paginated directory, so no hidden pages were guessed. Directory inclusion is not proof of owner identity, ethnicity, or current business status; all records require later reconciliation and destination checks.
