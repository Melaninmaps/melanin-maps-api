# Baltimore Black Business Guides — Evidence Report

## Scope and method
This pass covered credible public guides and first-party organizations focused on Black-owned businesses in Baltimore: Visit Baltimore, Charm City Table, Waterfront Partnership of Baltimore, and the Greater Baltimore Black Chamber of Commerce. Candidates were retained only when a physical listing had a published street address and a customer-facing website or social destination. No database/API was used and nothing was published.

## Sources reviewed
| Source | Role | Evidence used |
|---|---|---|
| [Visit Baltimore Black-owned businesses guide](https://baltimore.org/what-to-do/black-owned-businesses/) | Official destination marketing guide | Explicitly describes featured shops as Black-owned; supplies official customer destinations for many listings. |
| [Charm City Table Black Owned guide](https://charmcitytable.com/black-owned-food-businesses/) | Local dining guide | Labels the page “BLACK OWNED” and publishes names, addresses, and customer websites for Baltimore-area food businesses. |
| [Waterfront Partnership guide](https://www.waterfrontpartnership.org/blog/black-owned-businesses-on-baltimores-waterfront) | Official neighborhood/waterfront organization | Describes Black-owned businesses in Harbor East, Inner Harbor, Fells Point, and Harbor Point and links customer destinations. |
| [Greater Baltimore Black Chamber](https://www.greaterbaltimoreblackchamber.org/) | First-party chamber | Mission explicitly supports Black-owned and operated businesses; physical mailing address and social links are published. |

## Counts
The JSONL contains **21 candidates**: **19 physical business rows**, **1 online-only candidate**, and **1 community-resource organization**. The two Black Acres, three Connie’s, and two Neopol rows represent distinct addresses published by the guide.

## Exclusions and gaps
Charm City Table entries outside Baltimore city (Essex, Parkville, and Owings Mills) were excluded from this Baltimore-focused pass. Listings without a published street address were excluded as physical map candidates. Waterfront Partnership names several businesses but does not publish street addresses in the article; those names were therefore not added unless independently address-supported by the Charm City Table guide. Visit Baltimore’s guide includes many businesses without addresses, so most were excluded from physical inventory. Chamber membership or a guide’s inclusion was not treated as independent ownership proof; the source wording is preserved as the ownership evidence.

## Accessibility and verification limitations
The sources are web pages with varying page structure and no consistent accessibility metadata. The guides do not establish wheelchair access, language availability, hours, insurance, licenses, or current availability. Addresses and business status may change; confirm directly with each customer-facing destination before publication. Food businesses may involve regulated food service, but this pass did not infer licensing or assign regulated-profession status.
