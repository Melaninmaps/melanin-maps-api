# Source report: 11-richmond-black-business

## Source used

**BLK RVA**, a program of the Richmond Region Tourism Foundation: <https://www.visitblkrva.com/>. This is one independent Richmond-area public source family. I inspected the source homepage and its public category/profile pages, including <https://www.visitblkrva.com/interviews/rising-adiva-naturals>, <https://www.visitblkrva.com/rooted-rising>, <https://www.visitblkrva.com/grab-a-bite>, <https://www.visitblkrva.com/shopping>, and <https://www.visitblkrva.com/health-and-wellness>. I also inspected the linked official destination for Adiva Naturals at <https://adivanaturals.com/> and the linked Dames RVA Instagram destination at <https://www.instagram.com/dames_rva/>.

BLK RVA states that it is a Richmond Region Tourism Foundation program created to highlight the Black cultural experience, Black business, and Black culture in the Richmond region. Its public homepage displays a featured Dames RVA listing with a street address, hours, and an Instagram link. Its Rooted & Rising page links to the individual Adiva Naturals profile. The category pages were inspected, but their current server-rendered text exposed no additional business listing cards or individual profile links beyond the visible featured/editorial material. No pagination controls were exposed in the inspected pages, so no hidden pages were guessed.

## Candidate result

One candidate was retained in `source-passes/11-richmond-black-business-candidates.jsonl`.

The candidate is Adiva Naturals. The BLK RVA individual profile describes its natural personal-care and lifestyle products and links to the business site. The official site was reachable at collection time and states “Black Owned and Operated,” lists body care, facial care, hair care, soap bars, herbal-market products, and soak rituals, and gives an in-person shopping/pickup address of 1802 E. Franklin Street, Richmond, VA 23223. It also states local delivery and worldwide shipping. Because the official site itself publishes the ownership statement, the voluntary ownership designation and evidence URL were retained. No phone or direct social destination was publicly confirmed on the inspected pages.

## Held result

One held row was written in `held/11-richmond-black-business-held.jsonl` for Dames RVA. BLK RVA publicly states that Dames RVA is a curated vintage shop offering handpicked fashion, sneakers, and accessories and lists 5402 1/2 Lakeside Ave, RVA 23228. However, the only direct customer-facing destination linked from that source and inspected, <https://www.instagram.com/dames_rva/>, returned “Profile isn't available.” Under the contract, a physical business requires a reachable direct official website or official social destination in addition to its source URL and numbered address, so Dames RVA was held rather than promoted to candidate.
