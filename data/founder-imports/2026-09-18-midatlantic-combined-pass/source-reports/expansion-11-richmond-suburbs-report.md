# Source Pass 11: Greater Richmond Suburban Candidates

**Research scope.** This pass covered Henrico, Chesterfield/North Chesterfield, Highland Springs, Petersburg, and nearby Greater Richmond localities using the public Virginia Black Business Directory (VABBD), a program of the Virginia Black Chamber of Commerce Foundation. The directory describes itself as a statewide hub for Black-owned businesses. A Virginia Hispanic Chamber public directory was also reviewed for Latino/Hispanic coverage, but its rendered listings did not expose complete street addresses and customer-facing websites for qualifying suburban candidates in this pass.

## Included candidates

The JSONL contains **5 candidates**: two Henrico-area/nearby business-service or beauty listings, two North Chesterfield listings (one regulated mental-health candidate), and one Petersburg business-development listing. Each physical candidate has a published street address, city, phone where available, category, source URL, and official customer-facing website. The health candidate is marked `regulated_review`; no licensure, insurance, appointment availability, or service quality was inferred.

## Sources and evidence

1. [Virginia Black Business Directory](https://www.vablackbusinessdirectory.com/) — states that VABBD is a digital hub for Black-owned businesses across Virginia.
2. [VABBD public directory results](https://members.vablackbusinessdirectory.org/directory/FindStartsWith?term=%23%21) — public listings used for Beauty By Tish LLC, CDB Education Group, Centering Elements, and related locality/category fields.
3. [VABBD Graphic Design & Media results](https://members.vablackbusinessdirectory.org/directory/Search/graphic-design-media-275211) — public listing for FOBBS QUALITY SIGNS LLC with address, phone, website, and category.
4. [VABBD Collegiate Connections detail](https://members.vablackbusinessdirectory.org/directory/Details/collegiate-connections-4343151) — public detail page with Petersburg address, phone, description, category, and website.
5. [Virginia Hispanic Chamber member directory](https://www.vahcc.com/member-directory) — reviewed as the Latino/Hispanic source; public page exposed names/descriptions and some website/view-information links, but not enough complete address-plus-customer-destination records for inclusion here.
6. [Petersburg official Resource Directory](http://petersburgva.gov/BusinessDirectoryii.aspx) — reviewed; the queried category view returned no matching results, so it did not add a qualifying candidate.

## Exclusions and gaps

Listings were excluded when the public source lacked a street address, lacked an official customer destination, appeared to be a government/chamber/resource organization rather than a local commercial candidate, or provided only a post-office box/ambiguous locality. Money Smart with Tiffany, LLC was not included because the public directory supplied only “North Chesterfield, VA, 232237” rather than a street address; it was also marked financial/FINRA-related and would require regulated review. Cool BeanZ Cafe was not included because the fetched detail page did not expose the listing fields needed to verify a customer-facing website. Hispanic Chamber listings such as Daymar Foods and La Doña Cocina Mexicana were not included because the rendered public directory did not expose complete street addresses and official customer destinations in the accessible page.

The pass found **no qualifying online-only candidates**: no source provided an explicit online-only basis plus a customer destination. No map pin was invented. Ownership designations are recorded only as the VABBD directory’s stated scope; chamber membership alone was not treated as ownership proof. The candidate count is **5 included, with 3 principal directory/source families reviewed** (VABBD, Virginia Hispanic Chamber, Petersburg official resource directory).

## Accessibility limitations

The VABBD and Virginia Hispanic Chamber pages are dynamic directory pages; some detail pages returned incomplete fields or omitted website/address data in text extraction. The official websites were recorded as customer destinations but were not independently audited for accessibility, current hours, language, wheelchair access, licensing, insurance, or availability. All such fields remain unknown unless published by a first-party source.

## Caveat on ownership

The VABBD describes its mission and directory scope as supporting and connecting Black-owned businesses. The JSONL therefore records a directory-scope designation, not an independently inferred identity claim. No Hispanic/Latino ownership claim was added from names, language, cuisine, or chamber membership alone.
