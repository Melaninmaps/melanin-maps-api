# Mid-Atlantic regulated services — source pass 17

## Scope and method

This pass collected public Mid-Atlantic leads in pediatric/healthcare, dentistry, law, finance, childcare, and related regulated services from a public Black-business directory and first-party provider sites. Health and legal services are classified as `regulated_review`. Houses of worship were not included. No database/API was called and nothing was published.

## Results

The candidate file contains **7 sequential records**: **5 Black-owned dental practices** identified by Support Black Owned, **1 immigration law firm** retained as a regulated lead with ownership/diaspora status explicitly unverified, and **1 pediatric practice** retained as `manual_review` because the inspected first-party site did not publish qualifying ownership evidence. All physical candidates have a street address, city/state/country, phone, source URL, and customer-facing website or social destination.

## Evidence and source notes

The primary discovery source was [Support Black Owned’s Baltimore Black-owned dentists guide](https://www.supportblackowned.com/blogs/109-discoveries/331-black-dentist-you-can-support-in-baltimore-maryland), which names Jackson George, Shelton Family Dental, Advanced Dental Care of Baltimore, Perkins Dental Care, and Kelvin B. Smith, DDS, LLC, and publishes addresses, phones, websites, and service descriptions. Its editorial text explicitly frames the listed practices as Black-owned, but the guide also states it was assisted by AI and reviewed by its editorial team; therefore each ownership designation should be independently verified before publication. First-party websites were used as customer destinations where supplied.

[Jackson-George Dental](https://jacksongeorgedental.com/) independently publishes its Baltimore address, phone, Dr. Tomicka Jackson-George, and general/cosmetic/preventive/restorative dental services. [Anwari Law Firm](https://anwarilaw.com/) independently publishes its Alexandria address, phone, founder Dee Anwari, Virginia State Bar membership, and family/employment/business immigration services. [Washington Pediatric Associates](http://www.washingtonpediatric.com/) publishes its Washington, DC address, phone, family-centered pediatric positioning, and a Facebook destination.

The [DC Chamber minority-owned directory](https://members.dcchamber.org/directory/category/minority-owned-business) and [GWU DMV Black-business resource page](https://diversity.gwu.edu/black-owned-businesses-dmv-area) were reviewed as source-scope context. Chamber membership/category alone was not used as ownership evidence, consistent with the task constraints; those listings were excluded from the final candidate set where a regulated-service ownership designation could not be substantiated.

## Exclusions, gaps, and accessibility limitations

No finance or childcare lead met the combined requirements of a credible public identity/ownership signal, physical street address, and official customer destination during this pass. Washington Pediatric Associates was not promoted to a qualifying identity-owned lead because the inspected page did not publish such evidence. Anwari Law Firm similarly remains a review lead; no identity designation was inferred from its name, founder, or practice area. The Support Black Owned page is accessible as HTML, but its editorial ownership claims are not equivalent to a government license or corporate filing. Several official sites may be outdated or have limited accessibility metadata; current licensure, insurance acceptance, appointment availability, hours, language access, and physical accessibility were not inferred and remain unverified.
