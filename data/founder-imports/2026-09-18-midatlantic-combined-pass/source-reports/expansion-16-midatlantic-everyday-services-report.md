# Mid-Atlantic Everyday Services — Source Evidence Report

**Pass:** 16-midatlantic-everyday-services-candidates
**Scope:** Public Black/diaspora business sources for everyday services in Washington, DC; Baltimore, MD; Richmond, VA; and Hampton Roads, VA.

## Sources and method

This pass used two first-party destination/community guides: [Destination DC’s Black-Owned Businesses guide](https://washington.org/visit-dc/black-owned-business) and [Visit Baltimore’s Black-Owned Businesses guide](https://baltimore.org/what-to-do/black-owned-businesses/). Listings were retained only where the guide published an ownership/founder statement and a customer-facing website or destination. The Virginia Black Chamber directory and Buy Black Hampton Roads category page were reviewed as discovery sources, but their fetched pages did not expose individual listings with addresses and customer destinations suitable for import.

## Candidate counts

The JSONL contains **8 candidates**: **2 Washington, DC** and **6 Baltimore, MD**. Categories are beauty (2), groceries/food retail (5), and home services/home goods (1). Target kinds are 1 regulated_review and 7 business. No Richmond or Hampton Roads candidate met the strict evidence threshold in this pass.

## Exclusions and gaps

No childcare, laundry, HVAC, senior support, disability services, schools, or general home-repair candidate was imported because the reviewed public pages did not provide a complete combination of published ownership evidence, physical address, and an official customer destination. Health, legal, financial, childcare, and other regulated services remain **regulated_review** where applicable; Skin Beauty Bar is marked regulated_review because it advertises facial/massage/spa services, without any inference about licensure.

Several tourism-guide entries gave only a neighborhood, market, or city-level location rather than a street address. Those rows are retained as research leads with explicit address caveats and should not be mapped until a first-party location page confirms the street address. No accessibility, hours, language, insurance, license, availability, or service claims were inferred. Social fields are null where the reviewed source did not provide an official customer-facing social URL.

## Accessibility limitations

The source pages do not publish consistent information on step-free access, bathrooms, seating, sensory conditions, communication access, or disability accommodations. The inventory therefore makes no accessibility claims. Current operating status, address, and services should be verified directly with each business before publication.
