# Source report: Maryland Hispanic Chamber of Commerce

## Source used

The independent source family was the **Maryland Hispanic Chamber of Commerce public membership directory**, primarily the corporate directory index at <https://maryland-hispanic-chamber-of-commerce.org/membership-directory/corporate>. The chamber's membership-options page states that member companies receive a listing in the MDHCC website members directory: <https://maryland-hispanic-chamber-of-commerce.org/membership-options>. Individual public profile pages inspected included:

- <https://maryland-hispanic-chamber-of-commerce.org/membership-directory/corporate/2557229> (Baltimore Gas & Electric)
- <https://maryland-hispanic-chamber-of-commerce.org/membership-directory/corporate/2557336> (M&T Bank)
- <https://maryland-hispanic-chamber-of-commerce.org/membership-directory/corporate/3291418> (Maryland Hispanic Chamber of Commerce)
- <https://maryland-hispanic-chamber-of-commerce.org/membership-directory/corporate/3908760> (Media Star Promotions)

The legacy public alphabetical directory was also opened at <https://cca.maryland-hispanic-chamber-of-commerce.org/webforms/K_memberdirectory.aspx>. It exposed a larger set of names and, for some listings, direct websites and descriptions, but the fetched public page did not expose numbered street addresses and city/state for those rows. No hidden pages were guessed. The current corporate directory page rendered its complete visible alphabetical listing in one page; no pagination control was exposed in the fetched output.

## Collection method and controls

Only publicly stated facts were retained. Every promoted row has an individual public chamber profile URL, a direct official customer-facing website, a numbered street address, city, state, and country. Media Star Promotions was the only row meeting all ordinary-business controls from the inspected evidence. Its chamber profile publishes the business name, Events Services category, website, and 318 Clubhouse Rd, Hunt Valley. Its official website was opened at collection time and confirms the same address with Maryland, customer-facing experiential marketing, event planning and production, and creative studio services; the site also publishes official Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn destinations. No ownership identity or designation was inferred from chamber membership.

BGE, M&T Bank, and the chamber itself were held rather than promoted because of regulated/large corporate or community-resource classification and/or missing contract fields. The legacy directory rows were held where the public page lacked the numbered address and city/state required for a physical business, or where the entity was a nonprofit or financial service requiring a different target kind.

## Limitation

The chamber directory is membership evidence, not proof of a business-owner identity; this pass therefore records no ownership designations. The current directory exposed names and a small number of profile pages, while the legacy directory exposed additional descriptions but not sufficient addresses for ordinary map-ready candidates. Some profile fields may be member-only or dynamically hidden, so this report does not infer missing phones, states, owners, services, or locations.
