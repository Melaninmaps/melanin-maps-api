# Source report: 05-baltimore-heritage-community

## Source used

**Visit Baltimore, “Shop Baltimore’s Black-Owned Businesses”**: https://baltimore.org/what-to-do/black-owned-businesses/

The source is a publicly accessible Visit Baltimore community-focused guide. The guide’s linked individual listing/profile pages were opened and inspected for each retained row. The guide describes Black-owned shops, but directory/community focus is treated as source context rather than proof of owner identity.

## Collection method and scope

The guide page was inspected in full. No pagination control was present on this guide page; it contains sections for Sweet Treats, Apparel, Books & Paper Goods, Decor & Homeware, and Self-Care. Individual profiles were opened for Cajou Creamery, Natasha’s Just Brittle, Hiatus Cheesecake, The Dollhouse Boutique, DIFFERENTREGARD, Phina’s Fine Linens & Gifts, KSM Candle Co., and Sankofa African & World Bazaar. Drama MaMa Bookshop was inspected but held out because its published address is Savage, Maryland (Howard County), outside the requested Baltimore City and County target. Other linked entries without a profile page containing a numbered Baltimore City/County address and reachable direct official website were not promoted.

## Candidate and held counts

Candidate rows: **8**. Held rows: **0**.

Each candidate has a specific Visit Baltimore individual listing URL, a numbered street address in Baltimore, Maryland, a published phone number, and a direct official website link from the profile. No directory homepage, Google Maps URL, or Visit Baltimore social account is used as a customer destination. Ownership designations are left empty because the contract requires the individual business itself to publish a voluntary ownership claim; the source guide’s community focus and third-party wording are not treated as sufficient.

## Caveats and limitations

This is research collection only and is not a publication or production import. The Visit Baltimore profiles may be stale, and website reachability can change after collection. The source does not provide a complete Baltimore City/County census, and this pass did not infer additional listings from search results or hidden pages. Some profile pages publish amenities or historical descriptions that may not reflect current operations; downstream reconciliation must verify current destination, duplicates, operating status, and target lane.
