# Hampton Roads Suburbs Source Pass

**Scope.** This pass covered Virginia Beach, Chesapeake, Portsmouth, Suffolk, Newport News, and Williamsburg, prioritizing local chambers, public minority-business directories, official tourism guides, and first-party business sites. Research was conducted on 2026-09-18.

## Result

Five candidates were retained. Two are map-ready physical businesses with published street addresses: BJ's Hair Gallery in Newport News and Hope Fit VA in Chesapeake. Three are follow-up records: Cooke Brothers Funeral Chapel and Saving & Changing Lives are regulated Newport News services whose cited public guide supplies a customer website but no street address; Kayke! is explicitly described as an in-home shop and is therefore retained as an online/in-home record with no map pin.

The official Visit Newport News guide explicitly presents its entries as Black-owned businesses and services. The Macaroni KID article explicitly identifies Hope Fit VA's owner and describes the Chesapeake businesses as Black-owned. Kayke!'s directory listing states that it is owned and operated by Ke'Nyzjah and is tagged BIPOC-Owned. No ownership was inferred from chamber membership alone.

## City coverage and gaps

Chesapeake produced two usable records. Newport News produced three records. Virginia Beach, Portsmouth, Suffolk, and Williamsburg produced no map-ready candidates in this pass because the accessible public pages located were directory landing pages or list names without the required combination of street address, ownership evidence, and official customer destination. The YMCA Williamsburg-area list, for example, names businesses but does not publish addresses or customer URLs. Buy Black Hampton Roads confirms that all six cities are in scope, but its landing page does not expose individual listing details in the fetched public text.

## Exclusions and accessibility limitations

Several official Newport News entries were excluded from the map-ready business set because no street address was published, including BGR8 Fitness, C. C. Carter Funeral Home, Celebrations Inc., JB's Glass, Love Always Wedding Planning, M.E. Fisher Funeral Home, O.H. Smith & Son Funeral Home, Polished Natural Nail Bar, and Choice CraftZ. They may merit a later address-verification pass. The research used publicly fetchable pages; dynamic directory interfaces, inaccessible individual listings, and pages without exposed address fields limited coverage. No database/API was called and nothing was published.
