# Source report: 03-baltimore-latino-directory

## Source used
The single source family researched was the **Maryland Hispanic Chamber of Commerce Membership Directory**, whose mission page describes its purpose as promoting the establishment, growth, prosperity, and retention of Hispanic businesses and supporting entities in Maryland. The directory landing page is [Membership Directory | Maryland Hispanic Chamber of Commerce](https://maryland-hispanic-chamber-of-commerce.org/membership-directory/corporate). Individual public profile pages were opened and inspected for Maaco Gaithersburg, Nuestras Raices, Somos Baltimore Latino, and Chesapeake Employers’ Insurance Company.

## Coverage and method
The corporate directory landing page was inspected, including its A–Z listing and linked “More Info” profiles. Profile IDs linked in the rendered page were enumerated from the page HTML and public profile pages were requested. The relevant Baltimore-metro profiles were then opened individually. No forms were submitted and no production database was written. The chamber’s public source/about page was also inspected at [About Us](https://maryland-hispanic-chamber-of-commerce.org/about-us).

The output retains only factual fields published by the chamber profile or, for destination verification and community classification, the direct official website. The directory’s Latino/Hispanic focus was not treated as proof of ownership; no ownership designation was added.

## Included candidates
Two rows are included. Maaco Gaithersburg has a numbered Gaithersburg address, direct official location website, Automotive classification, and published collision-repair/painting service terms. Nuestras Raices has a numbered Baltimore address, phone, direct official website, and is classified as `community_resource` because its official site describes free Latino youth, cultural, arts, education, and community programs rather than an ordinary commercial business.

## Held rows
Three held JSONL rows are provided. Somos Baltimore Latino was held because its chamber profile publishes only Baltimore as city and no numbered street address, despite publishing a direct website and phone. Chesapeake Employers’ Insurance Company was held because insurance is regulated and the published website string is malformed (`hhtps://ceiwc.com`); correcting it would require guessing. A duplicate Somos profile is noted once in held output and not repeated in candidates.

## Counts and limitations
Candidate rows: **2**. Held rows: **3**. The chamber directory exposes a changing public membership set and the inspected page/profile responses do not establish business ownership. Some profiles are outside Baltimore metro and were not used. The research is a public-source lead pass only; later reconciliation must verify current destination reachability, duplicates, regulated-service treatment, and publication eligibility.
