# Source report: 07-dc-black-business

## Source used
[Greater Washington DC Black Chamber of Commerce Member Directory](https://business.gwbcc.org/member-directory) and individual public profile pages under `https://business.gwbcc.org/member-directory/Details/`.

## Coverage and method
Inspected the directory landing page, all 29 exposed category/listing views, and every unique exposed individual profile. No hidden pagination was guessed. Candidates require a numbered street address with city/state and a direct official website link on the individual profile. Directory and Google Maps URLs were not used as customer destinations.

## Results
Candidate rows: 0. Held rows: 22. No ownership designation was added because a community-focused chamber directory is not proof of individual owner identity.

## Limitation
Public GrowthZone profile completeness varies; profiles lacking a numbered address or direct official website were held. The directory may change after collection.
