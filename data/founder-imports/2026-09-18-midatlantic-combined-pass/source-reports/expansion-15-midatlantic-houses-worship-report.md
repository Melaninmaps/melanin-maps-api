# Mid-Atlantic Houses of Worship — Evidence Report

**Research scope.** This pass collected publicly listed houses of worship and worship ministries in Washington, DC; Baltimore; Richmond; and Hampton Roads (Norfolk and Virginia Beach), prioritizing first-party sites and official diocesan/public-history directories. Candidates were classified as `cultural_place` or `community_resource`; none are regulated services.

## Results

The candidate file contains **10 records**: **10 cultural_place** and **0 community_resource**. Geographic distribution is Washington, DC (**4**), Richmond (**3**), Baltimore (**1**), and Hampton Roads (**2**). Designation evidence is explicitly published for **6** records (Black/African American or Latino/Hispanic ministry); **4** are included as community-serving worship sites without an inferred identity designation.

## Exclusions, gaps, and limitations

Generic search pages, Yelp pages, chamber listings, and directory home pages were not used as customer destinations. Listings lacking a verifiable street address and official or official-directory basis were excluded. The search did not establish a complete census of every Black, Latino, diaspora, Muslim, Jewish, Hindu, Buddhist, or immigrant-led congregation in the four metros; Hampton Roads Latino/diaspora coverage was especially limited in the reviewed sources. No ownership, language, accessibility, hours, licensing, insurance, or service availability was inferred beyond published statements. Several diocesan entries publish no phone number; those fields remain blank. Accessibility features (ramps, elevators, ASL, language access, transit, sensory accommodations) were not reliably published in the cited pages and require direct follow-up. Current service times and address accuracy should be rechecked before publication.
