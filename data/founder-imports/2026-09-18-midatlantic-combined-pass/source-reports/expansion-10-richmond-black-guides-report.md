# Richmond Black business guides and organizations — evidence report

**Scope.** This pass researched credible Black-owned business directories, official guides, chambers, and business organizations in Richmond, Virginia, starting with Richmond Black business guides and organizations. Research was limited to public, first-party or official destination sources; no database/API was called and nothing was published.

## Result

The candidate file contains **4 candidates**: two customer-facing commercial/cultural destinations, one regulated legal-service listing, and one community business-support organization. Venture Richmond's official guide labels the first three in its Black-Owned Businesses guide. The Central Virginia African American Chamber of Commerce (CVAACC) is included as a community resource because its first-party site explicitly describes its African American business mission and publishes a Richmond address; its membership is not treated as ownership evidence for other businesses.

## Exclusions, gaps, and accessibility limitations

Venture Richmond's guide contains many additional names and addresses, but most entries lacked a directly exposed official customer-facing website or social destination in the fetched listing content; they were excluded rather than assigned an invented URL. Listings with only a directory page, map page, generic chamber social account, or guide homepage were excluded. The guide includes a clearly malformed address for Image Enhancement Center (“00 E. Broad Street”), so it was excluded. Organizations outside Richmond, such as the Virginia Black Chamber headquarters in Fredericksburg, were not treated as Richmond physical candidates; the chamber page says its Richmond office is “Coming Soon.”

Accessibility, language access, hours, parking, entrances, insurance, licensure, appointment availability, and current operating status were not inferred. The City of Richmond page provides a general language-access notice and 311/804-646-7000 contact, but that does not establish accessibility features for any candidate. Social URLs are populated only where the first-party CVAACC site itself exposes them; no social links were guessed for other candidates.
