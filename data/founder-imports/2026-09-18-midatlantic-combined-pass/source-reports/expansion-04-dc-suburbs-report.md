# Source Pass 04 — DC Suburbs Candidates

## Scope and method

This pass searched public Washington-metro-area Black/Latino business sources, prioritizing the Virginia Black Business Directory (VABBD) and checking customer-facing websites for usable destinations. The resulting six records are in Northern Virginia suburbs named in scope or nearby suburbs: Alexandria, Arlington, Tysons, Woodbridge, Aldie, and Manassas. Ownership is not inferred from names or geography: the ownership designation is recorded only because VABBD describes itself as a directory dedicated to Black-owned businesses and presents these listings there.

## Counts

Six candidates were included: three ordinary businesses, one online business, and two regulated-review candidates. The regulated-review records are legal and financial/tax services. No houses of worship were found or included.

## Exclusions and gaps

The Maryland Black Chamber member directory was reviewed, but its individual profile pages exposed a membership-package gate rather than public business addresses, phones, or customer URLs; those records were excluded rather than enriched by inference. The Montgomery County Black Business Database publicly states that it connects users with certified Black-owned businesses, but the landing page did not expose enough individual listing contact data in the accessible text, so it was not used for candidate rows. The Northern Virginia Hispanic Chamber corporate directory was member-only, and the Greater Washington Hispanic Chamber page described its mission but did not expose an open business directory in the reviewed page. Northern Virginia Black Chamber’s accessible home page linked to a member directory but did not expose listing-level public records in the retrieved text.

The VABBD page also contained entries with PO boxes or city-only locations; those were excluded because the task requires a street address for physical listings. Directory entries without a customer-facing website or social destination were excluded.

## Accessibility limitations

This is a public-web source pass only. No database/API was called and nothing was published. A directory listing does not establish hours, current operations, accessibility, language, insurance, licensing, inventory, appointment availability, or service area. The ecommerce site for 1105 Social is classified as `online_business` because its first-party page is a shopping destination; its directory address is retained as source evidence but no map pin or in-person retail availability is asserted.
