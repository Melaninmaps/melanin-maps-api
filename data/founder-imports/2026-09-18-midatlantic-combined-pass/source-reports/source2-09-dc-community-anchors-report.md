# Source pass 09: DC community anchors

## Result

This pass produced **3 candidate records** and **0 held records** from one independent public source family: the African American Historical and Cultural Institutions directory maintained by GU272 / American Ancestors. The source has a Washington, DC section with individual institution links. I retained only non-commercial cultural places for which the directory entry and the linked official destination supplied enough factual evidence for a community-resource record.

## Source and pages inspected

The source directory page was [African American Historical and Cultural Institutions][1]. I inspected its Washington, DC section and the three linked official institution pages:

- [National Museum of African American History and Culture][2]
- [African American Civil War Memorial Museum][3]
- [Anacostia Community Museum][4]

The directory page is a single long page rather than a paginated listing. Its DC section lists four entries, including the Civil Rights History Project, which is an initiative/project page rather than a distinct physical community destination; it was not converted into a candidate. No hidden or additional directory pages were assumed.

## Evidence retained

The directory identifies the National Museum of African American History and Culture as a national museum devoted exclusively to documenting African American life, history, and culture. Its official page publishes the Washington, DC address and describes its mission to share African American history and culture.

The directory identifies the African American Civil War Memorial Museum and describes its mission around the United States Colored Troops. Its official page publishes the Washington, DC address and telephone number, identifies the museum as an educational resource, and links official social destinations. The official homepage currently states that the museum is scheduled to reopen on September 30, 2026; this record preserves that limitation rather than inferring present operating status.

The directory identifies the Smithsonian Anacostia Community Museum and describes its role in exploring social issues affecting diverse populations in the Washington metropolitan area. Its official page publishes the Washington, DC address and telephone number and states that the museum documents and preserves community memories, struggles, and successes.

All three records use `targetKind: cultural_place`. None includes an ownership designation because a community-focused directory is not proof of a business owner identity. None is treated as an ordinary commercial business. Official websites are used only where directly published by the institution; the directory itself is not used as a customer destination.

## Held records

There are no held rows. The excluded Civil Rights History Project was not a failed candidate requiring a held row: the source describes it as a joint initiative, and the available evidence did not establish a distinct physical community destination suitable for this pass.

## Limitations

This source family is a historical and cultural-institutions directory, not a comprehensive current directory of every DC Black or Latino house of worship, school, nonprofit, or cultural organization. The source page does not provide systematic pagination beyond its visible DC section. Current hours, reopening status, and address accuracy remain subject to later review. The records are research candidates only and were not written to a production database, published, or submitted through any form.
