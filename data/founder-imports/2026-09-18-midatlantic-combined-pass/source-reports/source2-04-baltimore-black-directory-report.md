# Source report: 04-baltimore-black-directory

## Source used
[Visit Baltimore — Shop Baltimore’s Black-Owned Businesses](https://baltimore.org/what-to-do/black-owned-businesses/) (Visit Baltimore, public destination guide). The guide links individual Visit Baltimore listing/profile pages for several businesses. I inspected the guide page and these individual profiles: [Cajou Creamery](https://baltimore.org/listings/cajou/), [Natasha’s Just Brittle](https://baltimore.org/listings/natashas-just-brittle/), [Hiatus Cheesecake](https://baltimore.org/listings/hiatus-cheesecake/), [The Dollhouse Boutique](https://baltimore.org/listings/the-dollhouse-boutique/), [DIFFERENTREGARD](https://baltimore.org/listings/differentregard/), [Drama MaMa Bookshop](https://baltimore.org/listings/drama-mama-bookshop/), [Phina’s Fine Linens & Gifts](https://baltimore.org/listings/phinas-fine-linens-gifts/), [KSM Candle Co.](https://baltimore.org/listings/ksm-candle-co/), and [Sankofa African & World Bazaar](https://baltimore.org/listings/sankofa-african-world-bazaar/).

## Method and controls
The guide page had one rendered page and no visible pagination. Each candidate was retained only when the individual public profile published a business name, a numbered street address, Maryland city/state, phone, and a direct official website link. Directory/guide URLs were not used as customer destinations. No owner identity was inferred from the guide’s community focus. Ownership designations were copied only where the individual profile itself explicitly stated them (Natasha’s Just Brittle: “female and Black-owned”; Hiatus: “minority owned”; Drama MaMa: “Black and female-owned”).

## Results
Candidate rows: **9**. Held rows: **5**. Candidate businesses are physical businesses in Baltimore City or a nearby Maryland suburb (Drama MaMa profile publishes Savage, MD). All candidate rows have a direct official website and numbered address from the inspected individual profile. No official social links were added because the inspected individual profiles did not publish direct official social destinations.

Held items are guide-listed but incomplete for this contract: Taharka Bros., Aunt Kelly’s Cookies, Crust by Mack, L.R.L Clothing, and NuLanguage. Their guide entries lacked a qualifying numbered address and/or stated catering-only or online storefront information without a published service geography. Other guide entries were not promoted because their individual profile pages were not inspected in this pass.

## Limitations
Visit Baltimore’s guide is a curated destination guide, not independent proof of ownership identity. The guide and profiles may be stale, and some profile map links appear to be generated map links; map links were not used as customer destinations. This pass did not validate operating status beyond successful public page access, and did not independently verify that official websites remain reachable after collection.
