## Evidence and interpretation

The DC Chamber listing for [Fort Myer Construction Corporation](https://members.dcchamber.org/directory/category/minority-owned-business/fort-myer-construction-corporation-146) publishes 2237 33rd St., NE, Washington DC 20018, phone (202) 636-9535, and `www.fortmyer.com`; it also lists “Minority Owned Business” among related categories. This is recorded as directory evidence only, not an inferred identity claim.

The Virginia Black Chamber directory provides public individual pages for [PIES Fitness Yoga Studio](https://members.vablackbusinessdirectory.org/directory/Details/pies-fitness-yoga-studio-1603490?rendermode=print) and [PostNet - Alexandria](https://members.vablackbusinessdirectory.org/directory/Details/postnet-alexandria-3393568). PIES publishes its Alexandria address, phone, website, and a description of yoga, fitness, yoga therapy, massage therapy, and related wellness services. PostNet publishes its Alexandria address, phone, website, service description, and explicitly states “Veteran Owned: Yes,” while identifying Eric Bell as owner. The dataset therefore records veteran ownership only and does not infer Black ownership from directory placement.

The public [Virginia Black Chamber directory result](https://members.vablackbusinessdirectory.org/directory/FindStartsWith?term=%23%21) lists COWO & CRÈCHE at 2034 Eisenhower Ave, Suite 200, Alexandria, VA 22314-5302, with phone, website, and an explicit description as a Black-owned coworking, childcare, and event venue. Because childcare is published, the record is placed in `regulated_review`.

## Exclusions and gaps

The search surfaced Maryland’s [MDOT Minority Business Enterprise statewide directory](https://mbe.mdot.maryland.gov/) and local chamber directory landing pages, but the accessible results did not provide a sufficiently verified, individually attributable close-in Maryland listing with all required fields and a customer-facing official destination. Those sources were not converted into speculative candidates. Search results also surfaced Virginia directory entries outside the close-in suburb focus (for example Stafford, Dale City, and Fredericksburg), which were excluded. Listings without a street address or official customer-facing website/social destination were excluded. Chamber membership alone was not treated as ownership evidence.

## Accessibility limitations

The work used public HTML search/fetch access only. Some GrowthZone directory pages expose a dynamic or large result set, and one source URL is a directory result page rather than a stable individual detail URL; the source text nevertheless contains the candidate’s published facts. No login, database/API, or publication step was used. Phone numbers, websites, ownership statements, service descriptions, and addresses reflect the cited public pages and may change; the inventory does not infer hours, accessibility, language, insurance, licensing, availability, or service guarantees.
