# New Orleans, Louisiana directory research wave

**Research date:** 2026-09-18
**Scope:** New Orleans and nearby southeast-Louisiana parishes, bounded to records for which a source-supported customer destination could be confirmed.
**Status:** **Research only. These results have not been staged, integrated, or published.**

## Method

This pass used the public **New Orleans Regional Black Chamber of Commerce (NORBCC)** member directory, the public **StayLocal Black-Owned Directory**, the official **New Orleans & Company** Black-Owned Businesses guide, and official customer-facing sites where available. Every URL in the source log below was opened and inspected; search-result snippets were not used as evidence. Record facts were transcribed only when directly stated. In particular, no hours, accessibility details, languages, telephone numbers, social accounts, coordinates, licensing, or ownership information were added unless pertinent and directly stated by the cited source.

For inclusion in `candidates.jsonl`, a record needed an HTTP(S) official business/customer-facing site or official social profile. A physical commercial record additionally needed a numbered street address. The chamber is a Black-business advocacy organization and its member directory is a source basis for inclusion; chamber membership is not restated as a separate ownership certification unless a business's own site directly made a relevant statement.

## Deduplication

A feasible exact-key review was performed against the other existing local `candidates.jsonl` packages under `/home/ubuntu/directory-research-wave-2026-09-18/`, checking New Orleans-area city values and exact name/city/state/address combinations. No exact duplicate of a retained record was found in those local packages. Within this New Orleans set, each retained record has a distinct name plus city/state/address key.

## Limitations and follow-up boundaries

This is deliberately a **bounded** pass, not a complete inventory. Several tourism category pages and chamber member profile pages were dynamically limited or returned no extractable content. Some chamber category listings had an address but no exposed website/social URL; they were not retained. Two customer URLs linked by chamber profiles could not be fully extracted or resolved by the tool, so their records depend on the direct chamber-profile links and should be rechecked before any production use. The community-bookstore candidate domain conflicted with the directory evidence and was held rather than matched by inference. Health, spa/massage, and lab/phlebotomy candidates were held when a verified customer destination was missing and/or a regulated-review pathway was appropriate. No coordinate/pin data were created.

The JSONL files are **research inputs only**: they have not been staged, published, or otherwise made public.
