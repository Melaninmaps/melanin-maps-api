# Columbia, South Carolina — Local Directory Research Wave

## Result

This bounded single-pass review retained **34** candidate records and held **7** records for further review. The retained output includes businesses, community resources, cultural places, and **8** records routed to `regulated_review` because the public description involved health, legal, beauty/spa/barber, towing, liquor, or another potentially regulated offering.

## Coverage and evidence limits

Research focused on Columbia and nearby suburbs, including West Columbia, Cayce, Irmo, and Lexington. It used public tourism content, a Chamber category directory, City and University sources, first-party business sites, and public social destinations. A Chamber category of “Minority-Owned Business” or “Women-Owned Business” is preserved only as a **Chamber-provided designation**. It is not treated as independently verified ownership. Explicit first-party or publisher language is identified separately in each record.

The work did not infer ownership, ethnicity, language, hours, quality, health outcomes, professional licensure, alcohol authorization, availability, accessibility, or service area beyond what cited public pages state. Several first-party sites and tourism entries had stale, incomplete, or conflicting location/status information. Those records were held where a numbered current physical address, an official customer destination, or current operating status could not be responsibly established. The City’s 2022 MBO PDF was inspected as a municipal resource, but its contents describe procurement contacts and technical assistance rather than a current business directory; it was not used to promote business candidates.

A duplicate check used only existing research JSONL data in `/home/ubuntu/directory-research-wave-2026-09-18/` before writing this city output. No prior Columbia output existed. Exact duplicate keys were normalized as name + city + state + address, and no exact duplicate was written within this output. No production database, API, authentication, or publication action was used.
