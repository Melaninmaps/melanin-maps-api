# Tri-State Source-Backed Research Workspace

**Status:** Collection and review only. No record in this workspace has been written to the production database, made publicly searchable, placed on a map, or added to Kinfolk’s live recommendations.

## Geographic scope

The active collection begins with Philadelphia and its Southeastern Pennsylvania suburbs, all of New Jersey, and all of Delaware. The earlier nationwide research workflow has been paused so this tri-state scope receives the full attention first.

## Inclusion scope

The collection covers commercial businesses across everyday needs from childcare to elder services, including food, health, education, beauty, law, trades, finance, retail, grocery, culture, and nightlife. It also includes publicly listed houses of worship and community anchors where source evidence is available. Faith organizations, nonprofits, family support, disability and elder support, community centers, cultural institutions, libraries, mutual-aid organizations, civic support, food support, shelters, and education/training programs must be recorded as `community_resource` or `cultural_place`, rather than being presented as commercial business listings.

A physical commercial business is eligible for later map-pin review only when a public exact street address and source-supported customer destination are available. A legitimate online-only shop or service may be collected as `online_business` when it has a public official website or social destination, but it must not receive an invented address, map pin, or directions action. Regulated professions remain in `regulated_review` until the protected review process confirms the required evidence.

## Publication boundary

The collection does not alter users, authentication, passwords, tester access, sessions, waitlists, access records, or existing published businesses. Candidate data will be normalized, deduplicated, source-checked, and assembled into a specific review package before a founder/admin and the release process publish an approved subset together.
