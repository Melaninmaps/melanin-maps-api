## Pages and ranges traversed

The landing page was opened first. Its public navigation exposed A–Z links and category links. The public All-results URL (`FindStartsWith?term=%23%21`) was then opened; it reported **384** results and was used as the broad directory sweep. Restaurants (9), Shopping & Specialty Retail (4), Non-Profit (20), Education (13), Government, Education & Individuals (10), Economic Development (4), Arts, Culture & Entertainment (6), Health & Wellness (11), and Individual/Business Professional (2) were opened in full as public category pages. The category records were cross-checked against the All-results page where visible. Detail links were not needed for retained records because the public category result cards exposed the business fields; the required independent verification was performed by opening each retained business’s official website. No authenticated or production system was accessed.

## Omissions, blocks, and caveats

The directory’s All page exposed many additional members, but this pass did not retain records that lacked a public exact street address or an independently openable official customer website/social destination. Examples visible in the public results included Jovial Med Spa, Lil Trent’s Grille & Pizzeria, MWCW, The Bite Club LLC, and others with no usable address and/or no official destination in the exposed card. The directory also included organizational and governmental/resource records such as Community Behavioral Health, Philadelphia Orchestra and Ensemble Arts, Philadelphia Regional Port Authority, and Philadelphia Parking Authority; these were not retained as business candidates in this pass. Some member cards used malformed address text (for example, an email address in the address line), which was treated as unusable rather than corrected by inference.

Bellgoo, Inc. was visible with a Dover, Delaware address and directory website, but its opened URL returned only a minimal `bellgoo.com` page linking to a hosting/redirect-style URL. It was held out because a current, meaningful official customer destination could not be independently verified. Philly Fashion Week’s directory card did not show an address, but its official website independently published 901 Market Street, Philadelphia, so it was retained. Poppa’s Custard Company’s official site did not repeat the chamber’s street address, but the chamber card did, and the official site independently verified the business identity and customer-facing offerings, so it was retained.

The directory is a voluntary membership source. No identity, ownership, ethnicity, language, accessibility, license status, or service claim was inferred. The only ownership designation included is Hair Du Jour’s explicitly published “Black-owned” statement on its official site. Regulated-profession records were not retained in this pass; no license validity is claimed. No coordinates were generated.
