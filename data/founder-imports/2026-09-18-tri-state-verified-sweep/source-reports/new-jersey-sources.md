## Method and limitations

Every imported physical candidate has a public street address in the cited source, except Holmes & Company, which is explicitly marked `manual_review` because the verified source published only “Cherry Hill, New Jersey.” Regulated counseling and CPA records use `regulated_review`; no licensing status was inferred. Ownership designations are included only where the source directly used Black-owned, Black identity, or minority-business language. Hours and languages are left null unless explicitly published. The Newark tourism guide was reviewed but its dynamic business results did not expose a usable set of individual details in the fetched page. This is a research staging file, not a production import.
