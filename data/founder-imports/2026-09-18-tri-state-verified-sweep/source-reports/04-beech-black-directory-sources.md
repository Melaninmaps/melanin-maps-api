# Beech Companies Black Business Directory — source report

## Source and traversal

This pass used the Beech Companies directory landing page ([landing page](https://beechcompanies.com/beech-community-services/black-business-directory)) and its linked [2021 Black Business Directory PDF](https://beechcompanies.com/files/2021-black-business-directory.pdf). The landing page was opened directly; it exposes the 2021 edition as a PDF rather than a live paginated/detail directory. The complete PDF was downloaded and text-extracted for review (all pages/sections traversed; no public pagination or business-detail URLs were exposed). The PDF's Philadelphia-region business sections and community-oriented listings were reviewed, then official destinations were independently opened for each retained row.

## Retained candidates

Five candidates were retained: **5 physical records, 0 online-only records, and 0 held records**. By target kind: 4 businesses and 1 community resource. By category: professional services/accounting (1), business support (1), arts and culture (1), events and hospitality (1), and home services/pest control (1). The community-resource row is kept separate from commercial business map pins. No houses of worship, libraries, shelters, mutual-aid groups, funeral services, or other additional resource records were retained because the reviewed PDF entries did not provide a qualifying combination of exact public street address and an openable current official destination for those types.

## Omissions and blocks

The source is a static 2021 PDF, so it has no live pagination, searchable detail pages, or update timestamps. Many PDF entries have no website or only an email/phone; those were omitted because the task requires an independently opened current official website or official public social destination. Some listed domains were unreachable or did not provide an independently verifiable current destination during this pass (including A1 Credit Doctor, Treasures on the Ave, and South Kitchen & Jazz Bar), so they were not retained. The PDF itself supplies the source-specific Black-owned/operated designation; no identity, language, license validity, accessibility, hours, or other attributes were inferred. Coordinates were not invented.
