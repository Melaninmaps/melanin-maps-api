# Source report: New Jersey Hispanic Chamber directory

**Source family:** Statewide Hispanic Chamber of Commerce of New Jersey (SHCCNJ) public Business Link directory.

**URLs used:**
- https://business.shccnj.org/list (directory landing page; categories and A–Z navigation).
- https://business.shccnj.org/list/searchalpha/0-9 and `/a` through `/z` (all 36 alphabetical pages traversed).
- Each retained record links its public detail URL in the JSONL `sourceUrl`; detail pages were opened programmatically and their linked official destinations were independently requested/opened.
- https://shccnj.org/membership/ (official directory context; confirms Business Link).

**Coverage and counts:** Traversed 388 unique public member detail links discovered across all A–Z pages; retained 279 candidates after requiring exact New Jersey street address and an independently opened official website/social destination. Physical candidates: 279; online candidates: 0; held: 0. Target-kind counts: business=257, community_resource=22.

**Omissions and blocks:**
- Directory pages contain many records outside New Jersey, city/state-only addresses, records without a destination, and Chamber boilerplate social links; these were omitted rather than treated as evidence of an official business destination.
- Records with only a city/state (for example, Lyndhurst NJ 07071) were omitted because no exact street address was public.
- No ownership, ethnicity, language, accessibility, hours, license validity, or service claims were inferred. SHCCNJ membership itself was not converted into an ownership designation.
- Regulated categories (healthcare, dental, legal, insurance, financial, accounting/tax, counseling, veterinary and similar) are marked `regulated_review`; no license validity is claimed.
- The equivalent Latino Chamber of Commerce NJ homepage was checked, but it exposed no public member listings during this pass and was not used as a candidate source.
