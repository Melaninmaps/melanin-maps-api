# Source report: Mt. Zion Dover Black business directory

## Source and traversal

The collection source was [Mt. Zion AME Church Dover Black Owned Businesses](https://www.mtzionamedover.com/blackownedbusinesses), opened in full on 2026-09-18. The page is a single public directory page; no pagination or detail-page links were exposed. I traversed the complete rendered listing from the Child Care heading through Fashion, Food/Entertainment, Service/Repairs, Medical/Wellness, Beauty & Wellness, and Professional Services, plus the submission form and church footer. The directory itself states that the list is informational and not a recommendation. It marks some entries with an asterisk as owned or run by Mt. Zion AME Church members.

## Official destinations opened

The following public official destinations linked by the directory were independently opened: [Loretta Moore](https://vlorettamoore.wixsite.com/lorettamoore); [The Mind & Body Consortium](http://www.mindandbodyde.com); [Williamson Dental Solutions](http://www.wdssmiles.com); [Heather's Holistic Health](https://www.heathersholistichealth.com); [Parris Nail Lounge](https://parrisnaillounge.com/); [NProductions](https://www.nproductions.online/); [Mary Ann Curry Scentsy storefront](https://maryanncurry.scentsy.us/); and [Therapy for Black Girls](https://www.therapyforblackgirls.com). The directory's Facebook URL for Coco Couture was opened but returned “This content isn't available right now,” so it was omitted. The directory's malformed STIX URL was not usable and was omitted. The appliance-repair URL opened only a privacy message without verifiable business details and was omitted.
