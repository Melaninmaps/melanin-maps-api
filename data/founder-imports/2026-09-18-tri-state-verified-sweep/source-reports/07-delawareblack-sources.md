## Omissions and blocks
The directory exposes many listings without exact street addresses, only city/state, or without an official destination that could be independently opened. Those were omitted rather than geocoded or inferred. Online-only listings were not retained unless an official destination and online-only status could be established without ambiguity. Duplicate-looking directory entries (for example, repeated suffixed listings for Elite Feet, Rock Solid Contracting, Aja Myhe Wax & Beauty Lounge, ZOUP!, Couture Greek, and others) were not duplicated in this output when not otherwise eligible; the retained rows contain an evidence-summary duplicate check. No coordinates were invented. The source is a voluntary directory designation and was not treated as proof of identity, licensing, language, accessibility, or service quality.
