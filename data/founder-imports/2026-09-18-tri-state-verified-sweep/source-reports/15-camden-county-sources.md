# Source report: Camden County Black Business Month listings

## Source and traversal
The collection used exactly one source family: the official [Camden County National Black Business Month page](https://www.camdencounty.com/service/national-black-business-month/). The complete rendered page was traversed from top to bottom on 2026-09-18; it exposes a single static listing page with no public pagination or detail-page links. Each linked business destination was opened independently where technically possible. Opened official destinations included Rochester’s, Galerie Marie, LesbiVeggies, Wilson’s, Ms. Sweetea’s Facebook page, Free Haven Farms, Nuanced Café, Terina Nicole Design Studio, Aunt Berta’s Kitchen, Our Sister Bookshops, Wander Boutique, The Tea Store Facebook page, Artisan Art Gallery, and Hot Butter Soul. The SLK Signings Square customer site was also opened.

## Omissions and blocks
Records were omitted when the County page did not provide an exact physical address and no legitimate online-only retention was supportable (We See You Security); when the linked destination was unavailable or failed identity verification (Cat’s Creation; Back Alley Barbeque’s supplied URL resolved to an unrelated UK canal-festival site); when an official destination showed closure (East Coast Calligraphy, which states it closed in April 2026); when a listing’s official destination could not be independently opened/verified (Diagnostic & Professional Counseling); and when no exact address was exposed for a physical candidate (Holmes & Company). Corinne’s Place was not retained because the County page points to Aunt Berta’s Kitchen rather than an independently verified Corinne’s official destination. Little Hands Family Services was not retained because its supplied domain resolved to unrelated unsafe content rather than an official business site.

Several current-address discrepancies are explicitly flagged in row evidence: Rochester’s (County 130 vs official 150 White Horse Pike), LesbiVeggies (County Audubon address vs official Camden address), Aunt Berta’s Oaklyn/Clementon naming and address differences, and Galerie Marie’s stale/conflicting Maryland footer. No coordinates were invented. Ownership is recorded only as the County page’s source-specific Black-owned listing designation; no additional identity, language, accessibility, licensing, or service claims are inferred. SLK is marked `regulated_review` because notary/loan-signing services are professional/regulatory in nature, without claiming license validity.
