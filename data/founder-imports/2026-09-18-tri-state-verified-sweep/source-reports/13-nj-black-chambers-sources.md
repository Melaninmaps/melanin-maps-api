# Source report: New Jersey African American/Black chamber directory

## Conclusion
The active public source used was the **African American Chamber of Commerce of PA, NJ & DE Active Member Directory** [1]. It is a credible, currently active GrowthZone directory with alphabetic search and public detail pages. This pass retained **6 physical business candidates** with exact New Jersey street addresses and independently opened official websites. No online-only or community-resource rows met the required evidence threshold in the traversed pages.

## URLs and traversal
The directory landing page [1] was opened first to confirm activity and its public alphabet/category search. Alphabet result pages `FindStartsWith?term=A` through `FindStartsWith?term=Z` were requested programmatically (26 alphabet ranges); the directory exposed result counts on individual pages, but the rendered alpha pages did not reliably expose all detail hrefs to the parser. Public search results were then used only to locate detail URLs, and every retained candidate was opened on its chamber detail page. Retained detail URLs were: [2] Yung’ster Joos, [3] T1explorer, [4] Philadelphia 76ers, LP, [5] The Michaels Organization, [6] New York Life Insurance Company, and [7] Bill Rawlings State Farm Insurance Agency.

The official destinations linked by those detail pages were independently opened: `http://Yungsterjoos.com`, `http://www.t1explorer.com`, `http://www.sixers.com`, `http://www.tmo.com`, `http://www.newyorklife.com`, and `http://www.billrawlings.net`. Redirects and/or live HTTP responses were checked during collection.

## Omissions and blocks
BAM DESSERTS was omitted because its public detail page gives only “Somerset NJ 08873” and no exact street address, although it has a website and social links. Several directory records were outside New Jersey and were excluded. Some legacy `.htm` detail URLs returned no extractable page (for example, Complete Maintenance Services and 1st Colonial Community Bank), so they were not retained without verifiable current detail evidence. A chamber membership listing alone was not used to infer race, ownership, language, accessibility, licensing, or other attributes. T1explorer’s Black-owned statement is preserved only as the directory’s source-specific voluntary designation. Insurance records are flagged `regulatedProfession: insurance`; no license validity is claimed.
