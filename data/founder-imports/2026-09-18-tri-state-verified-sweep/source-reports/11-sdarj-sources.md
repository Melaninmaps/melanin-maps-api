# Source report: SDARJ Southern Delaware Black-owned business directory

## Source scope and traversal

The starting source was [SDARJ Directory of Black Owned Businesses in Southern Delaware](https://sdarj.org/directory-of-black-owned-businesses-in-southern-delaware/), accessed 2026-09-18. The complete single-page directory was traversed from the title/category links through all rendered categories: Arts & Entertainment; Construction Services; Consultants; Daycare; Financial Services; Fitness; Food & Beverage/Caterers/Restaurants; Healthcare; Home Services; Non profit; General Services; Shopping & Beauty; Transportation; and Wedding Services. No public pagination or detail-page sequence was exposed. The page also exposes a [PDF](https://sdarj.org/wp-content/uploads/SDARJ-Directory-Black-Owned-Businesses.pdf) and [Excel spreadsheet](https://sdarj.org/wp-content/uploads/SDARJ_Black-Owned-Business_draft-080220.xlsx) download; these were noted as alternate source artifacts but not treated as separate source families.

The directory page exposes 58 named listings by manual count. This pass retained 13 candidates after applying the required exact-street-address/official-destination rule. Every retained row has an independently opened official website or official public social destination. The source heading supports only the source-specific voluntary designation “Black-owned”; no identity or ownership inference was made.

## Omissions and blocks

Listings were omitted when the directory gave no exact street address (for example, several consultants, caterers, home-service providers, and beauty listings), only a PO Box without a suitable online-only treatment, or no official destination that could be independently opened. Platinum Inspection Services was outside the requested Delaware territory (Baltimore, Maryland) and was omitted. Precision Power Washing linked to HomeAdvisor rather than an independently verified official destination and was omitted. Monarch Transportation’s linked site opened only to a “Website Coming Soon” notice, so it was omitted. The source has address discrepancies for Aunt Mary’s Kitchen (the directory lists Milford while its official site’s primary displayed address is Dover and mentions Milford as a location) and Southern Delaware Medical Group (directory lists 119 Neurology Way while the current MDVIP page lists 100 Silicato Parkway Suite 301); these are explicitly recorded in row evidence rather than silently reconciled. No coordinates were invented. No production systems were accessed or changed.
