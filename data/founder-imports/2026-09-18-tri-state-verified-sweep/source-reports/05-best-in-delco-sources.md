# Best In Delco source report

**Source family:** [Best In Delco directory](https://bestindelco.com/directory), Delaware County, Pennsylvania.

## Collection method and pages traversed
The public all-business directory landing page was opened, along with category pages for [Nonprofits & Community](https://bestindelco.com/directory/nonprofits-community), [Education & Tutoring](https://bestindelco.com/directory/education-tutoring), [Funeral Homes](https://bestindelco.com/directory/funeral-homes), [Arts & Cultural Venues](https://bestindelco.com/directory/arts-cultural-venues), [Childcare & Family](https://bestindelco.com/directory/childcare-family), [Senior Living](https://bestindelco.com/directory/senior-living), and [Recreation](https://bestindelco.com/directory/recreation). Each exposed detail URL was fetched. Detail-page schema.org records were used only where an exact street address was publicly present.

## Results
The pass retained **66** candidates: **24 business**, **32 community_resource**, and **10 cultural_place**. No online-only records were retained. Category counts: business / childcare-family: 1, business / recreation: 12, business / senior-living: 11, community_resource / education-tutoring: 12, community_resource / funeral-homes: 8, community_resource / nonprofits-community: 12, cultural_place / arts-cultural-venues: 10.

Every retained row has a public exact address, the Best In Delco detail URL, and an independently opened official website or official social destination from the detail page's public sameAs data. Phone numbers are included only where expressly published. Funeral-home rows are marked `regulated_review` as requested; no license or ownership inference was made. Community and cultural records are kept separate from ordinary business map pins.

## Omissions and blocks
Listings lacking an exact street address, lacking a current official destination, or offering only a third-party directory destination were omitted. The all-business page publicly states 1,664 listings over 34 pages, but its server-rendered schema exposed only the first 12 detail URLs; the category pages above were therefore used for depth. Public directory pages did not expose reliable ownership, language, accessibility, or hours fields for most records, so those fields remain null. No coordinates were invented and no production system was accessed.
