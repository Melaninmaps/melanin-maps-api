# Source report: Visit South Jersey Black-owned business guide

## Source and traversal

The designated source was opened at [Visit South Jersey — Celebrating Black History Month: Supporting Local Black-Owned Businesses](https://visitsouthjersey.com/support-local-black-owned-businesses/). The full single article page was traversed; it contains 24 named entries and no pagination or detail-page directory. Each linked official destination was opened where technically accessible. Official destinations used for retained candidates were: http://www.corinnesplace.com; http://www.freehaveneducationalfarms.com; https://thewingkitchen.com/; https://thejuicebarjuicecompany.com/; http://wilsonslivemusic.com/; http://www.lesbiveggies.com; http://www.rochestersbbqandgrill.com; http://www.nuancedcafe.com; http://www.tasteofsoulbymsnancy.com; http://www.wander-boutique.com; https://www.facebook.com/lalusvegancafe/; and http://www.houseofcheesecakes.net.

## Results

Thirteen candidate rows were retained: **12 physical candidates** and **1 online business**. By target kind: commercial_business 11 physical, community_resource 1 physical (Free Haven Farms), and online_business 1. By category: restaurants/food service 8 physical rows (Corinne’s Place, both Wing Kitchen locations, Wilson’s, LesbiVeggies, Rochester’s, Taste of Soul, La Lus); cafe 1; retail 1; bakery 1; farm/agricultural education community resource 1; online cold-pressed juice 1. The Wing Kitchen is represented twice because its official site publishes two distinct exact New Jersey locations.

## Omissions and blocks

Striped Lion Distilling was not retained because the official site says the Woodbury location is currently closed and does not publish an exact street address. Aunt Berta’s Kitchen and LaBelle Art Gallery official destinations returned no extractable content, so exact address/current destination could not be verified. Galerie Marie’s official site published an address in Pikesville, Maryland, outside the South Jersey territory, so it was omitted. Ida’s Bookshop linked destination exposed the Harriett’s Bookshop Philadelphia address but no exact Ida’s address, so omitted. East Coast Calligraphy’s official site states it closed in April 2026, so omitted. The Camden Store returned a 401 Authorization Required response. The Tea Store official page opened but exposed no exact street address, so omitted rather than inventing or converting it to online-only. Little Hands Family Services resolved to an unrelated, unsafe Vietnamese site rather than an official business destination, so omitted. Bryant’s Barber Shop Instagram location page failed to load and exposed no exact address. The Juice Bar’s official customer storefront exposed products but no exact address, therefore it is the sole online_business row and is marked online_only_no_map_pin. No houses of worship, libraries, shelters, mutual-aid groups, funeral services, or other community-resource records were exposed by this single guide page.

The guide’s “Black-owned” framing is recorded only as a source-specific voluntary ownership designation; no additional identity, language, accessibility, licensing, or service claims were inferred.
