# Houston child-care demo source notes

## Purpose

These notes preserve public, source-backed facts for demonstration candidates. Every fact below must be independently revalidated during directory review before publication. Neither source substantiates a business-ownership designation, so no ownership designation is assigned from these sources.

## Big Dreams & Lil Wonders

The official site describes published 24-hour care Monday–Friday at its Veterans Memorial location, plus infant, toddler, preschool, and before/after-school programs. It lists a public address of 11944 Veterans Memorial Drive, Houston, TX 77067, phone 281-895-6760, and official Facebook/Instagram links. The same page says it serves children from six weeks to 12 years and identifies the program as Texas Rising Star certified. Its exact current capacity and room availability are not published; Kinfolk should use conditional language and direct the member to call or book a tour.

## Result-safe chat wording

Use: “Its official site lists 24-hour care Monday–Friday at the Veterans Memorial location; call the center to confirm the room, current availability, and enrollment fit.”

Do not use: “It has a spot tonight,” “it is Black-owned,” “it is bilingual,” or “it is open 24/7” unless a reviewed source directly supports that exact fact for the specific location.
